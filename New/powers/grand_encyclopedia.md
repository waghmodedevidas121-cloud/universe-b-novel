### PART 0: THE INVIOLABLE LAW OF DIVYASTRAS (SUPREME VARADAN MANDATE)
1. RANK ALONE CANNOT UNLOCK A DIVYASTRA:
   No matter how strong a warrior becomes (even S-Rank or Buffer Zone Maharathi),

   leveling up.

   Just as Arjuna had to perform extreme Tapasya on Indrakila mountain to receive
   the Pashupatastra directly from Lord Shiva, a warrior in Universe B MUST:
   - Undertake a life-shattering Tapasya (Austerity) or pass a Divine Trial.
   - Receive a direct **Varadan (Divine Boon)** from a Supreme Deity, a High
     Council Upadhi, or an Immortal Rishi-Guru (like Parashurama, Agastya, or
     Rudra) who personally imprints the secret **Avahana & Upasamhara Mantras**
     into the warrior's soul!

   Even if a thief steals a scroll with a Divyastra mantra written on it,
   chanting it without the Deity's Varadan will either do nothing or instantly
   incinerate the thief's tongue and meridians!
