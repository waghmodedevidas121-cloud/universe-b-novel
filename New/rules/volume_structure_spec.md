## 1. THE CANONICAL VOLUME ANATOMY

A true novel volume is never merely a flat list of chapter numbers. Every volume must possess a complete 7-part narrative architecture:

```text
========================================================================================
                          CANONICAL 7-PART VOLUME ANATOMY
========================================================================================
1. [FRONT MATTER]      : Dramatis Personae (Character Dossiers + Beast Profiles) + Regional Map
2. [PROLOGUE]          : Sets tone, macro tension, or outsider POV before the protagonist's descent
3. [NUMBERED CHAPTERS] : 14 to 16 Core Chapters organized into 4 narrative Acts
4. [INTERLUDES]        : 2 Crucial Interludes per volume (Third-party POVs showing the world reacting)
5. [EPILOGUE]          : Immediate aftermath of the climax, emotional breath, and Volume+1 cliffhanger
6. [SIDE STORY / OMAKE]: 1 Character-focused lighthearted/deepening extra chapter
7. [BACK MATTER]       : Universe Glossary, Beast/Ability Progress Ledger, Author's Afterword
========================================================================================
```
