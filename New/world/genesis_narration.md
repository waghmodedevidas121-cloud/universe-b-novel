### I. Beyond the Sky: The Exhalation of the Infinite

Look up from the highest granite rampart of Hastinapur at midnight, and you will see no soft blue heaven.

Above the mortal world hangs only The Infinite Void—a vast, freezing abyss of breathless black where no bird can beat its wings and no cloud dares to drift. And suspended across that silent gulf, thousands of miles apart, hang four colossal planetary masses, anchored in space by the sheer weight of their own gravity:

To the west, the volcanic calderas of the Asura Planetary Shard bleed orange magma against the dark, its subterranean magma-forges thudding like a buried iron heart.

To the north, the floating marble plateaus of the Deva Planetary Shard gleam in perpetual, blinding solar light, untouched by dust or toil.

To the south, the fifteen-hundred-foot ironwood trees of the Beast Planetary Shard claw at the void, a primeval wilderness of fang and scale.

And at the exact geometric center of them all floats the neutral white-stone metropolis of Central City, where the Five-Arch Rotunda binds the commerce and martial covenants of a fractured cosmos.

A young student in Hastinapur, kindling a tiny ember of flame upon his fingertip before twilight, believes the world was always built of five broken islands, ancient runes, and dormant magic.

He is wrong.

To understand why the earth was butchered into five shards, why the gods never descend to save the dying, and why a mortal’s breath can shatter celestial armor, one must look beyond the Void. One must look to the very beginning—to the eternal root of Sanātana Dharma, where the multiverse itself draws breath.

### II. The Causal Ocean and the Covenant of Restraint

Outside the walls of time and space lies the Kāraṇa Sāgara—the Causal Ocean of primordial consciousness.

Upon those boundless waters rests Kāraṇodakaśāyī Mahā-Viṣṇu in deep, timeless Yoga-Nidrā. As recorded in the ancient Vedic hymns, with every exhalation of His breath, trillions of golden spherical universes—Brahmāṇḍas—rise from the pores of His divine form like bubbles in sunlit water. With every inhalation, ages end and universes dissolve back into His chest.

In the mainstream sacred cosmos—Universe A—the cycle of existence follows a merciful rhythm. Whenever Dharma (cosmic order and righteousness) declines and Adharma rises, the Supreme Preserver descends in person. He walks the earth as Rāma, as Nṛsiṁha, as Kṛṣṇa, or as Kalki, slaying tyrants and restoring equilibrium for the sake of His creations.

But at the dawn of this divergent bubble—Universe B—a profound question echoed at the apex of creation.

When the four-headed Creator, Lord Brahmā, looked upon the blueprint of the new cosmos, he spoke to Lord Viṣṇu across the radiant waters:

> "In every cycle, I fashion mortal vessels endowed with the spark of the eternal Ātman. Yet in every cycle, when darkness rises, mortals grow frail. They drop their weapons, look to the heavens, and wait for Your Avatar to fight their battles. Without Your descent, can a mortal soul ever truly conquer its own frailty?"

And Lord Viṣṇu, resting upon the thousand-hooded serpent, answered with a solemn decree that became the bedrock law of this universe:

> "Then let this Brahmāṇḍa be the Crucible of Puruṣārtha—of earned human effort. In this world, I shall bind Myself by the Covenant of Restraint. Neither Rāma nor Kṛṣṇa nor Kalki shall descend to lift the burden of mortals. And Lord Śiva shall remain transcendent, reserving His hand only for the final dissolution of all things. Let no free miracles fall from the sky. Let every soul—Deva, Asura, Beast, and Human—forge its own salvation through Karma, Tapasya, and resolute action in the face of death."

Thus was enacted the Zero-Avatar Mandate.

The Supreme Trinity—Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Śiva—are 100% authentic, omnipotent, and all-loving. Yet they stand outside the combat hierarchy. In this Bālya-Avasthā (Child Stage) of Universe B, if Mahā-Viṣṇu or Mahādeva were to descend in full transcendental reality, their sheer infinite radiance would rupture the fragile dimensional envelope of this young cosmos. Like loving divine fathers, they placed the universe upon its own feet, set the immutable laws of physics and spirit in motion, and stepped behind the veil so that mortals could learn true Atma-Nirbhar self-reliance.

### III. The Singularity Apex and the Eternal Four

At the highest dimensional point of Universe B—the Singularity Apex—lies the Kṣīra Sāgara, the Ocean of Milk.

It is not an ocean of salt and water, nor does it possess a sandy shore, a harbor, or a single wooden ship. It is a blinding, living singularity of pure spiritual energy (Caitanya) and primordial Amṛta. Any mortal flesh or steel vessel that touches its core dissolves instantly into pure light. Here, Garbhodakaśāyī Viṣṇu reclines upon Ananta-Śeṣa, the infinite serpentine foundation of reality, and from this fountainhead flows the vital life-force (Prāṇa) that animates every soul in the four tiers of existence.

Because the Supreme Trinity vowed never to micro-manage the daily affairs of the world, they established The Eternal Four within the weaponless sanctuary of the High Council Rotunda (Tier 1):

1. **Ananta (The System Core):** The non-living, soulless, incorruptible bedrock of cosmic law. Ananta has no ego, grants no favors, and projects no childish floating blue screens. It is a read-only cosmic ledger and verification engine. When a mortal crosses the threshold of deathly crisis through sheer action (The Crisis Law), Ananta registers the evolution with a single, private acoustic resonance—The World Chime—that rings like a heavy brass bell solely inside the seeker’s own skull (Ājñā Cakra).
2. **Yama (The Sovereign Judge of Circulation):** The eternal Dharmarāja who governs death, karmic audits, and the lawful entry and exit of souls across the cosmic threshold.
3. **Vidhātā (The Architect of Destiny):** Yama’s primordial friend, who spins the Silver Cord (Soul-Thread), binding a reincarnating soul’s subtle body (Sūkṣma-Śarīra) to its physical flesh (Sthūla-Śarīra) and locking in the three to five moral crossroads of its Written Fate.
4. **Chitragupta (The Grand Scribe):** Yama’s inseparable right hand, keeper of the Grand Hall of Ledgers, who indexes every thought, breath, ancestral debt (Pañca-Ṛṇa), and three-fold disposition (Sattva, Rajas, Tamas) into an append-only Soul ID.

Alongside these four eternal pillars sit three rotating constitutional offices—the Mahā-Pālaka (Supreme Guardian of Defense), the Chief Sage (Supreme Inquisitor of Truth), and the Prime Seeker (Master of Dimensional Physics)—watched over by the three plenipotentiary envoys of the Trinity: Prajāpati, Deva-Rṣi Nārada, and Rudra, the sole operational manifestation of Śiva’s containment wrath.

### IV. The Four Eras: How the World Was Broken

#### Era 1: Satya Yuga — The Undivided Horizon
In the first dawn, the world was whole.

There were no floating shards, no customs gates, and no abyssal walls. Devas, Asuras, Humans, and the ancient sovereign Beasts walked upon a single, unbroken supercontinent under one sun.

In that age, Vedic metaphysics was the natural breath of life. Practitioners understood that the eternal Ātman (The Soul Core) cannot be cut by steel or burned by fire. They mapped the five vital airs (Pañca-Prāṇa), the three central conduits (Iḍā, Piṅgalā, Suṣumnā), and the 72,000 Meridians (Nāḍīs) coursing through mortal flesh. Every soul that died passed cleanly through Yama’s court, was washed of dross, and returned to the cycle of rebirth.

Yet as populations swelled into the hundreds of millions and the great dynasties—the Solar and Lunar houses—clashed in the first catastrophic Astra wars, the dimensional envelope of the young cosmos began to strain.

#### Era 2: Tretā Yuga — The Void Cataclysm and the Seven Seals
Two thousand years after creation, the southern sky tore open.

From the outer nothingness bled Seven Primeval Horrors—living vortices of anti-existence that rotted atomic bonds and devoured light. A Grand Alliance of Devas, Asuras, Sages, and Mortal Kings marched south to destroy them, only to discover a chilling truth: the Horrors had sunk their roots so deeply into the planetary mantle that killing them would shatter the entire world into dust.

Annihilation was impossible. Containment was the only salvation.

Through millions of sacrifices, the first six Horrors were driven into subterranean tectonic vaults and bound beneath Six Cosmic Seals.

The Seventh Horror—the Primordial Void—was too vast for stone to hold. In an act of eternal penance, the immortal Emperor Shrutayudha offered his own body as the living lock. Dragging the entity into the deepest volcanic pit of the Dead Chasm, Shrutayudha was bound to a meteoric iron pillar in divine chains, his eyes sealed with molten lead, watched forever by The Stone-Eyed Crow and the unblinking gaze of Rudra.

To quarantine the poisoned southern continent, the engineers of the Great Setu (Tier 2 Buffer Zone) erected The Grand Aegis—a five-thousand-mile rampart of black basalt crowned by a 360-degree golden spiritual veil resonating at 432 Hz.

Behind that wall, thirty percent of the original world was amputated.

Millions of dying souls trapped in the southern wasteland had their Silver Cords snapped by void radiation. Cut off from Chitragupta’s ledger and barred from Yama’s pure courts lest they poison the river of reincarnation, those unindexed souls rotted in the dark for centuries. Their indestructible Ātman survived, but their subtle bodies mutated into ravenous, light-starved abominations: The Devils of Tier 4: The Corrupted Abyss.

To keep the fractured tectonic plates from drifting apart, the Asura Grand Architect Maya and the divine smith Viśvakarmā drove 108 colossal Rotary Shadow Anchors into the earth’s crust—and welded the sixth dial setting, The Black Notch leading to the Abyss, under ten feet of solid lead.

#### Era 3: Dvāpara Yuga — The Great Partition and the Ashen Eclipse
The Horrors were sealed, yet the surviving seventy percent of the world was tearing itself apart.

The biological disparity between species had become unlivable. When a high Deva walked through a mortal village, his ambient solar radiation blistered human lungs; when Asura titans struck their forge-anvils, earthquakes leveled mortal towns; when forty-ton primeval beasts migrated, entire harvests were trampled into mud. Worse still, Devas and Asuras waged bloody Mineral Kin Wars over pure Aether-crystal veins.

Humanity, facing overwhelming physical pressure, demanded separate space to cultivate their own martial arts and civilization.

By emergency decree of the High Council, the earth underwent a surgical dimensional cleaving: The Great Partition.

The single continent was cut into Five Floating Planetary Shards, cast into the Infinite Void, each held stable by Gravitational Pillars:
1. **The Human Shard (East):** The mortal cradle of Hastinapur, the coastal ports of The Sea Citadel, and the mist-shrouded mountain ashrams of Tapovana.
2. **The Asura Shard (West):** A high-gravity volcanic crucible of basalt fortresses (The Iron Citadel) and glowing subterranean forges (The Obsidian Chasm).
3. **The Deva Shard (North):** The floating marble spires of Amaravati and the acoustic silk-looms of Gandharva-Puri.
4. **The Beast Shard (South):** The towering ironwood forests of The Primeval Canopy, The Sky Spires, and The Twilight Marsh.
5. **The Central Shard (Center):** The demilitarized, neutral diplomatic island of Central City.

To allow ordinary citizens, martial artists, and merchant caravans to travel between shards without dying in the vacuum of the Void, Maya and Viśvakarmā raised four hundred-foot Monolithic Stone Gateways (Mahā-Dvāras) on the highest mountain peaks of the outer realms. By iron geopolitical law, no outer shard connects directly to another. Every gateway opens exclusively into The Five-Arch Rotunda of Central City during four daily Muhūrtas, when the great temple bells chime and a breathable blue Aether-mist corridor stabilizes for two hours.

In this same era, the conscious Beast-Folk (Nāgas, Garuḍas, Vānaras) signed The Great Beast Accord, outlawing beast exploitation and establishing the sacred science of the Soul-Bond: wild beasts voluntarily linking with human practitioners whose 72,000 meridians act as a living refinery, purging toxic heat so the beast can undergo The Molt in months rather than centuries.

Yet Era 3 ended in horror. The Grand Aegis itself had been forged through the highest sacrificial mastery of Era 3's sages and master artisans: reverse-engineering the untouchable Kṣīra Sāgara into a living, unbreakable miniature Kṣīra-Core. Far from a mere cage, this 5,000-mile golden barrier was established as an eternal act of divine compassion and cosmic justice for the trapped, transformed souls of Era 2: radiating gentle, sustaining prāṇic nectar into the Corrupted Realm so they suffer no starvation or agony, while imposing an absolute physical crossing ban that prevents any Devil from breaching the upper world unless illegally summoned by mortals.

Yet a heretical cabal of occultists—The Hollow Sect—cast a forbidden Blood-Ward Contract across the dimensional boundary of the Grand Aegis. By the Flesh-Severance Law, the summoned Devil’s physical body could not cross the barrier, but its pure, incorporeal soul slipped through the needle-hole into the city of Panchala. Within seventy-two hours, Soul-Rot Contagion drove fifty thousand citizens into cannibalistic madness. Setu Marshals descended from the sky on bonded flying beasts (Garuḍas and War-Wyverns) and cleansed Panchala with Brahmāstra-class fire, enacting the Class-Omega Taboo: erasing all public knowledge of Devils and Tier 4 under penalty of soul dissolution.

### V. Era 4: Kali Yuga — The Age of Grounded Beasts, Adventurers, and Living Quirks

And so the cosmos arrived at the present day: Era 4, the Kali Yuga.

The gods do not descend to fight mortal wars. Miracles do not fall unearned from the clouds. Yet this is not a world of powerless wretches—this is an authentic, grounded anime-fantasy world where nature, beasts, and living quirks intertwine:
- **Grounded Beast-Based Transport (Zero Sci-Fi & Zero Flying Machines):**
  - *Land Travel:* Mortals and goods move in sturdy Beast-Carts (Bailgada, Rathas, Merchant Caravans) drawn by Ashvas (Horses), Vrishabhas (Giant Bulls), Mountain Yaks, and Armored Boars. Wealthy merchants and warriors travel atop swift Gale-Stallions (Vāyu-Ashva) or Shadow-Hounds.
  - *Water Travel:* Riverways and the Mortal Sea are navigated by wooden and iron-ribbed sailing ships and barges, guided and protected by harnessed River-Drakes (Makaras), Giant Sea-Turtles, and Bridled Leviathans.
  - *Air Travel (Extremely Rare):* Zero machines exist in the sky. Only high-rank vanguard elites, arch-couriers, and guild legends soar above the clouds atop Soul-Bonded Flying Beasts (Garuḍas, Giant Falcons, Firehawks, and Wyverns).
- In place of chemical firearms, combat is waged through **Pure Martial Arts, Elemental Magic, Soul-Bonded Beasts, Runic Blades, Dhanurveda Bows, and Divine Astras**. When an archer or swordsman notches an attack charged with focused prāṇa, the edge releases a razor-thin 0.2-second crystal hum—known to every warrior across the arenas as **The Sing**.
- In place of wireline cables, dispatches cross the void via **Acoustic Mantra-Stones, Astral Courier Birds, and Clairvoyant Divya-Dṛṣṭi**.

#### The Vanguard Adventurer Guilds & Dangerous Quests
In every regional capital and frontier border town, **The Vanguard Adventurer Guilds** organize martial life:
- **Quest Boards & Party System:** Warriors register, receive official **Metal Rank Tags (Copper [D-Rank] -> Silver [C-Rank] -> Gold [B-Rank] -> Mythril/Vajra [A/S-Rank])**, and form 3-to-5 member parties to undertake dangerous contracts.
- **Three Core Quest Types:**
  - *Type A (Uncontrolled Forest Beasts):* Hunting or subduing rampaging Feral and Stage-2 Beasts threatening agrarian settlements.
  - *Type B (Cursed Zones & Ancient Ruins):* Exploring forgotten battlegrounds, subterranean dungeons, and cursed groves to reclaim ancient runic relics.
  - *Type C (Rogue / Illegally-Summoned Devils):* When rogue cultists or corrupt warlords conduct illicit blood-summonings from Tier 4, elite parties are dispatched on lethal priority contracts to exterminate the rogue Devil before corruption spreads.

#### Cultural Heartbeat: The Two Grand Hindu Festivals
The life and breathing rhythm of Universe B is synchronized across all realms by two sacred festivals:
1. **The Festival of New Dawn — Hindu New Year (Chaitra Shukla Pratipada / Gudhi Padwa):**
   - Celebrated on the first sunrise of spring as the cosmic anniversary of creation.
   - Victory Gudhis and sacred silk pennants flutter from every rooftop.
   - **The Age-12 Awakening Ceremony:** Every child reaching twelve winters undergoes formal elemental resonance testing at local temples and guild halls; new young adventurers take their binding oaths.
2. **The 7-Day Grand Festival of Lights — Deepavali (दिवाळी):**
   - Celebrated in autumn across seven full days (from Vasubaras and Dhantrayodashi through Narak Chaturdashi, Lakshmi Pujan, Balipratipada, Bhaubeej, to the Seventh-Day Grand Deepotsav).
   - **Absolute Pan-Astral Truce:** All coliseum matches, border feuds, and guild bounties halt completely for seven days. Millions of clay lamps and everyday Ember Quirks turn the five floating shards into a sea of golden light, celebrating cosmic harmony and sweet feasts.

#### The Dual-Tier Economy: Everyday Coins & Warrior Relics
- **Mundane Currency:** Daily food, Beast-Cart fares, and lodging trade in Copper Mashas (CM), Silver Panas (SP), and gold Aether-Rupees (AR). A hot bowl of spiced dal-bhakri costs 2 CM; a city Beast-Cart ride costs 4 SP.
- **Warrior & Guild Currency:** Inside Adventurer Guilds and elite forges, true transactions demand **Beast Cores (श्वापदांचे ऊर्जा-गाभे)** harvested from wild beasts, Prāṇa-Crystals, ancient Cursed Relics, and Tapovana Healing Herbs, essential for runic equipment and rank advancements.

- **Inter-Shard Gateways & Deva Restrictions:** These are mere peacetime treaties. When cosmic catastrophe erupts, dimensional corridors will bridge the shards directly, forcing Devas to cast aside their celestial pride, descend into the mortal dirt, and bleed alongside humans and Asuras!

Beside the native mortals live the **5% Aliens Diaspora**—descendants of souls who drifted from parallel universes born of Mahā-Viṣṇu’s exhalations across non-linear time, bound by Vidhātā under Half-Written Fate. Bringing foreign philosophies, double-entry banking ledgers, and intricate runic crafts, they now direct the academic halls of the Hastinapur Alien Quarter and the counting houses of the Central Reserve Bank.

Today, the five realms are bound in a strict Five-Point Codependency:
- If Hastinapur halts river grain shipments, Asura miners and smiths starve within thirty days.
- If the Iron Citadel halts runic steel and basalt armor plates, human beast-armor, city fortifications, and defenses crumble.
- If Amaravati halts solar-quartz prisms, Asura subterranean forges cannot reach divine refining heat.
- If the Beast Canopy halts frost-lotus herbs, Deva patricians succumb to brain-boiling solar fever; in return, the giant herds require thousands of tons of human sea-salt to keep their bones from turning brittle.
- And over them all sits Central City, collecting a 17% customs tariff in Aether-Rupees—while illicit trade moves through the Veiled Grid via ancient Rotary Shadow Anchors buried beneath granite foundations.

To test this martial strength without total war, the **Proving Games** roar every week inside the hundred-thousand-seat Central Coliseum. Fighters from the four regional sister-circuits—Humans, Asuras, Beast-Tamers, and Aliens, with Devas strictly barred by peacetime covenant—clash across five shifting elemental arenas. Ninety percent of those who reach Apex S-Rank become wealthy arena legends; the fiercest ten percent accept the midnight summons to join the Setu Standing Army, marching up to the 5,000-mile trenches of the Grand Aegis to hold the line against the horrors of Tier 4.

### VI. The Tremor in the Deep

On the surface, the clockwork of the cosmos appears unbroken.

At the highest apex of existence, the Kṣīra Sāgara shines in eternal, untouchable perfection—an unbroken fountainhead of pure Prāṇa that no mortal flaw can ever stain. Below it, the Eternal Four sit in silent judgment, watching to see whether mortal beings will rise through their own Puruṣārtha or choke upon their own greed.

Yet down in the lower tiers, the mortal world is straining against its own chains.

In Hastinapur, the dawn temple bells call millions of students and cultivators to morning prāṇāyāma, where a bowl of hot dal and two coarse bhakris costs two copper mashas. In the Obsidian Chasm, Ulka ("The Red Hammer") rallies mine-guild fighters seeking fair distribution of runic ores. In Central City, Investigator Devayani ("The Concord Hound") signs midnight warrants against rogue shadow anchor cartels.

And it is there—in the subterranean foundations of the mortal shards—that the fabric of space is being recklessly strained.

Every time underworld cartels force a pre-Era Rotary Shadow Anchor to spin against ancient tectonic dials, the dimensional boundary between the shards frays a little further. In the deep mantle, the basalt pressure valves of Seal 1 (The Thermal Venom) are thinning from centuries of excavation. Along the misty ramparts of the Grand Aegis, Marshal-General Vajraketu watches the 432 Hz golden barrier flicker as Paundraka, the Counterfeit God, gathers his legion of Corrupted Fiends in the dark.

And where the outer mortal membrane of the five shards has been worn paper-thin by illegal spatial shearing and forbidden blood-wards, microscopic rifts have begun to bleed into the surrounding multiverse—occasionally dragging unindexed, Blank Slate souls straight into the mud and iron of the lower world, bypassing Yama’s courts entirely.

There is no chosen savior waiting in the wings. There is no divine avatar coming to rescue the weak.

There are only five floating shards, an unforgiving void, and the men and women who must pay for every inch of survival in sweat, copper, and blood.

Welcome to Universe B.
