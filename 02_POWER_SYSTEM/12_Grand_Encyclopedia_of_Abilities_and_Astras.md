## PART 0: THE SUPREME LAWS OF SANATAN COMBAT PHYSICS

### 1. The Varadāna & Tapasyā Mandate (वरदान व अस्त्र विधान)
- **Rank Alone Cannot Unlock an Astra:** No matter how physically muscular or skilled a gladiator becomes, a consecrated **Astra (अस्त्र)** cannot be bought with coins or awakened through ordinary physical exercise.
- **The Divine Trial / Tapasyā:** A warrior must undertake rigorous mental discipline (*Tapasyā*), make an irrevocable karmic vow (*Saṅkalpa*), or receive initiation from an immortal Guru, High Council Upādhi, or Sovereign Monarch who imprints the acoustic **Āvāhana (आवाहन)** and **Upasaṁhāra (उपसंहार)** mantras into the seeker's subtle body.
- **The Acoustic Tell (Gūñja — गूंज):** Exactly **0.15 seconds** before any high-order Astra condenses its elemental payload, the surrounding ether shrieks with an acoustic displacement frequency. A trained warrior reads this acoustic tell to parry, ground, or deflect the strike.

### 2. The Four Stages of Combat Mastery (योद्धा-श्रेणी)
Instead of arbitrary video-game numbers, Trimukha Brahmāṇḍa measures martial mastery through authentic Dhanurveda tiers:
1. **अर्ध-रथी (Adha-Rathi / Feeder & Neophyte):** Master of basic physical weaponry, kinetic body-mechanics, and initial vessel channel breath circulation.
2. **रथी (Rathi / Proven Contender):** Capable of projecting external elemental Prāṇa, wielding Stage-2 consecrated weapons, and forming voluntary bonds with Stage-2 Beasts.
3. **अतिरथी (Atirathi / Vanguard Champion):** Commands multi-vector elemental combinations, *Saṅkalpa-Bandha* binding vows, Stage-3 Beast-Fusions, and minor deity Astras.
4. **महारथी (Mahārathi / Sovereign Apex):** Commands consecrated celestial Astras, spatial *Kṣetra-Bandhana* boundary domains, and indestructible *Ātma-Tattva* thermodynamic grounding.
