### 2.5 Monetary Standards: The Tri-Metal Coin System (त्रिविध नाणी व्यवस्था — Copper, Silver & Gold Coins)

- **चांदी के सिक्के (Silver Coins / Silvers):** The commercial bloodline of trade, adventurers, and arena contracts.
  - *१ चांदी का सिक्का (= १०० तांबे के सिक्के):* कुशल सैनिक किंवा लोहाराची दैनिक मजुरी (Daily wage of a vanguard soldier or skilled smith).

## 3.6 The Dual-Language Novel Authoring Mandate (दुहेरी भाषिक कादंबरी रचना-नियम)

Every chapter of *Brahmand* is authored under the following definitive two-track standard:

### Track 1: Native Modern Hindi (Master Primary Track — अधिकृत हिंदी आवृत्ती)
1. **Style:** Modern conversational, punchy, cinematic Hindi (Crunchyroll/Muse India anime dub standard).
2. **Sanskrit Lore Preservation:** Authentic proper nouns, epic/Mahabharata references, Astras, Mantras, and spiritual disciplines (*Āgneyāstra, Antariksha-Setu, Prāṇa, Nādī, Tapasyā, Dharma, Granthi, Rinānubandha*) remain in their original Sanskrit form.
3. **English System Loanwords:** Small technical, tournament, and street concepts that modern readers intuitively grasp in English (*Target, Boss, Round, Knockout, Feeder Pit, Circuit, Bounty, Rank, Deal, Black Market*) are preserved as loanwords.
4. **The 'नही' Rule:** Every negation in prose and dialogue must strictly be written as `नही` (zero bindi / अनुस्वार-रहित). Never with bindi.

### Track 2: English Novel (Derived Track — अधिकृत इंग्रजी आवृत्ती)
1. **Style:** Atmospheric, sensory-grounded, visceral modern dark fantasy English prose (*Shadow Slave / Lord of the Mysteries* standard).
2. **Untranslatable Terms:** Sanatan core proper nouns and sacred concepts are **never** translated into clumsy literal English (e.g. *Āgneyāstra* is NOT "Fire Missile"; *Dhanurveda* is NOT "Bow Science"; *Antariksha-Setu* is NOT "Sky Viaduct"). They remain in clean Romanized Sanskrit (*Āgneyāstra, Dhanurveda, Antariksha-Setu, Prāṇa, Tapasyā, Kshira Sagara*).
3. **Derived Discipline:** The English track is derived beat-for-beat from the Hindi master scene—same pacing, same emotional impact, same visceral combat rhythm.

## 5. Dual Perspective: Writer's Architecture vs. Reader's Experience

### 5.1 The Writer's Technical Architecture (लेखकाचा तांत्रिक दृष्टिकोन)
1. **Dialogue Vitality without Immersion Breakage:**
   - Writing low-tier dialogue in 100% archaic Sanskrit makes common characters feel like academic textbooks, destroying organic pacing.
   - Using Layer 4 modern loanwords (*"Target", "Knockout", "Bounty", "Boss"*) keeps mortal dialogue snappy, muscular, and full of urgency.
2. **Strict Boundary Enforcement (The Anti-Gamer Line):**
   - Modern loanwords are permitted strictly for **conversational dialogue and physical street concepts**.
   - They are **नही** permitted to create gamer UI constructs: zero floating status screens, zero numeric hit-point or energy bars, zero artificial progression popups.
3. **Ascending Linguistic Tension:**
   - As a character rises from the Feeder Sand-Pits (Layer 4) toward the Central Court and High Council (Layer 2), the prose organically elevates. The shift in dialogue marks the character's ascent more powerfully than any artificial stat sheet.

### 5.2 The Reader's Visceral Experience (वाचकाचा भावनिक अनुभव)
1. **Instant Relatability at Ground Level:**
   - When a reader starts the web novel in the dust of the lower wards, the punchy, modern anime-dub dialogue hooks them immediately. The banter feels gritty, humorous, and relatable.
2. **Awe-Inspiring Reverence for High Beings:**
   - When a Deva commander or ancient King suddenly speaks in pure, unbroken Classical Sanskrit, the sudden contrast gives the reader chills. The reader immediately understands: *This entity belongs to a completely different cosmological tier.*
