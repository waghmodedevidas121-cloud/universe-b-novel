## 1. Executive Framework: The Dual-Language Authoring Standard

Every scene, chapter, and dialogue sequence in *Brahmand* is authored under a strict **Dual-Track Discipline**:

### 1.1 Track 1: Native Modern Hindi (Master Primary Track)
1. **Tone & Register:** Natural, punchy, cinematic Hindi modeled on modern official anime dubbing (Crunchyroll, Muse India) and epic Indian cinema (*Baahubali*, *Kalki 2898 AD*).
2. **Zero Archaic Grammar Sentences:** Characters do **नही** speak in ancient grammatical Sanskrit sentences. They speak living, muscular, conversational Hindi.
3. **Sacred & Epic Sanskrit Preservation:** Core Sanatan terms, Astras, Mantras, yogic physiology, and epic concepts (*Āgneyāstra, Antariksha-Setu, Prāṇa, Nādī, Tapasyā, Dharma, Granthi, Rinānubandha*) remain in their authentic Sanskrit form.
4. **Intuitive English System Loanwords:** Small terms that modern readers instantly understand in English (*Target, Boss, Round, Knockout, Feeder Pit, Circuit, Bounty, Rank, Deal, Black Market, Lock*) are retained as loanwords.
5. **The 'नही' Rule:** Every negative particle is strictly written as `नही` (zero bindi / अनुस्वार-रहित). Never with bindi.

### 1.2 Track 2: English Novel (Derived Track)
1. **Tone & Register:** High-grade, visceral, sensory-grounded dark fantasy English prose (*Shadow Slave*, *Lord of the Mysteries* standard).
2. **Untranslatable Term Integrity:** Culturally irreplaceable Sanatan concepts are **never** translated into clumsy literal English (e.g., *Āgneyāstra* is NOT "Fire Missile"; *Dhanurveda* is NOT "Bow Science"; *Antariksha-Setu* is NOT "Sky Viaduct"). They remain in clean Romanized Sanskrit.
3. **Beat-for-Beat Synchronization:** The English track is derived directly from the Hindi scene—same dramatic beats, identical emotional momentum.

## 2. The Indo-Global Title & Honorific Codex (पदवी व संबोधन नियमावली)

### 2.1 Approved High Traditional Honorifics (Hindi Track)
In authentic epic tradition, only recognized high sovereign, spiritual, and military stations carry traditional prefixes:
- **राजा (Raja):** *Raja Parikṣit, Raja Bali*
- **रानी / राणी (Rani):** *Rani Mādrāvatī*
- **सेनापती / सेनापति (Senapati):** *Senapati Ugrasen, Senapati Vajraketu*
- **आचार्य (Acharya):** *Acharya Āstīka, Acharya Somarūpa*
- **ऋषी / ऋषि (Rishi):** *Rishi Śamīka, Rishi Āruṇi*
- **सम्राट (Samrat):** *Samrat Aryaman, The Central Samrat*

### 2.2 Prohibited Title Prefixes (Both Tracks)
- ❌ **NO `sevak` (सेवक):** Never prefix "Sevak" before a name; describe their labor in prose.
- ❌ **NO `Solar Emperor`:** In Hindi, use *Samrat Aryaman*; in English, use *Emperor Aryaman* (avoid bloated multi-word titles).
- ❌ **NO `Dala-Nāyikā` (दल-नायिका):** Never attach artificial military compounds as prefixes. She is simply **Bhadrā** (describe her as commander of cavalry in context).
- ❌ **NO `Marshal` (मार्शल):** Western rank terminology is banned. In Hindi, use *Senapati Vajraketu*; in English, use *Commander Vajraketu*.
- ❌ **NO Low-Tier Post Prefixes:** Unranked brawlers, scrap dealers, and guards use pure personal names without titles.

### 2.3 English Title Standard (Clean & Un-overwhelming)
English titles must remain crisp, evocative, and classic:
- *King, Queen, Commander, Archon, Emperor, Sage, Master, High Priest, Warden, Jurist, Grandmaster.*
