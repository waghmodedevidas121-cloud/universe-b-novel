## 3. Labor Wages & Income Stratification (मजुरी व वर्ग-रचना)

The economic reality of *Brahmand* divides its mortal populace into strict functional earning brackets, driving tension between unawakened laborers and elite vanguards:

1. **अकुशल सुप्त वर्ग (Unawakened Mundane Laborers — 60% of Population):**
   - **दैनिक मजुरी:** ८ ते १२ तांबे के सिक्के / दिवस (महिन्याला सुमारे १५ ते २० चांदी के सिक्के).
   - पुरेशी बाजरी, डाळ आणि चाळीतील भाडे भागवण्याइतपत कमाई. हे लोक दुष्काळ किंवा कर-वाढ झाल्यास लगेच कर्जबाजारी होतात.
2. **कुशल कारागीर व लोहार (Skilled Artisans & Artificers — 20%):**
   - **दैनिक कमाई:** २ ते ५ चांदी के सिक्के / दिवस (महिन्याला सुमारे ६० ते १५० चांदी के सिक्के).
   - रथ-बांधणी, रनिक बाण-कोरणे आणि औषधी काढे तयार करणारे मध्यमवर्गीय कारागीर.
3. **गिल्ड साहसी व लढाऊ रक्षक (Adventurers & Vanguard Mercenaries — 15%):**
   - **D-Rank मोहीम:** १० ते २५ चांदी के सिक्के / मोहीम (रानटी श्वापदे हुसकावणे).
   - **C-Rank मोहीम:** ५० ते १५० चांदी के सिक्के / मोहीम (शापित अरण्यातील पिशाच्च-दमन).
   - **B-Rank मोहीम:** ३०० ते ८०० चांदी के सिक्के + लूट (रिफ्ट-दुरुस्ती व सीमा-रक्षण).
   - उच्च धोका, उच्च मरण-दर, परंतु यशस्वी झाल्यास जलद संपत्ती.
4. **कुलीन सरदार, बँकर व उच्च सेनापती (Nobles, Bankers & Marshals — 5%):**
   - सोने के सिक्के आणि सौर-सुवर्णात महसूल गोळा करणारे वर्ग. हस्तिनापूर दरबारातील सरदार किंवा आकाश-कोश बँकेचे संचालक.

## 4. Central Banking Hegemony: The Ākāśa-Kośa Levers

Central City maintains absolute geopolitical leverage over the outer four shards **नही** by standing armies, but through the fiscal levers of the **Ākāśa-Kośa**:

```
                              [ THE DEBT-TRAP SPIRAL ]
                                         │
        1. Outer Shard incurs grain/steel deficit during seasonal crises.
        2. Ākāśa-Kośa issues high-value Promissory Writs (हुंडी / Hundi) at 12% interest.
        3. Shard pledges its Monolithic Gateway tolls or mineral rights as collateral.
        4. Central City enforces 17% Customs Clearance Tariff on all transit.
```

### 4.1 The 17% Customs Clearance Tariff (सप्तदश-प्रतिशत सीमा शुल्क)
Any caravan, ore shipment, or merchant convoy passing through the Monolithic Stone Gateways (*Mahā-Dvāra*) must pay a **17% ad valorem tariff** in silver or gold to Central City customs magistrates. This revenue funds the Proving Games prize pools and the neutral municipal Peacekeepers (`CHR-CEN-03`).

### 4.2 The Double-Entry Bookkeeping Ledger (*Dvi-Prakāra Likhita-Vahī*)
Reincarnated banking lineages in Central City introduced strict double-entry accounting to the High Concord. Every gold deposit in the vault is mirrored by a numbered brass certificate. A merchant who defaults on a Central promissory note faces immediate pan-astral asset seizure: Sudeva's commercial court orders freeze their warehouses in Hastināpur, the Sea Citadel, and Central City simultaneously.

## 5. The Dual-Tier Economy: Physical Coins vs. Warrior Scrip

Due to the immense weight of carrying thousands of copper coins during wilderness expeditions, the economy operates on two parallel tracks:

1. **The Mundane Physical Track (नाणी व रोख व्यवहार):**
   - Coarse copper and silver coins passed hand-to-hand for hot meals, inn bunks, and livestock fodder.
2. **The Vanguard Scrip Track (गिल्ड पत-पत्र व हुंडी):**
   - High-ranking adventurer guilds and the Proving Games issue **consecrated wax-sealed parchment letters of credit (हुंडी / Hundi)** and engraved obsidian tokens. A warrior can redeem a 100-Silver-Paṇa Hundi at any registered guild hall across Hastināpur or Central City without carrying thirty pounds of metal.
