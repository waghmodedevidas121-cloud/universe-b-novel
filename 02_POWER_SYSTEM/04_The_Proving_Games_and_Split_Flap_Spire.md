### 2.1 The Mechanical Anatomy of the Spire
- **Height & Location:** A towering 1,200-foot hexagonal obelisk constructed of volcanic basalt and brass scaffolding, standing at the center of the Grand Plaza in Central City.
- **The Split-Flap Louvers:** Every face of the hexagonal obelisk contains thousands of split-flap brass plates—identical in mechanical principle to antique mid-20th-century railway and flight departure boards. Each flap is stamped with embossed Sanskrit names, realm insignias, and ladder ranks.
- **The Sound of the City (*Khat-Khat-Khat*):** Whenever a registered arena combatant dies, yields, or achieves victory, hydraulic counterweights drop inside the spire. Hundreds of brass flaps tumble in cascading waves, filling Central Square with a sharp, rhythmic clatter that echoes across five city wards.
- **Auditing Authority & Tactile Copper Tokens:** A specialized guild of blind clockwork scribes (*Lipi-Kāras*) resides inside the hollow belly of the spire. Because ink on parchment provides zero tactile feedback in the pitch-black shaft, fight slips are **tactile stamped copper tokens (Tāmra-Mudrā / ताम्र-मुद्रा)** embossed with raised cuneiform matrices by the Quad-Referees' mechanical punch-press. Sent via brass pneumatic tubes, the blind scribes read the raised pin-matrix with their calloused fingertips in complete darkness before pulling the escapement levers that flip the boards.

## 4. The Quad-Referee Panel (चतुष्पीठ प्राड्विवाक)

To prevent accusations of bias or clandestine poisoning, every ranked arena match is judged by an immutable **Quad-Referee Panel** seated in a reinforced granite pavilion suspended 40 feet above the combat sands.

```
                             [ THE QUAD-REFEREE PANEL ]
                                         │
        ┌───────────────────┬────────────┴────────┬───────────────────┐
        ▼                   ▼                     ▼                   ▼
 [ DHARMĀDHIKĀRĪ ]   [ YANTRA-ADHYAKṢA ]   [ GANDHARVA-SCRIBE ] [ VYĀGHRA ELDER ]
  (Human Jurist)       (Danava Metallurgist)  (Deva Acoustic Scribe)(Beast Master)
  Rule Law & Ethics   Weapon Legality & Toxins Mantra Timing & Tells Concussion & Yields
```

### 4.1 Duties of the Four Adjudicators
1. **The Dharmādhikārī (Human Jurist):** Enforces the canonical Articles of Combat. Ensures no combatant uses prohibited foreign alchemy or attacks after the surrender drum is sounded.
2. **The Yantra-Adhyakṣa (Danava Metallurgist):** Inspects weapons before entry. Checks blade balance, metallurgical composition, and tests edges for illegal paralytic viper-venoms or clandestine soul-binding needles.
3. **The Gandharva-Scribe (Deva Acoustic Validator):** Listens with supernatural acoustic acuity to every Astra invocation. Validates that the archer completed all Sanskrit syllables (*Avahana*) without skipping meters, and tracks the *Gūñja* acoustic tell.
4. **The Vyāghra Elder (Beast Realm Master):** Monitors physical vitality, pulse, and eye dilation. Has the sole authority to strike the heavy bronze gong (*Abhaya-Ghaṇṭā*) to declare a combatant unconscious or functionally dead before their head is severed.

## 7. The Unranked Contender's Entry: The Arena Disruption

Unranked mortal contenders enter the Proving Games not out of hunger for divine prestige, but out of absolute, desperate survival:
- **Zero Charity:** Central City does not hand out alms. Bread costs 2 Copper Coins; clean water costs 1 Copper Coin. Entering the lowest qualification bouts is the only legal way a penniless vagrant can earn coin and avoid starvation.
- **Grounded Pit Brawling:** Desperate mortals do not fight with aristocratic elegance or noble martial forms. They fight like alley cats: throwing blinding sand into eyes, using chains to hook Achilles tendons, feigning agony to lure arrogant opponents into over-committing, and executing bone-snapping Apāna ground-locks.
- **The Spire's Turn:** When an unknown feeder chokes out an arrogant ranked veteran in a debut match, the subterranean gears groan, and flap #4,912 clatters open to reveal an unranked mortal's name under the Human Realm header.
