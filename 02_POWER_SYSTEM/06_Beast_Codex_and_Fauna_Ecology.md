- **Jāṅgala (जाङ्गल):** Dry grassland and savanna fauna (Horses, Dholes, Antelopes).
- **Ānūpa (आनूप):** Riverine and swamp giants (Buffaloes, Wild Boars, Crocodiles, Elephants).
- **Bileśaya (बिलशय):** Subterranean burrowers (Cobras, Monitor Lizards, Porcupines).
- **Guhāśaya (गुहाशय):** Cave-dwelling apex ambushers (Tigers, Leopards, Lions, Bears).
- **Prasaha (प्रसह):** Aerial predators that strike by snatching (Falcons, Eagles, Vultures).
- **Grāmya (ग्राम्य):** Settlement-dwelling working beasts (Draught Bulls, Street Hounds, Pack Mules).

1. **Pack-Bulls & Draught Oxen (ओझे-बैल / भारवाही वृषभ):**
   - *Anatomy & Mass:* 1.8 to 2.6 metric tons; dense hump storing water, downward-swept horns capped in bronze.
   - *Role:* Hauls 10-ton basalt and grain wagons through Central City; slow, stoic, ignores crowd noise.
2. **Cart-Horses & Transport Mules (गाडी-घोडे व खच्चर):**
   - *Anatomy & Mass:* 500 to 750 kg; broad chests, thick cannon bones, basalt-resilient frog hooves.

3. **Scavenger Hounds & City Dogs (भटके श्वान / ग्राम्य-कुक्कुर):**

   - *Anatomy & Mass:* 45 to 70 kg, 3 to 4.5 feet; overlapping slate-red ceramic scales.

5. **Canal-Turtles & Freight Chelonians (कालवा-कासव / नदी-कूर्म):**
   - *Anatomy & Mass:* 600 to 1,200 kg; 7-foot smooth algae-crusted shell.
   - *Role:* Harness-tethered to grain barges in city canals, towing freight against gentle currents (lives up to 300 years).
6. **Desert-Camels & Sand-Porters (मरु-वाहक उष्ट्र):**
   - *Anatomy & Mass:* 800 to 1,100 kg; double-padded wide hooves, nostrils that seal against sandstorms.

## 6. Animal-Human Resonance & The Deva Taboo

### 6.1 ऋणानुबंध (Rinānubandha — The Ancient Karmic Thread)
* **Scriptural Authority:** *Garuḍa Purāṇa* (*Pretakhāṇḍa* 2.2.35):
  > **ऋणानुबन्ध रूपेण पशु पत्नी सुतादयः । ऋणक्षये क्षयं यान्ति का तत्र परिदेवना ॥**
* Animals in *Brahmand* do **नही** bond through magical contracts or treats. When an animal chooses a mortal or warrior, it is settling an unsevered karmic debt of reciprocal loyalty from prior cosmic breath cycles.

#### 6.1.1 How Common Folk Recognize Rinānubandha (सामान्य जनतेला ऋणानुबंध कसा समजतो?)
Common farmers, carters, and youth do **नही** possess psychic meters. They recognize an unsevered karmic bond through three unmistakable physical and behavioral signs:
1. **अकारण प्रीती (Unprompted Instinctive Affinity):** A wild or feral beast that barks and snaps at wealthy merchants will suddenly sit calmly, lowering its ears and resting its chin upon a specific penniless youth's knee.
2. **गूंज-सुसंवाद (Acoustic Synchronicity):** Both the human and animal prick their ears and turn in the exact same direction at the exact same millisecond when the 0.20-second *Gūñja* etheric hum resonates before a storm or Astra discharge.
3. **प्राण-स्पंदन (Pulse Harmonization):** When sitting together around a campfire, the warrior's breathing rate naturally locks to the animal's flank rhythm without conscious effort.

#### 6.1.2 The Three Great Animal-Bonding Festivals (उत्सवांतून ऋणानुबंधाची ओळख)
The cultural rhythm of the mortal realm formalizes animal partnerships through three seasonal festivals:
1. **बैल पोळा व वृषभ-महोत्सव (Bail Pola / Vṛṣabha-Utsava):**  
   Celebrated across agrarian settlements at the close of the monsoon. Working pack-bulls and draught oxen are unyoked, their horns capped in polished brass and smeared with vermilion, and fed sweet wheat flatbread (*Puran-Poli*). The farmer touches the bull's forehead (*Śiras-Sparśa*), recognizing that the beast carries the plow not out of slavery, but to settle an ancient karmic debt of shared labor.
2. **भैरव-अष्टमी व श्वान-पूजन (Śārameya-Utsava / Dog Festival):**  
   Commoners feed seasoned barley stew to street hounds (*Dusk-Hounds*) and fortress mastiffs. Elders watch which hound chooses to sleep on which family's doorstep; once a hound stakes out a doorstep unprompted, it is considered the household's sacred dharmic sentry.
3. **गुढीपाडवा व वसंत-दीक्षा (Gudhi Padwa Youth Resonance):**  
   At the onset of Chaitra (the Spring New Year), youths aged 12 to 14 visit the municipal pastoral enclosures (*Paśu-Vāṭikā*). Youths walk the outer fence empty-handed. When a young *Dusk-Hound* or *Ash-Falcon* breaks from the herd, walks past wealthy buyers, and presses its wet muzzle into a youth's palm, the temple priest declares: *"ऋणानुबंधः प्रस्फुटति — The karmic thread has spoken."* No amount of gold coins can overturn that bond.

* **Cosmological Truth:** **Swarga is नही the home of the Suras/Devas—Swarga is the Tier 2 Buffer Zone (अंतरिक्ष-सेतू बफर झोन).** The Suras reside strictly within their own sovereign civilizational shard in Tier 3: the **Sura Shard (सुर-खंड / Amarāvatī)**.
