## 2. Sura Combatants & The High-Orbit Protocol (सुर योद्धे व उच्च-कक्षा आखाडा नियम)

Contrary to the misconception that Suras are detached spectators, **Suras actively compete in the Proving Games (सुर योद्धे आखाड्यात अधिकृतपणे उतरतात)** under established constitutional protocols:

1. **Active Elemental Birthright & Cellular Longevity:** Because Suras are born with **100% active elemental affinities** from the 10 primal elements and enjoy a natural lifespan of ~1,000 years, Sura contenders naturally populate high-tier brackets (B, A, and S-Rank) on the Vanguard Hero Board.
2. **The Sura Arena Presence:** Suras participate in both Central City's Main Coliseum and their own regional circuit. While they avoid low-tier street brawling for mere copper scraps, high-ranked Sura duels in the upper brackets draw massive crowds and titanic betting pools in Silver and Gold Coins.
3. **Zero Adventurer Guilds on Sura Shard:** Unlike the other four shards, the Sura Shard contains **zero Adventurer Guilds** because no wild monster-dens or infested ruins exist in Amarāvatī. However, the Sura Shard maintains a prominent branch of the **Global Vanguard Hero Board**, directly tracking all ranked champions.
4. **The Buffer Zone Pipeline (Tier 2 Antariksha-Setu):** Apex S-Rank Sura victors, alongside top human, asura, and beast legends, are inducted directly into the **Setu Standing Army (The Solar Guard / Hiraṇya-Vāhinī)** to man the cosmic buffer against Tier 4 horrors.

## 3. The Five Regional Sister-Circuits (पाच अधिकृत प्रांतिक मंडळे)

No unvetted contender can simply walk into Central City's 100,000-seat Coliseum. A fighter must first earn an official **Combat License (रिंगण परवाना)** by qualifying through one of the five regional circuits:

### 3.1 The Hastina-Maṇḍala Iron-Ākhāḍas (मानव खंड — माती व अस्त्र आखाडे)
* **Terrain:** Red terrestrial clay, stone wrestling pits, and archery ranges.
* **Combat Focus:** Classical Dhanurveda ballistics, close-quarters *Malla-Yuddha* (wrestling), heavy boar-cavalry charges, and kinetic swordcraft.
* **Championship Token:** **The Hastināpur Iron Torc (हस्तिना-कंकण)**.

### 3.2 The Caldera Foundry Brawls (असुर खंड — अंगार-आखाडा)
* **Terrain:** Hardened black basalt rings surrounded by open trenches of churning molten volcanic slag. High ambient heat (70°C).
* **Combat Focus:** Heavy two-handed war-hammers, runic basalt cleavers, high thermal endurance, and brute physical armor-cleaving strikes.
* **Championship Token:** **The Agni-Kuṇḍa Basalt Seal (अग्निकुंड मुद्रा)**.

### 3.3 The Sea Citadel Pontoon Ring (मर्त्य-सागर — तरंगती रंगभूमी)
* **Terrain:** Massive interlocking cedar-wood pontoons floating upon the ocean swell, swaying with the tidal current.
* **Combat Focus:** Trident fencing, barbed net entanglement, river-drake tactical swimming, and dynamic balance on wet timber.
* **Championship Token:** **The Samudra Conch Token (समुद्र-शंख पदक)**.

### 3.4 The Primeval Canopy Trials (श्वापद खंड — किष्किंधा अरण्य-आखाडा)
* **Terrain:** Open rope-and-ironwood suspension platforms strung 1,000 feet in the air between giant primeval redwoods.
* **Combat Focus:** High-altitude aerial agility, archery from swinging vines, predator-riding, and terminal-velocity evasion.
* **Championship Token:** **The Emerald Leaf Crest (हरित-पर्ण मुकुट)**.

### 3.5 The Sun-Cliff Celestial Circuit (सुर खंड — सूर्य-शिखर आखाडा)
* **Terrain:** High-altitude floating white marble platforms suspended above cloud banks, connected by magnetic solar-crystal bridges. High wind-shear.
* **Combat Focus:** Aerial kinetic duelcraft, supersonic Astra archery, solar bladeplay, and light-weaving manipulation.
* **Championship Token:** **The Solar Crest Token (सूर्य-शिखर मुद्रा)**.
* **Guild & Board Status:** Features zero Adventurer Guilds, but maintains the official **Sun-Cliff Branch of the Global Vanguard Hero Board**.

## 4. The Subterranean Feeder Pit: *Adho-Raṅgaśālā* (अधो-रंगशाळा)

For those who have no family name, no clan torc, and no silver to pay official guild registration fees, there exists only one dark path into the games: **The Atala Black-Sand Feeder Pit (अधो-रंगशाळा)**.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        DOSSIER: THE ATALA BLACK-SAND FEEDER PIT                        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Location: Central City subterranean drainage level, adjacent to abandoned aqueducts. │
│ • Surface: Coarse black volcanic sand soaked in centuries of uncleaned blood.          │
│ • Rules: Zero Quad-Referees. Zero round timers. Disarming or killing yields victory.   │
│ • Contenders: Escaped pit-slaves, debt-ridden outlaws, disgraced soldiers, prodigies.  │
```

Winning ten bouts in the *Adho-Raṅgaśālā* allows a fighter to climb the iron spiral ladder straight into the lowest D-Tier qualifying feeder of the Central Coliseum, bypassing provincial noble recommendations.

## 5. The Apex S-Rank Crossroads: Tycoons vs. Cosmic Sentinels

When a contender survives the bloody ladder and attains **S-Rank** on the Split-Flap Brass Spire, they face the defining existential choice of their mortal life:

1. **The Arena Tycoon Route (८५% ते ९०% निवडतात):**
   - Retain celebrity champion status in Central City.
   - Earn 50,000 Silver Paṇas per sanctioned duel; own private basalt villas; sign lucrative sponsorship contracts with armor forge cartels.
   - Live as untouchable commercial royalty while teaching in private martial academies.
2. **The Cosmic Sentinel Route (१०% ते १५% निवडतात):**
   - Renounce commercial wealth and enlist directly into the **Antariksha-Setu 100,000 Standing Army** (`CHR-SET-05`).
   - Ascend to the 50,000-mile suspended viaduct to guard the **Grand Aegis Ozone Layer (४३२ Hz)** against real, terrifying abyssal void incursions from Tier 4.
   - Live in austere stone barracks, trading cheering crowds for the lonely, freezing vigil of the cosmic frontier.

## 6. Writer's Architecture vs. Reader's Experience

### 6.1 The Writer's Technical Architecture (लेखकाची तांत्रिक चौकट)
* **Tier-Locked Matchmaking:** Contenders only fight their own rank (D vs D, C vs C) or one tier difference during open draw promotions, preventing immersion-breaking power creep.
* **Mechanical Spectacle:** Arena rankings are updated **नही** by glowing videogame UI, but by the physical clattering brass tiles of the **Split-Flap Spire** echoing across the city.
* **Corporate & Guild Stakes:** Matches are sponsored by competing merchant cartels (e.g., Adler Forge vs. Vance Logistics); contenders wear branded runic mantles, turning every duel into an economic proxy war.

### 6.2 The Reader's Visceral Experience (वाचकाची संवेदना व अनुभव)
* **The Smells & Sounds of the Ring:** A reader smells the metallic tang of wet black sand, roasted chickpeas sold on wooden bleachers, and beast musk; they hear the deafening roar of 100,000 spectators stomping stone tiers in unison.
* **The High-Stakes Dread:** When an underdog contender enters the *Adho-Raṅgaśālā* with broken ribs, readers feel the desperate, claustrophobic pressure of fighting for the few silver coins needed to buy tomorrow's bread.
* **The Clattering Drama:** The physical sound—*CLACK-CLACK-CLACK-CLACK*—as the 300-foot Spire flips its heavy brass plates to announce a contender's sudden rise or humiliating fall, visible from every street corner in Central City.
