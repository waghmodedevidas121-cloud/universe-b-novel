## 1. Realm Overview & Metaphysical Clarifications

### 1.1 Tier 4: Śūnya-Garta (The Corrupted Chasm — शून्य-गर्त)
The southern 30% of the cosmic landmass was violently severed during the cataclysms of **Era 2 (The Severing)**. 
- **Not Patala (पाताळ नव्हे):** A critical cosmological distinction must be maintained: **Patala is Tier 3 (Bila-Svarga / बिल-स्वर्ग)**, the beautiful, jewel-lit subterranean realm ruled by Bali and the noble Nāgas. 
- **The True Nature of Śūnya-Garta (Tier 4):** Tier 4 is strictly the corrupted void-scar. It is an unindexed atmospheric abyss of ashen silt flats, petrified bone trees, and freezing necrotic miasma.
- **The Inhabitants (Deha-Hīna Pretas & Piśācas):** The entities lingering here are **नही** horned video-game demons with physical flesh. They are **Deha-Hīna (देहहीन — disembodied subtle souls)** whose gross physical bodies were vaporized in Era 2. Suffering from eternal, unquenchable spiritual thirst and hunger (*Kṣudhā-Tṛṣṇā* / *Garuḍa Purāṇa*), they desperately seek physical vessels (*Adhiṣṭhāna*) in the upper shards through possession or forbidden black-market summoning rites.
- **The Seventh Nightmare (सातवे भय):** At the absolute floor of the chasm slumbers the sacrificed Śrutāyudha, the living keystone holding the southern cosmic rupture from tearing open the fabric of the universe.

### 1.2 The Diaspora: Independent Guild Wanderers & Cross-Realm Entities
Operating between the political borders of the Five Realms are the **Diaspora Wanderers (सीमा-प्रवासी)**. They belong to zero royal courts, hold no hereditary land titles, and swear no allegiance to shard sovereigns. 

They are the independent nervous system of the cosmos:
- **Guild Guildsmen & Scribes (श्रेणी-सदस्य):** Independent historians, gem appraisers, and ore prospectors who ensure commerce flows between hostile shards.
- **Frontier Outcasts & Mercenaries:** Unawakened fighters (*Supta*) who train in pure cold iron, crossbow ballistics, and Dhanurveda leverage to hunt rogue beasts along the buffer zones.
- **The Moral Reality:** The Diaspora represents the gritty, unfiltered reality of ordinary mortal survival in a cosmos governed by aloof kings and ancient gods.
