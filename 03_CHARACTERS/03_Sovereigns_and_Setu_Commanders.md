### 2.1 Character & Political Philosophy
The Central Emperor is **not an immortal god or an avatar**. The Grey Basalt Throne is a constitutional office established at the start of Era 3 to ensure Central City remains neutral ground where all five species can trade without open slaughter.
- **The Three Economic Chokeholds:** The Emperor does not maintain an army capable of invading the outer shards. Instead, he rules through three unbreakable levers:
  1. *The Gateway Tolls:* Collecting 17% tariffs on all inter-realm freight entering through the Rotary Shadow Anchors.
  2. *The Central Reserve Bank:* Holding the gold and silver bullion that backs the Pan-Astral currency (*Pana* and *Suvarna*).
  3. *The Proving Games:* Channeling aggressive warlords and blood-feuds into regulated coliseum arenas.
- **The Fragility of the Throne:** If inter-realm commerce grinds to a halt or an army breaches Central Square, the Emperor's authority dissolves. He operates with cold, calculating precision, showing neither mercy nor cruelty—only unwavering commitment to civic equilibrium.

### 3.1 Puranic Grounding & Canonical Role
Bali is **never a demon, monster, or corrupted tyrant**. As recorded in *Srimad Bhagavatam* (5.24) and the *Mahābhārata*, Bali is the great-grandson of Prahlada, an exemplar of righteous kingship and supreme surrender (*Atma-Nivedana*).
- **The Living Bila-Svarga vs. The Chasm:** Bali rules the **70% living Astral subterranean realm** (Sutala, Vitala, Talatala, etc.), rich in subterranean oceans, glowing *Nāga-Maṇis*, and titanic marble architecture.
- **The Ward of the Abyss:** Bali stands as the cosmic guardian at the deepest threshold. His palace borders the boundary of **Śūnya-Garta (The Corrupted Chasm)**. His mighty Daitya legions ensure that the wandering Pretas and cursed weapons sheared off during Era 2 never breach the living subterranean strata.
- **Relationship with Treaty Wanderers:** When unindexed relics surface across the lower shards, Bali maintains steady watchfulness over the boundaries of the cosmos. Recognizing an ancient Era 2 relic, Bali observes from afar, wondering if this treaty soul with an unwritten destiny holds the key to purifying the severed abyss.

### 4.1 Engineering Supremacy & The Yantra Legacy
Maya Danava is the undisputed titan of mechanical, architectural, and metallurgical science across the Astral Cosmos.
- **The Split-Flap Spire:** It was Maya Danava who drafted the hydraulic gearworks, escapements, and counterweight shafts for the 1,200-foot Split-Flap Spire in Central City.
- **Contempt for Fragile Magic:** Maya views modern spellcasters with mild amusement. He believes only in physical mass, cold-worked iron, hydraulic pressure, and precise geometry.
- **Political Stance:** Maya protects the Asura Shard from Deva encroachment. He supplies heavy siege ballistas and high-durability armor to the Asura legions, but sells civil engineering yantras to any realm possessing pure Gold Coins.

### 8.1 The Shield of the Cosmos
Vajraketu is the supreme military anchor standing between the civilization of the 5 Living Realms and the howling nightmare of the severed abyss.
- **The 50,000-Mile Vigil:** For three centuries, Vajraketu has directed the defenses along the 50,000-mile suspended marble viaduct of Antariksha-Setu. He does not play palace politics in Central City, nor does he bow to the Deva Archon.
- **The Red-Line Protocols:** Vajraketu holds the sole operational authority to execute the **Mahā-Bhraṁśa (Cosmic Fracture), Śūnya-Ākramaṇa (Void Incursion), and Āvaraṇa-Bheda (Ozone Breach) Emergency Protocols**. If an abyssal horde breaches the Grand Aegis Ozone Layer, his orders are absolute: activate the resonant sound-dampeners, sever the suspended viaduct arches, and isolate the breach point to preserve the inner cosmos.
- **Respect for Cold Grit:** When Vajraketu scouts the Proving Games for conscripts, he ignores showy aristocrats with golden armor. He looks for fighters who survive with broken ribs and blood in their eyes—unyielding mortal warriors forged in street grit.
