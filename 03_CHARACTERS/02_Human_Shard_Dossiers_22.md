## 1. Realm Overview & Mortal Metaphysics

The **Human Shard (मानव-खंड / आर्यवर्त)** represents the densest concentration of mortal free will, physical labor, and karmic friction across the Five Realms. Unlike the celestial Suras or volcanic Asuras who possess elongated lifespans and rigid caste-duties, mortals operate under the relentless pressure of time (*Kāla*), mortality (*Mṛtyu*), and economic necessity (*Vārttā*).
