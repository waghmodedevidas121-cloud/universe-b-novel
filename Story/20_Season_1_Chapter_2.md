#### CHAPTER 2: RANK #84 AND THE SHIFTING FIELD

### SCENE 1: THE FOUNDRY ALLEY AND THE BRASS SPIRE

Beyond the South Gate, the air in the steep, uphill alley was suffocatingly thick.

Blacksmith furnaces roared on either side, heavy hammer strikes vibrating through stone walls—*CLANG... CLANG... CLANG!* The wind tasted of molten iron and biting coal smoke.

Neel’s legs felt like solid lead.

The sheer strain of lifting Boran's beast-cart axle and sprinting under the descending portcullis had settled squarely into his calves and ribs. Every single nerve screamed in protest.

His throat was completely parched, dry as desert sand.

That piece of dry rye bread Boran threw at him? Burned clean off in that desperate sprint under the gate spikes. Acid churned again in his hollow stomach.

"Just a few more steps, Sheru..." Neel panted. "Or we're both dropping dead right here on the cobbles."

Rounding a bend in the narrow lane, an ancient stone water trough appeared between the basalt walls.

From a thick iron spout anchored into the masonry, crystal-clear, ice-cold spring water poured continuously into a wide basin below.

Neel didn't waste a single second.

Stepping forward, he plunged both hands into the basin, cupped the cold water straight to his lips, and gulped it down.

It was freezing cold.

The burning ache in his throat and the fire in his chest died down in a single swallow.

Neel scooped another handful and splashed it straight across his face—*SPLASH!*

Sweat, soot, and the grime from the gate washed away in an instant. Neel let out a long, shuddering breath of relief, wiping his face against the soaked sleeve of his ash-grey hoodie.

Beside him, Sheru lapped hungrily from the lower spillway, then shook his neck with a violent rattle, scattering water droplets into the air.

"All right, take it easy, Sheru," Neel said, a faint grin touching his lips as he scratched behind the dog's torn ear. "At least the water in this city doesn't cost anything."

He patted his jeans pockets.

The soggy ₹10 note. The Dadar-Matunga local railway pass. The dead Redmi phone sitting at 0% battery.

Total cash in hand: 0 Panas.

"All right. Let's see what this city has waiting for us," Neel murmured, letting out a measured breath.

They reached the final incline of the alley.

Ahead stood a massive, soaring archway carved from seamless black stone. The moment Neel and Sheru stepped through the threshold—

*ROOOOAAAR!*

A deafening wall of sound slammed into their ears, like the gates of a jam-packed sports stadium bursting open right in front of them.

Blinding sunlight flooded his vision for a second.

When Neel blinked the glare away, his jaw slacked open.

Stretching before him was the **Central Grand Plaza**.

A colossal amphitheater-scale plaza paved with white and charcoal-veined marble—vast enough to swallow five of Mumbai’s Azad Maidans with room to spare.

Surrounding the plaza rose towering spires of five distinct architectural eras: shimmering crimson crystal spires, monolithic basalt fortresses, and floating sky-bridges arched across the clouds.

And the plaza was packed with an ocean of life.

Thousands of beings from across the Five Shards: blue-skinned Solarian nobles in shimmering silk, eight-foot Asura mercenaries in jagged plate, hunters with chained raptors and dire-wolves, and merchants rushing past with thick sheaves of ledger parchments. The air was rich with the scent of baked flatbread, roasted spice skewers, and heavy musk.

Yet Neel’s gaze didn't linger on the crowd.

It locked onto the monumental structure erupting from the center of the plaza—the **Split-Flap Leaderboard Spire**!

A colossal, 200-foot tower of burnished brass rose straight toward the clouds. Across its tiered faces, hundreds of thousands of thin metal plates were fluttering and flipping in mechanical madness—*clack-clack-clack-clack...*

Exactly like the giant split-flap indicator board at Dadar or CSMT railway terminus, magnified a hundred times over!

Every five seconds, the plates flipped with a sharp metallic rattle, revealing the top-ranked contenders in gleaming golden runes:

`#1 VIKRANT`  
`#2 HILDA`  
`#3 KAELOS`  

Directly beneath that 200-foot spire, suspended between two massive obsidian pillars, floated a 50-foot wide screen of liquid light—the **Water-Crystal Mirror**!

A glowing sheet of pressurized water and astral crystal, broadcasting with the fidelity of a colossal open-air IMAX display.

And on that mirror, a live ranked match of the Proving Games was underway!

A booming voice, magnified by brass amplification horns, roared across the plaza:

"Citizens, merchants, and Guild Masters of the Five Shards! Welcome to The Proving Games — B-Class Official Ranked Match!"

Tens of thousands of eyes turned toward the floating mirror simultaneously.

The commentator's voice crackled with pure, electrifying momentum:

"Rank #84 Ark, The Cinder-Lance... and his Stage-2 Firehawk 'Soot'!"

The screen flashed to a lean, nineteen-year-old youth with a predatory hawk perched on his shoulder, his right arm bound tightly in charred black bandages.

"VS... Rank #71 Korvus, of the Black-Anvil Syndicate... and his two-ton Armored Lava-Bison!"

The split-screen shifted to a towering, seven-foot Asura warrior clad in obsidian plate, standing beside a snorting beast plated in molten rock and magma veins.

"Today’s selected arena... **The Basalt Caldera**!"

*BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!*

The thunderous rumble of the Coliseum's war-drums reverberated through the flagstones, trembling straight up through the soles of Neel's worn sneakers. The entire plaza erupted into thunderous cheers.

Neel held his breath.

He had stood in Wankhede Stadium amid a screaming crowd of 40,000, but this energy was completely next-level.

Sheru’s ears stood locked at attention. The bristled black hair along his spine stiffened, and he leaned his solid shoulder firmly against Neel’s knee.

Neel stared up at the 200-foot brass spire where the live rankings shimmered in the afternoon sun.

A cold, stubborn smirk formed at the corner of his lips.

"Sheru..." Neel said quietly, his voice cutting through the roar of the crowd. "We haven't dropped into some backward backwater... This whole place is a massive, multi-realm sports and gladiatorial empire!"
