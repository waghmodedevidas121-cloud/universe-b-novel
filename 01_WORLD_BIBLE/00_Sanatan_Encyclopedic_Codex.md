- **Classical Definition:** In Sanatan cosmology, a *Brahmāṇḍa* is a closed, multi-layered spherical universe floating within the causal ocean. - **Tactical Mechanics:** Space within the Brahmāṇḍa has tangible acoustic density (*Śabda-Brahma*). Travel beyond the outer elemental shell requires divine dispensation or passing through the Kṣīra Sāgara boundary, where physical matter is subject to extreme dimensional stress.

### 11. Dhanurveda (धनुर्वेद)
- **Etymology & Root:** *Dhanus* (धनु — bow, missile projection, tension-arc) + *Veda* (विद् — sacred science, systematic knowledge). Literally: *The Science of Archery and Martial Warfare*.
- **Scriptural Authority:** *Vasiṣṭha Dhanurveda Saṁhitā*; *Agni Purāṇa* Chapters 249–252; *Mahābhārata* Śānti Parva.
- **Classical Definition:** An Upaveda (auxiliary Veda) associated with the *Yajurveda*. It provides exhaustive scholastic frameworks for weapon classification, footwork postures (*Sthānas*), string-draw techniques (*Muṣṭis*), mantric missile invocations, and ethical rules of chivalric combat.
- **Novel Context & Adaptation:** The primary canonical manual governing all martial training, military ranks (Padāti to Ati-Mahārathī), and weapon classifications in *Brahmand*.
- **Tactical Mechanics:** Every combatant in the Setu Army and royal armies is trained according to Dhanurveda principles. Victory is determined by posture alignment, breath vectors, and tactical foresight, not random button-mashing.
- **Trope Rejection:** Dhanurveda is **नही** a fantasy "skill tree" with unlockable perk points. It is a demanding martial discipline requiring years of physical conditioning and acoustic concentration.

### 12. Śastra (शस्त्र — Handheld Martial Weaponry)
- **Etymology & Root:** *Śas* (शस् — to cut, pierce, slaughter via physical edge or impact). *"शस्यते हिंस्यते अनेन इति शस्त्रम्"*.
- **Scriptural Authority:** *Vasiṣṭha Dhanurveda* 1.4–6; *Arthaśāstra* Book 2, Chapter 18 (*Āyudhāgārādhyakṣa*).
- **Classical Definition:** Any weapon that relies on physical momentum, leverage, biological muscle, and metallurgical edge to inflict damage. Divided into four technical classes:
  1. *Mukta* (Thrown: chakras, javelins).
  2. *Amukta* (Retained: swords, maces, spears).
  3. *Muktāmukta* (Dual-purpose: spears that can be thrusted or hurled).
  4. *Yantramukta* (Mechanically propelled: bows, crossbows, ballistas).
- **Novel Context & Adaptation:** Pillar 1 of the 6 Epic Pillars. Governs mundane, non-mantric physical combat across all realms.
- **Tactical Mechanics:** Strictly subject to Newton's Third Law and biological fatigue (*Śūnya*). A heavy mace swing requires hip torque and Apāna grounding; swinging carelessly on gravel causes loss of balance. Blades dull, shafts splinter, and armor dents under physical force.
- **Trope Rejection:** A Śastra does **नही** have an artificial "durability bar" floating over it. It obeys real metallurgy: iron can chip against granite, and bronze can deform under heavy kinetic shock.

### 14. Caturvidha Astra-Prayoga (चतुर्विध अस्त्र-प्रयोग — The 4-Stage Protocol)
- **Etymology & Root:** *Caturvidha* (fourfold) + *Prayoga* (practical application/deployment).
- **Scriptural Authority:** *Vasiṣṭha Dhanurveda Saṁhitā* Sarga II (verses on *Ādāna, Sandhāna, Vimokṣa, Upasaṁhāra*); *Mahābhārata* Sauptika Parva.
- **Classical Definition:** The four mandatory sequential stages required to operate a mantric missile (*Mantramukta*):

  2. **Sandhāna (संधान):** Nocking, aiming, and aligning the target vector through focused Saṅkalpa.
  3. **Vimokṣa / Prayoga (प्रयोग):** Physical release and vector acceleration of the payload.
  4. **Saṁhṛti / Upasaṁhāra (उपसंहार):** Recalling and safely disarming the cosmic ordinance.
- **Novel Context & Adaptation:** Combat canon in *Brahmand*. There are **zero instant-cast buttons**. Every Astra deployment must go through these four tactile stages.
- **Tactical Mechanics:** Vanguard defenders must protect an archer during the 2-second *Āvāhana* window. Skilled enemies exploit this stationary interval to strike before the chant concludes.
