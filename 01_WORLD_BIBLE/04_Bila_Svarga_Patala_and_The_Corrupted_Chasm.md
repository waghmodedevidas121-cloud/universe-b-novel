### 2.1 Sutala: The Incorruptible Court of Bali (`CHR-DIV-00B`)
- **Cosmic Status:** When Bali surrendered three paces of land to Lord Vāmana, Lord Viṣṇu granted him the sovereignty of Sutala, decreeing that his realm would be more opulent than Indra's heaven and promised that He Himself would personally stand as the eternal guardian at Bali’s palace gates.
- **In Brahmand:** Bali is an **Ati-Mahārathī sovereign**. His people are noble, cultured, and powerful uncorrupted Asuras. They draw clean spiritual prāṇa and trade openly with the surface calderas of Mayasur.
- **Total Rejection of "Devil" Label:** Bali is not a devil. He is one of the twelve Mahājanas (supreme authorities on Dharma) in Sanatan tradition.

### 2.2 Bhogavatī: The Jeweled Sunless City of the Nagas
- **The Lighting Science:** Sunlight does not penetrate the crust, yet the subterranean cities are never dark. The entire cavern sky glows with a soft, iridescent sapphire-and-gold brilliance emitted by the **Nāga-Maṇis (divine serpent jewels)** embedded in the crowns of billions of Nagas and palace arches.
- **Culture:** Masters of subterranean hydrology, thermal magma currents, and medicinal serpent alchemy.

### 3.1 The Cataclysmic Genesis: How the Soul Pipeline Was Severed
During the Era 2 Void Cataclysm, the southern edge of the cosmos was struck by the anti-existential void horrors:
1. **Chitragupta’s Ledger Severed:** The spatial radiation destroyed the cosmic ledger connections for 30% of the land. Millions of dying souls lost their **Soul IDs**, becoming permanently **Unindexed (Anirdeśita)**.
2. **Yama’s Threshold Blocked:** Because these subtle bodies were contaminated with void particles, Yama could not admit them into his courts without poisoning universal soul circulation.
3. **Vidhātā’s Prāṇa-Sūtra Broken:** Without lawful Soul IDs, Vidhātā could not spin new silver cords to grant them mortal biological bodies.
4. **The Mutation into Pretas:** Trapped in the dark abyss, these starving subtle bodies (*Linga-Śarīra*) condensed cold chthonic aether around themselves, mutating into **Pretas (अतृप्त प्रेत / Restless Spirits)** that wander the depths, desperate for the warmth of living Prāṇa.

### 3.2 Paundraka: The Counterfeit God (`CHR-ABY-01`)
- Deep within the ash-covered ruin of Netherholme sits **Paundraka (पौण्ड्रक)**.
- Drawing upon ancient blasphemy, Paundraka projects a false golden halo and carries imitation stone replicas of Vishnu’s conch and discus, gathering legions of desperate, unindexed Pretas by promising them false liberation.

a mortal acquires Loha-Pāśa from an ancient subterranean ruin outside Central City:
1. **The Symbiotic Bond:** Loha-Pāśa is bound to a mortal’s left wrist. The Preta spirit within the iron is trapped; if a mortal dies, the Preta remains locked in a cold corpse. Thus, the spirit is forced to cooperate with the wielder's will.
2. **Combat Execution (Vector Tug):**

   - The opponent stumbles into a dead zone, allowing a mortal to deliver a mortal physical strike.
3. **The Brutal Cost:** Every activation sends a shock of freezing necrotic agony through the wielder's left forearm, leaving his fingers numb and frostbitten. **There are zero free superpowers!**
