## 3. The Parliamentary Cycle & The Six Great Conflicts

The High Council convenes in formal session once every **three cosmic years** (*Tri-Vārṣika Satra*). The current docket of Era 4 is gripped by six unresolved structural crises:

### 3.1 Conflict 1: Tectonic Rotary Anchor Degradation
The ancient Rotary Anchors (*Ghaṭanā-Kīlaka*) forged by Maya Dānava and Viśvakarmā during Era 2 are suffering from mechanical wear due to off-grid smuggling. If an anchor slips, an entire planetary shard could tumble into the freezing void.

### 3.2 Conflict 2: The Legal Status of the 5% Diaspora & "Ardha-Rikta" Souls
Souls drifting through dimensional cracks from parallel Earths carry intact technical memories. Vidhātā and Citragupta enforce the **"Ardha-Rikta" (Half-Blank Destiny)** doctrine: their physical bodies are bound to mortal laws, but 50% of their karmic slate remains unwritten, granting them free will without cheating divine justice.

### 3.3 Conflict 3: Beast Territorial Sovereignty vs. Mortal Trade
Mortal caravans seek to cut carriage roads through the sacred ironwood groves of the Primeval Canopy. Chief Ṛṣi and Nārada defend beast territorial rights; Prime Seeker demands transit corridors for grain delivery.

### 3.4 Conflict 4: Asura Industrial Smog vs. Sura Solar Resonance
Vajra-Nagara furnace smoke drifts across aetheric channels, clouding the crystal mirrors of Amarāvatī. Suras demand an immediate smelting shutdown; Asuras warn that halting steel production will starve the Setu military of harpoons within six months.

### 3.5 Conflict 5: Tier 4 Pressure & The Grand Aegis Maintenance Deficit
Maintaining the **432 Hz Grand Aegis Ozone Layer** across the 50,000-mile meridian requires an immense prāṇic expenditure. The Council faces a growing deficit: diverting more prāṇa to the ozone layer starves mortal agricultural cycles; reducing it risks abyssal breaches.

### 3.6 Conflict 6: Pure Blank Destiny ([STATUS: RIKTA]) Integration
Completely blank souls entering from the void carry **zero divine boons, zero cheat screens, and zero inherited titles**. The Council's inviolable law is absolute: **no entity receives unearned divine charity**; survival is earned purely through mortal courage (*Kṛti-Breakthrough*).

## 4. The Limited Boons Protocol: The Conservation of Karma (*Varadāna-Maryādā*)

One of the foundational hard-fantasy laws of *Brahmand* is that **Gods and High Sages CANNOT grant omnipotence, invincibility, or cheat systems**:

```
                              [ THE LAW OF KARMIC EQUILIBRIUM ]
                                              │
              A Boon (वरदान) is a legally binding cosmic transaction (*Satya-Koṣa*).
              Total Prāṇa Granted  =  Total Prāṇa Withdrawn or Compensated.
```

### 4.1 The Three Inviolable Laws of Boons (*Varadāna-Niyama*)
1. **The Semantic Vulnerability Invariant (अर्थ-छिद्र नियम):**  
   Every boon must contain a semantic, physical, or temporal vulnerability (*Śūnya-Bindu*). A warrior can ask to be immune to metal weapons, but remains vulnerable to wood, bone, or water. A boon granting invulnerability by day leaves the wielder exposed at dusk.
2. **The Counter-Weight Curse (*Prati-Śāpa*):**  
   If a mortal demands immense physical strength through extreme tapasyā, the granting authority must deduct an equivalent amount of vitality from their lifespan, sensory clarity, or emotional peace. Universal energy can **नही** be fabricated from nothing.
3. **The Non-Transferability Clause:**  
   A boon belongs strictly to the subtle body (*Sūkṣma-Śarīra*) of the individual who performed the tapasyā. It can **नही** be traded, inherited by children, or transferred through magical rings.
