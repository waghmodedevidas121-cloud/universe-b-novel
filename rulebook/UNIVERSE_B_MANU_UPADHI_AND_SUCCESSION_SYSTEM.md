## 3. THE POWER BALANCE & ONTOLOGICAL REALISM: THE "DANDA" MECHANISM

### 3.1 Why S-Rank Heroes Cannot Overpower Manu
If Manu is mortal, why can't a rogue Grade 1 Marshal or an S-Rank Rathi simply assassinate him?
- **The Concept (Danda-Purusha):** In authentic Vedic and Arthashastra jurisprudence, sovereignty is not personal biological muscle; it is **Danda (The Rod of Enforcement)**.
- **The Engine:** The Seat of Manu in Central City is physically bonded to the **Pradhana-Kilaka (Prime Tectonic Anchor)**.
- **The Sovereign Field:** When the sitting Manu sits on the throne or holds the **Brahma-Danda (Scepter of State)**, the civic grid and tectonic pulse of the entire Buffer Zone flow through his body:
  - His *Pranamaya-Kosha* is artificially magnified tenfold by civic resonance.
  - Within Central City and in the official imperial arena (*Nyaya-Vedika*), Manu possesses absolute suppression authority over lower aetheric frequencies.
- **The Machiavellian Vulnerability:** **Outside Central City, Manu is mortal.** If Manu travels to the frontier or steps away from the Danda’s anchor, his power drops to normal mortal levels. This is why **Manu almost never leaves the Grey Citadel**, ruling through decrees, marshals, and secret agents.
