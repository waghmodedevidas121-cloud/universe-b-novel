- In authentic Puranic tradition, "Indra" is never an immortal person; it is a **rotating administrative tenure (*Manvantara-Upadhi*)**.
- Whoever sits upon the **Vajra Throne** of Swarga holds the executive veto over Divyastras, the Setu Legions, and the allocation of solar aether across the Five Shards.
- **The Paranoia Thriller Engine:**
  - The current sitting ruler—**Indra-Purandara**—knows his tenure is ticking down on the cosmic water-clock (*Maha-Ghati*).
  - Any mortal who climbs to **Rank #1 on the Proving Games Spire** or unlocks a forbidden Siddhi gains the constitutional right to launch an **Indra-Dharma-Yuddha (Throne Challenge)**!
  - Therefore, the Indra’s loyalists secretly suppress rising stars in the lower feeder leagues through predatory debt contracts, poisoned beast-cores, and hired assassins.

## 4. PATALA / CURSED REALM THRILLER ENGINE

### 4.1 How Patala Leaks into the Feeder Sand-Pits
- The **Adho-Rangashala (D-Tier Feeder Sand-Pits)** where Neel must fight in Chapter 3 are carved directly into the ceiling of the **Atala Stratum** (the first layer of Patala).
- **The Black Sand:** The arena sand is stained with void-grit. Wounds do not clot normally; they fester with cold miasma unless cauterized with high kinetic heat.
- **Contraband Devil-Cores:** Crooked trainers smuggle toxic Patala beast-cores (*Kala-Bijikas*) to artificially pump rookie beasts for low-tier betting syndicates.
- **Neel’s Counter-Exploit:** Neel’s shattered Nadis prevent him from channeling standard spells, but his **single active wrist filament** generates pure mechanical friction and blue thermal sparks. He uses applied physics to cauterize necrotic venom and shock mutated beasts whose senses are hypersensitive to raw friction!
