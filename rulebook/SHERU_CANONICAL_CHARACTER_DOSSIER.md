# SHERU (KURO) — THE VOID-IRON DUSK HOUND
*(Official Canonical Dossier & Companion Engine for Universe B)*

## 1. Species & Lineage

**Species:** Dusk-Scrapper  
**Lineage:** Dusk-Hound  
**Dual Type:** Void / Iron  
**Current Stage:** Stage 1 — Feral Dusk-Hound  
**Official Registered Name:** Kuro  
**Name Given by Neel:** Sheru  
**Combat Classification:** D-Rank / Feral  
**Evolution Potential:** Stage 2 — Ironclad Hound → Stage 3 — Abyssal Dread-Hound

Sheru is a rare Dusk-Scrapper with an unusual Void/Iron dual affinity. Although currently classified as a Stage 1 Feral beast, his physical structure and dormant energy core suggest an extraordinary evolutionary potential.

Unlike ordinary Dusk-Scrappers, Sheru possesses an unusually strong skeletal structure, exceptional instincts, and a natural resistance to physical attacks. His true potential remains hidden, even from himself.

He is not a domesticated pet. He is a wild survivor who chooses his own battles, his own territory, and eventually, his own partner.

---

## 2. Physical Appearance

### Overall Build
Sheru has the physique of a powerful wild wolf combined with the compact muscular build of a battle-hound. He is lean, athletic, and visibly strong, with a broad chest, sturdy shoulders, powerful hind legs, and a balanced frame designed for speed, sudden acceleration, and close-combat attacks.

His height reaches approximately a human's waist, making him large enough to appear intimidating without losing the agility of a smaller predator.

He never looks weak, helpless, or malnourished. Even while resting, his posture conveys alertness and restrained aggression.

### Fur & Coloration
His coat is predominantly charcoal-black, with rough ash-grey streaks running along his shoulders, spine, and flanks.

His fur is dense and coarse, especially around his neck and upper back, where it forms a naturally raised protective mane. The fur around his cheeks and shoulders becomes more pronounced when he senses danger.

Under dim lighting, his dark coat blends into the surroundings, making him difficult to distinguish from shadows.

His appearance is rugged and natural rather than artificially armored. He has no unnecessary spikes or exaggerated fantasy ornaments.

### Face & Eyes
Sheru has a distinctly wolf-like face with a strong jaw, a slightly elongated muzzle, and a black nose marked by a few faint scars.

His most recognizable feature is his pair of deep amber-gold eyes, framed by dark fur. His pupils narrow when he focuses on a target, giving him the intense stare of a calculating predator.

His expression is naturally serious and observant. He rarely appears openly frightened, and even when facing a stronger opponent, his eyes remain focused rather than submissive.

His left ear is partially torn from an old fight in the scrap yard. This permanent battle scar gives his silhouette a distinctive appearance and serves as a reminder of his life before meeting Neel.

Three faint claw marks cross his left shoulder. These scars are old and healed, earned during his struggle to survive among stronger creatures.

### Teeth & Claws
Sheru possesses strong, slightly serrated, gunmetal-grey teeth capable of gripping tough prey and crushing the hard outer shells of smaller armored creatures.

A pair of prominent canine teeth becomes visible when he growls.

His paws are broad and dark, with powerful joints and curved black claws that provide excellent traction on broken concrete, loose soil, and rocky terrain.

His attacks rely on precision, timing, and leverage rather than brute strength alone.

### Tail & Body Language
His tail is moderately long and covered in dense charcoal-grey fur.

When relaxed around Neel, it remains loose and natural. When tracking prey, it becomes still and controlled. During combat, it stays low as he shifts his weight forward.

His ears, eyes, breathing, and posture communicate his mood more clearly than exaggerated expressions.

He does not constantly wag his tail or seek attention. Every movement has a purpose.

## 3. Personality & Temperament

### A Survivor, Not a Beggar
Sheru grew up in the outer trenches of Central City, surviving among scrap heaps, abandoned structures, and hostile creatures.

He learned early that food, shelter, and territory had to be earned or defended.

He never approaches strangers with a pleading expression, nor does he instinctively submit when threatened. If someone tries to intimidate him, he plants his paws firmly, lowers his head, bares his teeth, and delivers a warning growl.

He understands danger and knows when retreat is necessary, but retreat is a tactical decision, never an act of cowardice.

### Intelligent & Observant
Sheru is highly perceptive for a Stage 1 beast.

He can recognize unfamiliar scents, detect changes in his surroundings, and notice when another creature's behaviour does not match its apparent intentions.

He does not blindly charge into every fight. He studies his opponent, searches for weaknesses, and prefers a precise attack over a reckless confrontation.

His intelligence is demonstrated through his actions rather than human-like speech or exaggerated expressions.

### Independent & Stubborn
Sheru dislikes being ordered around by strangers.

He may ignore commands that he considers pointless, dangerous, or unnecessary. Even after forming a bond with Neel, he retains his own instincts and personality.

Their relationship is built on mutual trust rather than ownership.

Neel does not control every movement Sheru makes, and Sheru does not exist merely to obey him. They learn to anticipate each other's decisions through experience.

### Fiercely Loyal
Sheru's loyalty is difficult to earn but exceptionally strong once established.

Initially, he observes Neel from a distance, uncertain whether the human is another threat or simply another survivor.

When Neel voluntarily shares his bread with him, Sheru recognizes an unfamiliar act of kindness: Neel offers food without demanding submission in return.

That moment becomes the beginning of their bond.

Sheru does not immediately transform into a friendly pet. He remains cautious, independent, and watchful. His trust develops through repeated experiences.

Once Neel becomes his chosen partner, Sheru will willingly place himself between Neel and danger.

He does not protect Neel because he has been commanded to do so. He protects him because he has chosen him.

## 4. The Cute Side — Exclusive to Neel

Despite his intimidating appearance, Sheru has a quieter, softer side that appears around the few individuals he genuinely trusts.

When resting beside Neel, he sometimes lowers his head onto his paws and allows Neel to scratch the fur behind his ears.

He may quietly follow Neel around without asking for attention, then settle nearby as though he simply happened to choose the same spot.

When Neel returns after a dangerous mission, Sheru's ears rise immediately, and his tail moves in a restrained, almost reluctant greeting.

He may occasionally nudge Neel's hand when hungry or place himself beside him when Neel is exhausted.

However, he never loses his wild character. Even during these quiet moments, his ears remain alert to unfamiliar sounds.

**The contrast is intentional:** strangers see a dangerous predator, while Neel gradually discovers the loyal companion beneath that intimidating exterior.

---

## 5. Combat Behaviour

Sheru is a close-range predator who combines speed, instinct, and tactical awareness.

His preferred combat techniques include:
- **Shadow Tracking:** Following a target through scent, movement, and environmental clues.
- **Iron Bite:** Delivering a concentrated bite using his powerful jaws.
- **Rupture Pounce:** Exploiting an opening with a sudden, forceful leap.
- **Predator's Guard:** Positioning himself between Neel and an approaching threat.
- **Void Instinct:** Sensing subtle disturbances associated with nearby Void energy, although this ability is initially limited and unreliable.

Sheru is particularly effective against opponents who underestimate him because of his D-Rank classification.

Nevertheless, he has genuine limitations:
He cannot overpower every enemy, and stronger creatures can injure or exhaust him. His Void affinity is unstable at Stage 1, while his Iron affinity primarily enhances his natural physical resilience rather than making him invulnerable.

When outmatched, he must rely on positioning, timing, and cooperation with Neel.

His victories feel earned because he is dangerous without being unbeatable.

## 6. Evolution Path

### Stage 1 — Feral Dusk-Hound
**Classification:** D-Rank  
**Appearance:** Compact, muscular, charcoal-black hound with ash-grey markings, amber-gold eyes, and a torn left ear.

At this stage, Sheru relies primarily on his natural physical abilities, instincts, and limited Void/Iron affinity. His most important strength is adaptability. He is quick, perceptive, and capable of surviving encounters against creatures that would overwhelm less experienced beasts. His energy remains largely dormant.

### Stage 2 — Ironclad Hound
During this evolution, Sheru's Iron affinity becomes more prominent. His skeletal structure and muscles grow denser, his natural defenses strengthen, and faint gunmetal patterns appear beneath selected areas of his fur. His shoulders and forelegs become more powerful, allowing him to withstand stronger impacts. His amber-gold eyes retain their original appearance, while his Void abilities become more controlled. Despite the increase in size and power, he remains agile.

### Stage 3 — Abyssal Dread-Hound
**Approximate Length:** 10 feet  
**Classification:** High-threat evolved beast

At Stage 3, Sheru becomes a massive, wolf-like war-hound with a powerful frame, dense charcoal-black fur, ash-grey streaks, and a heavily developed protective mane. His Iron affinity reinforces his body, while his Void affinity allows him to manipulate localized darkness and conceal his movements for brief periods.

His presence alone can intimidate weaker creatures.

However, his evolution does not erase his original personality. His torn left ear, amber-gold eyes, and distinctive scars remain visible, preserving the identity Neel recognized when Sheru was still a Stage 1 beast.

Even in this enormous form, Sheru recognizes Neel immediately and responds to their established bond.

---

## 7. Dual-Heart Synchronization — Neel & Sheru

Sheru's bond with Neel eventually develops into a rare phenomenon known as **Dual-Heart Synchronization**.

Rather than simply becoming a stronger beast, Sheru begins synchronizing his energy rhythm with Neel's own developing power.

During synchronization, Neel's blue energy sparks and Sheru's Iron affinity respond to one another. Their movements become more coordinated, their reactions improve, and they can execute combined attacks that neither could perform as effectively alone.

The synchronization is not permanent or effortless. It requires mutual trust, compatible energy rhythms, and considerable concentration.

They fight as two independent beings operating with extraordinary coordination.

---

## 8. The Core of His Character

Sheru's story begins with survival.

He has no legendary bloodline that everyone recognizes, no guaranteed path to greatness, and no reason to trust a human who approaches him in a hostile world.

Neel does not discover a perfect companion. He encounters a scarred, stubborn survivor who has learned to depend on nobody.

Their relationship develops because neither tries to erase the other's independence.

Neel gives Sheru a reason to trust someone. Sheru gives Neel a companion who stands beside him without pity, fear, or false promises.

* **To Central City's Arena Guild, he is Kuro, a registered Dusk-Hound with extraordinary evolutionary potential.**
* **To his enemies, he is a dangerous predator whose strength should never be underestimated.**
* **But to Neel, he will always be Sheru—the wild survivor who chose to walk beside him.**
