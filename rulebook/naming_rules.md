## 2. SEPARATE FIVE NAMING LAYERS

Every name, label, or designation in Universe B belongs to exactly one of the following five distinct layers. They must never be conflated:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Layer A: PERSONAL NAME (Primary identity, culture, lineage)            |
| Example: "Maitreyi", "Alistair Vance", "Ulka", "Parikshit"              |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                    |
                                    v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Layer B: OCCUPATIONAL DESIGNATION (Everyday functional craft/work)      |
| Example: archivist, herbalist, chemist, diver, smith, mechanic          |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                    |
                                    v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Layer C: INSTITUTIONAL RANK OR OFFICE (Formal legal/military authority) |
| Example: Commander, Station Master, High Admiral, Chancellor            |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                    |
                                    v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Layer D: EPITHET OR REPUTATION (Earned public nickname / battlefield)   |
| Example: "The Concord Hound", "Iron Line", "The Basalt Voice"           |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                    |
                                    v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Layer E: CEREMONIAL OR DIVINE TITLE (Sacred ritual or cosmic station)   |
| Example: "Mahadeva", "Aditya-Vansh Sovereign", "Garbhodakashayi"       |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Layer Details:
- **Layer A (Personal Name):** The core human or being identity. Should be distinct, memorable, phonetically natural, and culturally rooted. Does not describe a job or superpower.
- **Layer B (Occupational Designation):** Plain, recognizable words describing actual daily labor. No invented fantasy nouns when ordinary language suffices.
- **Layer C (Institutional Rank or Office):** Valid only if the governing body (e.g., Kuru-Panchala Army, Grand Concord, Telegraph Guild) actually maintains an explicit chain of command. "High", "Grand", and "Supreme" are banned unless constitutionally defined.
- **Layer D (Epithet / Nickname):** Must have an origin story—who coined it, why it stuck, and whether the bearer hates or embraces it. Never forced onto ordinary citizens.
- **Layer E (Ceremonial / Divine / Secret Title):** Strictly reserved for cosmic entities, sacred orders, or high metaphysical oaths. Banned from workplace environments.

## 4. UNIVERSE B'S CULTURAL AND HISTORICAL FIT

Universe B is an authentic Vedic High-Fantasy Anime cosmos where ancient Vedic metaphysics, classical epic kingdoms, and fantastical beast-cart/runic engineering collide:
1. **Linguistic Hierarchy:**
   - **Authentic Traditional Sanskrit:** Strictly reserved for sacred items, Vedic Astras (*Brahmashira*, *Āgneyāstra*), yogic energy physiology (*Prāṇa*, *Nāḍī*, *Ojas*, *Granthi*), and ancient dynastic lineages (*Kuru*, *Panchala*, *Aditya*).
   - **Grounded Fantasy English:** Used for secular craft, beast-cart networks, maritime shipping, runic weapons, and civil infrastructure (*Grand Station*, *mantra grid*, *runic arbalest*, *ironclad frigate*).
   - **Grounded Vernacular (Marathi / Hindi registers):** Used in dialogue and character interaction.
2. **Anti-Synthetic Rule:** Fabricated pseudo-Sanskrit compounds (e.g., *Agni-Manipura*, *Vastra-Yantra*) are banned.
3. **Anti-Corporate Rule:** Modern 21st-century corporate/HR jargon (e.g., *Director*, *Senior Project Lead*, *Audit Committee Chair*, *Chief Executive Officer*) is strictly prohibited.

## 5. THE NATURALNESS TEST (10 INVARIANTS)

Before any name or title is finalized for a character, it must pass all 10 questions:

1. **Dialogue Check:** Would an ordinary factory hand, soldier, or sage say this aloud in conversation without sounding ridiculous?
2. **Self-Introduction:** Would the character introduce themselves using this phrase, or is it merely third-person narrative exposition?
3. **Reality of Role:** Does it describe a genuine labor task, an authentic legal rank, or an earned deed?
4. **Natural Rhythm:** Does the phrasing roll off the tongue naturally in speech and subtitles?
5. **Emergent vs. Imposed:** Does it sound like it grew naturally out of the world's history, or does it sound like a writer sitting at a desk trying to sound cool?
6. **Simplicity Preference:** Could a simpler, ordinary word (e.g., "smith" instead of "Master Metallurgical Artificer") do the job better?
7. **Technological Coherence:** Does it fit the specific sub-realm's technological era (19th-century rail vs. forest hermitage vs. floating celestial dome)?
8. **Distinctiveness:** Does it distinguish the character without burying them under adjectives?
9. **Display Viability:** Is it clean, readable, and easy to parse in an interactive UI card or chapter heading?
10. **Authority Match:** Does it avoid falsely inflating the character's political power, combat scale, or institutional authority?

## 6. TITLE CREATION FORMULAS

Universe B uses six contextual formulas. Never apply one uniform template to all characters:

- **Formula A (Personal Name + Occupation):**  
  *Structure:* `[Name], the [job]`  
  *Use for:* Craftsmen, healers, civilian technicians, independent scholars.  
  *Example:* `Maitreyi, the archivist` | `Evelyn, the herbalist`

- **Formula B (Personal Name + Institutionally Valid Rank):**  
  *Structure:* `[Rank] [Name]`  
  *Use for:* Commissioned military officers, naval fleet commanders, accredited state magistrates.  
  *Example:* `Commander Ugrasena` | `Superintendent Vance` | `Magistrate Sudeva`

- **Formula C (Personal Name + Specialty):**  
  *Structure:* `[Name], [Specialty/Craft]`  
  *Use for:* Recognized masters of a specific scientific, alchemical, or engineering discipline.  
  *Example:* `Erika, foundry chemist` | `Rohini, steam engineer`

- **Formula D (Personal Name + Earned Epithet):**  
  *Structure:* `[Name] "[Epithet]"` or `[Name], the [Epithet]`  
  *Use for:* Notorious fighters, legendary builders, or infamous outlaws whose deeds generated public lore.  
  *Example:* `Ulka, the Red Hammer` | `Maya, the Obsidian Builder`

- **Formula E (Title as Public/Cosmic Identity):**  
  *Structure:* `The [Title]`  
  *Use for:* High constitutional rulers, mythic monarchs, or cosmic beings where personal name is secondary to the office.  
  *Example:* `The King of Hastinapur` | `The Sovereign of Amaravati`

- **Formula F (Personal Name Only):**  
  *Structure:* `[Name]`  
  *Use for:* Hermits, wanderers, lower-level assistants, or characters whose simple personal presence is stronger than any label.  
  *Example:* `Finn` | `Chandra-Lekha` | `Shamika`

8. **Shaila-Bhritya** (`CHR-ASU-15`) — A basalt construct whose name itself is descriptive.

### 8.2 Hall of Artificial Titles (What to Purge & Why)
The following categories of artificial titles are permanently banned from Universe B rosters:
- **The "Corporate HR" Category:**
  - *Director*, *Senior Project Lead*, *Audit Committee Head*, *Managing Superintendent*.
  - *Why it fails:* Breaks the mythic-industrial immersion by sounding like modern IT/banking middle management.
- **The "Video Game RPG Class" Category:**
  - *Magma Alchemist*, *Shadow Assassin*, *Iron Blade Master*, *Elemental Caster*.
  - *Why it fails:* Sounds like a player class selected from a character creation screen rather than a livelihood.
- **The "Inflated Grandeur" Category:**
  - Putting *High*, *Grand*, *Supreme*, or *Arch-* in front of every position (e.g., *Grand Marshal*, *High Chief Magistrate*, *Supreme Scholar*).
  - *Why it fails:* When everyone is "Grand", nobody is. Real institutions reserve such words for at most one or two constitutional figureheads.

Before extending this audit across the remaining 87 characters, the following worldbuilding facts must be explicitly confirmed by the author:
