## 2. The Earth Background: Worli Wits & Practical Science

- **Urban Origins:** Raised near the saltwater breeze of Worli, Neel was a self-taught tinkerer who spent his evenings repairing discarded electronics, assembling bicycle gears, and salvaging scrap components in mechanic shops.
- **Mental Framework:** Neel views the world through **physical cause-and-effect**. Where Astral priests see divine wrath, Neel sees thermodynamic transfer, hydraulic pressure, and kinetic momentum. He does not pray for miracles; he checks the center of gravity.
- **The Dadar Glitch:** On a sweltering July evening during a monsoon squall at Dadar Station, a reality-shear glitch occurred beneath the overhead railway viaduct. Neel—diving to pull an injured, one-eared stray dog (*Sheru*) away from an oncoming local train—plunged headlong through the dimensional fracture and tumbled into the cosmic veil.

## 4. The Biological Vessel: Mortal Fragility & Atma-Tattva

Unlike the Devas with their radiant light bodies, or the Asuras with their dense tectonic muscle fibers, Neel's physical body is pure **mortal earth-clay (पञ्चभूत-शरीर)**:
- **Caloric Necessity:** Neel must eat real food. If he goes without water for 24 hours, his lips crack, his vision blurs, and his pulse flutters. If he does not consume calories, his muscles enter starvation mode.
- **Weapon Recoil & Bruises:** When Neel blocks a heavy weapon blow, the shock travels straight into his skeleton. His wrists sprain, his collarbones ache, and his ribs bruise deep purple.
- **The Mortal Asset (Zero Radiance):** Because Neel's vessel has no overflowing divine aether or celestial light, he is practically invisible to high-tier astral detection arrays. To divine seers, he registers as an insignificant speck of dust—allowing him to walk undetected through heavily guarded districts.
- **Ātma-Tattva (आत्मतत्त्व):** Deep within his mortal frame lies the dormant, indestructible spark of consciousness (*Bhagavad Gita 2.23*). In moments of extreme survival crisis—when pain, fear, and hopelessness threaten to extinguish him—this spark flashes like an incandescent beacon, providing superhuman mental clarity and burning back demonic curses.

- Sheru is not an obedient pet; he is an alley-smart survivor just like Neel.

Neel must carefully monitor Sheru's breath, ending fights quickly to protect his hound.
