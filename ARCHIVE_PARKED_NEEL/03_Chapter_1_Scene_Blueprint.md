### Beat 1: Awakening in the Cattle Cart (गोमय-शकटात जागृती)
- **Environment & Atmosphere:**
  - Harsh, blinding midday light burning through dusty desert haze.
  - The pungent, suffocating stench of dried manure fuel patties, crushed barley straw, and old animal hide.
  - Distant clanging of heavy iron hammers from the Asura foundry quarter.
- **The Traveler's Physical Introduction:**
  - The Traveler wakes face-down in a high-sided timber freight hauler loaded with dried dung patties, parked in an unpaved transit alley.
  - Wiry, lean mortal build; knuckles scarred from years of manual labor. Wears dust-caked, coarse homespun traveler's tunic and hemp arm-wraps.
  - His mouth is dry, tasting of bitter copper and dust. His ribs ache from the bone-jarring impact of the trans-cosmic crossing.
- **The Internal Audit & Soul Status:**
  - Reaching to his chest, he feels the subtle metaphysical vibration of his causal body.
  - Under the Vidhātā Inter-Brahmāṇḍa Treaty, his record in the Agra-Sandhānī is registered: `BRAHMA-03-ATMA-90812`.
  - His Prārabdha status is stamped: **`[STATUS: RIKTA / रिक्त — UNWRITTEN DESTINY]`**.
  - In his belt-pouch: an empty water horn, a plain lead transit slug, and exactly **zero Silver Coins**. He has no inherited aristocratic pedigree and no patron deity.

---

### Beat 2: The Karmic Recognition (ऋणानुबंध व श्वान)
- **The Encounter:**
  - Crawling down from the dung wagon, The Traveler stumbles into the shaded alley behind an outer butcher stall.
  - Beneath the butcher's offal rack lies a large, scarred black mastiff hound—missing half of its left ear, ribs showing beneath an ash-black coat.
  - The mastiff tenses, raising its hackles with a low warning growl.
- **The Resonance of Rinānubandha (ऋणानुबंध):**
  - The Traveler does **नही** flinch or raise a stone. He meets the hound's golden-amber eyes.
  - A profound, ancient wave of karmic recognition passes between them—the unbroken thread of mutual loyalty (*Mitra-Rina*) forged across a prior cosmic breath cycle.
  - The mastiff lowers its hackles; its ears flatten in quiet recognition.
- **The Unspoken Pact:**
  - The Traveler pulls a hard, dried crust of roasted millet from his pouch, snaps it in half, and places one portion before the hound.
  - The mastiff devours it, then rises to stand at The Traveler's flank, its coat subtly shimmering with faint wisps of shadow.
  - The Traveler names him **Mastiff Companion (श्वान)**, sealing their pact of mutual survival in the crucible of Central City.

---

### Beat 3: The Basalt Thoroughfare (कृष्ण-पाषाण महामार्ग)
- **Scale and Architecture:**
  - The Traveler and Mastiff Companion step out from the alley onto a grand avenue paved with colossal slabs of dark volcanic basalt.
  - The monumental scale is overwhelming: cyclopean stone lintels, iron-reinforced timber gateways, and massive four-wheeled wagons drawn by armored bison.
  - Crowds of diverse beings move with cold, focused purpose:
    - 7-foot-tall Asura metalworkers with slate-grey skin hauling stacks of hot-smelted bronze plates.
    - Cloaked Sārthavāha merchant factors wearing intricate serpentine armlets.
    - Haughty Swarga envoys draped in radiant, dustless silks, flanked by armed guards with runic halberds.
- **The Indifference of the Astral World:**
  - No one stares at The Traveler with awe, and no divine messenger descends to grant him a blessing.
  - To the passing guards and merchants, he is merely another penniless vagrant with empty hands.
  - In Central City, existence is governed strictly by economic utility, contract law, and strength.

---

### Beat 4: The Thunder of the Split-Flap Spire (कपाट-स्तंभाचा गडगडाट)
- **The Seismic Clatter (*KHAT-KHAT-KHAT*):**
  - The basalt street vibrates beneath The Traveler's boots.
  - A deafening, mechanical roar echoes across the Grand Plaza:
    $$\mathbf{*KHAT-KHAT-KHAT-KHAT-KHAT!*}$$
  - The crowds pause for a heartbeat. Ox-drivers pull on their reins.
  - Rising 1,200 feet into the brass-tinted sky is the gargantuan hexagonal obelisk of black basalt and polished bronze—**The Split-Flap Spire**.
  - Tens of thousands of interlocking bronze louvers tumble down the six faces of the tower in cascading mechanical waves.
- **The Blood Toll of Rank #4,912:**
  - High on the lower tier, flap #4,912 flips violently shut. A crimson skull glyph snaps into place beside an erased name.
  - A gladiator has died in the Ayomaya Rangabhūmi.
  - Shouting bookmakers around the base of the tower exchange heavy bags of copper and silver coins.
  - Beside The Traveler, an elderly spice merchant spits red betel juice onto the stone and mutters:
    *"Another feeder bled out in the first clash. Odds were four-to-one against him anyway."*
  - Central City offers **नही** charity. Without silver, food, or status, blood is the only open trade for an unranked mortal.

---

### Beat 5: The Crucible of Puruṣārtha (पुरुषार्थाचा संकल्प)
- **The Assessment of Resources:**
  - The Traveler stands in the shadow of the Spire. His stomach cramps with sharp, empty hunger.
  - Mastiff Companion nudges his hand with a cold nose, alert and ready.
  - The Traveler's inventory is spartan: a plain lead transit slug, rough homespun clothes, two empty hands, and a Soul ID with an unwritten destiny.
- **The Path Forward:**
  - He looks at the lower enrollment pavilions of the Proving Games, where unranked fighters register for the bottom feeder brackets.
  - He knows that entering the arena means facing bone-shattering impacts, lethal blades, and corrupt bookmakers.
  - But with a RIKTA soul status, his destiny belongs to his own will (*Puruṣārtha*). He will **नही** die in a gutter waiting for divine pity.
- **Closing Image:**
  - The Traveler tightens the hemp wraps around his knuckles, turns toward the registration pavilion, and steps forward into the roaring current of the arena plaza.
