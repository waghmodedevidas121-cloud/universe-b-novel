Iron Law 1: The Commoner vs. Apex Rule

Species ≠ Guaranteed Power: In every species, 90–95%+ are ordinary commoners with zero magic or combat ability.

  • Common Devas: Scribes, weavers, temple-sweepers with no battle-skills or flight.
  • Common Asuras: Blacksmiths, miners, farmers with zero dark magic.
  • Common Beasts: Ordinary wildlife living in nature; only rare Apex Beasts awaken spatial gifts.
  • Common Humans: 95%+ live ordinary mortal lives; only 1–2% ever awaken cultivation.

Iron Law 2: Known Spheres, Insuperable Void

Universal Scientific Reality: All five sub-realms are an established cosmological fact (taught in schools, printed in daily newspapers).

  • The Neighboring Planet Model: Like 19th-c. mortals knowing Mars and the Moon exist through telescopes, people know the other realms exist.
  • The Spatial Void Barrier: The gulf between sub-realms is an Aetheric Spatial Void. Commoners have zero means to cross; physical bodies without shields are crushed.
  • Apex Monopoly: Interdimensional transit is locked to ~1% Apex Masters or multi-million-coin chartered spatial bubbles.
