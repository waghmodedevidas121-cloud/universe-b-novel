📜 Dynastic Lore & Comprehensive History

The Aditya Dynasty traces its lineage to the primordial Sun deity at the dawn of the cosmos in Era 1. Sovereign Aurelius and High Queen Seraphina rule from the golden spires of Amaravati, suspended upon perpetual cloud plateaus above the mortal sky.

Having survived the Void Cataclysm of Era 2 by raising solar prismatic shields, the Devas maintained their pristine isolation through Era 3's Great Partition. In the present Era 4: Kali Yuga, the Aditya Dynasty fiercely enforces a zero-smog mandate across its airspace, looking down with disdain upon mortal industry while privately worrying about the rapid militarization of human and Asuran dimensional artillery.

📜 Dynastic Lore & Comprehensive History

Samudra-Pattanam is the wealthiest seaport in the mortal realm. While inland Hastinapur laid railway tracks, seafaring merchant families banded together to form the Coastal League in Era 3. Under High Admiral Silas Suhotra, they established a monopoly over all navigable sea lanes across the Mortal Sea (Nashvar Samudra).

Operating heavily armored paddle-wheelers and steam frigates, the League is a plutocracy backed by private naval firepower. They finance the Hastinapur royal treasury's railway bonds, giving Silas Suhotra immense indirect leverage over King Parikshit's domestic policies.

Coastal fisher clans and early shallow-water traders under inland kingly tributes.
ERA 2: VOID CATACLYSM
Harbor fortifications built as the seas surged during tectonic cataclysms.
