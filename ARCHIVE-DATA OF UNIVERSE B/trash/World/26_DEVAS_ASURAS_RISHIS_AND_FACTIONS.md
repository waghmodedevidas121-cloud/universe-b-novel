## 1. Foundational Doctrine — Beyond Good vs Evil [CANON LOCK]

### 1.1 The Rejected Model

Universe B **explicitly does not run on "Deva = good, Asura = evil."** The scripture-fed cosmology of Universe A leaves that reading available; Universe B's child-cosmos constitution does not.

- Both are **Kaśyapa-lineage cousins** descended from the same primordial Prajapati stock (Aditi → Devas; Diti → Daityas; Danu → Dānavas). See [`06`](./06_ENTITIES_AND_RACES.md).
- Both **live together** in the Astral Realm and are jointly recruited into the Buffer Zone.
- A Deva can be venal, a paranoid bureaucrat, a usurper. An Asura can be a saint-scholar, a loyal marshal, a monk. The vamsha predicts **tendency**, never **morality**.

### 1.2 The Twin-Function Doctrine (*Yugala-Dharma*)

Cosmic stability requires **two complementary functions**, not one winner:

| Axis | Custodians (Deva function) | Catalysts (Asura function) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Doctrinal name** | *Sthiti-Pāla* — Custodian of Order | *Vikāsa-Preraka* — Catalyst of Evolution |
| **Prime mandate** | Preservation, ritual regularity, boundary integrity, legal continuity | Material innovation, engineering ambition, frontier expansion, transformative change |
| **Temporal posture** | Long-horizon, precedent-bound, ceremony-legitimate | Fast-iterating, prototype-first, feat-legitimate |
| **Institutional home** | Order of Palaks, ritual academies, Devas' ritual-funding base | Guild of Seekers, foundry houses, Seeker enclaves |
| **Characteristic virtue** | Reliability (*Sthairya*) | Ingenuity (*Pravartanatā*) |
| **Characteristic vice** | Rigidity that strangles growth (*Sthiti-Glaciation*) | Velocity that shatters structure (*Vikāsa-Chaos*) |
| **Failure consequence** | Cosmos freezes into a museum of correct forms | Cosmos tears itself apart in runaway experimentation |
| **Governing check** | Assembly of Sages audit | Assembly of Sages audit |

### 1.3 The Two Cosmic Pathologies

1. **Sthiti-Glaciation (स्थैर्य-जडत्व, via Roman: *Sthiti-Jadatva*):** Order outlives its purpose. Ritual becomes empty machinery, laws outnumber living needs, innovation is strangled. Observable symptom: stagnating Prana, decaying rail, deva-funding surplus with no world to fund.
2. **Vikāsa-Chaos (विकास-अनarchy, via Roman: *Vikāsa-Anarchya*):** Change outruns cohesion. Unlicensed yantra, unsealed rifts, reckless realm-stress tests. Observable symptom: rift frequency spikes, boundary leaks, Corrupted Realm probes.

**Canonical consequence:** neither function may be abolished. An anti-Asura purge **and** an anti-Deva purge are both constitutional catastrophes.

### 1.4 The Balance Mandate

> *"Order without growth is a corpse preserved in salt. Growth without order is a fire without a hearth. The cosmos is neither the salt nor the fire; it is the house that survives both."* — attributed to Sanatkumara, *Philosophical Counsel* ([`22`](./22_DIVINE_DELEGATES_AND_BUFFER_ZONE_HIERARCHY.md) §3.1)

The Balance Mandate is enforced not by sentiment but by **structure**: every institution that protects order is audited by sages, and every institution that innovates is also audited by sages. The auditor belongs to neither camp.

### 2.2 The Birth Equality Rule (Janma-Samatā Niyam)

**Locked text:** no being is born with authority. Standing is earned through exactly three currencies:

1. **Kruti (कृति) — Deeds / Feats:** verifiable action, service, construction, discovery, protection.
2. **Tapasya (तपस्या) — Austerity:** accumulated ascetic discipline; measurable in the Tapasya track of the 6 Pillars.
3. **Karma (कर्म) — Causal record:** the three-ledger account maintained by Chitragupta's Office.

**Corollaries:**

- A Deva birth grants **no** Buffer Zone seat, **no** vote, **no** legal superiority.
- An Asura birth grants **no** guild rank, **no** research licence, **no** exemption from audit.
- A royal or rishi household may **not** fast-track a child into recruitment ([`25`](./25_BUFFER_ZONE_OPERATIONAL_SYSTEM_AND_REGULATIONS.md) §4.1 — *No Dynastic Privilege*).
- Karma ledgers are **vamsha-blind**: the record does not ask whether the soul was born Aditya, Daitya, Dānava, Nāga, Yaksha, Vānara, Kinnara, Gandharva, or human.

### 3.4 The Code of the Great Setu — Equal Legal Rights [CANON LOCK]

Five locked clauses that make vamsha legally inert:

1. **Kula-Samatā Adhikāra (Equal Clause):** no vamsha or kul holds legal privilege, tax immunity, or exemption from audit. Every dispute between unequal-vamsha parties is heard by a **neutral** bench.
2. **Karma-Üdghāṭana Adhikāra (Karma-Opacity Clause):** no party may demand that a verdict be adjusted by the accused's lineage. The ledger, not the pedigree, decides.
3. **Samāna-Bhāra Adhikāra (Equal-Burden Clause):** defence levy, rail labor, quarantine service and frontier rotation fall on all vamshas in proportion to registered population, not to ritual status.
4. **Chikitsā-Samatā Adhikāra (Equal-Care Clause):** medical care, including Nāga pharmacopoeia and rift-toxin antidotes, is dispensed without lineage discrimination.
5. **Vāk-Samatā Adhikāra (Equal-Voice Clause):** every kul holds one seat in its Kshetra-Sabha, regardless of ritual eminence; guild charters cannot be granted or revoked for lineage reasons.

**Enforcement:** the **Tri-Departmental Tribunal** ([`25`](./25_BUFFER_ZONE_OPERATIONAL_SYSTEM_AND_REGULATIONS.md) §6) is deliberately cross-manned — Satyaketu (human-origin auditor) sits as judicial head, Vajraketu (asura-origin marshal) speaks for security, Yuktinath (asura-origin researcher) as technical expert. No single-vamsha bench can decide a cross-lineage dispute.

## 4. Rishis & Sages — Independent Philosophical Authority [CANON LOCK]

### 4.1 The Non-Subordination Rule (Asambandha Niyam)

**Locked text:** *Sages are advisers, observers, and structural analysts. They are never subordinate lackeys.*

| Guarantee | Meaning | Practical test |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Advisory-Only Clause** | A sage's counsel may be refused by the body it advises | Journal of refusals is public in the Kshetra-Sabha |
| **Resignation Right** | Any sage may quit any advisory seat with no penalty, retaining full Rishi status | No "desertion" charge may be filed |
| **Conscientious Refusal** | A sage may refuse a task as *adharmic* or *structurally unsound*, in writing | Refusal is logged, not punished |
| **No Astra Conscription** | Pure Rishis cannot be ordered to discharge astras, curses or boons | Command attempts are void in law |
| **No Wage Relation** | Rishis do not draw Buffer Zone salary; they hold *honoraria of office*, revocable without humiliation | No "employee" status may be recorded |
| **Independent Dignity** | A sage's vamsha, ashram and reputation are untouched by service | Confirmed in Six Anti-Confusion Rules, [`22`](./22_DIVINE_DELEGATES_AND_BUFFER_ZONE_HIERARCHY.md) §6.2 |

*Interpretation:* this is why a sage may **advise Yama** and **advise Yama's political opponent** in the same season, without perjury. The Rishi order is a standing conscience of the Setup, not its staff.

### 4.3 The Sapta-Rishi Council (*Saptarshi Sabha*) [CANON LOCK]

- **Composition:** seven seats, filled by peer election among Maharshi-tier and above. **Seats rotate** on the Manvantara-scale clock; no permanent dynasty holds a seat.
- **Chair:** rotating, single-term. The chair speaks for the Council but holds no coercive power.
- **Scope of authority:**
  1. **Structural Analysis** — assess whether a law, yantra, or institution will cause Sthiti-Glaciation or Vikāsa-Chaos.
  2. **Non-Binding *Nirnaya* (Advisory Finding)** — a written finding delivered to any of the Six Divine Offices, the Four Buffer Zone Departments, or a Kshetra-Sabha.
  3. **Binding-on-Kings *Upadesha*** — the one binding form: counsel delivered to a **Rajya throne** on governance/conduct. Refusing a sage's *Upadesha* is lawful but carries social and karmic weight, never physical compulsion.
  4. **Non-Binding on the High Council** — the Council may not overrule the Six Offices. It observes, analyses, reports. *(This preserves [`22`](./22_DIVINE_DELEGATES_AND_BUFFER_ZONE_HIERARCHY.md) §6.5: no extra-constitutional power.)*
  5. **Crisis Witness** — may sit as formal witness in the Tri-Departmental Tribunal and in High Council review of a contested law.
- **Relationship to the Assembly of Sages:** the Assembly is the **field-operational arm** (auditors, chroniclers, hermit researchers) under the **Chief Sage** office. The Sapta-Rishi Council is the **independent peer body** that may be consulted *by* the Assembly but never commands it, and which the Assembly may never command.

```
                  [ CHIEF SAGE  (Divine Office) ]
                                │
                                ▼
             [ ASSEMBLY OF SAGES ]  ← FIELD ARM (audit, evidence)
                     │                    ▲
                     │ consults           │ peer findings
                     ▼                    │
             [ SAPTA-RISHI COUNCIL — Saptarshi Sabha ] ← INDEPENDENT PEERS
                     │
                     └──► Non-binding Nirnaya  → Offices · Departments · Kshetra-Sabhas
                     └──► Binding-on-Kings Upadesha → Rajya thrones
```

### 5.2 *Prāṇa-Bandhana* — the Beast Partnership Accord [CANON LOCK]

**Locked rule:** bonded beasts are **partners, not property, not slaves.**

| Clause | Content |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Mutual Bond** | The bond requires *both* parties' consent; it is recorded in the local Karma-adjacent ledger as a joint undertaking |
| **Dignity** | Beasts may not be sold, inherited as chattel, or worked to death; bonded service ends at the beast's refusal |
| **Reciprocal Duty** | The citizen owes the beast food, shelter, rest and medical care; the beast owes companionship and service |
| **Legal Personhood (partial)** | A bonded beast may be a **witness** in animal-related tribunal matters; injury to a bonded beast is judgeable as an offence against a being |
| **Non-Powered Citizens** | The accord is the constitutional answer for citizens without personal powers — partnership replaces power, not inferiority |
| **No Battlefield Conscription** | Bonded beasts cannot be mass-drafted away from their households except under declared *Apat-Dharma* |

### 6.4 Cross-Cutting Realities (Anti-Simplification Rules)

1. **No monolith.** A Dānava architect may be a Traditionalist about ritual and a Modernizer about viaducts. A human auditor may be a Setu Loyalist and a Modernizer.
2. **No purity test.** Vamsha does not predict faction. The Deva/Asura officer register ([`23`](./23_BUFFER_ZONE_NAMED_OFFICERS_AND_ORIGINS.md)) deliberately mixes: Satyaketu (human), Vajraketu (asura), Yuktinath (asura), Medhavi (deva), Ranvay (deva), Yantrika (human).
3. **The Buffer Zone Five Frictions** ([`20`](./20_EVOLVING_COSMOS_REALMS_AND_COUNCIL_SYSTEM.md) §3.2) are the *daily* engine of these conflicts: jurisdiction clash, blame game, prana allocation, promotion/lineage rivalry, motherland bias.
4. **Both are loyal.** Ideological friction is constructive — Red-Ink Seal, Emergency Hold, and the Tri-Departmental Tribunal exist to *channel* it, not to abolish it.
5. **Corrupted Realm is the shared external.** No faction defends exile-trade with the Abyss. Any faction caught doing so faces the Treason row of the penalty matrix ([`25`](./25_BUFFER_ZONE_OPERATIONAL_SYSTEM_AND_REGULATIONS.md) §8).

## 8. Canon Lock Rules — The Ten Invariants of Module 26 [CANON LOCK]

1. **No moral black-and-white.** Deva ≠ good; Asura ≠ evil. Functions differ; worth is individual.
2. **Twin-Function Doctrine holds.** Order-custodians and evolution-catalysts are both constitutionally indispensable.
3. **Wrong residency doesn't exist.** Deva and Asura both live in the Astral Realm and serve in the Buffer Zone.
4. **Birth Equality is absolute.** Kruti, Tapasya and Karma alone confer standing. No vamsha privilege, ever.
5. **Vamsha is culture, not rank.** It grants a head start in tradition and lore; it grants zero legal advantage.
6. **Equal rights are codified.** The five clauses of the Code of the Great Setu may not be suspended by any faction, including Setu Loyalists.
7. **Non-Subordination Rule is inviolable.** Sages advise, observe, analyse, and may resign; they are never salaaried lackeys or order-following staff.
8. **Sapta-Rishi Council is independent of the Assembly of Sages.** Neither commands the other; both answer to the audit principle, not to each other.
9. **Bonded beasts are partners.** Prāṇa-Bandhana forbids ownership, inheritance-as-chattel, and mass conscription outside Apat-Dharma.
10. **No faction may claim divine endorsement.** Delegates are non-partisan; a faction asserting otherwise is doctrinally censured.
