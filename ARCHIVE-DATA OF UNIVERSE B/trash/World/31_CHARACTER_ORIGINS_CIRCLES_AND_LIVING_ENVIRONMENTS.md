## 0. How to Read This Module

- **Scope of Part 1:** 13 roster entries — 3 from Tier 2 (Buffer Zone) and 10 from Tier 3 (Astral Realm). Part 2 will cover Tier 4 (Corrupted Realm).
- **Four fixed blocks per character:** *Origin & Roots* · *Immediate Circle* (Trusted Deputy/Apprentice, Primary Rival/Ideological Foil, Standing with Ordinary Folk) · *Living Environment* · *Roster Links*.
- **Grounded by rule:** Every figure is born to ordinary work and rises through **Kruti** (deeds) and **Tapasya** (austerity), per the Birth Equality Rule of Module 29. No divine patronage, no lost-royal reveals, no tangled bloodlines.
- **Supporting cast:** Named deputies and rivals not in the Module 30 roster are marked *(supporting cast)*. They exist to give each leader a living inner circle — they are not additions to the main cast.
- **Register:** Roman script throughout. Sanskrit/Marathi terms are italicised on first use. 19th-century industrial-mythic flavour — steam, brass, telegraph, rails.

## Part 1-A. Tier 2 — The Buffer Zone (Setu)

### 1. Acharya Shrutasena — Chief Proctor, Setu Vidya-Peeth

**Origin & Roots**
Born in the clerks' quarter of Madhya-Nagara to a court stenographer and a schoolmistress. He passed the Vidya-Sandhi examination on scholarship, then spent nine years as an arbitration clerk in the Nyaya-Pitha evidence vaults. He was promoted to the Vidya-Peeth for one reason only: he never once signed a report he had not personally verified. No noble line, no patron.

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — Deputy Proctor Shraddha** *(supporting cast):* a former audit scribe and the only person permitted to mark papers in his stead. She knows his grading mind better than he does and quietly softens his harshest rulings.
- **Primary Rival — Wing-Master Sulabha** *(supporting cast):* master of the Mechanics Wing, who argues that the Setu needs bodies and the academy should pass borderline cadets. Shrutasena calls this corruption with a kind face; Sulabha calls Shrutasena a stone that breaks the tools it needs.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** Cadets dread his examination hall and would still take a bullet for him. Porters, clerks and kitchens respect him because he fights for them when the High Council trims the academy budget — and because he is scrupulously polite to every one of them.

**Living Environment**
The highest plateau of the Central Hub: granite walls and brass domes, unheated lecture halls, chalk dust and brass polish, brass slates and humming memory-projection crystals. His study smells of lamp oil and old paper, and his black runic cane leans always within reach of the exam dais.

**Roster Links**
- **Yuktinath** (Tier 1, Guild of Seekers) — constant friction: Yuktinath wants practical tinkerers fast, Shrutasena refuses to certify a half-trained officer.
- **Yantrika** — trains the Mechanics Wing under him and thinks he grades the wrong things.
- **Satyaketu** — professional respect; Shrutasena's transcripts become audit evidence in field cases.
- **Akampana** and **Susharma** — guest instructors on siege works and rail drill.
- **Mayasura** — an old grievance: Shrutasena once failed a Vajra-Foundry cadet, and the Grand Chancellor has not forgotten.

### 2. Susharma — Chief Trainmaster, The Grand Setu Lines

**Origin & Roots**
A rail-town boy, son of a fireman and a crossing keeper in the Vajra-Nagara shunting yards (*Ratha-Shala*). He rose from coal-passer to driver to controller. He is proud of one statistic above all: no train under his control ever missed its scheduled departure.

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — Signal-Master Dhruva** *(supporting cast):* the one man whose pocket watch he trusts without checking. Steady, silent, and the only person allowed to countermand an order mid-run.
- **Primary Rival — charter agent Chanchala** *(supporting cast):* the Merchant League's man in the Central Hub, who campaigns for freight priority over military timetables. Susharma considers her a saboteur with a ledger.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** Civilians grumble at the tyranny of his punctuality, then thank him when his trains run through burning frontier nights. Conductors and firemen worship his standards; he still remembers which of them has a sick child.

**Living Environment**
The steel cab of an armoured locomotive — brass chronometer swinging, gauges ticking, soot in the teeth — and the Grand Central Setu Terminus telegraph room, where a dozen clocks tick against each other and he decides, every minute, what the whole Setu will move.

**Roster Links**
- **Uttanka** — total mutual dependence: without the telegraph, the timetable dies; without the rails, the wires cannot be strung.
- **Akampana** — armoured plating, rail sabotage repair, trench-line supply runs.
- **Mayasura** — Vajra-Nagara builds his locomotives; the two argue endlessly over specifications.
- **Vajraketu** — troop movements on the Western Front flow through Susharma's boards.
- **Dootan** — the White Courier trains get the one priority slot Susharma guards personally.

### 3. Akampana — Chief Sapper & Siege Architect

**Origin & Roots**
From a stone-mason family in the cliffs near Shaila-Gadha. He learned load-bearing from his father and learned blasting from a mine accident that killed his uncle. He served four decades on the *Lauha-Prakara* and was promoted for holding a breached trench for nine days without reinforcement — the deed that made him "the unshaken one".

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — Junior Sapper Stambha** *(supporting cast):* a quiet youth who can read a wall's stress by ear. Akampana has named him his successor and teaches him everything, including what not to build.
- **Primary Rival — Field Captain Ranvay:** the aggressive operations captain who wants to sally beyond the wall and break the enemy in the open. Akampana's answer never changes: the wall wins wars; sorties lose men.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** Soldiers trust him because he digs the trench he orders others to hold. Frontier townsfolk see him as the wall itself made human; he surveys their homes before storms.

**Living Environment**
Mud, chalk and dynamite on the trench line: runic barbed wire, prana mines, steam searchlights and heavy artillery overhead. Indoors, a buried bunker — a drawing table under an oil lamp, a steel level-gauge, a runic concrete mixer churning in the corner, and the smell of wet earth and blasting powder.

**Roster Links**
- **Vajraketu** — his commanding officer; Akampana is the Marshal-General's answer to every breach.
- **Ranvay** — tactical foil; their disagreement is the spine of Western Front doctrine.
- **Dhritika** — coordinates barrier outposts; she trusts his fortification maps over her own.
- **Shrutasena** — lectures at the Vidya-Peeth on trench doctrine.
- **Mayasura** — imports runic alloy and cement from Vajra-Nagara.
- **Ilvala & Vatapi** — supply limestone, coal and steel for the fortifications.

## Part 1-B. Tier 3 — The Astral Realm

### 4. Mayasura — Grand Chancellor & Master Foundry Architect, Vajra-Nagara

**Origin & Roots**
An Asura-lineage machine-family of the Foundry Quarter (*Dhata-Shala*). He apprenticed in a boiler shop at eleven and built a working pressure governor at nineteen that left the old Yantra-Parishad speechless. He holds no inherited fortune; he won the Chancellor's seat through the Forge-Trial and forty years of blueprints. He believes machines have souls — not as poetry, but as engineering fact.

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — Chief Foreman Ghataka** *(supporting cast):* his oldest friend and the only person permitted to strike out a line on his drawings. Ghataka lost an arm to a boiler and still works the foundry floor.
- **Primary Rival — High Rishi Dhaumya:** the forest elder who calls machines "the web of Maya" and taught a generation to distrust the foundry. Their debate is the realm's oldest argument, and neither has ever conceded a point.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** Workers both admire and fear him. He pays on time, works his crews hard, and knows every foreman by name — but he will replace a man before he will slow a hammer.

**Living Environment**
A soot-filled foundry roaring at all hours: drop-hammers, brass automata walking the gantries, the High Pressure Boiler House, gauges and blueprints pinned to every wall. He moves through it wearing the steam-driven mechanical harness strapped to his back, while the clock towers of *Ghanta-Chowk* strike outside.

**Roster Links**
- **Yuyutsu** — employer and uneasy antagonist; the two built the same city and want different cities from it.
- **Dhaumya** — ideological rival across the realm.
- **Akampana** — supplies the frontier's armour and runic cement.
- **Susharma** — builds the locomotives that keep the Setu moving.
- **Uttanka** — supplies his telegraph apparatus.
- **Vikarna** — industrial liability and contract cases land in the Free City's courts.
- **Ilvala & Vatapi** — his largest ore suppliers.

### 5. Yuyutsu — The Moral Iron Marshal, Worker Militia

**Origin & Roots**
Born to a forge-owning house of Vajra-Nagara but raised among the boiler crews his family employed. He read his family's wage-ledger at fifteen, found the names of the dead written off as "wear", and renounced the inheritance in public. He kept the house name only so that factory owners must answer him as an equal. His rank was voted to him by the men he leads.

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — Forewoman Karmini** *(supporting cast):* a union steward who keeps the roster, the strike funds and his conscience in the same ledger. She tells him the truth the crowd will not.
- **Primary Rival — Dhanada, head of the Foundry-Owners' League** *(supporting cast):* an old-money industrialist who treats maimed workers as "cost of throughput". Yuyutsu has beaten him in three arbitrations and lost to him in ten.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** Adored by labourers; his militia would march into fire for him. He eats in the same canteens, works the same shifts at inspection, and is hated precisely in proportion by the owners.

**Living Environment**
Foundry gates and union halls: the heat of molten metal on the skin, the strike-line at dawn, the clatter of canteens, and the whirr of steam-driven automatic rifles at the gate when negotiations fail. His office is a converted workshop — banners, ledgers, a dented workers' badge in red steel.

**Roster Links**
- **Mayasura** — the tense employer above him.
- **Dhaumya** — moral ally; both refuse to treat a living being as a tool.
- **Vikarna** — the judge he files his cases before, again and again.
- **Ilvala & Vatapi** — grudging mutual respect: the brothers also defend their own miners to the death.
- **Uttanka** — allies in the worker telegraph clubs.

### 6. Dhaumya — High Rishi Elder, Aranya-Ashram Republic

**Origin & Roots**
A forester's son in the Aranya-Vana. He entered the Maha-Ashram as a boy and spent sixty years in herbal, prana and Vedic study. He is known for the *Aranya-Bandhana* — the mantra that binds an enemy in living roots. He sits on the Rishi-Sabha and refuses any title beyond "elder".

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — Shishya Parijata** *(supporting cast):* his young herbalist apprentice, quick, curious, and the only student he lets question a mantra mid-recitation.
- **Primary Rival — Grand Chancellor Mayasura:** to Dhaumya, the foundry is a wound in the world's balance; to Mayasura, Dhaumya is a man who would keep the realm in the dark to keep the forest green.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** Villagers travel days for his medicine; forest clans revere him; he is unhurried with every one of them and remembers the names of their children.

**Living Environment**
A tranquil forest ashram: herb gardens, oil lamps, the hum of bees, the rustle of leaves, and the crystal caves of the *Krystal-Khani* glowing just beyond the treeline. There is no brass here, no steam, no ticking clock — only birdsong and the slow drip of water.

**Roster Links**
- **Vrikshamitra** — his strongest ally and the forest's fist.
- **Mayasura** — philosophical opposite.
- **Uttanka** — tension: telegraph poles and wire-lines cut through the forest he protects.
- **Suhotra** — trades in Aranya herbs and medicines.
- **Muchukunda** — fellow ascetic; the two exchange long silences and short, weighty words.

### 7. Vrikshamitra — High Beast Ranger

**Origin & Roots**
A Ghatotkacha-lineage giant born in the *Prana-Van* sanctuary to a family of beast-keepers. At fifteen he bonded with a four-horned armoured bear, his *Prana-Bear*. He is no noble; he is a working ranger who rose by breaking three poaching syndicates with his own hands.

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — Junior Ranger Kishora** *(supporting cast):* a young ranger with his own bonded hound, eager, clumsy, and the closest thing Vrikshamitra has to a son.
- **Primary Rival — Vyadha, the syndicate's head buyer** *(supporting cast):* a soft-spoken middleman who buys illegal timber and beast-goods for city markets. Vrikshamitra has broken his caravans four times; Vyadha keeps coming back with better paperwork.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** Gentle with villagers — children climb on his shoulders — and utterly terrifying to anyone who enters the forest with a trap or an axe.

**Living Environment**
Deep forest patrol paths: moss and pine, cold rain, the dens of bonded beasts, a ranger's lean-to open to the sky. He sleeps beside his bear and wakes to the smell of wet fur and bark.

**Roster Links**
- **Dhaumya** — his mentor and moral compass.
- **Suhotra** — friction: Vrikshamitra suspects League ships of carrying contraband timber and beast-goods.
- **Uttanka** — refuses to grant wire-line clearances through his forest.
- **Mayasura** — mutual distrust; the forest has no foundries and Vrikshamitra intends to keep it that way.

### 8. Muchukunda — The Deep Vault Crystal Hermit

**Origin & Roots**
An old soldier of the Parvata-Khani clan wars who walked away from a thousand years of killing and sealed himself in the deepest crystal vault of the *Gambhira-Khani*. He is not a mystic by calling — he is a man too tired to live above ground, and the vault is where he sleeps the centuries off.

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — Vault Warden Guhaka** *(supporting cast):* a miner who left offerings at the vault mouth for thirty years and was finally allowed inside to keep the lamps and the silence.
- **Primary Rival — clan heir Khanaka** *(supporting cast):* a young chief who argues that the vault's crystals could fund hospitals and schools for every mine family. Muchukunda's answer is the same as his silence: no.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** The miners below whisper of him as a protective spirit. They leave offerings at the vault mouth, and not one of them dares disturb the third gallery.

**Living Environment**
A cold crystal cave — total silence, gems glinting in the lamplight, a bare stone slab, and the slow breath of a man who has slept longer than the clans have existed. When a stranger wakes him, the vault opens its eyes.

**Roster Links**
- **Ilvala & Vatapi** — a standing pact: no mining past the third gallery, and the brothers enforce it.
- **Vikarna** — arbitrates the vault boundary disputes.
- **Dhaumya** — fellow ascetic.
- **Mayasura** — wants the vault's crystals for the foundries and has been refused every time.

### 9. Ilvala & Vatapi — The Iron-Forge Brothers

**Origin & Roots**
Two miner-brothers who inherited a half-flooded shaft in the northern range and turned it into the realm's largest black-steel mines. Ilvala is the brain — contracts, prices, tricks and grudges; Vatapi is the brawn — first into the pit, first to swing a sledge. They are cynical about every outsider and devoted to their own crews.

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — Pit-Boss Ayasa** *(supporting cast):* an iron-willed shift boss who runs the deepest gallery and speaks to both brothers as equals.
- **Primary Rival — High Merchant Admiral Suhotra:** the maritime monopolist who sets the ore price and squeezes the inland mines. The feud is old, bitter, and fought entirely in ledgers.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** The brothers are paternal patrons to their miners — wages paid in copper, canteens kept full, a festival every payday. A mine family never starves, and every miner knows it.

**Living Environment**
Deep shafts lit by headlamps, steam drills chewing black rock, ore carts rattling through drifts, slush underfoot. Above ground, a clan-hall and a ledger room that smells of coal and hot brass.

**Roster Links**
- **Suhotra** — open price war fought through brokers.
- **Uttanka** — ships ore orders down the telegraph wires.
- **Akampana** — their fortress-grade stone and steel arm the frontier wall.
- **Muchukunda** — a respected boundary and an unspoken truce.
- **Yuyutsu** — mutual respect for defending one's own workers.
- **Mayasura** — their single biggest ore buyer.

### 10. Suhotra — High Merchant Admiral, Kshira-Coastal Merchant League

**Origin & Roots**
Heir to a great trading house of Samudra-Pattanam, schooled in letters of credit and sea-lanes. He rose to head the Syndicate by cornering the pearl route — legally, ruthlessly, and permanently. He measures everything, including people, in Aether-Crystals.

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — Comptroller Ganaka** *(supporting cast):* his first clerk and first mate, the only man who can read his private ledger and tell him he is wrong.
- **Primary Rival — High Chief Magistrate Vikarna:** the judge who refuses his gifts and rules against his ships. Suhotra respects him the way a sailor respects a reef.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** Dockside workers both fear and curse him. He pays on time and squeezes every other term; the harbormen know exactly what he is and take his coin anyway.

**Living Environment**
The Grand Harbor (*Maha-Bandar*): warehouses, spice markets, the Pearl Exchange, the *Deepa-Stambha* lighthouse sweeping the bay by night. His office is a velvet-and-brass counting room with a private telegraph key clicking against the wall.

**Roster Links**
- **Romasha** — his best and most unreliable navigator.
- **Ilvala & Vatapi** — the ongoing ore-price war.
- **Uttanka** — Sutra-Banking and trade confirmations by wire.
- **Vikarna** — a recurring defendant in the Free City's courts.
- **Dhaumya** — his herbal import line.
- **Vrikshamitra** — a quiet, mutual suspicion.

### 11. Romasha — Ghost-Sea Navigator & Scout

**Origin & Roots**
An orphan taken in by a coastal sailor and raised on deck. He has sailed the Kshira Ocean and the memory mists for fifty years and is the only captain who has charted the Ghost-Sea and come back to joke about it. He drinks, he tells strange jokes, and he remembers every reef and every fog bank.

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — Apprentice Navigator Timira** *(supporting cast):* a young navigator he is training in the mists — quick-eyed, respectful, and the only crew member who can hold a straight face at his jokes.
- **Primary Rival — League Harbour-Master Agraha** *(supporting cast):* a regulation officer who wants every chart filed and every route standardised. Romasha has never filed a chart in his life.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** Sailors adore him; taverns name drinks after his voyages; he hands his pay out to whoever needs it and sleeps in the crew's quarters, not the captain's cabin.

**Living Environment**
A creaking ship's cabin smelling of salt and rum, lanterns swinging over hand-drawn charts, fog pressed against the glass. On deck, a steam-and-sail skiff pushing through the white memory mists of the Kshira Ocean.

**Roster Links**
- **Suhotra** — his employer and his foil.
- **Setuvrata / Setu** — he ferries Route B soul-arrivals to the Buffer Zone's Kshira-Tira checkpoint and reports to the warden directly.
- **Dootan** — hands off sealed courier runs at the ghost docks.
- **Dhaumya** — trades for herbal remedies against the mists.

### 12. Vikarna — High Chief Magistrate, Madhya-Nagara

**Origin & Roots**
A clerk's son from the Printing Quarter (*Mudrana-Shala*), educated at the Madhya-Vidya University. He became a magistrate, and then High Chief Magistrate, for the one qualification the Madhya-Sabha could ever agree on: he could not be bought. He keeps no estate and wears plain cotton.

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — Junior Magistrate Nishchala** *(supporting cast):* his understudy, steady and slow-speaking, who files every verdict in duplicate so that no one can later alter it.
- **Primary Rival — Advocate Kuhaka** *(supporting cast):* the Free Council's back-room fixer, who trades verdicts for influence and has never once been caught. Vikarna has been trying for years.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** "The People's Judge." He holds sessions in the open gallery, and common citizens crowd in to watch; his verdicts are read aloud in the market squares the same evening.

**Living Environment**
A bustling public court in the heart of Madhya-Nagara — the noise of the Grand Exchange leaking through the windows, the copper seal stamp resting on the bench, a forensic lens at his eye. Even his chambers are open to the corridor, so that no one can meet him in secret.

**Roster Links**
- **Suhotra** — his most persistent courtroom antagonist.
- **Mayasura** — industrial liability cases from the foundries.
- **Uttanka** — evidence transmitted by telegraph across the realm.
- **Ilvala & Vatapi** — clan boundary and contract disputes.
- **Yuyutsu** — labour-rights cases, filed again and again.
- **Satyaketu** — the Buffer Zone's audits and the Free City's courts must overlap, and they do it carefully.
- **Muchukunda** — has ruled on the vault boundary more than once.

### 13. Uttanka — Guildmaster of the Aetheric Telegraph

**Origin & Roots**
Son of a lineman; he climbed his first pole at twelve. He walked the whole Grand Astral Rail corridor twice, repairing wire, before he was given a station. He keeps one vow: no message he accepts is ever left undelivered — if the wire fails, he carries it himself.

**Immediate Circle**
- **Trusted Deputy — First Lineman Vahaka** *(supporting cast):* the carrier who can climb a brass pole in a storm, his right hand on every repair run.
- **Primary Rival — Courier-Master Pathika** *(supporting cast):* leader of the Beast-Courier Guild traditionalists, who call the wires unnatural and lobby the council to keep the old bird-and-hound routes. Fast wire against trusted beast — the argument will outlive them both.
- **Standing with Ordinary Folk:** Workers and villagers wave at his rail-trolley as it rattles past; he fixes farmers' wire fences for free and never stays to be thanked. He is beloved and permanently late for dinner.

**Living Environment**
Brass poles and gutta-percha wire strung across the realm; a hand-cranked rail-trolley that carries him to the break; the telegraph offices where keys click in runic cipher; insulated gloves, wire-cutters, and a runic signal transmitter. Outdoors, the wind whistling along the strung wire — his favourite sound.

**Roster Links**
- **Susharma** — train orders and signal control; each depends absolutely on the other.
- **Vikarna** — evidence and court records sent by wire.
- **Mayasura** — supplies his apparatus and insulators.
- **Ilvala & Vatapi** — ore shipments ordered and tracked by telegraph.
- **Yuyutsu** — allied through the worker telegraph clubs.
- **Romasha** — coastal signal stations and storm warnings.
