## 1. Tactical Combat Mechanics & Skill Structure

In our universe, powers operate under standard modern fantasy/anime battle logic integrated with mythic weight:

```
[ INNATE POTENTIAL / BOON ] ──> [ TAPASYA & FORGING ] ──> [ COMBAT MASTERY ]
(Raw capacity, passive gift)    (Training, discipline, cost)   (Active skills, domains, counters)
```

- **Power vs Skill:** Power is the raw engine (e.g. generating celestial flame); Skill is the efficiency, shaping, and precision (e.g. compressing flame into a microscopic needle that pierces armor without collateral loss).
- **Mastery Tiers:** Novice (unstable, high energy drain) -> Adept (controlled) -> Master (lossless, instant casting) -> Sovereign (concept-level manifestation / Domain).
- **Resistances & Nullifications:** High Tapasya grants passive resistances (Heat, Cold, Poison, Spatial shearing, Mental hypnosis, Soul corruption).
- **The Domain / Barrier Mechanic:** Advanced cultivators and entities project their subtle field to create an absolute territory (similar to *Kṣetra-Bandhana* / Reality Marbles / Domain Expansions).

## 2. Comprehensive Master Taxonomy (12 Core Power Classes)

### Class 1: Elemental Powers (तत्त्व शक्ती)
- **Sub-types:** Fire (Agni), Water (Varuna/Jala), Wind/Atmosphere (Vayu), Earth/Metal (Prithvi/Loha), Lightning (Vajra/Vidyut), Ice/Frost (Hima), Light (Surya/Tejas), Darkness/Void (Tamas/Chhaya).
- **Mythic / Anime Manifestation:** Elemental avatars, compressed elemental spears, temperature nullification, kinetic storm manipulation.
- **Pillar Mapping:** Primarily **Energy** (fuel) + **Tapasya** (purity and heat resistance).

### Class 2: Space & Dimensional Powers (अवकाश व दिक शक्ती)
- **Sub-types:** Short-range Blink / Shunpo, Long-range Teleportation, Spatial Folding (folding space to shorten travel), Spatial Slashing (severing space itself rather than physical matter), Pocket Dimensions (Sub-space vaults / Maya pockets), Gravity & Compression fields.
- **Counters:** Spatial stabilization seals (*Dik-Bandhana*), dimensional anchors.
- **Pillar Mapping:** **Authority** (governing realm coordinate rules) + **Energy**.

### Class 3: Time & Causality Powers (काल व गती शक्ती)
- **Sub-types:** Relative Time Acceleration (moving at hyper-speed while world freezes), Localized Time Deceleration, Time-Dilated Training Realms, Precognition / Tactical Foresight (seeing 3-10 seconds ahead), Rewind / Causality Snapping (extreme tier).
- **Pillar Mapping:** Restricted strictly to high **Authority** or the **Sixth Principle**. Ordinary energy cannot buy time-reversal.

### Class 4: Mental & Illusion Powers (चित्त, मन व माया)
- **Sub-types:** Telepathy, Sensory Hijacking (Sammohana), Hallucinatory Battlefields (Maya-Yuddha), Fear Induction, Memory Sealing, Absolute Hypnosis (Vashitva).
- **Counters:** Unbending Willpower (Indomitable Tapasya/Sankalpa), Third Eye awakening (Ajna Chakra / Divya-Drishti).
- **Pillar Mapping:** **Tapasya** (mental fortitude) + **Energy** (Manomaya field).

### Class 5: Soul & Existence Powers (आत्मा व सूक्ष्म शरीर शक्ती)
- **Sub-types:** Soul Sight (reading the aura and causal sheath), Soul Severing (bypassing physical armor to cut the silver cord), Soul Puppet / Binding, Spirit Possession (Parakaya-Pravesha), Soul Fortification (protecting against instant-death hexes).
- **Pillar Mapping:** Directly linked to **Ātma-Śuddhi**, **Karma**, and the **Sixth Principle**.

### Class 6: Life, Death & Regeneration (प्राण, जीवन व संहार)
- **Sub-types:** Cellular Acceleration, Limb Regeneration, Toxic Blood Purification, Instant Poison Weaponry, Necrotic Decay, Resurrection Arts (Mrita-Sanjeevani formulas), Soul Recovery before departure timestamp.
- **Pillar Mapping:** **Authority** (Yama/Shukra boundary rules) + **Boons** + **Tapasya**.

### Class 7: Physical & Kinetic Overdrive (वज्र देह व गती)
- **Sub-types:** Superhuman Density (Vajra-Deha / Diamond Body), Kinetic Energy Redirection, Atmospheric Shockwaves (Sonic Punches), Flight / Levitation (Laghima), Seismic Stomps (Mahima).
- **Pillar Mapping:** **Tapasya** (hardening the physical flesh) + **Energy** (Vyana-Vayu circulation).

### Class 8: Martial & Weapon Mastery (धनुर्वेद व युद्धकला)
- **Sub-types:** Legendary Swordsmanship (Asi-Vidya), Peerless Archery (Dhanurveda - infinite tracking, multi-arrow splitting), Spear & Mace Artistry, Weapon Imbuement (coating steel with Pranic aura / Haki-like hardening), Living Weapon Bond.
- **Pillar Mapping:** Pure **Tapasya** & Combat Skill.

### Class 9: Divine Astras & Relic Armaments (दिव्यास्त्र व दैवी शस्त्रे)
- **Sub-types:** Homing Cosmic Discs, Planetary Cleaving Beams (Brahmastra yields), Sure-Kill Tracking Spears, Calamity Arrows, Divine Armor (Kavacha-Kundala absolute negation).
- **Operational Rule:** Requires invocation formula (*Mantra*), mental purity, and retraction mastery (*Upasamhara*).
- **Pillar Mapping:** **Boons** (granted weapons) + **Authority** (sanction) + **Karma** (backlash safety).

### Class 10: Sealing, Wards & Formations (बंधन, चक्रव्यूह व मंत्र)
- **Sub-types:** Anti-teleportation Wards, Prana-Drain Arrays, Chakravyuh (multi-layered shifting tactical formations), Elemental Barriers, Soul Imprisonment Urns.
- **Counters:** Finding the core keystone (Brahma-bindu), raw overpowering output, or loophole disruption.
- **Pillar Mapping:** **Tapasya** (knowledge/geometry) + **Authority**.

### Class 11: Transformation & Morphology (कामरूप व विवरोधन)
- **Sub-types:** Shapeshifting (Kamarupa - beast, human, inanimate), Size Manipulation (Anima / Mahima), Beast Spirit Fusion, Divine / Asuric Battle Form releases (Asura-Rupa / Vira-Rupa awakening).
- **Pillar Mapping:** **Tapasya** + **Energy** reservoirs.

### Class 12: Sensory & Cosmic Perception (ज्ञान चक्षू व दिव्य दृष्टी)
- **Sub-types:** 360-degree Danger Sensing, Energy Flow Vision (detecting hidden traps and subtle nadis), Truth Perception (piercing shapeshifters and disguises), Clairaudience across dimensions.
- **Pillar Mapping:** Ajna / Crown Chakra awakening via **Tapasya**.
