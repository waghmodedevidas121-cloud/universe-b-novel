## 0. Module Scope & Canon Lock Status

This module is the **ground-truth specification** for the two living mortal realms of Universe B:

- **Tier 3 — Astral Realm (The Shared Living Realm):** home to 60-70% of the cosmic population (Devas, Asuras, Humans, Animals/Beasts). The realm of birth equality, mixed coexistence, 19th-century industrial citadels, ancient mythic kingdoms, religious pluralism, and the Grand Astral Rail economy.

## 1. Tier 3 — The Astral Realm (The Shared Living Realm)

### 1.1 Macro-Geography & Continental Layout

The Astral Realm is not a single landmass. It is an **ocean-world** of floating continental plates, archipelagos, inland seas, and drifting isles, threaded together by the Grand Astral Rail Network. Gravity is anchored by cosmic Guru-Keela (gravitational anchors) identical to those in the Buffer Zone, but here they are buried deep and invisible.

The Astral Realm is governed by **five distinct geopolitical powers**, each with its own capital, economy, military flavor, and cultural identity. They are connected by the Grand Astral Rail Network and bound by the **Astral Concord (Samvad-Sandhi)** — a non-aggression and free-trade pact enforced by mutual economic dependence.

#### Power 1 — Vajra-Foundry Kingdom (Vajra-Rajya)

- **Capital:** Vajra-Nagara (The Iron City) — a sprawling steam-metropolis of brass domes, clock towers, foundry chimneys, and rail yards.
- **Government:** **Forge-Monarchy (Raja-Yantra).** A hereditary Forge-Monarch (Raja-Yantra) rules with the advice of the **Yantra-Parishad** (Industrial Council of guild-masters, engineers, and merchants). Succession is merit-tested: the heir must pass the Forge-Trial (Yantra-Pariksha) — designing and building a working steam engine under examination.
- **Economic Base:** Heavy industry — steam locomotives, bolt-action rifles, artillery, brass instruments, telegraph equipment, printing presses. The kingdom is the **primary arms and rail supplier** of the Astral Realm.
- **Culture:** Work-ethic, punctuality, industrial pride. Clock towers dominate the skyline. Newspapers (Samachar-Patra) are printed here and distributed realm-wide.
- **Military Flavor:** Disciplined line infantry with bolt-action rifles; armored war-trains; heavy Prana-artillery; steam dirigible reconnaissance. Victorian-style trench doctrine adapted for Prana-warfare.
- **Key Districts:** The Foundry Quarter (Dhata-Shala), The Rail Yards (Ratha-Shala), The Brass Exchange (Pitala-Bazaar), The Clock Tower Square (Ghanta-Chowk).

#### Power 2 — Aranya-Ashram Republic (Aranya-Gana-Rajya)

- **Capital:** Tapovana (The Grove of Penance) — a city of ashrams, temples, herb gardens, and beast sanctuaries woven into living forest.
- **Government:** **Rishi-Sabha (Council of Sages + Elected Representatives).** A rotating council of twelve Rishis (six permanent elder sages, six elected from forest clans) governs. No single ruler; decisions by consensus (Sammati). The **Rishi-Mukhya** (Chief Sage) is first among equals, elected for a fixed term.
- **Economic Base:** Medicinal herbs (Auṣadhi), Aether-Crystals mined from forest nodes, beast-bred goods (wool, leather, messenger birds), herbal medicines, tapas-cultivation services. The republic is the **primary supplier of Aether-Crystals and herbal medicine** to the realm.
- **Culture:** Ascetic dignity, reverence for nature, beast partnership as sacred bond. Gurukuls and ashrams are the heart of education.
- **Military Flavor:** Beast cavalry (bonded wolf-hounds, war-elephants), mantra-archers, forest rangers (Vana-Rakshak), herbal warfare (smoke, toxins, healing). No heavy artillery; relies on terrain and beast synergy.
- **Key Districts:** The Great Ashram (Mahā-Ashram), The Herb Market (Auṣadhi-Bazaar), The Beast Sanctuary (Prana-Van), The Crystal Mines (Krystal-Khani).

#### Power 3 — Parvata-Khani Mining Clans (Shaila-Kula-Sangha)

- **Capital:** Shaila-Gadha (The Mountain Fortress) — a cliff-carved city of tunnels, mine-shafts, gem-vaults, and clan-halls.
- **Government:** **Clan Oligarchy (Kula-Sangha).** A council of seven Clan Chiefs (Kula-Mukhya) from the seven great mining clans (Shaila, Loha, Ratna, Khaani, Dhatu, Shila, Giri) governs. The **Maha-Kula-Pati** (High Clan Lord) is elected by clan vote. Decisions require a 5-of-7 majority.
- **Economic Base:** Iron ore, coal, copper, gold, gemstones (rubies, sapphires, emeralds), precious metals. The clans are the **primary raw-material and metal supplier** of the realm.
- **Culture:** Clan loyalty, mountain pride, mining tradition, oral history. Songs of the deep (Giri-Geet) are a cultural treasure.
- **Military Flavor:** Miner-militia with pick-axes and blasting charges; tunnel fighters; explosive sappers; mountain rangers. Heavy use of terrain (caves, passes, rockfalls).
- **Key Districts:** The Deep Mines (Gambhira-Khani), The Gem Vault (Ratna-Kosh), The Clan Hall (Kula-Bhavan), The Forge-Temples (Dhata-Mandir).

#### Power 4 — Kshira-Coastal Merchant League (Samudra-Vanijya-Sangha)

- **Capital:** Samudra-Pattanam (The Sea-City) — a harbor city of wharves, warehouses, spice markets, and lighthouses.
- **Government:** **Merchant Council (Vanijya-Sabha).** A council of twelve senior merchants (Vyapari) from the twelve great trading houses governs. The **Pradhana-Vanij** (Chief Merchant) is elected annually. Wealth-based voting (one vote per trading house, not per person).
- **Economic Base:** Maritime trade, pearls, salt, fish, spices, imported goods from all powers. The league is the **primary maritime trade and logistics hub** of the realm.
- **Culture:** Cosmopolitan, multilingual, deal-making. Every language of the realm is spoken in the harbor district. Festivals of the sea (Samudra-Utsav) are grand.
- **Military Flavor:** Privateer skiffs, harbor defense guns, beast-courier riders, light infantry. No standing army; relies on hired mercenary companies (Yoddha-Sena) and privateer fleets.
- **Key Districts:** The Grand Harbor (Maha-Bandar), The Spice Market (Masala-Bazaar), The Pearl Exchange (Moti-Vyapar), The Lighthouse (Deepa-Stambha).

#### Power 5 — Madhya-Nagara Free City (Madhya-Nagara)

- **Capital:** Madhya-Nagara itself — the neutral central free city, the Grand Crossroads.
- **Government:** **Neutral Free Council (Madhya-Sabha).** A council of five representatives (one from each power, plus five neutral citizens elected by the city) governs. The **Madhya-Nayak** (City Head) is elected by the council. Strict neutrality is constitutionally mandated; no power may garrison troops in the city.
- **Economic Base:** Trade, banking, printing, news, logistics. Madhya-Nagara hosts the **Grand Astral Exchange** (Maha-Vyapar-Kendra) — the central commodity and currency exchange of the realm — and the **Prana-Kosha Central Bank**.
- **Culture:** Cosmopolitan, intellectual, journalistic. The city is the printing and news capital; the **Astral Samachar** (realm-wide newspaper) is printed here.
- **Military Flavor:** City Guard (Nagar-Rakshak) — light infantry, police, and mercenary companies. Neutral but well-armed. The city is protected by the **Madhya-Prakara** (City Wall) and a garrison of hired veterans.
- **Key Districts:** The Grand Exchange (Maha-Vyapar-Kendra), The Central Bank (Prana-Kosha), The Printing Quarter (Mudrana-Shala), The Neutral Plaza (Madhya-Chowk).

### 1.3 Mixed Coexistence & Birth Equality

**Birth Equality Rule (Janma-Samata Niyam):** No being is born with Authority. Deva, Asura, Human, or Beast — status and power depend solely on **Karma (deeds), Tapasya (austerity), and Kruti (feats/achievements)**. A human sage who has performed great Tapasya outranks a Deva who has squandered his merit. A bonded beast who has served faithfully for decades is honored as a partner, not property.

**Mixed Coexistence (Sanyukta-Vas):**
- Devas, Asuras, Humans, and Animals live side-by-side in the same cities, towns, and villages.
- No segregated quarters by race; segregation by profession or clan is common but not enforced by law.
- Intermarriage between races is permitted and not uncommon; children inherit mixed traits.
- Workplace and guild membership is open to all races; skill and merit determine advancement.

**Non-Powered Citizens & Beast Partnership (Aether-Bond):**
- Not every being is born with powers. The majority of the population are ordinary citizens — farmers, traders, artisans, laborers, clerks, engineers.
- Those without individual power form **symbiotic Beast Partnerships (Aether-Bond)** with bonded animals. This is a bond of equal respect, not servitude.
  - **Work Beasts (Karma-Prani):** massive boars, rhinoceroses, elephants for farming and construction.
  - **Transport Beasts (Vahana-Prani):** horses, winged messengers, draft oxen for transport and communication.
  - **Bonded Hounds (Raksha-Shvana):** Aether-awakened hounds for protection, tracking, and guard duty.
  - **Messenger Birds (Doot-Pakshi):** trained birds for rapid message delivery between cities.
- The bond is formalized by a **Bonding Oath (Bandhan-Shapath)** registered at the local guild; breaking the bond without cause is a social offense.

**Combat Hierarchy (Yuddha-Shreni):**
- Ordinary citizens, police, and guards use **bolt-action rifles, revolvers, and bonded beasts** for everyday threats (bandits, wild animals, minor Rakshasa incursions).
- Cultivators (Tapasvi) and Astras outrank firearms; a single Siddha with an Astra can defeat an entire rifle company.
- The hierarchy is respected, not feared; cultivators serve as community protectors and are integrated into civic life.

### 1.4 Economy, Currency & Banking

**Currency — The Aether-Coinage (Aether-Mudra):**

All Astral Realm commerce uses the **Aether-Mudra** system, a four-tier coinage backed by Aether-Crystals held in reserve by the Aether-Kosha Central Bank.

| Denomination | Material | Value (base units) | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Copper Coin (Tamra-Mudra)** | Copper | 1 | Daily small transactions, wages, market purchases |
| **Silver Coin (Rajata-Mudra)** | Silver | 100 | Mid-value trade, salaries, equipment |
| **Gold Coin (Suvarna-Mudra)** | Gold | 10,000 | Large trade, property, military contracts |
| **Aether-Crystal** | Crystallized Aether | 1,000,000 | Banking reserve, inter-power trade, astral transactions |

**Exchange Rate (Fixed by Astral Concord):** 100 Copper = 1 Silver; 100 Silver = 1 Gold; 100 Gold = 1 Aether-Crystal.

**Banking Institutions:**

| Institution | Location | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aether-Kosha Central Bank** | Madhya-Nagara | Issues currency, holds Aether-Crystal reserves, sets exchange rates, clears inter-power debts. |
| **Vitta-Parishad (Financial Council)** | Madhya-Nagara | Regulatory body; audits banks, sets interest rates, prevents fraud. |
| **Power Banks (Rajya-Kosha)** | Each capital | Local banking, loans, deposits, tax collection for each power. |
| **Vanijya-Griha (Trade Houses)** | All cities | Merchant banking, letters of credit, trade finance. |
| **Gandharva Exchange (Gandharva-Vyapar)** | Madhya-Nagara | Commodity exchange for ores, herbs, crystals, beast goods, weapons. |

**Banking Practices:**
- **Letters of Credit (Patra-Rin):** Paper-based credit instruments accepted across all five powers.
- **Prana-Bonds (Prana-Patra):** Government bonds issued by each power, backed by future tax revenue.
- **Insurance (Raksha-Nidhi):** Crop insurance, trade insurance, and beast-bond insurance offered by mutual guilds.
- **Telegraph Banking (Sutra-Banking):** Large transactions verified by Aetheric Telegraph between bank branches.

**Trans-Continental Trade Rails:**

The **Grand Astral Rail Network (Maha-Astral-Rail)** is the economic spine of the realm. Double-gauge steel tracks connect all five capitals and major towns. Trains are steam-and-Prana powered, armored for bandit and Rakshasa defense.

| Rail Line | Route | Cargo | Operator |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vajra-Madhya Line** | Vajra-Nagara ↔ Madhya-Nagara | Weapons, machinery, rifles | Vajra-Foundry Railway Co. |

| **Surya-Madhya Line** | Surya-Dwipa ↔ Madhya-Nagara | Sun-goods, salt, exotics | Free Port Rail |

**Trade Goods Flow:**
- Vajra-Foundry exports: rifles, artillery, rails, telegraph gear, printing presses.
- Aranya exports: herbs, Aether-Crystals, beast goods, medicines.
- Shaila exports: ores, gems, coal, metals.
- Samudra exports: spices, pearls, salt, fish, imports.
- Madhya-Nagara exports: banking, printing, news, logistics services.

### 1.6 Religious Pluralism & Philosophical Schools

**No Religious Monopoly (Dharma-Svatantrya):** There is no official religion, state church, or compulsory worship in the Astral Realm. The existence of Devas is a known fact, but **how each culture defines divinity is its own affair**. Six major philosophical interpretations coexist:

| # | School | View of the Divine | Adherents |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Cosmic Architect School (Vishwa-Nirmana-Vada)** | The Divine is the "Prime Source" (Mula-Srota) — cosmic architect and universal law, not a person. | Magitech cities, scientists, engineers |
| 2 | **Bhakti Devotion (Bhakti-Marga)** | Devas are living protectors and guardians; worship through temples, rites, idols, hymns. | Ancient mythic kingdoms, rural communities |
| 3 | **Asura Scientific Study (Asura-Jnana-Marga)** | Devas are real but are "supreme cosmic principles and laws" to be studied, not worshipped blindly. | Asura scholars, Seeker enclaves |
| 4 | **Nature Ancestor Worship (Prakriti-Purva-Vada)** | Life-force, sacred trees, forest spirits, and ancestors are divine. | Forest clans, beast tribes |
| 5 | **Independent Path (Svatantra-Marga)** | "Devas exist, but they are not my gods; my loyalty is to my own path." | Independent thinkers, cultivators |
| 6 | **Secular Pragmatism (Laukika-Vada)** | Daily industry, trade, and law take priority over theology. | Merchants, urban workers, officials |

**Religious Freedom (Dharma-Svatantrya Niyam):** All six schools (and any others) are protected by the Astral Concord. Persecution on religious grounds is a crime. Temples, ashrams, laboratories, and groves coexist in the same city. Festivals of all traditions are celebrated publicly.

**Prime Source (Mula-Srota):** The shared concept across all schools — the ultimate origin of cosmic law, energy, and consciousness. Whether called by name (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva, Prime Source, Cosmic Architect, Nature) varies by school, but all acknowledge a single supreme origin.

### 1.8 Law, Order & Defense (Astral-Level)

- **Astral Concord (Samvad-Sandhi):** The foundational treaty binding the five powers. Guarantees free trade, free movement, non-aggression, religious freedom, and mutual recognition of degrees and currency. Enforced by the **Concord Council (Samvad-Sabha)** — one representative from each power plus three neutral judges.
- **Astral Guard (Astral-Rakshak):** Each power maintains its own police and militia. No standing realm-wide army; the Concord provides a small joint peacekeeping force (Samvad-Sena) for border incidents and piracy.
- **Criminal Justice:** Each power has its own courts; serious cross-border crimes are tried by the **Concord Tribunal (Samvad-Nyaya-Pitha)** in Madhya-Nagara.
- **Defense Against Corrupted Realm:** The Astral Realm relies on the Buffer Zone (Tier 2) for protection against Corrupted Realm incursions. Astral powers contribute levies and supplies to the Setu Mahā-Senā.

| **Niraya-Kshetra (Hell-Zones)** | Regions of extreme distortion; reality itself is unstable. |
| **Khanda-Droni (Fractured Chasm)** | The rift-valley border zone; space-time fractures frequent. |

**Atmosphere:** Perpetual twilight-to-night sky; red-black clouds; corrupted prana storms (Tamas-Vatavaran); air is mildly toxic — long-term residents develop immunity, newcomers sicken. No sun; illumination comes from corrupted bioluminescence, fire, and Prana-lamps.

**"Might is Right" (Shakti-Hi-Niyam):** No cosmic law, no High Council authority, no dharma applies here. Power is the only law. The strong rule; the weak serve or die.

### 2.2 Dual Civilization A — The Sovereign Abyss (Native Corrupted Lords)

**Identity:** The **Native Corrupted Lords** are not exiles — they are the indigenous inhabitants of the Corrupted Realm. Their subtle bodies have been mutated by Tamas (toxic distortion) into **Devils (Soul-Lifeforms)** — beings of vast corrupted prana, shapeshifting ability, and night-strider power.

**Self-Proclaimed Status — Fake Gods (Khoṭe Deva / Andhakāra-Īśvara):**
- They declare themselves "Dark Sovereigns" and "Lower Gods" (Adho-Deva).
- Weaker beings worship them out of terror and necessity, building temples of fear.
- They demand tribute, sacrifice, and absolute obedience.
- Their "divinity" is a fraud — they are powerful Devils, not true Devas.

**Hierarchy & Titles:**

| Title (Roman) | Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Abyssal Lord (Mahā-Prabhū)** | Supreme ruler of a dominion; self-styled "god" |
| **Warlord (Durga-Pati)** | Military commander of a fortress-territory |
| **Fiend Commander (Piśāca-Sena-Pati)** | Leader of corrupted armies and night-striders |
| **Dark Priest (Tamas-Pujari)** | Ritualist who channels corrupted prana; "miracle" performer |
| **Beast Master (Prani-Swami)** | Controller of corrupted beasts and mutations |

**Powers & Abilities:**
- **Vast Corrupted Prana (Mahā-Tamas-Prana):** Enormous reserves of toxic energy; can fuel devastating attacks.
- **Kama-Rupa (Shapeshifting):** Spontaneous transformation into any beast, maiden, titan, or object.
- **Niśācara (Night-Strider):** Strength, regeneration, and illusion arts quadruple between dusk and dawn; peak at midnight.
- **Māyā-Yuddha (Illusion Warfare):** Conjuring raining blood, illusory armies, falling boulders, sonic screams.
- **Mutation Induction (Vikriti-Janana):** Can corrupt and mutate lesser beings, creating monstrous servitors.

**Weaknesses:**
- Direct sunlight weakens deepest illusions.
- Holy sanctified fire (Yajñāgni) dissolves enchantments.
- Pure mantra recitation (Shuddha-Mantra) causes pain.

**Economy (Sovereign Abyss):**
- **Toxic Wells (Tamas-Kunda):** The primary resource; corrupted prana is drawn, refined, and weaponized.
- **Corrupted Crystals (Tamas-Krystal):** Crystallized toxic prana; used as currency and power source within the Abyss.
- **Tribute (Bali):** Demanded from subject populations; paid in slaves, goods, and worship.
- **Slave Labor (Dasa-Karma):** Enslaved weaker beings (mutated humans, lesser devils, captured beasts) work the wells and forges.

**Territories:**
- **Netherholme:** The capital city; a dark metropolis of corrupted towers, temples of fear, and slave markets.
- **Dread-Hold:** The great fortress-citadel; impregnable, carved from black basalt and corrupted metal.
- **Shadow-Hollow:** Cities that exist only at night; by day they are ruins or invisible.

### 2.3 Dual Civilization B — The Penitent Outlaws / Exiled Convicts

**Identity:** The **Penitent Outlaws** are not native to the Corrupted Realm. They are **high-level criminals exiled from the Buffer Zone or Astral Realm** for grave crimes (treason, illegal rift-opening, corrupted experiments, mass murder, forbidden Astra use). They are humans, Devas, Asuras, and even former Buffer Zone officers who fell from grace.

**Self-Styled Status:** They do not claim divinity. They are **survivors and builders** — they have carved out territories, built penal fortresses, and established black markets in the toxic wasteland. Some seek redemption (Penitent); most seek power and profit (Outlaw).

**Origin Crimes (Exile Triggers):**
- Treason against the Buffer Zone or Astral powers.
- Unauthorized rift-opening (Rift-Kholna).
- Corrupted experiments on living beings.
- Mass murder or Astra terrorism.
- Forbidden Astra use in populated areas.
- Collusion with Sovereign Abyss lords.

**Hierarchy & Titles:**

| Title (Roman) | Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Outlaw Lord (Vidrohi-Raja)** | Supreme ruler of a penal territory |
| **Syndicate Boss (Bazaar-Nayak)** | Black-market kingpin; controls trade routes |
| **Rogue Seeker (Vidrohi-Seeker)** | Former Buffer Zone researcher; now experiments freely |
| **Pirate Rail Captain (Chaya-Rail-Nayak)** | Commander of a pirate train; controls a rail line |
| **Contraband Smith (Kala-Lohar)** | Arms dealer; forges hybrid weapons |
| **Shadow Courier (Chaya-Doot)** | Smuggler; runs goods across the wall |

**Powers & Abilities:**
- **Stolen Tech & Mantras:** Exiles carry knowledge of Buffer Zone technology (steam engines, telegraph, rifles) and forbidden mantras.
- **Seal Codes (Bandha-Kood):** Some exiles possess knowledge of cosmic seal codes — valuable for bypassing or weakening seals (illegal).
- **Hybrid Weapons (Sankara-Astra):** Forged from stolen Astral tech and corrupted Abyssal materials.
- **Rogue Cultivation (Vidrohi-Tapasya):** Unregulated, dangerous cultivation practices that boost power at great cost (mutation, madness).

**Economy (Penitent Outlaws):**
- **Black Markets (Kala-Bazaar):** Underground cities where contraband is traded — stolen tech, forbidden mantras, seal codes, corrupted crystals, weapons.
- **Pirate Rail (Chaya-Rail):** Stolen or modified trains running on hidden tracks; used for smuggling and transport.
- **Contraband Syndicates (Kala-Sangha):** Organized crime networks controlling specific trade routes and goods.
- **Penal Fortresses (Vidrohi-Kila):** Self-governing fortified settlements; each is a mini-state.

**Territories:**
- **Vidrohi-Kila:** Penal fortresses scattered across eastern Corrupted Realm; each ruled by an Outlaw Lord.
- **Kala-Bazaar:** Underground market cities; neutral ground where Sovereign Abyss and Outlaws trade.
- **Chaya-Marga:** Hidden rail network; pirate trains run on secret tracks.

### 2.4 Fluid Dynamics (Conflict, Trade & Symbiosis)

The two civilizations are not permanently at war. Their relationship is **fluid** — shifting between conflict, trade, and symbiosis.

**Conflicts (Sangharsha):**
- **Toxic Well Wars (Kunda-Yuddha):** Fought over control of Tamas-Kunda (toxic wells) and mineral veins.
- **Territory Raids (Pradesh-Chadhayi):** Sovereign Abyss lords raid Outlaw settlements for slaves and tech; Outlaws raid Abyssal caravans for crystals and power.
- **Night Raids (Niśācara-Chadhayi):** Rakshasa night-striders attack Outlaw fortresses at midnight when their power peaks.

**Partnerships & Trade (Vyapar & Sanyoga):**
- **Tech-for-Power Exchange (Yantra-Shakti Vyapar):** Outlaws trade stolen Astral technology, mantras, and seal codes to Sovereign Abyss lords in exchange for corrupted prana, crystals, and mutant servitors.
- **Hybrid Weapon Forges (Sankara-Karkhana):** Joint factories where Outlaw engineers and Abyssal smiths produce hybrid weapons.
- **Information Trade (Suchana-Vyapar):** Outlaws sell intelligence on Buffer Zone patrols and Astral Realm movements to Abyssal lords.

**Integrated Societies (Mishra-Samaj):**
- Many large fortresses house both Native Devils and Exiled Outlaws.
- In some, an Outlaw scholar serves as chief advisor to a Rakshasa lord.
- In others, Rakshasa warriors serve in an Outlaw lord's army.
- **Kala-Bazaar** is the ultimate mixed city — neutral market where all factions trade.

### 2.5 Impenetrable Cosmic Seals & Dark Entities

**The Abyssal Vaults (Mahā-Kāra-Griha):** In the southern Dead Zones of the Corrupted Realm lie regions so dangerous that even Sovereign Abyss lords and Outlaws avoid them. These are the **Abyssal Vaults** — absolute quarantine zones housing **Dark Entities (Kāla-Asstitva)**: primordial, realm-level threats capable of destroying the entire Corrupted Realm and beyond.

**Dark Entities (Kāla-Asstitva) — Classification:**

| Entity | Designation | Nature | Threat Level |

| **Mahā-Rāhu (The Great Eclipse)** | Kāla-Graha-1 | Cosmic swallower; devours light and prana | Realm-destroying |
| **Kāla-Ketu (The Comet of Doom)** | Kāla-Graha-2 | Tail of destruction; brings plague and madness | Realm-destroying |
| **Tamas-Śeṣa (The Dark Serpent)** | Kāla-Nāga-1 | Corrupted counterpart of Ananta; seeks to unmake the cosmos | Cosmic |
| **Nirṛti-Mahārāja (Lord of Decay)** | Kāla-Raja-1 | Entity of entropy and dissolution; unmaking of form | Realm-destroying |
| **Andhakāra-Brahma (The Corrupted Creator)** | Kāla-Brahma-1 | Twisted creator-being; spawns abominations | Cosmic |
| **Mahā-Piśāca (The Great Fiend)** | Kāla-Piśāca-1 | Arch-fiend; commands all lesser piśācas | Region-destroying |
| **Kāla-Kālī (The Dark Mother)** | Kāla-Mata-1 | Entity of corrupted feminine power; spawns horrors | Realm-destroying |

**Impenetrable Cosmic Seals (Abhedya Mahā-Bandha):**

These entities are not merely imprisoned — they are bound by **impenetrable cosmic power-system seals** created by the Supreme Trinity and the High Council in ancient times. The seals are:

- **Permanent (Nitya-Bandha):** Cannot be undone by any being in the Corrupted Realm, Buffer Zone, or Astral Realm.
- **Cosmic Power-System Based (Mahā-Prana-Bandha):** Powered by the fundamental laws of the cosmos, not by any individual's power.
- **Multi-Layered (Bahustariya):** Each seal has multiple layers — physical, energetic, karmic, and cosmic.
- **Self-Repairing (Svayam-Punarsthapana):** If damaged, the seal repairs itself using cosmic energy.
- **Monitoring (Nirikshana):** Ananta (the System Core) continuously monitors seal integrity; any anomaly triggers a Cosmic Mandate alert to the High Council.

**Seal Types:**

| Seal Type | Creator | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trinity-Seal (Trimurti-Bandha)** | Supreme Trinity | The strongest seal; used for cosmic-level entities. |
| **Ananta-Bandha** | Ananta (System Core) | System-level seal; self-repairing, unbreakable. |
| **Rudra-Bandha** | Shiva Delegate | Destruction-aspect seal; for entities of chaos. |
| **Vishnu-Bandha** | Vishnu Delegate | Preservation-aspect seal; for entities of corruption. |
| **Brahma-Bandha** | Brahma Delegate | Creation-aspect seal; for entities that spawn abominations. |
| **Mahā-Pālaka Seal** | Mahā-Pālaka (High Guardian) | Guardian seal; used for region-level threats. |

**Seal Integrity Protocol:**
- Ananta monitors all seals in real-time.
- Any seal fluctuation triggers an alert to the Mahā-Pālaka and the Buffer Zone's Vajra-Durga.
- The Vajra-Dal (elite commandos) conduct periodic seal inspections in the Abyssal Vaults.
- Seal breach is a **Cosmic Emergency (Mahā-Apatkāl)** — triggers Apat-Dharma protocol and High Council intervention.

**Access to Dead Zones:** Strictly forbidden. No being — Sovereign Abyss lord, Outlaw, or Buffer Zone officer — may enter without Supreme Trinity mandate. The Vajra-Dal enters only with Tier 1 clearance and a specific mission. Entry without authorization is punished by permanent exile to the Corrupted Realm (if from Astral/Buffer) or execution (if from Corrupted Realm).
