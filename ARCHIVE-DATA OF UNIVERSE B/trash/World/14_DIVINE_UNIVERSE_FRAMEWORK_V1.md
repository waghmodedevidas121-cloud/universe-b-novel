## 1. The Permanent Bedrock: Supreme Trinity (परम त्रिमूर्ती - Almighty)

The three Supreme Beings are the absolute source and terminus of the entire universe. 

```
                       [ SUPREME TRINITY (Almighty) ]
                                     │
      ┌──────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┐
      ▼                              ▼                              ▼
  [ BRAHMĀ ]                    [ VIṢṆU ]                       [ ŚIVA ]
  Creation of System            Preservation of System          Destruction & Dissolution
  & Entire Universe             & Sustaining Cosmic Laws        of the System & Universe
```

### The Transcendent Rule
- **Immunity from System Mechanics:** The Supreme Trinity exist completely **above** the 5 Pillars of the Power System (Authority, Energy, Karma, Tapasya, Boons). 
- These mechanical constraints govern gods, asuras, and mortals, but **cannot bind the Supreme Trinity**.
- No extra bureaucratic micro-management or arbitrary duties are pinned to Them; Their cosmic roles are pure, ultimate, and absolute.

## 2. Cosmic Structure: Stratified Unknowns

Rather than a simplistic linear ladder, the cosmos is structured into multi-dimensional strata with vast unknown spaces:

```
[ LEVEL 1: SUPREME TRINITY ]
Brahmā · Viṣṇu · Śiva (Almighty Source)
              │
              ▼
[ LEVEL 2: UNKNOWN COSMIC LAYER ]
Primordial Cosmic Entities & Transcendent Watchers (Names & Authorities yet to be revealed)
              │
              ▼
[ LEVEL 3: DIVINE & ASURIC POLITIES (Sovereign Powers) ]
- Divine Federation / Devas (Rulers of Celestial Spheres & Cosmic Law)
- Asura Civilizations (Autonomous Empires, Scholars & Independent Clans)
              │
              ▼
[ LEVEL 4: THE MULTIPLE WORLDS & UNKNOWN REALMS ]
Mortal Earth, Parallel Dimensions, Subterranean Paradises, Hidden Pockets
(Where our Isekai characters awaken with strictly localized knowledge)
```

## 4. Asuras: Complex Civilization, Not a Monolith

Asuras are not a barbaric horde of one-dimensional monsters. They represent a high-order, ancient civilization split into distinct ideological factions:

1. **The Sovereign Faction (प्रभुत्व गट):**
   - Focused on political autonomy, imperial strength, defending their realm from celestial encroachment.
2. **The Scholar Faction (ज्ञान व विज्ञान गट):**
   - Deep investigators of cosmic laws, metaphysical mechanics, ancient languages, and the nature of the Archive.
3. **The Warrior Faction (योद्धा गट):**
   - Dedicated to unyielding martial excellence, discipline, honor, and protecting their kin through overwhelming physical/prāṇic might.
4. **The Independent Faction (स्वतंत्र गट):**
   - Refuse allegiance to both Deva federations and Asura kings; nomadic cultivators, wanderers, and isolationists living by their own code.

## 5. Mystery Management: The 4-Layer Gradual Reveal

To maintain tension and wonder without overwhelming readers with lore-dumps, the truth of the universe unfolds in four controlled layers:

```
[ LAYER 1: LOCAL HORIZON ]
Local world powers, mortal struggles, fragmented legends, immediate survival.
            │
            ▼
[ LAYER 2: SYSTEM CRACKS & CONTRADICTIONS ]
Characters discover glaring inconsistencies between orthodox religious myths and actual reality.
            │
            ▼
[ LAYER 3: THE COSMIC EXPANSION ]
Signals and appearances of previously unknown Cosmic Entities, forgotten Realms, and the Ocean.
            │
            ▼
[ LAYER 4: THE FRONTIER OF THE SUPREME ]
Even the most ancient gods and sages discover that their knowledge of the universe has profound limits.
```
