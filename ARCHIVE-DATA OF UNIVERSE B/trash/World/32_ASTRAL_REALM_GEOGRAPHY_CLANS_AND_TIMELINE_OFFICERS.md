## 0. Executive Canon Redesign: The 5 Sub-Realms

The Astral Realm (Tier 3) is **not a single continuous landmass with open walking borders**. It consists of **five distinct, spatially isolated Sub-Realms (उप-लोक)** suspended in the Astral void, connected exclusively through **Interdimensional Magic (आयामी जादू)**.

```
       [ 1. DEVA SUB-REALM ]                 [ 2. ASURA SUB-REALM ]
```

## 1. The Two Universal Iron Laws

### Law 1: The Commoner vs. Apex Rule (सामान्य विरुद्ध शिखर नियम)
Lineage never guarantees power. Across all four living species, **the overwhelming majority are ordinary commoners with ZERO combat power or magic**:

| Species | The Commoners (The Vast Majority) | The Apex Elites (Few % Awoken) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Humans** | **95%+ of population:** farmers, shopkeepers, weavers, miners. Purely physical, ordinary life. | **1–2% Awoken:** Scholars, martial practitioners, talisman-inscribers who master spatial arts. |
| **Devas** | Simple artisans, scribes, temple-tenders, gardeners. No battle-skills or flight. | **Deva Lords:** Masters of celestial Aether resonance, harmonic barriers, and ancient astras. |
| **Asuras** | Blacksmiths, stonemasons, scrap-sorters, farm hands. No dark arts or destructive force. | **Asura Warlords & Masters:** Possess immense biological vigor, void-tearing, and runic steam smithing. |
| **Beasts** | Natural wildlife, birds, deer, draft oxen, field animals. | **Apex Beasts:** Spiritual titans, sky-cleaving rocs, and void-stepping beasts with innate spatial slips. |

### Law 2: Known Dimensional Spheres, Insuperable Void (ज्ञात विश्व, अभेद्य पोकळी)
- **Universal Knowledge (Zero Ignorance Cliché):** In all four species, the existence of the 5 Sub-Realms is an established geographical and astronomical fact. 
  - 19th-century schools, textbooks, and daily newspapers (*Astral Samachar*) openly publish maps of the five dimensional spheres.
  - A common Human farmer or Asura foundry-apprentice knows that the Deva Sub-Realm sits in the celestial ether, the Asura Sub-Realm in the volcanic depths, the Beast Sub-Realm in the wild expanse, and The Central in the high dimensional core.
  - Luxury goods imported from The Central (Deva silks, Asura basalt-steel tools, Beast herbal extracts) are sold in metropolitan markets.
- **The Physical Void Barrier (Why Commoners Cannot Cross):**
  - Just as 19th-century Earth mortals knew the Moon and Mars existed through telescopes but could not travel there, the space separating sub-realms is an **Aetheric Spatial Void (आयामी पोकळी)**.
  - No rail tracks, telegraph cables, steamships, or ordinary aircraft can bridge the vacuum between sub-realms.
  - The pressure, gravitational shear, and atmospheric hostility of the void would crush a non-powered mortal instantly.
  - **The Transit Monopoly:** Crossing requires high-tier Interdimensional Magic (mastered only by ~1% Apex Big Ones) or multi-million-coin pressurized guild-transports. To an everyday worker, life remains rooted in their home sub-realm.

### Law 3: The Historical Genesis — The Great Sundering (महा-विभाजन)
Originally, in Eras 1 & 2, the Astral Realm was a single, contiguous continent. It was severed into five distinct sub-realms for two existential reasons:
1. **Biological Scale Mismatch:** Mortals (95% non-powered humans) could not survive alongside primeval titans and asuric smelting cauldrons without being crushed or poisoned.
2. **The Kin Wars:** Rampant resource wars over pure Aether-crystal veins threatened to fracture the continental crust. Sages and High Council marshals enacted a controlled spatial sundering to preserve civilization.

## 2. The Five Sub-Realms in Detail

---

### Sub-Realm 1: The Deva Sub-Realm (देव लोक — Celestial Order)
- **Topography:** Luminous floating cloud-plateaus, crystalline marble peaks, sacred water groves, and serene temple-citadels.
- **Societal Reality:** Not all Devas are saints or divine warriors. Common Devas live peaceful, modest lives as weavers of silk, scribes of classical literature, and incense-harvesters.
- **The Apex Devas:** Elite ritualists and celestial martialists who harness **Celestial Spatial Resonance**. They guard their sub-realm against outside disruption and maintain ancestral energy bastions.
- **Technology & Magic:** Classical divine arts, resonance bells, Aether-purification arrays. Machinery is largely absent; value is placed on ancestral harmony.

---

### Sub-Realm 2: The Asura Sub-Realm (असुर लोक — Chthonic Ambition)
- **Topography:** Volcanic mountain chains, subterranean magma vaults, basalt towers, and dense industrial smelting valleys.
- **Societal Reality:** Asuras are not mindless monsters. Common Asuras are hard-working, family-oriented artisans, heavy-metal smiths, and trench diggers. They are blunt, proud, and pragmatic.
- **The Apex Asuras:** Masters of **Dimensional Tearing** and ancient chthonic magic. Certain advanced Asura clans have harnessed heavy steam machinery, bolt-action ordnance, and high-pressure metallurgy, creating fortress-foundries like *Vajra-Nagara*.
- **Technology & Magic:** Heavy runic ironworks, steam turbines, biological enhancement seals, and destructive elemental arts.

---

### Sub-Realm 3: The Beast Sub-Realm (श्वापद लोक — Primeval Wilderness)
- **Topography:** 100,000-mile boundless ancient canopies, primordial marshes, colossal bone-canyons, and untamed Aether-lakes.
- **Societal Reality:** A pure hierarchy of the natural food chain. The vast majority are common animals and docile wildlife living in natural balance.
- **The Apex Beasts:** Mythic leviathans, colossal four-horned bears (*Vajra-Bhalluka*), and void-panthers possessing **Innate Spatial Slip** capabilities.
- **Cross-Realm Presence:** Approximately 5–10% of beasts exist across the Human, Deva, and Asura sub-realms through ancestral migration or voluntary **Aether-Bonds** (symbiotic partnership with mortals for labor, tracking, and transport).

---

### Sub-Realm 4: The Human Sub-Realm (मानव लोक — The Mortal Cradle)
- **Topography:** Diverse continental geography — fertile river valleys, bustling 19th-century brick-and-timber market towns, railway junctions, and farming plains.
- **Societal Reality:** The world of common humanity. Over 95% of inhabitants are everyday mortals living under local kings, mayors, and guild masters. They have no access to magic and believe gods and demons belong to holy scriptures.
- **The Rare Awoken (1–2%):** Scholars, ascetics in remote hermitages (*Rishis*), and wandering martialists who master Qi/Aether cultivation, talisman inscriptions, and rudimentary spatial rituals.
- **Technology:** 19th-century mechanical baseline — watermills, early steam engines, double-gauge local rail, printing presses, and hand-cranked telegraphs.

---

### Sub-Realm 5: The Central Sub-Realm (मध्यवर्ती महा-लोक — The Apex Political Core)
- **Location & Nature:** Completely detached from the mortal plane. Geographically and dimensionally **inaccessible to commoners**. No physical road, sea, or railway leads here.
- **Population Scale (500,000 to 800,000 Total):**
  - **Apex Big Ones & Diplomats (~10% / 50k–80k):** High Deva Lords, Asura Patriarchs, Human Diplomatic Rishis, Beast Kings, and Grand Merchant Oligarchs with their elite retainers.
  - **Hereditary Servant & Artisan Clans (~90% / 4.5L–7.2L):** Generations born within the service quarters. They manage plumbing, steam conduits, kitchens, and gardens—completely indifferent and disconnected from cosmic wars.
- **The Four Faction Quarters & Grand Concord Dome:**
  1. *The Celestial Pavilion (Deva Quarter):* White marble spires and consecrated water gardens.
  2. *The Basalt Bastion (Asura Quarter):* Volcanic basalt towers, steam turbines, and ore-exchange chambers.
  3. *The Sages' Haven (Human Quarter):* Timber pavilions, telegraph exchanges, embassies of mortal dynasties, and high academies.
  4. *The Primal Grove (Beast Quarter):* Colossal ancient canopies and aether-flora sanctuaries.
  5. *The Grand Concord Dome (मध्यवर्ती महा-सभागृह):* Demilitarized central council hall where the **Absolute Non-Violence Truce** is strictly enforced.
- **How The Central Indirectly Controls All 4 Sub-Realms (अप्रत्यक्ष नियंत्रण यंत्रणा):**
  Because direct armies cannot march across the spatial void, the Big Ones in The Central dominate the outer sub-realms through four indirect strangleholds:
  1. **Monetary & Credit Squeeze:** The *Aether-Kosha Central Bank* in The Central issues realm currency (Aether-Mudra). A single interest rate hike or credit freeze from The Central can destabilize mortal kingdoms overnight.
  2. **Resource Cartels & Embargoes:** The Central's Grand Exchange controls vital trade flows—Deva silks, Asura refined weaponry, Beast alchemical herbs, and Human grain/coal. Defiant kings face devastating commodity embargoes.
  3. **Spatial Transit Licensing:** High-tier spatial bubbles required for inter-realm cargo are owned exclusively by Central-chartered guilds. Cutting a kingdom from transit isolates its economy.
  4. **Shadow Politics & Kingmaking:** Using high-speed Aetheric telegraphs and intelligence syndicates, Central oligarchs fund proxy rebellions and manipulate royal successions across the outer sub-realms.

---

### Special Doctrine: The Three Tiers of Rishis & Sages (ऋषी व मुनींचे तीन स्तर)
1. **Grassroots Sages (Human Sub-Realm):** Reside in remote ashrams and sacred riverbanks. Guide the 1–2% Awoken mortals in shastras, talismans, and traditional medicine.
2. **Diplomatic Rishis (The Central Sub-Realm):** Dwell in *The Sages' Haven*. Act as fierce intellectual ambassadors protecting mortal human interests against Deva and Asura oligarchs.
3. **Cosmic High Sages (Tier 1 High Council):** Belong to the *Assembly of Sages* under the Chief Sage; study cosmic rifts, Ananta anomalies, and universal metaphysics.

## 3. The Interdimensional Magic System (आयामी जादू)

Because physical portals and glowing sci-fi doors do not exist, transition between Sub-Realms is governed entirely by specialized spatial arts:

```
[ CASTER'S SPECIES ] ────────► [ SPATIAL MECHANISM ] ────────► [ MANIFESTATION ]
Deva Apex              Celestial Resonance             Harmonic Dissolution & Golden Reform
Asura Apex             Dimensional Tearing             Violent Void-Tear / Runic Breach
Apex Beast             Innate Spatial Slip             Subconscious Slip through Space-Grain
Awoken Human           Artifact Array / Circles        Aether-Crystal Matrices & Talisman Anchors
```

### 1. Requirements for Self-Transit
1. **High Aether Reserves:** A practitioner must possess Tier 4 (Maha-Siddha) or equivalent spiritual density to withstand dimensional sheer.
2. **Spatial Coordinate Anchor (दिक्-अंक):** Without an exact mental or runic coordinate of the destination sub-realm, spatial folding collapses into the void.
3. **Physical Resistance:** Common bodies are crushed by the gravitational differential between sub-realms; crossing requires personal shielding.

### 2. Transporting Commoners (The Bubble Ritual)
When an Apex Master must transport common servants or goods to the Central Sub-Realm:
- They construct a **Spatial Stabilizer Array (आयामी संरक्षण मंडळ)** powered by high-grade Aether-Crystals.
- The commoners are enclosed within an isolated atmospheric bubble that absorbs spatial friction during the fold.
