## 1. The Core Architecture & Mainstream Rules

This compendium harmonizes the most recognizable, beloved powers across world fiction into a single, cohesive framework:

```
[ THE FIVE PILLARS FOUNDATION ]
- AUTHORITY : Laws, Spheres, Domains, Cosmic Enforcement Keys.
- ENERGY    : Prāṇa, Mana, Ki, Aura, Chakra, Stamina (Direct Fuel).
- KARMA     : Probability, Cosmic Momentum, Backlash, Retribution, Destiny Threads.
- TAPASYA   : Discipline, Cultivation, Refinement, Mastery Tiers, Internal Hardening.
- BOONS     : Granted Gifts, Contracts, Divine Armaments, Conditional Immunities.
[ THE SIXTH PRINCIPLE ] : The Secret Existence/Soul Mystery (Mechanics Hidden).
```

### Universal Mainstream Combat Rules
1. **Power vs. Skill:** Power provides output; Skill dictates precision, fuel-efficiency, and trajectory.
2. **Mastery Progression:** Basic -> Adept -> Expert -> Master -> Conceptual / Sovereign.
3. **Resistances & Immunities:** Built through Tapasya, lineage, or Boons (e.g., Elemental, Poison, Mental, Spatial, Soul, Curse Resistances).
4. **Energy Economy & Recoil:** Overdrawing energy causes internal vessel trauma, burning nadis, or biological exhaustion.
5. **Counters & Circumvention:** Brute force cracks basic barriers; spatial cuts bypass physical armor; pure will shatters illusions; loopholes unravel boons.
6. **Domain / Barrier Expansion:** Projecting an internalized reality outward to command the battlefield (*Kṣetra-Nirmāṇa*).

### Naming & Linguistic Conventions [CANON]
- **Promote Authentic Hinduism:** Core mythological weapons, astras, and concepts retain their iconic original names (*Brahmāstra, Sudarśana Cakra, Pāśupatāstra, Nāgapāśa, Vajra, Garuḍāstra, Karma, Tapasya, Ātma-tattva, Kundalini, Chakravyūha*).
- **English for Technological & Industrial Terms:** 19th/early-20th-century mechanical and industrial terms use clean modern English without contrived Sanskrit (*Steam Locomotives, Railways, Telegraph Wires, Printing Press, Newspapers, Bolt-Action Rifles, Revolvers, Pocket Watches, Clock Towers, Brass Gears - Zero AI*).
- **Modern Language for Complex/Obscure Concepts:** Unfamiliar or heavy Sanskrit philosophical terms are rendered in accessible modern English (*Domain Expansion, Spatial Severance, Dimensional Anchor, Retraction Code, Karmic Backlash*).
- **Multi-Linguistic Texture:** Different civilizations naturally reflect distinct linguistic registers (English/scientific for Magitech metropolises, Sanskrit/Hindi for ancient kingdoms, engineering terms for Asura enclaves, and nature idioms for beast clans).
