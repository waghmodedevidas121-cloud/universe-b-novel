### Seam 2: Why Other Reincarnated Souls (80–90% Bracket) Do Not Expose the Truth
- **Problem:** If 10% of the population are reincarnated souls, why hasn't someone disclosed the cosmic framework?
- **Watertight Fix (Vidhata's Filter vs. Neel's Raw Glitch):**

  * Lord Vidhata scrubs previous worldly memories, retaining only combat reflexes and C-Rank potential.
  * Neel alone arrived via an irregular **Spatial Glitch**, retaining his full memory—yet his natural skepticism keeps him from connecting the dots prematurely.

### Seam 3: Acoustic Masking of Rotary Shadow Anchors (No Steam Engines)
- **Problem:** With 19th-century steam hammers removed, how is the 180-decibel grinding of the basalt Shadow Anchors concealed?
- **Watertight Fix (Natural & Civic Acoustic Masking):**

  * In the Asura Shard, masking is provided by volcanic detonations; in the Deva Shard, by the roaring *Weeping Cascades*.

### Seam 4: The Origin of "The Sing" Without Firearms
