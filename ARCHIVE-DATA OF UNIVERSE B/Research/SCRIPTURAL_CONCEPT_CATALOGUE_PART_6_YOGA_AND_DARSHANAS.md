SECTION 0 — HOW TO READ DARŚANA SOURCES
============================================================

1. “Darśana” म्हणजे तत्त्वज्ञानात्मक दृष्टिकोन/परंपरा; तो आधुनिक RPG skill tree नाही.
2. प्रत्येक परंपरेचा प्राथमिक ग्रंथ, सूत्र, भाष्य आणि ऐतिहासिक context वेगळा नोंदवा.
3. एका परंपरेचा शब्द दुसऱ्या परंपरेतील त्याच शब्दाशी नेहमी समान अर्थाने वापरला जातो असे नाही.
4. Yoga, Sāṃkhya, Nyāya, Vaiśeṣika, Mīmāṃsā आणि Vedānta हे एकाच मताचे सहा विभाग नाहीत; त्यांच्या मूलभूत मतांमध्ये महत्त्वाचे फरक आहेत.
5. योगसूत्रातील siddhi-वर्णनांना साध्या “ability unlock” यादीत कमी करू नये. पतंजलीच्या ग्रंथात सिद्धी आणि समाधी यांच्यातील संबंध गुंतागुंतीचा आहे; विभूती साध्य आणि अंतिम मुक्ती एकच गोष्ट नाहीत.
6. Universe B च्या Power System मधील Authority, Energy, Karma, Tapasya, Boons आणि Secret Sixth Pillar हे novel-specific organization आहे; ते सहा दर्शनांचे किंवा हिंदू तत्त्वज्ञानाचे अधिकृत वर्गीकरण नाही.

ENTRY 01 — PĀTAÑJALA YOGA / पतंजली योग
============================================================

Primary source:
Patañjali, Yoga Sūtra; especially Samādhi Pāda, Sādhana Pāda, Vibhūti Pāda and Kaivalya Pāda. Exact sutra numbering should be checked in a named edition/commentary.

Core idea:
पतंजली योगसूत्र मन, वृत्ती, अभ्यास, वैराग्य, ध्यान, समाधी आणि कैवल्य यांवर केंद्रित एक विशिष्ट तत्त्वज्ञानात्मक/साधनात्मक परंपरा मांडते. आधुनिक “yoga = physical exercise” हा अर्थ योगसूत्रातील संपूर्ण चौकट पकडत नाही.

Source-supported observations:
- Yoga Sūtra 1.2 defines yoga in terms of the cessation/restraint of mental fluctuations; translations differ in how they render citta-vṛtti-nirodha.
- अभ्यास आणि वैराग्य हे मनाच्या स्थैर्याशी संबंधित महत्त्वाचे घटक आहेत.
- आठ अंगांचा योग (aṣṭāṅga) Sādhana Pāda मध्ये मांडला आहे.
- Vibhūti Pāda मध्ये संयम आणि विशिष्ट ज्ञान/सामर्थ्यांशी संबंधित सूत्रे येतात.
- Kaivalya हे अंतिम उद्दिष्ट म्हणून मांडले जाते; प्रत्येक विभूतीला अंतिम ध्येय समजणे चुकीचे ठरू शकते.

What this DOES NOT automatically establish:
- योगाभ्यास करणाऱ्याला हमखास supernatural powers मिळतात.
- प्रत्येक siddhi आधुनिक अर्थाने प्रयोगशाळेत पडताळलेली क्षमता आहे.
- सर्व योगपरंपरा पतंजलीच्या चौकटीशी पूर्णपणे समान आहेत.
- समाधी म्हणजे combat mode किंवा invulnerability.

[PROPOSAL] Discipline, attention, perception and liberation-related themes यांचा अभ्यास करण्यासाठी source reference म्हणून वापरता येईल.
[CAUTION] योगसूत्रातील siddhi-वर्णन आणि fictional ability यांमध्ये स्पष्ट भेद ठेवा.
[OPEN] Universe B मध्ये meditation-related abilities असतील का, याबाबत कोणताही rule canon नाही.

ENTRY 02 — SĀṂKHYA / सांख्य
============================================================

Primary source frame:
Classical Sāṃkhya tradition; Sāṃkhyakārikā of Īśvarakṛṣṇa is a key surviving classical text. Earlier Sāṃkhya ideas also appear in other Indian sources, but they should not all be treated as identical.

Core idea:
Classical Sāṃkhya commonly distinguishes Puruṣa (consciousness/witness) and Prakṛti (primordial nature), and explains experience and manifestation through a set of tattvas. Exact interpretation varies across texts and historical presentations.

Source-supported observations:
- Puruṣa and Prakṛti are central categories in classical Sāṃkhya.
- The three guṇas—sattva, rajas and tamas—are associated with Prakṛti in this framework.
- Sāṃkhya's account of tattvas is a philosophical account, not automatically a catalogue of magical elements.
- Classical Sāṃkhya is often described as non-theistic in its standard form, but historical relations between Sāṃkhya ideas and other theistic traditions are complex.

What this DOES NOT automatically establish:
- Sattva, rajas and tamas are three energy types or elemental attacks.
- Every character can choose a guna like a class.
- Puruṣa is a character's combat soul-stat.
- Every Hindu tradition uses the same Sāṃkhya metaphysics.

[CAUTION] That is a novel-specific selection inspired by Hindu concepts, not proof that classical Sāṃkhya describes Universe B's migration system.
[OPEN] The exact mechanics of the guṇas in Universe B remain open; do not convert them into numerical stats without user approval.

ENTRY 03 — NYĀYA / न्याय
============================================================

Primary source:
Nyāya Sūtra attributed to Akṣapāda Gautama, with a long commentarial tradition.

Core idea:
Nyāya is a philosophical tradition concerned with knowledge, reasoning, inference, debate and the conditions of valid cognition. It develops a detailed account of pramāṇas (means of knowledge), though exact lists and interpretations should be tied to a specific Nyāya school and period.

Source-supported observations:
- Nyāya gives central importance to valid knowledge and reasoning.
- Perception, inference, comparison and testimony are standard pramāṇa categories in classical presentations; later schools may expand or refine the account.
- Debate and reasoning are not merely rhetorical tricks; they are connected to knowledge and the removal of error.
- Nyāya's technical terms should be read in their philosophical context.

What this DOES NOT automatically establish:
- Nyāya is a supernatural truth-detection power.
- A good logician is always morally good or omniscient.
- Every inference is infallible.
- All Indian schools accept the same pramāṇa list.

Universe B use:
[PROPOSAL] For mystery-writing, Nyāya can inspire disciplined evidence evaluation, inference and detection of reasoning errors—without claiming supernatural certainty.
[OPEN] Whether Universe B has a formal investigation discipline or knowledge system is undecided.

ENTRY 04 — VAIŚEṢIKA / वैशेषिक
============================================================

Primary source:
Vaiśeṣika Sūtra attributed to Kaṇāda; later histories show extensive interaction and synthesis with Nyāya.

Core idea:
Vaiśeṣika develops a system of categories (padārthas) used to analyze reality. Classical presentations discuss substance, quality, motion/action, universality, particularity and inherence; later classifications may include absence/non-existence as an additional category.

Source-supported observations:
- Vaiśeṣika is a philosophical classification and analysis of reality.
- Its categories are technical philosophical concepts, not automatically fantasy “elements.”
- Nyāya and Vaiśeṣika traditions interacted closely over time; treating them as always completely separate can hide their shared history.
- Different authors and periods present category lists differently.

What this DOES NOT automatically establish:
- Every category is a literal cosmic substance.
- Vaiśeṣika's categories are the same as a fantasy world's five elements.
- The system gives characters the ability to manipulate atoms.
- One textbook summary covers all historical Vaiśeṣika thought.

Universe B use:
[PROPOSAL] A mystery investigation framework could borrow the general habit of separating object, quality, action and relation, while keeping the adaptation fictional.
[OPEN] No Vaiśeṣika-derived power or reality-manipulation mechanic is canonized.

References:
https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/indian-philosophy/
https://iep.utm.edu/vaisesik/

ENTRY 05 — PŪRVA MĪMĀṂSĀ / पूर्वमीमांसा
============================================================

Primary source:
Mīmāṃsā Sūtra attributed to Jaimini; later commentarial traditions include Śabara and other major thinkers.

Core idea:
Pūrva Mīmāṃsā focuses strongly on Vedic injunction, ritual interpretation, dharma and the authority/meaning of Vedic language. Its schools debate how language, ritual action and results are understood.

Source-supported observations:
- Mīmāṃsā is deeply concerned with interpreting Vedic sentences and injunctions.
- Dharma is examined through a specific hermeneutic framework; it should not simply be equated with a generic “goodness meter.”
- Ritual action and its result are analyzed within the tradition's own philosophical terms.
- Mīmāṃsā schools differ in their theories of language, knowledge and ritual efficacy.

What this DOES NOT automatically establish:
- Every ritual has a visible supernatural effect.
- Karma is just a points system awarded by a cosmic judge.
- All Hindu traditions define dharma exactly as Mīmāṃsā does.
- A fictional ritual contract is identical to Vedic ritual theory.

Universe B use:
[PROPOSAL] It can inform careful thinking about rules, instructions, ritual context and consequences in a fictional system.
[CAUTION] Do not use Mīmāṃsā as a source for a universal “spell contract” mechanic without direct textual evidence.
[OPEN] Universe B's ritual and Boon mechanics remain undecided.

ENTRY 06 — VEDĀNTA / वेदान्त: A FAMILY OF SCHOOLS
============================================================

Primary source frame:
Upanishads, Brahma Sūtra and Bhagavad Gita form the commonly named prasthāna-traya in many Vedānta traditions. Their interpretations differ significantly across schools.

Core idea:
Vedānta is not one single interpretation. Advaita, Viśiṣṭādvaita, Dvaita and other traditions offer different accounts of Brahman, individual self, world, devotion and liberation.

Source-supported observations:
- Advaita Vedānta is associated with Śaṅkara and a non-dual interpretation of ultimate reality.
- Viśiṣṭādvaita, associated with Rāmānuja, articulates a qualified non-dual account.
- Dvaita, associated with Madhva, maintains a strong distinction between God and individual souls.
- These short labels are introductions, not complete summaries of each school.
- Each school has its own interpretive tradition, arguments and internal nuance.

What this DOES NOT automatically establish:
- All Vedānta schools teach that the individual self is simply identical with Brahman.
- All Vedānta schools teach that the self is eternally separate in exactly the same sense.
- A single translation resolves all philosophical debates.
- Universe B's Supreme Trinity hierarchy is a direct statement of any one Vedānta school.

Universe B use:
[CANON CONTEXT] The novel's cosmology is fictional and must not be declared the universal teaching of Vedānta.
[OPEN] If a character or narrator discusses Brahman/Ātman, the intended philosophical lens must be selected deliberately or the wording should remain neutral and clearly fictional.
[CAUTION] Do not blend contradictory positions without acknowledging the difference.

ENTRY 07 — PRAMĀṆA: MEANS OF KNOWLEDGE / प्रमाण
============================================================

Concept frame:
Pramāṇa means a means or source of valid knowledge in Indian epistemology. Schools disagree about which pramāṇas are accepted and how they work.

Common categories in selected traditions:
- Pratyakṣa — perception
- Anumāna — inference
- Upamāna — comparison/analogy
- Śabda — verbal testimony
Other traditions may accept additional categories such as arthāpatti or anupalabdhi, depending on the school.

Source-supported observations:
- These categories are not accepted identically by every school.
- A pramāṇa is a technical epistemological term, not simply “a clue.”
- The reliability of a source and the conditions of valid cognition are debated differently across traditions.

What this DOES NOT automatically establish:
- A character can never be deceived if they use inference.
- Testimony is always true.
- Perception is infallible.
- Every school accepts the exact same list.

Universe B use:
[PROPOSAL] A detective protagonist can distinguish direct observation, inference, testimony and analogy as different evidence types. This is a writing tool, not a supernatural power.
[OPEN] A formal in-world epistemology system is not canonized.

ENTRY 08 — KLEŚA, ABHYĀSA AND VAIRĀGYA / क्लेश, अभ्यास आणि वैराग्य
============================================================

Primary source:
Yoga Sūtra, especially Sādhana Pāda for kleśas and Samādhi Pāda for abhyāsa and vairāgya. Verify numbering against the chosen edition.

Core idea:
In Pātañjala Yoga, kleśas are afflictions/causes of suffering or misperception, and practice plus dispassion are important to mental discipline. Translation details differ.

Source-supported observations:
- The Yoga Sūtra discusses avidyā, asmitā, rāga, dveṣa and abhiniveśa as the five kleśas in a standard presentation.
- Abhyāsa and vairāgya are discussed as supports for steadiness.
- These are part of a specific liberation-oriented philosophical practice, not a universal moral score.
- The terms should not be equated automatically with modern psychology diagnoses.

What this DOES NOT automatically establish:
- Emotional detachment makes someone invincible.
- Every desire is evil.
- The five kleśas are five literal demons or energy types.
- A person can diagnose another's inner state with certainty from external behavior.

Universe B use:
[PROPOSAL] These concepts may inspire character conflicts, discipline and self-misunderstanding.
[OPEN] They are not canonized as a new combat stat or power tier.

ENTRY 09 — SIDDHI AND VIBHŪTI / सिद्धी आणि विभूती
============================================================

Primary source:
Patañjali's Yoga Sūtra, Vibhūti Pāda; other Indian texts also discuss extraordinary capacities in their own contexts.

Core idea:
The Vibhūti Pāda contains descriptions of capacities associated with saṃyama on particular objects or topics. Interpretations vary, and the text's relationship between such capacities and the goal of liberation must be preserved.

Source-supported observations:
- The text describes a number of unusual capacities in a technical yogic context.
- The descriptions are not a generic universal ability catalogue.
- The tradition often cautions that extraordinary capacities are not identical with liberation; interpretations of the relevant sutras differ.
- The meaning of individual sutras should be verified in the original and a named commentary before using them in fiction.

What this DOES NOT automatically establish:
- Every practitioner can gain every siddhi.
- Siddhis are guaranteed or experimentally demonstrated modern superpowers.
- Every siddhi has a simple activation condition.
- A fictional ability based on a sutra is what the sutra literally teaches.

ENTRY 10 — KAIVALYA / कैवल्य
============================================================

Primary source:
Patañjali's Yoga Sūtra, Kaivalya Pāda; interpretation depends on the broader Yoga-Sāṃkhya framework and commentary.

Core idea:
Kaivalya is the culminating liberation-related concept in Pātañjala Yoga. It should not be treated as simply the strongest combat state or a higher level in an ordinary progression ladder.

Source-supported observations:
- Kaivalya is connected to the final aim of the Yoga system.
- It belongs to a particular philosophical framework in which Puruṣa and Prakṛti are distinguished.
- Other Indian traditions use different concepts or theories of liberation.
- A translation such as “isolation,” “independence” or “liberation” can carry interpretive commitments; check the translator/commentary.

What this DOES NOT automatically establish:
- Kaivalya is identical to every tradition's moksha.
- It means a character becomes a god or ruler of the universe.
- It is a combat rank.
- All Indian philosophies agree on its precise meaning.

Universe B use:
[PROPOSAL] Use it as a source for studying liberation themes, not as a ready-made power-tier name.
[OPEN] Universe B's liberation-associated path remains open and separate from standard Soul Migration mechanics.

CROSS-ENTRY FINDINGS
============================================================

1. The six commonly grouped darśanas are not one unified doctrine.
2. Pātañjala Yoga and classical Sāṃkhya share important concepts but should not be treated as identical in every respect.
3. Nyāya emphasizes reasoning and valid knowledge; Vaiśeṣika analyzes categories of reality; Mīmāṃsā focuses strongly on Vedic interpretation and ritual duty; Vedānta has multiple schools.
4. Pramāṇa lists differ by school.
5. Siddhi descriptions must be read in context and must not be treated as guaranteed modern superpowers.
6. Sattva, rajas and tamas in Sāṃkhya are not automatically three game energy types.
7. Liberation terms such as kaivalya and moksha are tradition-specific and not automatically combat ranks.
8. Universe B's five main pillars and Secret Sixth Pillar are novel-specific organization, not the official taxonomy of Hindu philosophy.
9. No new Universe B ability, rank, eligibility rule or soul-migration mechanic is approved by this document.
