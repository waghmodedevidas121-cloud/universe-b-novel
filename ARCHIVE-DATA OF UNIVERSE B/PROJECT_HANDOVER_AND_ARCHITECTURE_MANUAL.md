## 1. Executive Summary & Project Vision

Welcome to **Universe B** (चतुर्मुख ब्रह्मांड) — a serialized original epic fantasy web novel rooted in **Authentic Vedic Metaphysics** fused seamlessly with a **Pure Vedic High-Fantasy Anime Reality**.

This is **not** a generic Xianxia/Cultivation story, nor is it a casual LitRPG with floating blue game menus, free cheat skills, or teenage power fantasies. Universe B is an intricate, gritty, socio-economically grounded world where:
- **True 4-Bracket Magic Reality:** Everyone possesses magic (Bracket 1: 0–60% Dormant Quirks/Ember Magic; Bracket 2: 60–80% D-Rank Active Magic; Bracket 3: 80–90% C-Rank Reincarnates; Bracket 4: 90–100% Vanguard Elites).
- **Technology is strictly pure Vedic High-Fantasy:** Grounded Beast-Based Transport (Beast-Carts/Bailgada, Rathas, Caravans pulled by horses, oxen, yaks, boars; sailing ships guided by Makaras/Leviathans; rare bonded flying beasts like Garuḍas/Wyverns), Mahā-Dvāra teleportation gates, dimensional ley-lines, Dhanurveda, Runic Blades, elemental quirks, acoustic mantra-stones, astral birds, and Divya-Dṛṣṭi (100% purged of 19th-century firearms, railways, steam/magma pistons, drop-hammers, and coal/slag factories; Zero modern AI/electronics; Zero sci-fi or flying mechanical vimānas).
- **Gods are cosmic guardians and judges**, not arrogant punch-bags. The Supreme Trinity (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva) are transcendent, non-combatant, and bound by the Child Cosmos (Bālya-Avasthā) logic where raw divine descent would tear the fragile young universe apart.
- **Micro-economics are real:** Characters must pay in copper coins (*Copper Mashas*), silver coins (*Silver Panas*), and international bank notes (*Aether-Rupees*) for tea, porridge, rent, transit tokens, and runic materials.
