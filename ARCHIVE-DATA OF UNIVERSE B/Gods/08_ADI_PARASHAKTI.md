## 1. Cosmic Nature & Fundamental Law

In Vedic metaphysics, the universe consists of two complementary realities:
- **Puruṣa (Consciousness):** The unmoving, eternal witness (Lord Śiva / Brahman).
- **Prakṛti / Śakti (Dynamic Force):** The creative, pulsating, manifesting power (Ādi Parāśakti).

> *"Śivaḥ śaktyā yukto yadi bhavati śaktaḥ prabhavituṁ, na ceccevaṁ devo na khalu kuśalaḥ spanditum api."*  
> (Without Śakti, even Śiva cannot move or manifest a single vibration; He remains inert).

- **The Engine of Magic & Astras:** Every Mantra, every fire in a Yajña, every Prāṇic pulse through the 72,000 Nāḍīs, and every burst from a Divyāstra is a microscopic wave of Her cosmic energy (*Śakti*).
- **Mahāmāyā (The Great Illusion):** She casts the cosmic spell that causes souls to identify with their mortal flesh, forgetting their divine eternal origin.

## 2. Role in this Novel: The Power Fuel & The Unforgiving Balance

1. **The Source of Internal Prāṇa:**
   - When cultivators awaken the **Kuṇḍalinī serpent** coiled at the base of the spine (Mūlādhāra), they are awakening a dormant spark of Ādi Parāśakti.
   - Her power cannot be forced through raw brute greed; forcing Kuṇḍalinī without moral purity burns the user's subtle channels to ash.
2. **The Weapon Matrix:**
   - Astras are not inert metal missiles; they are conscious energetic entities powered by Her kinetic manifestations.
   - The Cosmic Ledger and mortal bio-energetic vessels read and interact directly with Her vibrational frequencies (Mantras, Bījas, Yantras).
3. **Beyond Deva-Asura Politics:**
   - She loves and slays both Devas and Asuras impartially when either faction threatens the balance of the multiverse (*Mahiṣāsuramardinī* role).
