## 1. Canonical Identity in Universe B

In Universe B, **Yama (Dharmaraja)** is an immutable, eternal pillar of cosmic reality, standing as one of **The Eternal Four** (alongside Ananta, Vidhata, and Chitragupta):

- **Nature of the Office:** Lord Yama is an eternal, primordial entity established at the dawn of the cosmos (Era 1: Satya Yuga) directly by the Supreme Trinity. He is **not** a temporary role-holder or mortal designee.
- **Zero Tenure & Zero Retirement:** Lord Yama never changes, never retires, has no lifespan expiration, and can never be succeeded or removed. His sovereign authority is permanent and absolute.
- **Subordinate Staff Distinction:** While subordinate magistrates, marshals, inspectors, and wardens (*Warden Setuvrata*, triage dūtas) across Tier 1 and Tier 2 may rotate or be appointed, **Lord Yama himself is eternal and irreplaceable**.
- **Authority Scope:** Oversees the sovereign administration of departed souls, the Death Side of the Cosmic Ocean, karmic adjudication, and the operational integrity of soul transit and entry/exit gateways.

## 2. Limits and System Boundaries [CANON LOCK]

- **Ananta's Primacy:** Yama coordinates with **Ananta (The System Core)**; he cannot override immutable cosmic read-only logs legally established by the Supreme Trinity.
- **Vidhata's Sacred Dynamic:** Vidhata coordinates destiny weaving and birth connections; Yama and Vidhata share an unbroken primordial friendship (*Anadi Maitri*) since Era 1.
- **Chitragupta's Right Hand:** Chitragupta serves as Yama’s indispensable right-hand chronicler, providing tamper-proof ledger facts for all judicial decrees.
- **Judicial Boundary:** Yama adjudicates justice and grants transition approvals, but does not write or edit individual destiny plots (which is Vidhata's sole constitutional mandate).
