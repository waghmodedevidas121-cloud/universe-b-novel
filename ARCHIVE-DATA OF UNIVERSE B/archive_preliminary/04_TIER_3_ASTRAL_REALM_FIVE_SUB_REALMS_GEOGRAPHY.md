## 2. Interdimensional Transit & The Commoner Rule
*(आयामी जादू व सामान्य प्रवास नियम)*

### 2.1 The Insuperable Spatial Void
- Between any two sub-realms lies hundreds of thousands of miles of violent, vacuum-like spatial turbulence.
- One cannot walk, sail, or fly conventional aircraft between sub-realms. 

### 2.2 The Two Modes of Transit
1. **Self-Transit (Apex Practitioners Only - 1–2%):**
   - Requires minimum Tier 3 Cultivation (Awoken Spatial Master). Practitioners fold space-time around their body, surviving the void crossing for brief periods.
2. **Pressurized Rail Transit (The Bubble Car - 98%+ of Travelers):**
   - For non-powered commoners, merchants, and cargo trains, transit occurs via **Dimensional Runic Rail Gates** anchored in The Central Sub-Realm.
   - Locomotive passenger cars are sealed with lead-lined rubber gaskets and enchanted with **Aether-Bubble Shields** to protect mortal lungs from void decompression.

## 3. The Central Sub-Realm: The Apex Political Core
*(मध्यवर्ती महा-लोक: मध्य-नगर — सर्वोच्च सत्ता केंद्र)*

Inspired by the diplomatic neutrality of Geneva and the grand political tension of Tensura's Council of the West, **The Central Sub-Realm** is the undisputed nerve center of Universe B:

```
[ THE CENTRAL SUB-REALM METRICS ]
- Population: 500,000 to 800,000 residents.
- Demographics: 
  * ~10% Apex Elite (High Deva Lords, Asura Patriarchs, Human Rishis, Titan Ambassadors).
  * ~90% Detached Service Clans (Multi-generational clerks, telegraph operators, bank auditors, neutral police).
- Sovereign Status: 100% Demilitarized Zone under the Absolute Non-Violence Truce.
- Supreme Executive: High Chancellor Sulabha Al-Mansur (President of Grand Concord Assembly).
- Supreme Court: High Chief Magistrate Vikarna Vance (Central Concord Courts).
```

### 3.1 Indirect Hegemony: How The Central Controls the Cosmos
The Central maintains zero aggressive foreign armies. Yet, it exercises **absolute indirect hegemony** over all four outer sub-realms through four non-military levers:

1. **Monetary Monopoly (Aether-Kosha Central Bank):**
   - Managed by **High Governor Ambika Sterling**. The Central issues the official trade currency: *The Central Aether-Rupee*. 
   - If an outer king threatens war, Ambika freezes their foreign gold vaults and voids their bank drafts within two hours.
2. **Resource & Ammunition Embargoes (The Grand Exchange):**
   - All cross-realm trade (Asura high-tensile steel, Human medicinal grain, Deva aether-silk, Beast alchemy bones) must clear the Grand Exchange. A single trade embargo by The Central can starve a rebellious kingdom of coal and rifle powder.
3. **Transit Licensing (The Spatial Gate Keys):**
   - The Central controls the spatial frequencies of all dimensional gates. Revoking a realm's transit license cuts them off from the rest of the universe.
4. **Information Monopoly (Aetheric Telegraph Grid):**
   - Under **Telegraph Guildmaster Uttanka Klein** and **Chandra-Lekha Meyer**, every diplomatic wire, market quote, and military mobilization report passes through the Central Telegraph Switching Room.

## 4. The Four Outer Sub-Realms

### 4.1 The Human Sub-Realm (मानव उप-लोक — The Mortal Cradle)
- **Topography:** Rolling fertile plains, vast coal basins, heavy industrial river deltas, and deep misted coastal boundaries.
- **Key Metropolises & Dynasties:**
  - *Hastinapura (Seat of Kuru-Panchala Confederation):* Governed by **King Parikshit II** and **Queen Madhavi**. Massive brick parliament, steam locomotive depots, rifled artillery foundries.
  - *Samudra-Pattanam (Coastal Merchant League):* Governed by **High Admiral Silas Suhotra** and **Commodore Linnea Brandt**. Vast deep-water ports, privateer steam cutters, naval drydocks along the Kshira Ocean border.
  - *Tapovana (Northern Hermitage Valleys):* Co-ruled by **Maharishi Dhaumya** and **Maharishi Gargi**. Pristine, steam-free pine forests, stone pavilions, sacred seed vaults, and meridian healing hospitals.

### 4.2 The Asura Sub-Realm (असुर उप-लोक — The Chthonic Foundries)
- **Topography:** Colossal volcanic mountain ranges, active tectonic rifts, black obsidian gorges, and perpetual sulfur cloudbanks.
- **Key Metropolises & Clans:**
  - *Vajra-Nagara (The Iron Fortress):* Governed by **Grand Architect Maya Richter** and **Forge-Queen Mandor Vane**. A tiered metropolis built over roaring magma vents; produces armored trains, bolt-action ordnance, and pneumatic exo-harnesses.
  - *Shaila-Gadha (The Deep Basin Clans):* Governed by **The Eisen Chieftains (Viktor Ilvala & Gregor Vatapi)** alongside **Baroness Greta Simhika**. Two-mile-deep obsidian mine shafts producing raw coal, iron, and aether-crystals.

### 4.3 The Deva Sub-Realm (देव उप-लोक — The Celestial Heights)
- **Topography:** Luminous antigravity floating islands, crystalline spires, cloud-silk plateaus, and pristine solar waterfalls.
- **Key Metropolises & Houses:**
  - *Amaravati (The Solar Citadel):* Governed by **Deva-Sovereign Aurelius Somaka** and **High Queen Seraphina Shachi**. Pure white marble and diamond spires; solar resonance batteries; guards the celestial perimeter via **High Commander Lysandra Urvashi**.
  - *Gandharva-Puri (The Artisan Sanctuaries):* Governed by **Lady Chitralekha Fontaine**. The cultural capital of celestial music, mathematical architecture, and enchanted *Aether-Silk* looms.

### 4.4 The Beast Sub-Realm (श्वापद उप-लोक — The Primeval Wilderness)
- **Topography:** Dense ancient rainforests with five-hundred-foot World-Trees, needle-sharp granite sky peaks, and light-absorbing shadow basins.
- **Key Territories & Apex Monarchs:**
  - *Mahā-Kāntāra (Heartwood Sanctum):* Ruled by **Titan King Ursus-Krodha** (The Four-Horned Armored Bear) and **Great Serpent Empress Kadru-Jormun** (Sovereign of Deep Roots).
  - *Pakshi-Shaila (The Sky Needles):* Ruled by **Sky Empress Sampati-Valkyrie** and **Suparna-Raja Aquila**. Razor granite crags guarded by giant raptors with supersonic spatial wind blades.
  - *Gupt-Kandara (The Shadow Basin):* Ruled by **Void-Matriarch Nyx-Chaya**. Soundless twilight marsh inhabited by light-absorbing void-panthers capable of innate spatial slip.
  - *The Diplomatic Conduit:* Managed by mortal ascetic **Shaman Lopamudra Morvath**, who translates beast ground-vibrations into diplomatic pacts with mortal lumber companies.
