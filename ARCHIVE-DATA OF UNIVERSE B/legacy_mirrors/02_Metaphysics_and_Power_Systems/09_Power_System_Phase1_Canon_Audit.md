### The Inviolable Core Principles
1. **Rule 1 (No Free Effect):** Power never generates from a void. Yantras do not manufacture energy (they channel and shape external fuel). Mantras do not grant ownership (they require initiation and fuel). Astras do not fire without consequence (they require a formal three-stage commitment).
2. **Rule 2 (Power is Not a Single Number):** There are no universal stats, combat power levels (scouters), or gaming screens. A practitioner may possess vast Prāṇa reserves but terrible control; another may wield supreme Mantra initiation but lack physical throughput.
3. **Rule 3 (The Separation of Four Stages):** `Access ≠ Capacity ≠ Control ≠ Consequence`. Knowing a formula does not mean being initiated; initiation does not guarantee enough Prāṇa; having Prāṇa does not guarantee steady aim; hitting a target does not erase the karmic or bodily backlash.
4. **Rule 8 & Transcendence of the Supreme Trinity:** Brahma (Creator), Vishnu (Preserver), and Shiva/Mahesh (Destroyer) exist completely outside and above the ordinary Power System. They are unquantifiable and eternal. Boons requested through penance bind the **Divine Office / Delegate**, never coercing the Supreme Persons.
5. **Zero-Avatar Protocol:** Universe B possesses no physical descents of Rama, Krishna, or Kalki. The mortal power ceiling is capped strictly at **Tier 7 (Transcendent / Realm Monarch)**. T8 is reserved for unascended primordial Rishis/Chiranjivis; T9 and T10 are permanently inaccessible.

## 3. Inventory: Established Canon vs. Undefined Mechanics

### A. Established Canon ([CANON])
1. **The Two-Axis Model (F-01):** Axis A adjudicates; Axis B presents. Neither bypasses the other.
2. **Prāṇa Mechanics:** Circulates through 72,000 Nādīs; Iḍā and Piṅgalā active; Suṣumnā remains dormant until Brahma-Granthi, Viṣṇu-Granthi, and Rudra-Granthi are systematically unsealed. Four explicit axes: Reserve, Throughput, Control, Stability. Overdrawing causes Nādī-burn and biological exhaustion.
3. **Mantra Execution Pipeline:** Requires all four registers: Pāda-Integrity (sound syntax), Dīkṣā (initiatory license), Sankalpa (conscious intent/target), and Prāṇa-load (fuel). Failure to hold all four results in *Mantra-Vismaraṇa* (slip of mind) or catastrophic misfire.
4. **Kinetic Lamina (F-03):** Compressed sonic/aetheric shield generated by continuous Mantra recitation. Susceptible to acoustic harmonic disruption (resonance-breaking).
5. **Yantra Three-Register Taxonomy (F-11):** 
   - Register A: Sacred / Devotional (Sri Yantra - non-gamified, devotional only).
   - Register B: Sādhana-Assisting (Geometric anchors, Chakravyūha formations, boundary wards).
   - Register C: Secular / 18th–19th Century Industrial (Bolt-action rifles, steam engines, telegraph lines - zero AI). Prāṇa only interacts with Register C if etched with conductive conduits.
6. **Astra Three-Stage Pipeline (F-02):** `MANASA` (mental tuning, free cancellation) ➔ `SPARSHA` (carrier binding, active commitment) ➔ `VISARJANA` (formal release and cosmic grounding). Omitting Visarjana results in environmental blights and karmic decay.
7. **Time Manipulation Ban (F-10):** Chrono-kinetic abilities cannot be developed or trained through mortal Axis B craft. Tactical foresight (Ajna precognition) is an exception of perception, not true temporal control.
8. **Technology vs. Cultivation Scale Bands (F-04):**
   - Mortal (T1–T3): Bolt-action rifles and steam cannons are lethal and decisive.
   - Trained (T4): Firearms are effective; massed volleys remain deadly.
   - Crossover (T4+ ➔ T5−): First Granthi pierced, rudimentary Vajra Deha; rifles lose single-shot kill potential against center mass; vulnerable at eyes/joints.
   - Awakened (T5): Standard rifles deflected by Kinetic Lamina; requires prana-resonant ammunition.
   - Ascendent (T6): Personal immunity to conventional firearms; vulnerable only to saturated siege weaponry.
   - Transcendent (T7): Immune to conventional mortal technology.
9. **Soul-Bond / Sahajeevi Bandh (F-05):** Mutual consensual bond between human and beast; restricted to T1–T4 beasts; token-mediated; ceases upon death with no inheritability.
10. **Supreme Trinity & Zero-Avatar Lock:** Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva stand outside ordinary systems; no physical avatars in Universe B; mortal rank ceiling is T7.

```markdown

**1. Identity & Classification**
- **Unique ID:** `ABL-[CAT]-[NN]` (e.g., `ABL-ELE-01`, `ABL-BOD-04`, `AST-T1-02`)
- **Canon Name:** Primary English / Sanskrit formal title
- **In-World Aliases:** Vernacular names across Sub-Realms (e.g., Human vs. Asura slang)

- **Ritual Vehicle (Axis B):** Prāṇa / Tapasya / Mantra / Yantra / Astra

**2. Operating Concept & Mechanism**
- **One-Sentence Definition:** Exact summary of what the ability does.
- **Underlying Mechanism:** Which Nādī, chakra, seed-syllable, or geometric conduit it operates through.
- **What It Fundamentally Alters:** (e.g., thermal kinetic energy, optical index, flesh density).
```

## 5. The Ability Evolution Framework (Universe B Native)

To achieve the compelling progression found in series like *Slime* or *Pokémon* without copying their mechanics or violating Universe B's non-gaming philosophy, we establish **Five Distinct Progression Stages**:

```
[ LEVEL 1: MASTERY ] ────► Sādhana Refinement (Better aim, faster casting, 40% less Prāṇa burn)
                              │
[ LEVEL 2: GROWTH ]  ────► Capacity Expansion (Wider flame spread, thicker stone armor)
                              │  (Critical Crisis / Granthi Breakthrough / Sankalpa Metamorphosis)
                              ▼
[ LEVEL 3: EVOLUTION ] ──► Qualitative Transformation (Elemental Flame ➔ Sub-Atomic Tejas Ray)
                              │
           ┌──────────────────┴──────────────────┐
           ▼                                     ▼
[ LEVEL 4: BRANCH A ]                 [ LEVEL 4: BRANCH B ]
(Singular Destructive Focus)         (Spatial Geometry / Ward Synthesis)
```

### The Three Legitimate Evolution Triggers in Universe B
1. **Granthi-Piercing Metamorphosis (Physiological Breakthrough):**
   - Unsealing the **Brahma-Granthi** (Base/Root unbinding), **Viṣṇu-Granthi** (Heart/Compassion unbinding), or **Rudra-Granthi** (Ajna/Cranial unbinding) alters the user's Nādī throughput permanently.
   - An ability bound to the lower physical body qualitatively shifts into an energetic or conceptual state when higher Granthis open.
2. **Kruti-Crisis Crystallization (Action Under Rupture):**
   - In accordance with the *Kruti Law*, when a practitioner makes an irreversible, high-stakes moral or tactical decision at the absolute threshold of death/exhaustion, their inner Sankalpa permanently reconfigures their technique.
   - Triggers the silent **Ananta Chime** within the Ajna Chakra, certifying that their internal matrix has evolved.
3. **Resonant Synthesis (Yantra/Mantra Harmonization):**
   - Two disparate techniques that have been practiced to total mastery fuse into a single composite art (e.g., combining *Vajra Deha* flesh hardening with *Electrokinesis* to create bio-magnetic rail-strikes).

## 6. Audit of Unresolved Decisions Requiring Author Confirmation

Before locking Phase 2 architecture, the following specific questions require authorial input:

1. **Evolution Reversibility & Stability:**
   - *Option A (Permanent & Irreversible - Recommended):* Once an ability undergoes qualitative evolution, the user's soul-nadi matrix is permanently reshaped. The previous base form is subsumed into the new art.
   - *Option B (Temporary Transformed State):* Evolution is a high-cost "overdrive" form that drains massive Ojas and reverts to the base ability when exhausted.
2. **Standard Ability Prefix Convention:**
   - Proposal: Use a clean 3-letter alphanumeric code: `ABL-[CAT]-[NN]` (e.g. `ABL-ELE-01` for Pyrokinesis, `ABL-SPA-01` for Blink, `ABL-BOD-01` for Vajra Deha).
