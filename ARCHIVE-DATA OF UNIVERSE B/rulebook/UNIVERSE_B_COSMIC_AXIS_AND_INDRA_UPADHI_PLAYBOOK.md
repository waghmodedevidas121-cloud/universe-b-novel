## 3. THE TWO GOVERNING COUNCILS & UPADHI MECHANICS

### 3.1 The High Council of the Buffer Zone (Tier 2 — Headed by Indra-Upadhi)
- **The Indra as a Contested Office (`Upadhi`), Not a Deity:**
  - In authentic Puranic tradition, "Indra" is an administrative tenure (*Manvantara-Upadhi*).
  - Anyone of sufficient warrior merit (regardless of whether they are Sura, Asura, Human, or Outworlder) can rise to claim the **Vajra Throne of Swarga**.
  - The Indra is the **Supreme Administrator & Protector of the Buffer Zone**.
  - Indra is **not an absolute autocrat**: all major policy must be deliberated with the High Council.
- **The High Council Composition:**
  1. **The Sacred Soul Trinity (Yama, Chitragupta, Vidhātā):**
     - Directly accountable to the **Divine Delegates** and the **Supreme Trinity** for metaphysical and karmic judgments.
     - **Indra CANNOT give direct orders to Yama, Chitragupta, or Vidhātā.**
     - However, because their offices and archives physically operate within the Buffer Zone, **their security, military protection, and perimeter defense are strictly under Indra’s command.** Thus, they are permanent constitutional members of the High Council.
  2. **Mahā-Pālaka (Chief Guardian of the Setu):** Directly under Indra's command.
  3. **Chief Sage (Inquisitor of Truth):** Directly under Indra's command.
  4. **Prime Seeker (Expeditions & Boundary Pioneer):** Directly under Indra's command.

### 3.2 The Grand Concord & Sovereign-President of Central City (Tier 3 Decentralized Hub)
- **The Central Emperor (`CHR-CEN-00`):**
  - Governs the living civilizations across the Five Shards from the **Central Controlling Hub (Central City)**.
  - Sits hierarchically **underneath the higher jurisdiction of the High Council**.
- **The 7 Sovereign Seats:**
  1. **High Arbitrator of the Central Reserve Bank (Donna Beatrice Vanezza — Debt & Currency Enforcement)**
  2. **Solar Primus (Deva Shard Representative)**
  3. **High Artificer of Asura-Nagara (Runic Metallurgy & Magma Foundries)**

- **The Grand Aegis is a SPHERE, Not a Flat Wall:**
  - Surrounds the entire **Corrupted Realm (Patala / Tier 4)** as a complete 360-degree cosmic energy sphere.
  - Developed through ancient sages studying the basic frequency resonance of **Kshira Sagara**.
  - Because it is an enclosed spherical bubble, linear distance metrics (miles) do not apply to its containment geometry.
- **The Two-Stage Chronology:**

- The **Adho-Rangashala (D-Tier Feeder Sand-Pits)** where Neel must fight in Chapter 3 are carved directly into the ceiling of the **Atala Stratum** (the first layer of Patala).
