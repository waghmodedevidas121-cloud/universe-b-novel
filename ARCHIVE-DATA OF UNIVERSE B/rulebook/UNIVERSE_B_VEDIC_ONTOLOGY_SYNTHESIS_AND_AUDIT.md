## CHUNK 1: THE MICRO-COSMOS — SUBTLE ANATOMY, METABOLISM & MARMAS

### 1.1 The Five Concentric Sheaths (*Pancha-Kosha*)
Every living vessel in Universe B consists of five nested layers wrapping the immortal soul (**Atman**):
1. **Annamaya-Kosha (Gross Flesh-Sheath):** Muscle, bone, blood, skin. Sustained by food, water, salt, and rest. Normal iron strikes only damage this layer.
2. **Pranamaya-Kosha (Vital-Channel Sheath):** The **72,000 Nadis** and **Three Spine-Locks (*Tri-Granthi*)**. Governs *The Hold* and *The Blowout*.
   - *Neel's Anomaly:* During his fall through the Kshira Sagara, Neel's 72,000 channels were blasted open and purged, then collapsed into cold dormancy. Only **one microscopic filament in his right wrist** remains vibrating with active friction.
3. **Manomaya-Kosha (Reflex & Instinct Sheath):** The seat of nervous reflex, sensory timing, and fear. This is the physiological seat of Neel's **[Pulse-Sync]**—his ability to read kinetic rhythm and muscle tension before an enemy strikes!
4. **Vijnanamaya-Kosha (Discernment Sheath):** Awakened by masters who pierce the *Brahma-Granthi*; grants structural awareness of matter, space, and weak points (*Weak-Point Sight*).
5. **Anandamaya-Kosha (Causal Soul-Sheath):** The deepest veil over the Atman. Touched only by Divyastras, Soul-Arts, or Seal-7 Miasma.

### 1.2 The Five Vital Airs (*Pancha-Prana*) & Five Minor Airs (*Upa-Prana*)
Warriors route ambient Aether-breath into five internal physiological gears:
- **Prana-Vayu (Heart/Chest):** Controls respiratory rhythm, eye-tracking speed, and the combat *Breath Reset*.
- **Apana-Vayu (Pelvis/Downward):** The Gravity Anchor! Heavy tanks (*Bheem, Asura juggernauts*) drive Apana into their heels to anchor against kinetic blows and ground out electrical (*Bolt*) arts.
- **Samana-Vayu (Navel/Thermal Core):** The internal alchemical furnace (*Jatharagni*). Digests Beast-Meat and fuels thermal/fire quirks (like *Ark's* Cinder-Lance).
- **Udana-Vayu (Throat/Ascending):** Reduces body mass for high leaps (*Laghima*) and powers 0.2s vocal *Bija-Syllables*.
- **Vyana-Vayu (Full-Body Pervasion):** Circulates Prana across the skin for Iron-Skin defenses and maintains the pulse tether with a Bonded Beast.

*Minor Airs (Upa-Prana):*
- **Naga:** Expels poison gas via reflex vomiting.
- **Kurma:** Locks eyelids against blinking during sword clashes.
- **Krikara:** Clears throat against toxic spores.
- **Devadatta:** Induces 60-second restorative micro-sleep.
- **Dhananjaya:** Lingers in the flesh for 12 minutes after clinical death, allowing resuscitation before Yama's seal locks.

### 1.3 Tri-Dosha & Sapta-Dhatu Architecture
- **Vata (Wind/Ether — Neel, Kael):** Wiry, ultra-fast reflexes, erratic pulse. Prone to dry channels and nerve tremors under cold.
- **Pitta (Fire/Bile — Ark, Kira, Hilda):** High heat, explosive damage output, fast digestion. Prone to internal scorching (*Pitta-Daha*) and self-charring.
- **Kapha (Earth/Water — Bheem, Rudrakh):** Massive bone density, 40 BPM resting heart rate, colossal Hold capacity. Prone to joint calcification (*Kapha-Stambha*).

### 1.4 The Seven Dhatus & The Sacred Fuel: *Ojas*
Tissue refines across seven days:  
`Rasa (Plasma) ➔ Rakta (Blood) ➔ Mamsa (Muscle) ➔ Meda (Fat) ➔ Asthi (Bone) ➔ Majja (Marrow) ➔ OJAS (Supreme Golden Essence).`
- **Combat Resource Hierarchy:**
  1. *Prana (Breath):* Normal fuel. Recovers with a 4-second *Breath Reset*. Depletion = *Going Dry*.
  2. *Nadi Structural Integrity:* Overdrawing Prana tears the channel walls = *The Blowout* (internal charring, weeks to heal).
  3. *Ojas (Heart-Marrow Gold):* Every mortal has only **8 Drops (*Ashta-Bindu*)** around the heart. Desperate limit-breaks burn a drop of Ojas: power multiplies 5x for 10 seconds, but **ages the vessel by 2 years**, turns hair white, and risks immediate cardiac arrest.

### 1.5 The 108 Vital Points (*Marma-Shastra*)
Close-quarters strikers target three classes of Sushruta Marmas:
