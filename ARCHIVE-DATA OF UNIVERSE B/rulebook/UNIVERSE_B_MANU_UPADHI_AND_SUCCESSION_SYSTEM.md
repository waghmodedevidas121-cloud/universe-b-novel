## 2. THE SOVEREIGN-PRESIDENT OF CENTRAL CITY (`CHR-CEN-00`)

### 2.1 The Open, Contestable Crown
The ruler of Central City is **The Central Emperor (सम्राट) of Central City (केंद्रीय नगराध्यक्ष / Sovereign of the Central Presidium)**:
- **No Sacred Bloodline / No Mythical Lock:** This position is NOT a mythical hereditary title. It is a **pragmatic, high-stakes political office**.
- **Open to Anyone:** Anyone can acquire the Grey Basalt Throne—regardless of origin, species, background, or moral alignment (honorable statesman, greedy banker, ruthless warlord, or cunning gladiator)—through:
  1. *Electoral Consensus:* Backed by the Grand Concord Assembly and Central Bank.
  2. *Political Coup / Usurpation:* Maneuvering within the 7 Sovereign Seats.
  3. *Martial Challenge / Regicide:* Slaying or subduing the sitting President in sanctioned combat or duel.

### 2.2 The Four Pillars of Indirect Hegemony
Central City has no offensive army invading other shards. Its dominance is purely **Indirect Hegemony**:
1. **Currency Monopoly (Akash-Kosha Central Reserve Bank):** Controlled by Governor Ambika and private banking head Donna Beatrice Vanezza. The Aether-Rupee is the cross-realm reserve currency. Defaulting shards face asset freezes.
2. **Trade Chokehold (The 4 Monolithic Stone Gateways):** 85–90% of bulk grain, metals, and cargo must pass through Central City's gates, subject to the 17% Central Bank tariff.
3. **Cultural Dominance (The Proving Games & Coliseum Spire):** The 250-ft clockwork Split-Flap Spire ranks the world's greatest fighters. Arena culture commands pan-astral attention.
4. **Diplomatic Nexus (The Grand Concord Assembly):** Led by Assembly Speaker Sulabha. Acts as the multi-national parliament where treaties and trade pacts are arbitrated.

## 3. HIGHER TIER NON-INTERVENTION & THE EXISTENTIAL EXCEPTION

```text
========================================================================================
              THE HIGHER TIER NON-INTERVENTION & EXCEPTION PROTOCOL
========================================================================================

1. GENERAL LAW: MORTAL AUTONOMY (Zero Divine Interference)
   - If Central City experiences a bloody coup, if a tyrant takes the Grey Throne, or if
     Hastinapur wages a trade war against Asura foundries, the Higher Tiers (Swarga, Indra,
     Setu Standing Army) REMAIN COMPLETELY SILENT AND UNINVOLVED.

2. THE RAVANA / KANSA DOCTRINE (Existential Soul & Seal Threat)
   - Trigger: A mortal warlord or cult attempts to weaponize Tier 4 Preta miasma, crack the
     Seven Cosmic Seals, or manipulate the sacred reincarnation cycle (Chitragupta's ledger).
   - Action: Operational Ansha Rudra or Setu strike legions descend with overwhelming force
     to vaporize the threat and restore quarantine.

3. THE MAHABHARATA DOCTRINE (Total Dimensional Rupture Threat)
   - Trigger: An inter-shard war escalates to forbidden cosmic Astras (Brahmashira) or
     tectonic anchor shearing that threatens to collapse the Grand Aegis or fracture the shards.
   - Action: The Indra-Upadhi declares a Cosmic State of Emergency; Setu Army Commander
     Vajraketu deploys vanguard pacification forces to enforce an immediate ceasefire.
========================================================================================
```
