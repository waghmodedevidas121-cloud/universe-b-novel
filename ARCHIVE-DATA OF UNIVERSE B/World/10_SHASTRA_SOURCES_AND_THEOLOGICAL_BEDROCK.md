## 2. Textual References & Interpretive Authority

### 2.1 Mahā-Viṣṇu (Brahma-Saṁhitā 5.48)
- **Sanskrit Source:**
  > *yasyaika-niśvasita-kālam athāvalambya  
  > jīvanti santata-bhuvani-patir eka-rūpaḥ |  
  > viṣṇur mahān sa iha yasya kalā-viśeṣo  
  > govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi ||*
- **Significance for the Novel:**  
  Mahā-Viṣṇu's breathing creates and dissolves infinite independent Brahmāṇḍas. This directly legitimizes our story's premise: Mahā-Viṣṇu breathing out an **alternate, experimental modern Brahmāṇḍa** separate from the mainstream timeline.

### 2.2 Garbhodakaśāyī Viṣṇu & Brahmā (Śrīmad Bhāgavatam 1.3.2)
- **Sanskrit Source:**
  > *yasyāmbhasi sphurato yāna-yānena  
  > vicitra-saṁsthānam ananta-śaktyā |  
  > nānā-vicitrāṇi sasarja sarva-  
  > lokāni padmādbhuta-garbha-janmā ||*
- **Significance for the Novel:**  
  Confirms that in each universe, Brahmā is tasked with structural architecture and mortal form creation, while depending on the sustaining lotus of Garbhodakaśāyī Viṣṇu.

### 2.3 Kṣīrodakaśāyī Viṣṇu / Paramātmā (Śrīmad Bhāgavatam 1.3.5)
- **Sanskrit Source:**
  > *tan-nābhi-hrada-padminyāḥ  
  > sambhūto bhagavān svayam |  
  > prādurbabhūva sarveṣāṁ  
  > prāṇināṁ hṛdi saṁsthitaḥ ||*
- **Significance for the Novel:**  
  As Gaudiya Vaishnava and classical Puranic schools establish, Kṣīrodakaśāyī Viṣṇu is the inner witness (*Paramātmā*) and cosmic sustainer who observes every mortal's heart and maintains communication with Brahmā. In our novel, He is the party agreeing with Brahmā's plan to let mortals fight their own battles via the Cosmic Ledger without avatar descent.
