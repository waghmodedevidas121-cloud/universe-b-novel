## 0. Overview: The Spatial Quadrants of the Astral Void

Following the Great Sundering (Era 3), the living world was partitioned into four isolated planetary-scale landmasses (Sub-Realms) suspended around the central spatial axis:

```
                  [ 1. DEVA SUB-REALM (NORTH) ]
            Celestial Cloud-Plateaus • Zero-Machinery
                               │
[ 2. ASURA SUB-REALM (WEST) ] ──┼── [ 4. HUMAN SUB-REALM (EAST) ]
Basalt Forges • Heavy Industry  │   Mortal Cradle • 19th-C. Rail
                               │
                 [ 3. BEAST SUB-REALM (SOUTH) ]
            100,000-Mile Wilderness • Primal Food Chain
```

## 1. The Human Sub-Realm (मानव उप-लोक — The Mortal Cradle)

- **Total Geography:** 2.5 million square miles of diverse continental terrain — broad alluvial river valleys, rolling deciduous forests, coastal archipelagos, and mild temperate plains.
- **Atmospheric Baseline:** Standard breathable atmosphere; pure seasonal Aether; 19th-century mechanical footprint.
- **Population:** ~120 Million (95%+ ordinary non-powered mortals; 1–2% Awoken practitioners/Rishis).

### 1.1 The Kuru-Panchala Confederation (कुरु-पांचाल महा-राज्य)
- **Topography:** The vast Ganga-Yamuna river basin; dense agricultural patchwork interspersed with brick-and-timber market towns, coal distribution hubs, and double-gauge rail tracks.
- **Capital:** **Hastinapura (The Steam Citadel)** — A fortified brick-and-granite metropolis featuring colossal brass clock towers, telegraph central stations, and the Grand Union Rail Terminus.
- **Major Cities:**
  - **Kampilya (The Grain Emporium):** The agricultural trade heartland; sprawling river wharves and grain silos.
  - **Indra-Prastha (The Southern Gateway):** A rail-junction city connecting internal farmland to the coastal trade spurs.
- **Ruling Dynasty:** **The Bharata Dynasty (भारत घराणे)** — Constitutional Forge-Monarchy governing alongside an elected Parliament of Town Guildmasters and Merchant Syndicates.
- **Key Infrastructure:** The **Eastern Iron Line** (double-gauge steam railway connecting Hastinapura to the coast); 4,000 miles of copper telegraph poles.

### 1.2 The Coastal Merchant League (समुद्र-वाणिज्य संघ)
- **Topography:** Rocky coastal fjords, natural deep-water bays, tidal salt flats, and maritime river deltas bordering the mysterious **Kshira Ocean**.
- **Capital:** **Samudra-Pattanam (The Sea-City)** — Gleaming white-limestone trading houses, maritime banks, steamship dry-docks, and a 400-foot lighthouse powered by focused Aether-lamps.
- **Major Cities:**
  - **Moti-Bunder (Pearl Harbor):** Center for coastal oyster harvesting, sea-silk weaving, and pearl exchanges.
  - **Kshira-Spur (Border Outpost):** The easternmost rail depot facing the fog-shrouded waters of the Kshira Ocean (monitoring Route B cosmic arrivals).
- **Ruling System:** **Admiralty Council (Vanijya-Parishad)** — Led by the High Merchant Admiral and the Heads of the Twelve Trading Houses.

### 1.3 The Northern Hermitage Valleys (उत्तर ऋषी खोरे — Tapovana)
- **Topography:** Misty pine-covered foothills, cascading glacial streams, deep granite valleys, and sacred cedar groves.
- **Capital / Hub:** **Tapovana (The Grove of Sages)** — A tranquil settlement of thatched-roof timber pavilions, herb drying courtyards, and ancient stone meditation platforms. Zero machine noise allowed by religious ordinance.
- **Ruling System:** **Rishi-Sabha** — Council of elder ascetics and naturalists led by *Maharishi Dhaumya*.

## 2. The Asura Sub-Realm (असुर उप-लोक — The Chthonic Foundries)

- **Total Geography:** 3 million square miles of volcanic basalt ridges, subterranean magma caverns, slag plateaus, and geothermal river systems.
- **Atmospheric Baseline:** Scent of sulfur, hot mineral steam, and furnace smoke. Heavy gravity (+15% higher than Human Sub-Realm); rich in chthonic ores and volcanic crystals.
- **Population:** ~80 Million (Hardy, muscular humanoids; 90% common foundrymen, miners, and stonecutters; 10% warlords, forge-masters, and runic engineers).

### 2.1 The Vajra Kingdom (वज्र साम्राज्य — Heavy Industry & Armaments)
- **Topography:** The Black Caldera Basin — jagged volcanic mountains surrounding an enormous iron-rich valley crisscrossed by industrial rail lines and aqueducts of cooling water.
- **Capital:** **Vajra-Nagara (The Iron Spire)** — A massive industrial fortress-city built into an extinct volcanic cone. Dominated by roaring blast furnaces, steam-hammer halls, and multi-tiered rail platforms.
- **Major Cities:**
  - **Loha-Giri (The Armory City):** The center for ordnance testing, rifle manufacturing, and heavy artillery casting.
  - **Vashpa-Pattanam (Steam Port):** Geothermal pumping station supplying high-pressure steam conduits to surrounding valleys.
- **Ruling Dynasty:** **The Maya Dynasty (मय घराणे)** — Founded by the legendary architectural engineer *Mayasura*. Succession is meritocratic via the *Yantra-Pariksha* (Forge-Trial).

### 2.2 The Deep Basin Clans (पाताळ-खाण संघ — Shaila-Gadha)
- **Topography:** Subterranean ravine networks stretching miles beneath the volcanic peaks; enormous limestone caves lit by carbide lanterns and glowing mineral veins.
- **Capital:** **Shaila-Gadha (The Mountain Fortress)** — A tiered city carved directly into obsidian cliffs; suspended iron rope-bridges, mining elevators, and smelting halls.
- **Major Cities:**
  - **Agni-Vapi (Magma Sump):** The lowest industrial basin where slag is refined directly over subterranean magma vents.
- **Ruling System:** **The Seven Mining Clans (Shaila, Loha, Ratna, Khaani, Dhatu, Shila, Giri)** — Governed by the fraternal oligarchs *Ilvala & Vatapi*.

## 3. The Deva Sub-Realm (देव उप-लोक — The Celestial Heights)

- **Total Geography:** 2 million square miles of luminous floating islands (cloud-plateaus) hovering above a bottomless sky of azure and golden mist.
- **Atmospheric Baseline:** Pure, thin, high-frequency Aether; perpetual spring; zero industrial machinery.
- **Population:** ~40 Million (Graceful, luminous humanoids; 90% common silk-weavers, temple-sweepers, incense gatherers; 10% celestial ritualists and martial lords).

### 3.1 Amaravati Dominion (अमरावती अधिराज्य)
- **Topography:** The Central Cloud-Plateau — colossal crystalline peaks, terraces of white marble palaces, cascading sacred waterfalls that dissolve into mist before falling into the void.
- **Capital:** **Amaravati (The Sun-Glade City)** — Majestic colonnades of celestial stone, solar-focusing crystal domes, and harmonic bell towers that resonate with cosmic frequencies.
- **Major Settlements:**
  - **Meru-Skanda (The High Bulwark):** Crystalline defensive towers guarding the celestial perimeter against spatial turbulence.
- **Ruling Lineage:** **Aditya-Vansh (आदित्य वंश — Solar Houses)** — Led by High Celestial Sovereign *Deva-Raja Somaka*.

### 3.2 The Silver Cloud Sanctuaries (श्वेत-मेघ क्षेत्र — Gandharva-Puri)
- **Topography:** Whispering floating groves of silver-barked trees, tranquil lake-islands, and floating open-air amphitheaters.
- **Capital:** **Gandharva-Puri** — The artistic, musical, and textile center of the Devas. Famed for weaving *Aether-Silk* (clothing that repels elemental friction) and refining incense.
- **Ruling Order:** **Soma-Vansh & The Guild of Harmonic Artists**.

## 4. The Beast Sub-Realm (श्वापद उप-लोक — The Primeval Expanse)

- **Total Geography:** 5 million square miles of untamed, primordial wilderness — colossal million-year-old canopy forests, boundless toxic-edge marshes, jagged bone-canyons, and subterranean animal lairs.
- **Atmospheric Baseline:** Raw, heavy, untamed natural Aether; high humidity; seasonal monsoons of pure spiritual rain.
- **Population:** Uncounted millions of natural wildlife; thousands of Aether-Awakened Beasts; dozens of colossal Apex Titans.

### 4.1 The Great Canopy Dominion (महान छत्र-वन — Mahā-Kāntāra)
- **Topography:** An unbroken 1,000-mile forest of iron-wood trees reaching 1,500 feet into the sky. Entire ecological communities live among the branches without ever touching the forest floor.
- **Alpha Bastion:** **The Heartwood Sanctum (Vajra-Vana)** — Enormous cavernous hollows inside the roots of primordial world-trees.
- **Dominant Clan:** **The Vajra-Bhalluka (Four-Horned Armored Bears)** — Colossal ursine titans with stone-crushing density, ruling the forest floor.

### 4.2 The Sky-Cleaving Roost (गगन-कडा — Pakshi-Shaila)
- **Topography:** Vertiginous needle-peaks rising 20,000 feet above the forest canopy; sheer vertical cliffs exposed to fierce dimensional crosswinds.
- **Alpha Roost:** **Suparna-Giri** — The wind-carved granite aeries of the avian titans.
- **Dominant Lineage:** **The Suparna / Garuda Lineage** — Golden-feathered rocs with razor wing-blades capable of spatial wind-shears.

### 4.3 The Shadow Marsh (छाया दलदल — Gupt-Kandara)
- **Topography:** Dark mangrove swamps, quicksand basins, and moss-choked chasms bordering spatial rift zones.
- **Alpha Den:** **Chaya-Kandara** — Submerged cavern labyrinths beneath mist-covered peat bogs.
- **Dominant Lineage:** **The Shunya-Vyaghra (Void-Panthers)** — Solitary apex predators possessing innate spatial slips and shadow-crawling capabilities.
