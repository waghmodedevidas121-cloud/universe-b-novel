### 2.1 PILLAR I — PRĀṆA (Vital Life-Force) `[CANON: World/03]` + `[DESIGN LOCK]` for the mechanics block
**Grounding [SOURCE CONTEXT]:** Prāṇa as vital cosmic breath circulating through 72,000 nāḍīs; ten vāyus (5 major: Prāṇa, Apāna, Samāna, Vyāna, Udāna / 5 minor: Nāga, Kūrma, Kṛkara, Devadatta, Dhanañjaya); three prime channels Iḍā, Piṅgalā, Suṣumnā; three knots Brahma-Granthi, Viṣṇu-Granthi, Rudra-Granthi; seven chakras.

**Mechanics (implements Axis A "Energy" four questions):**
| Prāṇa dimension | Practitioner expression | Axis A term |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Reserve | Breath capacity, nāḍī width, chakra storage | Energy RESERVE |
| Throughput | Rate of prāṇic discharge in a technique or shield | Energy THROUGHPUT |
| Control | Prāṇāyāma precision — retention (*kumbhaka*), ratio, aim | Energy CONTROL |
| Stability | Resistance to nāḍī burn, backlash, overload | Energy STABILITY |

- **Breath control is the regulator, not the fuel tank.** Retention and ratio govern how much of the reserve is safely expressible at once.
- **Internal currents:** Prāṇa moves Iḍā ↔ Piṅgalā on the breath cycle; Suṣumnā stays dormant/blocked until the three granthis break (`03` §2.2). Astral-tier invocation *requires* Suṣumnā flow — which is why Astra and Prāṇa tiers are coupled.
- **Overdraw consequence [CANON: `18` Rule 4]:** internal vessel trauma, burned nāḍīs, biological exhaustion. Recovery through rest / environment / defined practice only — no universal regen rate.

**Failure modes:** reserve depletion (effect cannot sustain), throughput bottleneck (effect weaker/slower than intended), control failure (misfire), stability failure (unsafe shutdown), compatibility failure (source unusable by this user). — inherited verbatim from FINAL Bible §4.

**Guardrail [CAUTION]:** Prāṇa is never written as "mana with a Sanskrit name." In-world it is breath, vitality, and circulation; no HUD bar; no claim that any real Hindu school teaches a game-like prāṇa meter.

**Failure modes:** inconsistent practice (slow/unstable gain); wrong method (no gain or a different gain); misunderstanding (skill without interpretation); over-specialisation (excellent in one domain, poor transfer).

**Guardrail [CAUTION]:** Tapasya is never "XP." Extreme self-denial is not automatically stronger than balanced discipline; the story must show *why* a practice works. No ordinary Tapasya path makes a being equal to Brahmā, Viṣṇu, or Śiva. *(See audit finding F-08 before writing any "a tapas grants a boon automatically" scene.)*

### 2.3 PILLAR III — MANTRA (Vibrational Algorithms, Sound Resonance, Spoken Reality) `[CANON: World/03 §5]` + `[DESIGN LOCK]` mechanics
**Grounding [SOURCE CONTEXT]:** *Śabda-Brahman* — Sanskrit phonetics correspond to real oscillation frequencies of Prakṛti. Bīja mantras are single-syllable primordial triggers: **OM** (foundation vibration), **HRĪṀ** (māyā/creation matrix), **ŚRĪṀ** (sustaining radiance), **KLĪṀ** (binding attraction), **PHAṬ** (termination syllable — detonates astras). **Nyāsa** projects sacred vibration onto anatomical points; a Nyāsa-treated body becomes Vajra-Deha.

**Mechanics — mantra as algorithm (four registers):**
| Register | Function | Analogue |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Pāda integrity** | Correct phoneme sequence + accent + meter | Syntax — a broken sequence is a broken program |
| **Dīkṣā (initiation)** | Lineage/teacher transmission granting *right to run it* | Licence — matches FINAL Bible RULE 4 |
| **Sankalpa (will-charge)** | Practitioner's intent fixes target, scope, duration | Parameters / arguments |
| **Prāṇa charge (via Pillar I)** | Fuel supplied to the utterance | Current through the circuit |

**Operational rules:**
1. A mantra expresses an effect only when **Authority (valid utterance) + Energy (prāṇa charge) + Dīkṣā (right) + Sankalpa (scope)** are all present. Missing any one = no effect or misfire.
2. **Kinetic Lamina (Mantra Kinetic Shield)** `[DESIGN LOCK — RECOMMENDED]` — a compressed prāṇa/air lamina held by a sustained bīja; the standard mid-tier defence. Specified in §6.4.
3. Counter-mantras and higher-tier utterances nullify lower ones; **resonance shatter** (matching the defender's bīja frequency) is the doctrinal counter to any sustained lamina.
4. **Forgetting is a real failure mode** — the *Mantra-vismaraṇa* clause: under terror, moral decay, or a broken vow, syllables leave the mind at the critical second (`04` §4.1, Karṇa case).

**Guardrail [CAUTION] (F-01b):** No universal spellbook. Every mantra-based technique must record tradition-inspired design, learning conditions, performance requirements, target, limits, and failure conditions. The novel must never claim its mantra mechanics are what "Hinduism teaches."

### 6.5 Scaling do-not-break rules (write-time hard limits)
1. **No rifle kills T5+.** A T5 survives a single bolt-action hit even without a lamina. Massed artillery remains a *terrain* threat, not a personal one.
2. **No T4 is bulletproof.** Trained mortals die to gunfire — that is what keeps the mortal POV, Prāṇa-Bandhan, and frontier stories honest.
3. **No "technology is useless" writing.** Telegraphs win wars at T1–T4; the empire with wires beats the empire without them. Information is not tier-gated.
4. **No cultivator is immune to logistics.** A T6 with an empty stomach, cut supply line, or severed ward-anchor is a story problem, not a power-level problem — and none of those are "attacks" his lamina can block.
5. **Prāṇa-resonant munitions exist** `[DESIGN LOCK]` — the industrial answer to awakeners: yantra-etched tips and prāṇa-damped propellant, produced only by Magitech metropolises (`18` §1). They make T5 *contested*, never *routine*.
6. **Machines have no intent** — they cannot be sensed by intent-based danger sense, cannot be bluffed, and their kills carry the karma of the one who ordered them (§2.4 rule 4).
7. **Zero AI** `[CANON: 18 naming rule]` — no thinking machines, no algorithmic targeting; brass, steam, optics, and human marksmanship only.
8. **Astra vs artillery is not a contest** — an astra does not "out-damage" a battery; it out-ranges, out-targets, and ignores armor while the battery out-numbers, out-sustains, and out-logs. Never reduce this to a single winner.

## 7. Prāṇa-Bandhan — The Symbiotic Mortal–Beast Bond (F-05)

**Source status:** referenced in `WORLDBUILDING_BIBLE_V0.1.md` §Tier 3 and `World/20_EVOLVING_COSMOS_REALMS_AND_COUNCIL_SYSTEM.md` line 68. **Under-specified for a power module → locked here** `[DESIGN LOCK — RECOMMENDED]`.

**Definition:** Aether-Bond (formerly Prāṇa-Bandhan) is a **voluntary, mutual, non-coercive bond between a non-powered mortal and a beast**, exchanging Aether-warmth for labour, mobility, and protection. It is the everyday answer to "what do ordinary people do in a world with awakeners?"

### 7.1 Core rules
1. **Mutual consent, both directions.** The beast is a partner, never property (`20`: "गुलाम म्हणून नव्हे" — not as slaves). Coerced bonds do not form; attempting one is a karmic offence and fails at the pattern level.
2. **It is not a power grant.** The bond gives the mortal *linkage and service*, not a tier. A bonded farmer is still T1–T3.
3. **It is not combat tier elevation.** Bonded beasts defend at **their own** tier, capped at the T1–T4 band for common frontier fauna. An awakened/apex beast is a different relationship entirely and is never filed under "non-powered mortal" (see F-12).
4. **Conductor requirement (Yantra pillar link):** every bond is anchored by a **bandhan-token** — a brass/iron pendant or yoke-plate with an inscribed conduit pattern. Without the token the link is only scent-and-trust; with it, the bond carries a narrow sensory thread (direction, distress pulse, day/night intent) and prāṇa-warmth.
5. **Throughput is deliberately narrow** `[DESIGN LOCK]`: the token moves *feeling and intent*, never technique. A bonded hound cannot relay a mantra; a bonded ox cannot carry a ward.
6. **Severance rule:** normal retirement (age, death of partner) is peaceful grief; **forcible severance** damages the mortal's nāḍīs and the beast's vitality, and the severer carries the karma — which is why frontier law treats bond-breaking as a serious crime.
7. **No military conscription of bonds.** Armies may *request* bonded logistics support; commandeering a bond is illegal in every Tempest-adjacent polity (`20` framing).

### 7.2 Uses (the three canonical roles)
| Role | Typical pairs | Function | Tier context |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Agriculture** | Human ↔ Varāha (boar), Gaveḍa/ox, elephant-class | Ploughing, terracing, heavy hauling, flood sensing | T1–T3 belts |
| **Logistics** | Human ↔ horses, pack-beasts, bonded birds | Freight, courier runs, **bonded messenger flights that interlock with the telegraph grid** | T1–T4 — the hybrid frontier comms net |
| **Frontier defence** | Human ↔ Bonded Hounds (*prāṇa-jāgr̥t śvān*), guardian beasts | Outpost alarm (intent-pulse reaches the handler before the breach), night watch, wounded recovery | T1–T4 defence only |

### 7.3 Interface with technology `[DESIGN LOCK]`
- **Yantra-C (industrial) and Prāṇa-Bandhan share infrastructure:** a frontier fort has (a) telegraph wire, (b) bonded hound pack, (c) ward-anchored walls. Each covers another's gap — wire is cut by raiders, hounds are blinded by terrain, wards need a cultivator to recharge.
- **Bandhan-tokens are mass-produced by workshops** (brass, stamped geometry) — the one place where industrial Yantra routinely touches prāṇa in the hands of *non-powered* people. This is why token-forges are strategic targets, and why token metallurgy is a state secret.
