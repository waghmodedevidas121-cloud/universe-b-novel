## 0. Multi-Cultural Naming & Clan Sociology Architecture
*(बहु-सांस्कृतिक नामकरण, कुळ-परंपरा व सामाजिक स्वातंत्र्य)*

Universe B is an expansive, ancient cosmos where thousands of years of trade, migration, and isolation after the Great Partition created **distinct cultural, linguistic, and naming traditions** across each sub-realm. Not all names are Indian; rather, each realm has its own organic linguistic heritage:

1. **Human Sub-Realm (मानव उप-लोक): Dual Heritage (Dynastic & Maritime-Industrial)**
   - *Hastinapura Heartland:* Classical Indo-Aryan dynastic traditions (King Parikshit II, Queen Madhavi, Vidura-Das, Senapati Dhristadyumna, Colonel Shikhandi).
   - *Samudra-Pattanam (Coastal Trade):* Anglo-Dutch and Victorian maritime naming from coastal merchant syndicates (High Admiral Silas Suhotra, Commodore Linnea Brandt, Captain Douglas Romash, Diver Finn Matsya, Helmswoman Coraline Vance).
   - *Imperial Rail Works & Tapovana:* Industrial and Vedic synthesis (Chief Engineer Alistair Vance, Master Engineer Rohini-Bai, Maharishi Dhaumya, Maharishi Gargi, Herbalist Evelyn Devika).

2. **Asura Sub-Realm (असुर उप-लोक): Germanic, Nordic & Chthonic Foundry Culture**
   - The Asura realm is defined by active volcanoes, massive steam boilers, pneumatic pistons, and heavy ballistics. Their naming culture blends ancient Danava roots with harsh, industrial Germanic, Nordic, and Slavic phonetic structures:
   - Grand Architect Maya Richter, Forge-Queen Mandor Vane, Iron-Marshal Hilda Ayomukhi, General Boris Prahasa, Baroness Greta Simhika, The Eisen Chieftains (Viktor Ilvala & Gregor Vatapi), Dr. Erika Ulupi, Comrade Ulka Kaelen, Apprentice Kshema Keller, Artisan Astrid Karni, Matron Olga Bala.

3. **Deva Sub-Realm (देव उप-लोक): Greco-Roman & Classical Elysian Aristocracy**
   - High celestial cloud plateaus, crystalline spires, and solar resonance courts carry elevated Latinate, Hellenic, and Persian aesthetic naming traditions:
   - Deva-Sovereign Aurelius Somaka, High Queen Seraphina Shachi, High Commander Lysandra Urvashi, Celestial Marshal Jayantus Cassian, High Justice Themis Tara, High Priestess Rohini Selene, Lady Chitralekha Fontaine, Master Weaver Julian Chitraratha, Archon Theron Vrihas, Courier Zephyr Hemamala.

4. **Beast Sub-Realm (श्वापद उप-लोक): Primal Nordic, Mythic & Gaelic Apex Titles**
   - The wild canopies, sky needles, and shadow marshes carry ancient nature titles rooted in mythic beasts and primal languages:
   - Titan King Ursus-Krodha, Serpent Empress Kadru-Jormun, Sky Empress Sampati-Valkyrie, Suparna-Raja Aquila, Void-Matriarch Nyx-Chaya, Shardul-Rani Freya-Vyaghri, Shaman Lopamudra Morvath, Hermit Matanga Eldir.

5. **The Central Sub-Realm (मध्यवर्ती महा-लोक): Cosmopolitan International Neutrality**
   - As the global crossroads and diplomatic hub (like Geneva, London, and Alexandria combined), figures bear sophisticated international and hybrid names:
   - High Chancellor Sulabha Al-Mansur, High Governor Ambika Sterling, Grand Marshal Satyabhama Cruz, High Magistrate Vikarna Vance, Senior Investigator Devayani Voss, Telegraph Guildmaster Uttanka Klein, Wire Director Chandra-Lekha Meyer, Archivist Maitreyi Holloway.

- **Moral Alignment:** **Lawful Neutral (Heavy Grey).** Desperately desires his mortal subjects to live without fear of Devas or Asuras, but will coldly sanction 14-hour shifts in locomotive shell foundries and sign secret execution warrants for rebellious guildmasters to prevent economic collapse.
