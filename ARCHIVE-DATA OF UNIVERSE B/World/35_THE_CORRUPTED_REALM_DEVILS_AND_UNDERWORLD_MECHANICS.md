## 1. Origin: The Soul Sorting & Migration Collapse

During the **Void Cataclysm (Era 2)**, chaotic void fissures tore through the southern regions, causing a systemic breakdown of the universe's soul-cycle:

1. **Chitragupta's Ledger Severed:** The intense void radiation severed the karmic threads connecting millions of mortal souls to the High Council's administrative ledger. Their **Soul IDs were wiped/corrupted**, rendering them unindexed.
2. **Yama's Death-Cycle Blocked:** Because their subtle bodies were irradiated with toxic void matter, they could not cross the sacred threshold to the Death Side without poisoning the cosmic stream.
3. **Vidhata's Bond Invalidation:** Vidhata could not forge lawful Karmic Bonds (*Aether-Bonds*) to grant them new mortal vessels across the five sub-realms.
4. **The Abyssal Genesis:** Trapped for millennia in the southern chasm, these forsaken souls absorbed mutating toxic Aether. While their indestructible core (**Ātma-tattva**) survived, their subtle bodies mutated into **Devils**.

## 2. Geography: Modern Settlements (No Compound Clichés)

The Corrupted Realm contains **zero 19th-century railway lines or civil locomotives**. The landscape is a shattered toxic wasteland where heavy trains cannot run; survival is maintained through subterranean vaults and reinforced scrap-crawlers.

### A. The Sovereign Abyss (Devil Dominions)
* **Netherholme (Capital of the Abyss):** The colossal, spire-studded metropolis carved from black obsidian and petrified bone. Seat of the self-proclaimed "Fake Gods" and supreme Devil Lords like *Paundraka*.
* **Dread-Hold:** A brutal chthonic fortress overlooking the central toxic chasm. Garrison of the armored fiend warlord *Shataghati* and heavy toxic war-engines.
* **Shadow-Hollow:** A perpetually dark, mist-shrouded rift basin where ambient light cannot pierce. Home to intangible, illusion-weaving night-strider devils.

### B. The Penitent Outlaws (Exile & Smuggler Territories)
* **Rust Haven:** A jagged canyon fortress constructed entirely from welded scrap-plates, salvaged boilers, and reinforced iron shelters. Inhabited by exiled humans, asuras, and disgraced seekers.
* **The Trench Exchange:** A clandestine subterranean black-market bazaar nestled inside deep cliff caverns beneath the reach of toxic storms. Masterminded by the contraband king *Maricha*.
* **Scavenger-Crawler Convoys:** Instead of railway trains, outlaws traverse the wasteland using steam-powered, treaded scrap-crawlers commanded by the inseparable warlords *Hamsa & Dimbaka*.

## 3. The Grand Aegis (The 5,000-Mile Spiritual Resonance Barrier)

The boundary separating the Corrupted Realm from the Buffer Zone and Astral Sub-Realms is **not merely an iron wall (Lauha-Prakara)**. It is **The Grand Aegis (महा-चैतन्य प्राकार)**:

1. **Dual-Layer Defense:**
   - **Base Physical Wall:** A 300-foot-high runic basalt barrier engineered to block toxic soil drift and mutated fauna.
   - **The High-Frequency Spiritual Shield:** A towering energy veil of golden celestial frequency anchored directly to the High Council's cosmic grid.
2. **The Disintegration Rule:**
   - If an unauthorized Devil (physical or incorporeal) attempts to walk, crawl, or fly across The Grand Aegis, the spiritual resonance field immediately **ruptures their subtle body and vaporizes their toxic container**.
   - Mass invasion across the frontier is mechanically impossible.

## 4. The Summoning Mechanics: How Devils Enter the Astral Realm

If The Grand Aegis is completely impenetrable, **how can Devils ever appear in the living Astral Realm?**

### 1. The Interdimensional Soul Contract
- A summoner inside the Astral Realm does not open a physical hole in the wall.
- Using an advanced ritual array (**Aether-Mudra / Spatial Soul Contract**), the summoner projects a micro-spatial thread deep into the Abyss, locking onto a Devil's soul frequency.

### 2. The Soul-Only Filter (काय-त्याग नियम)
- The spatial thread is a pure metaphysical conduit. When the Devil accepts the summons:
  - **The Physical Vessel Remains Behind:** The Devil's toxic, heavy, grotesque body in Netherholme or Dread-Hold **cannot pass** through the spiritual needle-hole. It stays behind in a comatose hibernation state.
  - **Physical Traits & Gained Armor Are Stripped:** All physical bulk, forged iron armor, biological venom, and raw muscle density gained after birth are completely left behind.
  - **Pure Spiritual Soul Form (केवळ आत्मा):** The Devil arrives in the Astral Realm **strictly as a spiritual soul-construct**.

### 3. The Four Inviolable Summoning Restrictions (चार कडक मर्यादा)
1. **The Pure Aether Decay (हवेतील क्षय व वेळ मर्यादा):**
   - The pure Aether atmosphere of the Astral Realm naturally dissolves toxic devil soul-matter.
   - Without a massive, continuous Aether feed from the summoner, the devil's soul cannot persist permanently; it dissolves or snaps back to Netherholme within hours or days.
2. **Power Suppression (५०% ते ७०% नैसर्गिक मर्यादा):**
   - Stripped of their native abyssal miasma and physical chassis, devil offensive output is suppressed by 50% to 70%.
   - They cannot deploy devastating wasteland blasts; they are restricted to subtle soul-arts (illusions, psychic dread, telepathic interrogation, spiritual barriers).
3. **Vessel Burnout & Fragility (पात्राची झीज व तुटणे):**
   - An unsheathed devil soul cannot exist loose in ambient Aether without unraveling.
   - Ordinary living mortal flesh rots quickly from toxic soul friction. Summoners must house them in specialized artificial vessels (consecrated mechanical puppets, inscribed runic armor, or Aether-crystal cores). High-intensity combat shatters these vessels.
4. **Mental Backlash & Reversal (बंडखोरीचा धोका):**
   - Devils are unindexed entities harboring profound hunger. If a summoner's willpower wavers or their mental defenses fracture, the devil soul will instantly turn upon the contractor, consuming their consciousness (*Psychic Devour*).

## 5. The Class-Omega Taboo: The Ashen Eclipse & Universal Secrecy

### 1. The Catastrophe: The Ashen Eclipse (राखेचे ग्रहण)
- Toward the close of Era 3, an arrogant human-asuric cabal (*The Hollow Sect*) attempted to bypass the spatial partition by summoning high-tier abyssal devils into a major mortal city.
- Control ruptured: the summoned devils slipped their bonds, triggering a contagious soul-rot plague that possessed and devoured 50,000 citizens in one night.
- The High Council's Marshals and Sages deployed divine astras to incinerate the entire metropolis into white ash to quarantine the spiritual contagion.

### 2. Universal Secrecy & Legal Prohibition
- **Absolute Secrecy for Commoners:** Everyday citizens have zero knowledge that summoning is possible. To them, "devils" are merely terrifying figures from bedtime ghost folklore.
- **Class-Omega Forbidden Art (महा-निषिद्ध विद्या):** All summoning texts, mudra sequences, and soul-contract formulas were permanently confiscated or burned.
- **Capital Offense:** Any individual caught attempting a devil summoning faces instant summary execution or perpetual soul-imprisonment by the High Council.
