## 1. Cosmic Nature & Identity

Lord Viṣṇu is the sustainer of the universe. While Mahā-Viṣṇu creates multiverses, Lord Viṣṇu resides within the individual Brahmāṇḍa, resting on the serpentine coils of Ādi-Śeṣa on the Ocean of Milk.

- **Primary Duty:** Maintaining cosmic balance (*Sthiti*) and upholding the moral fabric (*Dharma*).
- **Emblems & Weapons:**
  - *Śaṅkha (Pāñcajanya):* The primordial sound of creation; shatters adharmic delusions.
  - *Cakra (Sudarśana):* The ultimate wheel of time, space, and unerring destruction.
  - *Gadā (Kaumodakī):* The mace of primordial intellect and physical crushing force.
  - *Padma (Lotus):* The flower of spiritual purity and non-attachment.

## 2. Role in this Novel: The Great Dialogue with Brahmā

Lord Viṣṇu plays the foundational role in the prologue / opening premise of our novel:

```
[ LORD BRAHMĀ (Creator) ] <────── The Great Dialogue ──────> [ LORD VIṢṆU (Preserver) ]
"Every cycle, my creations fail;                              "Let us not send an Avatāra this time.
 mortals become weak and dependent                           Let mortals hold the pen of their own destiny.
 on Your descent to slay demons."                             If they conquer adharma through their own
                                                             Puruṣārtha, they transcend mortal frailty."
```

### Key Decisions Made by Lord Viṣṇu for this Novel:
1. **The Covenant of Restraint (Avatāra-Saṁyama):**
   - He bound Himself not to incarnate as Kalki in this modern world.
   - He will not manifest to split pillars like Nṛsiṁha or fire arrows like Rāma. The mortal plane must fend for itself.
2. **The Authorization of Chitragupta:**
   - Lord Viṣṇu granted permission for **Chitragupta's cosmic database** to interface with mortal seekers and administrative officers strictly through mortal Puruṣārtha and Tapasya over Karma, Boons, and Astras (no free system screens or cheat gifts).
3. **The Silent Observer:**
   - He watches from the Ocean of Milk. His presence is felt through the absolute precision of karmic returns, not through sudden miracles.
