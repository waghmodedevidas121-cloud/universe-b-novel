## 1. Canonical Identity in Universe B

In Universe B, **Chitragupta** is an immutable, eternal pillar of cosmic reality, standing as one of **The Eternal Four** (alongside Ananta, Yama, and Vidhata):

- **Nature of the Office:** Chitragupta is an eternal, primordial entity established at the dawn of the cosmos (Era 1: Satya Yuga) directly by the Supreme Trinity. He is **not** a temporary role-holder or mortal designee.
- **Zero Tenure & Zero Retirement:** Chitragupta never changes, never retires, has no lifespan limit, and can never be succeeded or removed. His position is permanent and absolute.
- **Subordinate Staff Distinction:** While lower administrative scribes, record-keepers, and clerks (*Lekhya*, archivists, filing dūtas) in the Soul Administration Bureau may rotate or be appointed, **Lord Chitragupta himself is eternal and irreplaceable**.

## 2. Primary Duty: Universal Ledger & Preparation of the Soul ID

Chitragupta's signature canonical responsibility in Universe B is the **maintenance of the Universal Ledger (Maha-Lekha) and preparation of the Soul ID**:

1. **Drafting the Soul ID:**
   - Every soul processed through Official Migration or regular karmic cycles receives an administrative record prepared by Chitragupta.
   - **Data Fields:** Includes microsecond timestamping, past-life indexing, *Kul* (family/clan destiny), innate aptitudes, and objective karmic metrics.
2. **Submission to Ananta:**
   - Chitragupta drafts the record and interfaces with **Ananta (The Non-Living Core System)** for review and indexing.
   - Ananta cross-references the entry across the immutable cosmic core before Vidhata executes the birth-thread connection.

## 3. Clear Boundaries & System Balance [CANON]

- **The Eternal Four Cohesion:** Functions as Chief Administrator Yama's indispensable right-hand assistant; Yama's tribunal cannot issue judgments without Chitragupta's verified ledger entries.
