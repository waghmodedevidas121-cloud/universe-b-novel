### [REGION 1] THE EASTERN CROWNLANDS (मानव खंड — THE MORTAL CRADLE)
- **Public Title:** The Eastern Crownlands (The Realm of Plow & Wheel).
- **True Cosmic Identity:** The Bharata-Kuru Mortal Cradle (Chandra-Vansh).
- **Geographic Terrain:** 100,000 miles of fertile river valleys, alluvial grain plains, and the vast **Mortal Sea**.

#### Clan 1.1: The Royal Crownland Dynasty (हस्तिनापूर राजवंश)
- **Public Name:** House of the Wheel / The Crownland Sovereigns.
- **True Lineage:** The Kuru-Bharata Dynasty (Direct descendants of Emperor Parikshat I).
- **Current Head:** **The Crownland King (King Parikshat)** & The High Council of the Throne.
- **Key Figures:** 
  - **Crown Prince Janamejaya** (Overseer of Fortifications).
  - **Princess Uttara II** (Patroness of the Akharas).
- **Traditions & Arts:**
  - *Dhanurveda Mastery:* The seven sacred draws of the composite horn-bow.
  - *The Ratha Vanguard:* Heavy iron-wood chariots drawn by twin Lava-Bulls.
  - *The Iron Torc (हस्तिना-कंकण):* Inscribed iron wrist-band awarded to recognized champions.
- **Attire & Arms:** Quilted cotton tunics beneath bronze lamellar armor; recurve horn-bows, curved talwars, and long patās (gauntlet-swords).

#### Clan 1.2: The Maritime Merchant Guild (समुद्र-पत्तनम् नौदल घराणी)
- **Seat:** The Port of Seven Tides (Samudra-Pattanam).
- **Leader:** **Admiral Suhotra** and the Council of the Five Oars.
- **Traditions:** The Silver Ledger (double-entry shark-skin accounting) and sea-offerings (*Samudra-Puja*).
- **Weapons:** Flexible Urumi whip-swords and pneumatic harpoon carbines.

#### Clan 1.3: The Northern Hermitage Sages (उत्तर तपोवन ऋषी कुळे)
- **Seat:** The High Shal-Forests of Mount Gandhamadana.
- **Head:** **Kulapati Shaunaka** & **Evelyn, the Herbalist** (`CHR-HUM-15`).
- **Traditions:** Complete rejection of machinery; mastery of breath regulation (*Pranayama*), herbal alchemy, and preservation of palm-leaf lore.

#### Clan 1.4: The Western Clockwork Enclave (५% परदेशी वसाहत — The Multiverse Diaspora)
- **Seat:** The Clockwork Quarter (Hastinapur West).
- **Leadership:** **Alistair Vance, the Yardmaster** (`CHR-HUM-07`) and the Syndic Board.
- **Origin:** Multiversal travelers who fell through spatial rifts over the last 150 years.
- **Traditions:** Printed broadsheets (*The Hastinapur Gazette*), brick drainage networks, public libraries, and clockwork spring assemblies.
