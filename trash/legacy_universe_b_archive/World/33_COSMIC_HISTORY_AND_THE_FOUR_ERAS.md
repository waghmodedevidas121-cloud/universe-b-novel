## 1. The Four Grand Eras

### Era 1: The Primordial Genesis (आदि-युग — The Dawning Age)
* **The Creation:** The Supreme Trinity formed Universe B as a living, dynamic test cosmos, establishing the fundamental laws of Karma, Dharma, and the Five Elements.
* **Core Installations:**
  * **Ananta (The System Core):** Installed as an unfeeling, incorruptible, read-only universal archive to record cosmic events and soul milestones without favoritism.
  * **The Six Divine Constitutional Offices:** Yama, Vidhata, Chitragupta, Chief Sage, High Guardian, and Prime Seeker established in the neutral High Council Realm.
* **The Living Era:** Devas, Asuras, Humans, and Beasts originally coexisted on a single vast, unpartitioned continental expanse. Early civilizations developed ancient geomancy and spiritual arts.

---

### Era 2: The Void Cataclysm & The Great Sealing (महा-भ्रंश युग — The Calamity Age)
* **The Cosmic Rupture:** Anomalous cosmic fissures torn by chaotic void currents ripped across the southern borders, spawning the **Seven Primeval Dark Entities (Kāla-Astitva)**.
* **The Catastrophic War:**
  * Ancient heroes, early sages, and apex titans across all races formed an emergency alliance to halt cosmic unraveling.
  * The Dark Entities could not be killed; their soul cores were too deeply entwined with cosmic matter.
* **The Sealing & The Wall:**
  * The southern toxic wasteland was quarantined as **The Corrupted Realm**.
  * The High Council and ancient masters established **Six Impenetrable Cosmic Seals** around the Seventh Rift.
  * The Buffer Zone anchored **The Grand Aegis (महा-चैतन्य प्राकार)**—a 5,000-mile runic fortification crowned with a high-frequency spiritual resonance shield—guarded by the standing Setu Mahā-Senā.

---

### Era 3: The Great Partition & The Ashen Eclipse (महा-विभाजन व राखेचे ग्रहण)
* **The Drivers of the Great Partition:**
  1. **Biological Scale Mismatch:** Fragile mortal humanity (95%+ non-powered) faced imminent extinction living on the same continuous ground as trampling primeval titans and chthonic asuric magma foundries.
  2. **The Kin Wars:** Ruthless clashes over pure Aether-crystal veins threatened to destabilize the continental tectonic plate into the void.
* **The Spatial Severance:**
  * Ancient sages and the High Council executed the **Great Sundering**, carving the continent into **4 species-specific Sub-Realms** (Human, Deva, Asura, Beast) isolated across an aetheric spatial void, with all walking land bridges permanently dissolved.
  * Established **The Central Sub-Realm** as a demilitarized apex crossroads under an **Absolute Non-Violence Truce** for trade and high diplomacy.
* **The Ashen Eclipse (Class-Omega Taboo Disaster):**
  * Toward the end of Era 3, an arrogant research syndicate (*The Hollow Sect*) attempted to bypass the spatial partition by summoning abyssal devils using soul-contracts.
  * Control collapsed: summoned devil souls triggered a catastrophic soul-rot plague, possessing and devouring an entire metropolis of 50,000 citizens in a single night.
  * High Council Marshals and Sages incinerated the ruins with consecrated Astra fire.
  * **The Universal Ban:** Devil Summoning was permanently outlawed across all realms as a **Class-Omega Forbidden Art (महा-निषिद्ध विद्या)**. All instructional texts were confiscated or destroyed; to the common public, devils were relegated to fireside ghost folklore.

---

### Era 4: The Steam-Aether Age (वाष्प-आयामी युग — Present Day)
* **The Industrial Shift:**
  * Asura master artisans and Human inventors discovered how to compress superheated steam through Aether-inscribed brass conduits.
  * Birth of double-gauge locomotives, bolt-action rifles, pneumatic mining drills, and the 10,000-mile Aetheric telegraph network.
* **The Cold War & Proxy Strife:**
  * Because full-scale inter-realm armies cannot march across borders, the Great Powers clash through economic embargoes, ore cartels, illegal black-market pipelines, and covert spy networks.
* **The Incipient Crisis (Novel Starting Point):**
  * Dimensional stability is beginning to show micro-fluctuations.
  * Deep ocean rifts in the **Kshira Ocean** are experiencing unprecedented drift events, drawing anomalous souls (**Route B Arrivals**, including the Protagonist) with blank, unindexed destinies into Universe B!
