## 1. Human Sub-Realm Key Personnel

### 1.1 The Kuru-Panchala Confederation (Hastinapura)

#### 1. King Parikshit II (महाराज परीक्षित द्वितीय) — The Reformist Sovereign
- **Role:** Constitutional Sovereign of the Kuru-Panchala Confederation.
- **Personality:** Prone to deep melancholy, pragmatic, highly educated in both ancient Dharma-shastras and Victorian-style administrative economics.
- **Backstory:** Ascended the throne following a brutal border skirmish with Asura smugglers. Realizing that human flesh cannot compete with Asuric biological muscle, he modernized the army with bolt-action rifles, nationalized the rail grid, and signed the *Astral Education Concord*.
- **Weapon / Tool:** The *Raja-Mudra* (Gold-Inscribed Imperial Seal) and a custom ivory-gripped service revolver.

#### 2. Senapati Dhristadyumna (सेनापती धृष्टद्युम्न) — Commander of the Royal Line Infantry
- **Role:** Supreme Commander of the Kuru-Panchala Armed Forces.
- **Personality:** Stoic, unyielding disciplinarian, master of trench warfare and logistical mobilization.
- **Relation to King:** Completely loyal to the crown, but privately fears that relying on steam guns weakens human spiritual cultivation (*Tapasya*).
- **Equipment:** Officer's brass sabre and a double-barrel rifled carbine firing Aether-slugs.

#### 3. Maha-Mantri Vidura-Das (महामंत्री विदुर-दास) — High Chancellor of State
- **Role:** Prime Minister and Chief Legislative Drafter of the Parliament.
- **Backstory:** Born to a family of humble village scribes; rose through sheer merit by reforming the tax code and preventing famines through telegraph grain-tracking.
- **Circle & Associates:** Mentored the young neutral magistrate *Vikarna* before Vikarna relocated to The Central Sub-Realm.

---

### 1.2 The Coastal Merchant League (Samudra-Pattanam)

#### 4. High Admiral Suhotra (महा-नाविक सुहोत्र) — Sovereign of the Twelve Houses
- **Role:** Elected President of the Admiralty Council; Commodore of the Coastal Steam Fleet.
- **Personality:** Impeccably groomed, shrewd, aristocratic, views the world through commodity margins and Aether-Crystal exchange rates.
- **Backstory:** Built his fortune financing iron shipments from the Asuras to the Humans. Survived a kraken attack in the outer Kshira Ocean by detonating his own flagship's steam boilers.
- **Weapons:** Gold-plated revolver, velvet officer's coat, and a secret ledger cipher book.

#### 5. Diver Matsya (गोताखोर मत्स्य) — Chief of Amphibious Salvage
- **Role:** Deep-water salvage expert and scout for the Merchant League.
- **Personality:** Taciturn, smells perpetually of brine and sea-kelp; can hold his breath for twenty minutes using rudimentary lung-cleansing techniques (*Pranayama*).
- **Relation to Suhotra:** Suhotra’s personal underwater troubleshooter; unearths pre-Sundering relics and sunken Aether-crystals from the ocean floor.

---

### 1.3 The Northern Hermitage Valleys (Tapovana)

#### 6. Maharishi Dhaumya (महर्षि धौम्य) — Chief Sage of the Living Forests
- **Role:** High Chancellor of the Rishi-Sabha; Warden of Ancient Sacred Seeds.
- **Personality:** Serene, soft-spoken, possessor of boundless patience; his eyes reflect deep cosmic calm.
- **Backstory:** One of the few mortals who survived the Era 3 Ashen Eclipse disaster; personally treated thousands of dying victims of the soul-rot plague. He strictly barred steam machinery from Tapovana to keep the local Aether pure for natural healing.
- **Artifact:** The *Kashtha-Danda* (Petrified Iron-Wood Staff) that purifies contaminated soil upon contact.

#### 7. Aruni of the Waters (जल-साधक आरुणी) — First Disciple & Medical Master
- **Role:** Senior Physician of the Tapovana Healing Pavilions.
- **Personality:** Humble, self-sacrificing, obsessed with plant physiology and meridian alignment.
- **Relation:** Dhaumya's chosen successor; manages emergency herbal relief missions to outer agricultural towns during epidemics.

## 2. Asura Sub-Realm Key Personnel

### 2.1 The Vajra Kingdom (Vajra-Nagara)

#### 8. Grand Architect Mayasura (महा-शिल्पी मयासुर) — Forge-Monarch of Vajra-Nagara
- **Role:** Hereditary Forge-Monarch; supreme technological authority of the Asura Realm.
- **Personality:** Visionary engineer, demanding, impatient with bureaucratic whining; lives inside his brass drafting studio.
- **Backstory:** Built the original mechanical ventilation grids that allow Asura cities to survive over active volcanic magma vents. Pioneered the fusion of runic talisman matrices with high-pressure steam boilers.
- **Equipment:** The *Vishva-Mapaka* (Mechanical Slide-Rule & Compass) and a steam-powered pneumatic drafting quill.

#### 9. Apprentice Kshema (शिल्पी क्षेम) — Master of Mechanical Prosthetics
- **Role:** Chief Artisan of the Vajra Prosthetic Guild.
- **Personality:** Quiet, intensely focused, missing his left forearm from a blast-furnace accident (replaced with a multi-jointed articulated brass arm of his own invention).
- **Relation to Mayasura:** Mayasura's most brilliant young apprentice; constructs specialized prosthetic chassis for wounded miners and veterans.

#### 10. General Prahasa (सेनापती प्रहस) — Commander of the Iron Rail Grenadiers
- **Role:** Military protector of the volcanic foundry borders.
- **Personality:** Boisterous, laughs in the face of volcanic ash and shrapnel, unshakeable physical constitution.
- **Equipment:** Four-barrel heavy volley-gun and a breastplate forged from hardened basalt-slag.

---

### 2.2 The Deep Basin Clans (Shaila-Gadha)

#### 11. Ilvala (इल्वल) & Vatapi (वातापि) — The Fraternal Mine Oligarchs
- **Roles:** Joint High Chieftains of the Seven Mining Clans.
  - *Ilvala:* The calculating contract master; negotiates mineral concessions and crystal exports.
  - *Vatapi:* The biological titan; leads deep-bore sapper units through unstable subterranean cave-ins.
- **Backstory:** Born into the lowest strata of slag-sorters, they seized control of the Shaila-Gadha mines during a corporate collapse in early Era 4. Fiercely protective of their mining workforce, they refuse to let Central oligarchs buy up mineral rights.
- **Weapons:** Enormous steam-powered pneumatic rock-breakers and blasting crystal det-caps.

#### 12. Foreman Khani-Nayaka (खाण-नायक) — Chief Pit Overseer
- **Role:** Operational director of the two-mile-deep obsidian shafts.
- **Personality:** Gruff, soot-covered, carries a brass speaking-trumpet and a safety canary in an Aether-gauge cage.
- **Relation:** Reports directly to Vatapi; maintains the safety air conduits that prevent gas explosions.

## 3. Deva Sub-Realm Key Personnel

### 3.1 Amaravati Dominion

#### 13. High Celestial Sovereign Deva-Raja Somaka (देव-राज सोमका)
- **Role:** Paramount Sovereign of the Solar Houses (Aditya-Vansh).
- **Personality:** Aloof, regal, radiant, obsessed with cosmic symmetry and acoustic purity.
- **Backstory:** Inherited the leadership of the cloud-plateaus during the peace accords following the Great Sundering. He views technology as a noisy, barbaric crutch used by lesser beings to mimic what Devas achieve through spiritual purity (*Rta*).
- **Weapon / Astra:** The *Surya-Kanti* (Solar Ray Diadem) that blinds hostile intruders attempting spatial intrusions.

#### 14. Celestial Marshal Jayanta (सेनापती जयंत) — Commander of the Golden Auras
- **Role:** Guardian of the celestial perimeter barriers (*Meru-Skanda*).
- **Personality:** Chivalrous, strict observer of classical duel etiquette, intolerant of Asuric crude manners.
- **Abilities:** Harnesses *Celestial Spatial Resonance* to walk upon thin air without wings; projects blinding light-lances from his consecrated spear.

#### 15. High Priestess Rohini (मुख्य पुरोहिता रोहिणी) — Keeper of the Harmonic Chimes
- **Role:** Supreme ritualist of the Amaravati Celestial Temple.
- **Personality:** Meditative, blindfolded to heighten her acoustic senses; can detect microscopic micro-spatial ripples across the void.
- **Relation:** Spiritual advisor to King Somaka; maintains the resonance bells that alert the Devas whenever an anomalous drift occurs in the Kshira Ocean.

---

### 3.2 The Silver Cloud Sanctuaries (Gandharva-Puri)

#### 16. Master Weaver Chitraratha (शिल्पकार चित्ररथ) — Lord of the Aether-Looms
- **Role:** Chief of the Celestial Silk Artisans.
- **Personality:** Flamboyant, perfectionist, regards silk-weaving as the highest form of mathematics.
- **Backstory:** Designs the *Aether-Silk* robes worn by ambassadors in The Central Sub-Realm. His garments absorb up to 40% of elemental friction.

## 4. Beast Sub-Realm Sovereign Titans

### 4.1 The Great Canopy & Primeval Wilderness

#### 17. The Armored Titan Krodha (शिखर-भल्लूक 'क्रोध') — King of the Forest Floor
- **Role:** Apex Sovereign of the *Vajra-Bhalluka* (Four-Horned Armored Bears).
- **Nature:** 18 feet tall; weighs over four tons; possesses natural calcified bone armor that deflects rifle bullets like pebbles.
- **Intelligence:** Highly intelligent ancient spiritual titan; communicates through low-frequency subterranean earth-tremors (*Mridangam Vibrations*). Protects the ancient seed-trees from outside logging incursions.

#### 18. Roc Monarch Suparna-Raja (गरुड-सम्राट सुपर्ण-राज) — Sovereign of the High Needles
- **Role:** Alpha Sovereign of the Sky-Cleaving Needle Peaks (*Suparna-Giri*).
- **Nature:** Wingspan exceeding 120 feet; golden feathers sharp as tempered rapiers.
- **Combat Ability:** Flaps wings to create high-velocity spatial razor-winds capable of cleaving granite cliffs. Guards the upper spatial boundaries of the Beast Realm against rogue interdimensional travelers.

#### 19. Void-Panther Matriarch Chaya-Netri (छाया-नेत्री) — Queen of the Shadow Basin
- **Role:** Eldest Matriarch of the *Shunya-Vyaghra*.
- **Nature:** Sleek obsidian coat; completely soundless movement.
- **Innate Ability:** Possesses innate **Spatial Slip**—can submerge her body directly into the grain of shadows and reappear thirty yards away in a microsecond.
