## 0. Character Philosophy: Grounded, Grey & Human

In Universe B, **no character is purely a cartoon villain or an infallible saint**. Every figure is shaped by:
1. **Physical Friction & Scars:** Real biological consequences—prosthetics, chronic aether-burn, nerve damage, sensory degradation.
2. **Psychological Trauma & Obsessions:** Grief from the Great Sundering, survivor's guilt, administrative burnout, fear of cosmic obsolescence.
3. **Moral Divergence (The Grey Spectrum):**
   - *Pragmatic Protectors* who commit atrocities to feed their borders.
   - *Weary Idealists* whose rigid adherence to law destroys the people they love.
   - *Self-Serving Capitalists* who build hospitals and railways purely out of pride.
   - *Monstrous Outlaws* with touching, unbreakable personal loyalties.

### 1. King Parikshit II (महाराज परीक्षित द्वितीय) — The Melancholic Reformer
- **Faction & Role:** Sovereign of the Kuru-Panchala Confederation (Hastinapura).
- **Archetype / Literary Inspiration:** Parikshit (The doomed king awaiting destiny) + King James I / Meiji Emperor (Modernizing a feudal realm under technological shock).
- **Moral Alignment:** **Lawful Neutral (Heavy Grey).** Desperately desires peace and prosperity for common humans, but will coldly order the secret hanging of corrupt guild-masters or sanction labor sweatshops in rail factories if it means humans stay armed against Asuras.

```
[ PHYSICAL DOSSIER ]
- Age: 52 | Height: 5'11" | Build: Once athletic, now stooped with chronic desk fatigue.
- Physical Traits: Silver-streaked dark hair, tired brown eyes with dark rings.
- Scars & Trauma: Severe shrapnel burn across his left collarbone and shoulder from an Asura mortar shell during the border skirmishes of his youth; aches violently during monsoon rains.
- Sensory / Health: Suffers from chronic insomnia and migraine headaches; dependent on bitter herbal decoctions brewed by Tapovana physicians.
```

- **Psychological Profile:**
  - *Core Driving Desire:* To prove that fragile, non-powered mortal humanity can stand on equal footing with Devas and Asuras through steam, law, and education.
  - *Deepest Fear:* That human firearms and railways are merely an artificial illusion, and that one day an Awakened Titan or Deva Lord will wipe out Hastinapura in an afternoon.
  - *Habit:* Twirls his gold signet ring incessantly when listening to parliamentary debates.
- **Powers, Skills & Equipment:**
  - *Cultivation Tier:* Tier 1 (Nadi-Shuddhi Novice) — Only possesses basic breath-control; cannot cast combat magic.
  - *Statecraft & Law:* Master of legal diplomacy, economic mobilization, and logistics.
  - *Equipment:* The *Raja-Mudra* (Imperial Gold Seal) and an ivory-handled .44 caliber service revolver with inscribed rifling.
- **History & Turning Point:**
  - Witnessed an entire human infantry regiment slaughtered by a single Asura brute with a spiked basalt hammer when he was twenty. That horrific massacre broke his faith in traditional swordsmanship and spurred his obsessive drive to import machine guns and build the rail grid.
- **Relationships & Relatives:**
  - *Maha-Mantri Vidura-Das:* His father figure and ethical compass; the only person who can scold him.
  - *Senapati Dhristadyumna:* Mutual respect tinged with friction; Parikshit fears Dhristadyumna’s military extremism.
  - *High Admiral Suhotra:* Distrusts Suhotra’s merchant greed, but relies on his maritime loans to balance the state treasury.
