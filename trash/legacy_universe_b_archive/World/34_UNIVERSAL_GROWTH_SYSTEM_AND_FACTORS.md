## 0. Fundamental Philosophy of Growth

In Universe B, **power is never a free hereditary gift**. Regardless of whether one is born Deva, Asura, Human, or Beast:
1. **The 90%+ Baseline:** Over 90% of every species remain non-combatant, non-magical commoners throughout their lives.
2. **Growth Requires Resistance:** Power cannot be accumulated purely through passive meditation in a comfortable room. Growth demands **Kruti (demonstrated action, trial by fire, and real-world friction)**.
3. **The Voice of the World:** Ananta (System Core) never gifts cheat skills. It merely observes and acknowledges genuine soul-milestones through a personal mental chime (**Sound of the World / अंतर्नाद**).

## 1. Species-Specific Growth Engines

Each living species possesses an entirely different biological and spiritual mechanism for cultivating strength:

```
[ SPECIES ] ────────► [ GROWTH ENGINE ] ────────► [ ADVANCEMENT METHOD ]
Humans                Nadi-Shuddhi & Study         Chakra Unlocking, Talismans, Aether Alchemies
Asuras                Stress & Smelting            Physical Trauma, Heat Exposure, Bloodline Hardening
Devas                 Harmonic Resonance           Purity of Mind, Celestial Attunement, Astra Focus
Beasts                Metamorphosis & Predation    Essence Consumption, Territory Clashes, Molting
```

### 1. Humans (Manushya): The Scholastic & Yogic Path
* **Mechanics:** Humans have fragile mortal vessels. They must systematically cleanse their 72,000 subtle energy channels (*Nadis*) through pranayama, yogic postures, medicinal herbs, and runic talisman arrays.
* **Bottlenecks:** 98% fail to progress past physical conditioning. Only 1–2% unlock internal energy reserves to become **Awoken Practicers**.
* **Advantage:** Humans possess the highest cognitive adaptability; they can master complex talisman inscriptions, spatial arrays, and engineering blueprints faster than any other race.

### 2. Asuras: The Crucible of Pain & Smelting
* **Mechanics:** Asuras do not grow by reading scrolls or meditating in quiet forests. Their growth is triggered by **extreme physical stress, bone fractures, searing furnace heat, and intense combat trauma**.
* **Biological Hardening:** When an Asura endures severe physical damage and survives, their muscular density and chitinous skin calcify into natural armor. Their bloodline awakens chthonic void magic directly proportional to the trials they overcome.
* **Aesthetic:** "Forged like high-pressure steel."

### 3. Devas: Celestial Resonance & Harmonic Purity
* **Mechanics:** Devas cultivate by aligning their internal spirit with higher cosmic frequencies (*Rta*). 
* **Harmonic Cultivation:** Rather than absorbing rough elemental energy, they filter ambient Aether into pure celestial light. Their advancement enhances their personal aura, spatial perception, and mastery over ancestral Astra projections.
* **Bottleneck:** Corruption or emotional turmoil causes severe degradation of their celestial frequencies (*Patana* / Spiritual Fall).

### 4. Beasts: Essence Consumption & Metamorphosis (Molting)
* **Mechanics:** Beasts grow through pure natural predation, consuming high-density Aether-crystals, ancient celestial flora, and the cores of fallen rivals.
* **Metamorphosis:** Upon reaching energy saturation, an Apex Beast enters a hibernation state (Molting) where their physical chassis transforms—growing reinforced horns, thicker bone plating, or innate spatial abilities.

## 2. The Breakthrough Architecture: Kruti & The Sound of the World

### 1. Kruti (Consequential Action)
- True tier ascension requires a life-or-death crisis, a profound moral realization, or an unprecedented feat of engineering/combat (*Kruti*).
- Safe, simulated training can build foundation, but will **never trigger a qualitative tier breakthrough**.

### 2. The Sound of the World (कर्मसंक्रमण घंटा)
- When a practitioner breaks through an insurmountable personal limitation, Ananta's automatic archive registers the soul's structural expansion.
- **Audio Rule:** A clear, pure brass chime resonates **exclusively within that individual's private consciousness**. No external trumpets, no system windows floating in public space, no public notifications.

## 3. Failure Risks & Cosmic Bottlenecks

Power acquisition in Universe B carries severe, irreversible physiological consequences:

### 1. Aether-Toxicity (क्रिस्टल-विषबाधा)
* **Cause:** Ingesting or absorbing unrefined, high-concentration Aether-Crystals to force quick breakthroughs.
* **Consequence:** The crystalline Aether solidifies inside the bloodstream, turning muscles and organs into brittle stone or mutating the practitioner into a mindless Aether-ghoul.

### 2. Subtle Body Rupture (नाडी-भंग)
* **Cause:** Attempting to cast an advanced Astra or perform Interdimensional Magic without the prerequisite spiritual density.
* **Consequence:** The subtle channels tear open under spatial shear, causing permanent loss of energy circulation and leaving the practitioner crippled for life.
