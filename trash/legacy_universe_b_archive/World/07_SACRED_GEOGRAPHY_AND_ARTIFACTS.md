### 1.1 Mount Meru (The Golden Pivot of Reality)
- The physical and metaphysical central spire of the universe.
- All planetary spheres, stars (*Śiśumāra-cakra*), sun, and moon revolve around Mount Meru.
- Deep underground roots penetrate into the Pātāla layers, anchoring the tectonic and spiritual balance of the terrestrial plane.

### 1.2 The Seven Concentric Island-Continents (Sapta-Dvīpas)
The physical plane is surrounded by seven alternating concentric oceans of milk, ghee, sugarcane juice, wine, freshwater, and saltwater:
1. **Jambūdvīpa (The Central Continent):**
   - The heart of the earthly plane, shaped like an open lotus leaf.
   - Divided into 9 realms (*Varṣas*). The southernmost realm is **Bhārata-varṣa**—the designated land of sacred works (*Karma-Kṣetra*), where salvation and downfall are won.

### 1.3 Mystical Mountains & Landmarks
- **Mount Mandara:** The ultra-dense celestial mountain uprooted by Devas and Asuras to serve as the churning spindle (*Manthāna-Daṇḍa*) during the churning of the Ocean of Milk.
- **Mount Gandhamādana:** The aromatic Himalayan ridge where Sage Nara-Nārāyaṇa and immortal Chiranjīvīs dwell; home of the glowing medicinal herbs (*Mṛtasañjīvanī*).
- **Mount Maināka:** The golden winged mountain that hid deep beneath the southern ocean to escape Indra's wing-clipping lightning bolts; offered a resting haven to Hanumān.
- **Kṣīra Sāgara (The Ocean of Milk):**
  - The primordial ocean within the universe where Lord Viṣṇu reclines on Ādi-Śeṣa in deep contemplative slumber (*Yoga-Nidrā*). Source of all primordial treasures.

### 1.4 The Tri-Pathagā Gaṅgā (The Three-Stream River)
The sacred river Gaṅgā descends from the higher realms through the locks of Lord Śiva, dividing into three distinct dimensional flows:
1. **Mandākinī:** The celestial river flowing through the gardens of Svarga.
2. **Bhāgīrathī / Gaṅgā:** The terrestrial river purifying mortal human karmas on Earth.
3. **Bhogavatī:** The subterranean river irrigating the palaces of Nāgaloka in Pātāla.

### 2.2 Flora, Fauna & Abundance Relics

- **Kalpavṛkṣa (The Wish-Fulfilling Tree):**
  - Dwells in Indra's celestial grove (*Nandana Kānaṇa*). Instantly manifests any food, wine, garment, or jewel imagined beneath its canopy.
- **Pārijāta Tree:**
  - The divine night-flowering tree with golden-hued bark and celestial fragrance. Flowers never fade. Brought down from Svarga to Dvārakā by Lord Kṛṣṇa to end a domestic dispute between Satyabhāmā and Rukmiṇī.
- **Kāmadhenu (Surabhi - The Mother of Cows):**
  - Emerged from the churned ocean. Grants endless rivers of divine milk, ghee, and can summon an entire invulnerable celestial army from her pores to protect her master (as seen when King Kārtavīryārjuna attempted to steal her from Sage Jamadagni).
- **Akṣaya Pātra (The Inexhaustible Vessel):**
  - Copper bowl gifted by Sūrya to Yudhiṣṭhira. Provides limitless, steaming-hot gourmet food to an infinite number of guests every day, until the matriarch of the household (*Draupadī*) washes and turns the pot upside down for the night.
- **Mṛtasañjīvanī:**
  - Bioluminescent herb growing on Himalayan slopes. Reverses fatal trauma, restores severed Prāṇa, and resuscitates fallen warriors before the silver cord to the subtle body fully detaches.

### 2.3 Divine Regalia & Personal Armaments

- **Karṇa’s Sahaja Kavaca & Kuṇḍala:**
  - Born fused to Karṇa’s chest and ears from the essence of the Sun God.
  - *Effect:* Total absolute physical and magical invulnerability. While attached, no weapon—not even the Brahmāstra or the Vajra—could scratch Karṇa’s mortal flesh.
- **Gāṇḍīva:**
  - The peerless bow forged by Lord Brahmā, held by Varuṇa, and gifted to Arjuna during the burning of Khāṇḍava forest.
  - Equipped with 108 celestial bowstrings that emit sonic thunderclaps, disorienting opposing archers. Cannot be strung or lifted by an ordinary human.
- **Akṣaya Tūṇīra (Infinite Quivers):**
  - Two twin quivers strapped to the warrior's back that continuously regenerate arrows of all types; never runs dry during sustained battle.
- **Pāñcajanya Śaṅkha:**
  - The massive sacred conch blown by Lord Kṛṣṇa, carved from the shell of the demon Pañcajana.
  - The sound wave (*Nāda*) directly shatters enemy morale, induces sudden cardiac arrest in adharmic troops, and clears demonic miasmas from the atmosphere.
