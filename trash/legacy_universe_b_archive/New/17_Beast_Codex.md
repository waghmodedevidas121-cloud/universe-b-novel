## SECTION 1: THE 4-BRACKET BEAST POPULATION PYRAMID (EVALUATED LIKE HUMAN SHARD)

Just like human practitioners, the beast population across Universe B (both the 40–50% living in the Beast Shard and the 40–60% scattered across the Human, Asura, Central, and Setu realms) is naturally stratified into Four Power Brackets:

### 1. Bracket 1: 0% to 60% of All Beasts — "Utility, Draft & Ember-Fauna"
*(Stage 0 / Juvenile / Domesticated & Minor Wild Beasts)*
- **Power Level:** Ember-Tier (Non-Combat or Basic 1-Move Utility).
- **Role in the World:** The backbone of daily living, agriculture, and civilian transport!
  * **Land Transport:** Draft-Bulls, Caravan-Horses, Pack-Yaks, and Silt-Boars that pull Beast-Carts across the inter-shard roads and markets.
  * **Water Transport:** Domesticated River-Turtles and Mild Makaras that tow river-barges through the Ganga and Yamuna basins.
  * **Everyday Companions:** Messenger Falcons, Lantern-Eels (used as living cold-flame lamps by deep trench divers), Cinder-Salamanders (kept in royal and guild smithy hearths to eat iron slag and maintain forge fires), and Moss-Hares (used by Tapovana herbalists to sniff out toxic miasma and Devil poisons).
- **Evolution Potential:** Most remain in Bracket 1 for their natural lifespans (60–80 yrs). However, if a juvenile possesses a mutated core or forms an early bond with a diligent cultivator, it can break through into Bracket 2!

### 2. Bracket 2: 60% to 80% of All Beasts (20%) — "Stage-1 Feral / D-Rank Beasts"
*(Active Single-Element Wild Predators & Rookie Adventurer Partners)*
- **Power Level:** D-Rank Combat Capable (Single Elemental Type).
- **Role in the World:**
  * **In the Wild:** Territorial pack-hunters prowling the fringes of the Khandava Forest, Asura volcanic foothills, and Setu perimeter trenches (the primary targets of D-Rank Adventurer Guild bounties).
  * **As Partners:** Starter beasts bonded by rookie D-Rank Adventurers and 12-year-old Gurukul initiates (e.g., Dusk Scrapper, Cinder Falcon, Rime Viper).
- **Combat Capability:** Possess 2 to 4 basic elemental or physical arts (e.g., *Iron Snap, Spark Talon, Frost Needle*) and hides tougher than folded steel.

### 3. Bracket 3: 80% to 90% of All Beasts (10%) — "Stage-2 Bonded / C & B-Rank"
*(First Molt Completed | Dual-Elemental Armored Beasts)*
- **Power Level:** C-Rank to B-Rank (Equivalent to Reincarnated Veterans and Guild Officers).
- **How They Reach This Bracket:**
  * **In the Wild (Slow Path):** Requires 50 to 100 years of territorial survival in the primeval canopy of the Beast Shard.
  * **Via Soul-Bond (Fast Path):** Requires 6 to 24 months of absorbing purified Prana through a bonded Human or Asura partner's 72,000 meridians!
- **Biological Transformation (The First Molt — Nirmoka):** Sheds its juvenile skin in a bloody molt to grow runic bone-plates or metallic scales; unlocks a **Second Elemental Type (Dual-Typing)** and 4 advanced tactical moves.

### 4. Bracket 4: 90% to 100% (Top 10%) — "Stage-3 Apex Titans & Sovereigns"
*(Second Molt Completed | A-Rank, S-Rank & Buffer-Zone Mythical Vahanas)*
- **Sub-Tier 4A (90%–99% | A-Rank & S-Rank Apex Beasts):**
  * Deva-Scale Mythical Titans (20 to 60 feet tall or wingspan).
  * Automatically project a **Passive Field Dominion** (altering weather, gravity, or light within 50 paces) upon entering an arena or battlefield!
  * Capable of **Stage-4 Weapon-Fusion (Astra-Sāyujya)** — temporarily transmuting physical mass into living elemental plasma to fuse directly into their master's weapon or armor!
- **Sub-Tier 4B (Top 0.1% | Sovereign Rulers & Conscious Beast-Folk):**
  * Possess full human-or-higher intelligence, resonant Sanskrit speech, telepathic wind-calls, and sovereign city-states (e.g., Titan Ursus, Empress Jormun, Empress Valkyrie, and the conscious Vanara, Naga, and Garuda clans).
  * Governed by **The Great Beast Accord**; cannot be "captured" or treated as livestock—they are sovereign monarchs and citizens under cosmic law.

## SECTION 2: NATURAL PHYSICAL SOUL-BOND LAW (ZERO POKEBALL / ZERO TATTOO STORAGE!)

```text
================================================================================
PRANA-BANDHANA (THE MERIDIAN SOUL-BOND) — 100% REAL PHYSICAL PRESENCE
================================================================================
```

### 1. 100% Real Physical Presence (Zero Pokéball / Zero Tattoo Storage)
Beasts NEVER dissolve into mechanical balls, rings, capsules, or body tattoos! A bonded beast is a permanent, living, breathing physical partner in the mortal world:
- **Small/Medium Stage-1 Beasts:** Walk alongside the cultivator, perch upon their armored shoulder, coil around their torso, or sleep inside the frontier camp tent.
- **Large Stage-2 & Stage-3 Beasts:** Serve as direct war-mounts, pull reinforced iron Beast-Carts and armored chariots, roost on specialized citadel aeries, or patrol the airspace directly above their partner!

### 2. The Meridian Refinery Law (Why Wild Beasts Bond With Humans/Asuras)
While the 12 Sovereign Beast Clans in the Beast Shard are born sapient and evolve through royal bloodlines, 40–60% of wild Aether-Beasts suffer from raw, mineral-clogged ambient Prana.
- By forming a mutual **Nādi-Bandhana (Soul-Bond)** with a Human or Asura, the cultivator's 72,000 meridians act as a **Living Refinery**, filtering out toxic heat and delivering pure refined Prana to the beast's core.
- This symbiotic filtration allows wild beasts to undergo **The Molt (Nirmoka)** in months rather than centuries!
- **The Deva Taboo (0% Deva Bonds):** The Devas of Amaravati view sharing blood or meridians with beasts as "unclean biological pollution." Consequently, Devas have zero bonded beasts and are strictly banned from competing in the Beast divisions of the Proving Games.

### 3. The Three Authentic Ways to Form a Soul-Bond
1. **Method 1 (Trial of Martial Respect):** Defeating a wild Stage-1 or Stage-2 beast in honorable combat without crippling it, then offering a drop of Prana-infused blood upon its brow. If the beast respects the warrior's spirit, it accepts the bond.
2. **Method 2 (Sanctum Egg / Pup Imprint):** Receiving a dormant Beast-Egg or juvenile at the Hastinapur Awakening Sanctums, Drona-Vana Academy, or Morvath's Hollow Grove, raising it from birth with one's own Pranic warmth.
3. **Method 3 (Rescue & Shared Crisis):** Rescuing a wounded beast from a Cursed Forest or rogue Devil; the shared near-death *Crisis Law* naturally fuses their meridians in a flash of survival resonance.

### 4. Evolution Triggers & Branching Catalysts
A beast evolves from Stage 1 -> Stage 2 -> Stage 3 through three concrete biological requirements:
1. **Meridian Synchronization & Battle XP:** Reaching 100% resonance through real life-and-death combat.
2. **Catalyst Ingestion (Branching Paths):** Feeding the beast a specific **Elemental Beast-Core or Sacred Herb** during its Molt (e.g., feeding a Dusk-Hound a *Meteoric Iron Core* produces an Ironclad Hound; feeding it an *Abyssal Shadow-Lotus* branches it toward an Umbral Stalker).
3. **The Crisis Molt (Nirmoka Breakthrough):** When pushed beyond physical limits in defense of its partner, the beast breaks its outer shell, triggering an instantaneous evolutionary surge!

## SECTION 4: THE 12 SOVEREIGN SAPIENT CLANS OF THE BEAST SUB-REALM
*(Equal in Intelligence, Royalty & Civilization to Humans, Asuras & Devas — Rooted in the Puranic Lineages of Sage Kashyapa's Wives: Vinata, Kadru, Krodhavasha)*

### 01. The Suparna / Garuda Clan (Sovereigns of the 20,000-ft Sky Spires)
- **Hindu Mythic Root:** Lineage of Vinata & Lord Garuda.
- **Rulers:** Empress Valkyrie (Vinata-Scion) & War-Prince Aquila (Sampati-Scion).
- **Civilization & Culture:** Proud aerial warrior-monarchs who inhabit open granite palaces carved into 20,000-foot mountain needles. They speak in resonant archaic Sanskrit and telepathic wind-calls; sworn enemies of corrupt Nagas and Deva border-trespassers.
- **Visual Anatomy (ते नेमके कसे दिसतात):**
  * **Size & Weight:** 40 to 80 feet wingspan; 3 to 8 tons.
  * **Feathers & Body:** Broad eagle-humanoid chest plated in natural metallic bronze-and-gold feathers that ring like temple bells in high winds and deflect steel arrows.
  * **Head & Eyes:** Curved, hooked beak of black Damascus-patterned horn; four-ringed blazing amber eyes capable of spotting movement 10 miles distant; erectile golden crest-plumes flaring with solar heat when angered.
  * **Talons & Arsenal:** Four-foot curved obsidian talons capable of crushing an iron war-wagon in a single grasp.

### 02. The Maha-Naga / Uraga Clan (Sovereigns of the Sunken Waterways)
- **Hindu Mythic Root:** Lineage of Kadru, Vasuki, & Takshaka (Bhogavati Kingdom).
- **Rulers:** Empress Jormun (Kadru-Line) & Rebel Warlord Takshak.
- **Civilization & Culture:** Ancient, scholarly, and deadly subterranean royalty dwelling in bioluminescent crystal-and-root palaces beneath the Twilight Marsh. Masters of *Rasāyana* (venom and elixir alchemy), water-wards, and sensory illusions.
- **Visual Anatomy (ते नेमके कसे दिसतात):**
  * **Two Forms:** High-blood Nagas shift between their **True Leviathan Form** (80 to 180 feet long) and a **Half-Human Sovereign Form** (pale jade human upper torso with slit golden eyes, transitioning below the waist into a 30-foot coiled serpent body).
  * **Scales & Hood:** Interlocking emerald-jade or abyssal-black scales denser than folded plate armor; flaring 12-foot cobra hood marked with glowing silver Vedic mandalas across the back.
  * **The Nagamani Crown:** Sovereign elders grow a natural, luminescent *Nāgamani* gemstone embedded in the forehead skull, casting clear light through pitch-black subterranean waterways.

### 03. The Vanara Clan (Warrior-Kings of the Ironwood Canopy — Kishkindha Line)
- **Hindu Mythic Root:** Lineage of Bali, Sugriva, Angada, & Nala-Nila.
- **Civilization & Culture:** Master engineers, bridge-builders, and peerless hand-to-hand martial artists inhabiting suspended tree-cities woven between 1,500-foot ironwood giants. They constructed the original cyclopean foundations of the Great Setu!
- **Visual Anatomy (ते नेमके कसे दिसतात):**
  * **Size & Build:** 7 to 14 feet tall; heavily muscled ape-warriors walking upright in ornate bronze-and-leather battle kilts.
  * **Fur & Mane:** Thick, coarse fur of burnished copper, silver-white, or deep crimson; faces framed by lion-like manes and side-whiskers; prehensile tail strong enough to shatter a wyvern's neck.
  * **Eyes & Armament:** Molten-gold eyes; wield 500-lb petrified ironwood pillars or bronze war-maces (*Gadā*) carved with atmospheric runes.

### 04. The Riksha / Jambavan Clan (Ancient Ursine Sage-Titans)
- **Hindu Mythic Root:** Lineage of King Jambavan (born from Brahma's primordial yawn).
- **Ruler:** Titan Ursus ("Old Four-Horns").
- **Civilization & Culture:** Solitary, long-lived philosopher-warriors and guardians of the primeval Heartwood Sanctum. Few in number, but revered across all shards for unbroken defense and ancient historical memory.
- **Visual Anatomy (ते नेमके कसे दिसतात):**
  * **Size & Weight:** 15 to 25 feet tall on hind legs; 10 to 40 tons.
  * **Hide & Horns:** Shaggy midnight-black fur matted with living moss and basalt rock-plates fused over the spine and shoulders; **four massive curved black-stone horns** sweeping backward from the brow like a sovereign crown.
