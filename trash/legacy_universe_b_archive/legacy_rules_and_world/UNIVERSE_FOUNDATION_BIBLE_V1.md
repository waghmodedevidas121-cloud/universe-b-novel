======================================================================
0. DOCUMENT CONTROL AND CANON POLICY
======================================================================

This document organizes the currently established concepts for the
fictional universe. It is intended to reduce contradictions and keep
future worldbuilding systematic.

Status labels: [CANON] Explicitly established or corrected by the
author. [PROPOSAL] Suggested structure that is not canon until approved.
[OPEN] A question or mechanic that remains undecided. [EXCLUDED] A
concept that must not be applied in the specified context.

Do not silently convert a proposal or open question into canon. When new
information is approved, update the relevant section rather than
creating a conflicting rule elsewhere.

Core creative principles: - Genre: Mystery, Isekai, Fantasy, and
cosmic-scale storytelling. - The story has multiple protagonists whose
storylines gradually connect. - The mystery should expand in stages,
from local events to systemic contradictions and then to cosmic and
supreme frontiers. - Hindu mythology and shastric concepts may inspire
the fictional world, but the story is an independent fictional
adaptation, not a claim about canonical scripture. - Do not introduce a
final villain, hidden mechanism, or rule merely to fill a gap.
Unresolved matters should remain explicitly open until developed.

TWO-UNIVERSE MODEL
======================================================================

1.1 UNIVERSE A — TRADITIONAL HINDU MYTHOLOGY UNIVERSE

Status: [CANON] [EXCLUDED from the custom migration system]

-   Traditional Hindu deities, sacred timelines, cosmic arrangements,
    and the Universe’s own Soul Administration remain unchanged.
-   This universe’s religious and mythological continuity must not be
    rewritten to accommodate the novel’s custom systems.
-   The custom Soul Migration System described in this document does not
    operate in Universe A.
-   Universe A may serve as a background or inspiration reference, but
    its internal administration must not be treated as a branch of
    Universe B’s custom system.
-   Do not imply that the novel’s Ananta, custom migration rules, or
    custom administrative workflow changes or governs Universe A.

1.2 UNIVERSE B — THE NOVEL’S DIVERGENT UNIVERSE

Status: [CANON]

-   Universe B is an independent fictional universe within a broader
    multiverse.
-   It has its own cosmic geography, Power System, Soul Administration,
    and mysteries.
-   The custom Soul Migration System applies only within the framework
    established for Universe B.
-   Events in Universe B may be relevant to certain souls’ arrival, but
    the exact mechanics remain under development.
-   References to concepts from Hindu mythology are adaptations for this
    fictional setting, not replacements for Universe A’s traditional
    rules.

1.3 BOUNDARY RULE

Universe A and Universe B must be documented separately. A rule
established for Universe B must not automatically be applied to Universe
A. Similar names or inspirations do not mean that the two universes
share identical administration or mechanics.

2. SUPREME TRINITY
======================================================================

Status: [CANON]

BRAHMA — Supreme Creator - Associated with supreme creation.

VISHNU — Supreme Preserver - Associated with supreme preservation.

SHIVA / MAHESH — Supreme Destroyer - Associated primarily with

must personally resolve every conflict in the story. - The reason they
do not directly intervene in every major event has not yet been
finalized.

ZERO-AVATAR PROTOCOL: - Direct Rama, Krishna, and Kalki avatars will not
enter Universe B. - The exact in-world explanation for this restriction
remains [OPEN]. - Do not invent a definitive explanation without author
approval.

MAHAVISHNU REFERENCE: - Brahma-samhita 5.48 is an inspiration for the
concept of universes emerging from Mahavishnu’s breath. - The novel’s
use of this idea is a fictional adaptation and should not be presented
as an exact statement of the complete scriptural cosmology. - Karana
Ocean is not part of Universe B’s internal cosmic geography.

3. COSMIC GEOGRAPHY OF UNIVERSE B
======================================================================

3.1 CURRENTLY ESTABLISHED STRUCTURE

The current internal structure identifies two broad sides separated by a
major boundary:

-   Birth Side
-   Kshira Sagara — the main boundary between the two sides
-   Death Side

This is a conceptual structure, not yet a complete navigable map.

3.1 THE EVOLVING CHILD COSMOS & THE FOUR REALMS

[CANON] Universe B is in its Cosmic Childhood (बाल्य अवस्था) — created thousands of years ago by the Supreme Trinity, it is a living, actively evolving cosmos. Its operational structure is organized across four distinct tiers:
1. TIER 1 — DIVINE HIGH COUNCIL REALM (सभा-लोक): Sacred, neutral assembly realm where the periodic High Council meets. 100% combat/violence ban. Six Divine Constitutional Upadhis (Yama, Vidhata, Chitragupta, Chief Sage, Mahā-Pālaka, Prime Seeker) alongside Ananta (System Core) and Supreme Trinity (Source). 100% pure dharmic coordination, zero petty corruption.
2. TIER 2 — THE BUFFER ZONE / THE SETU (मध्यम-लोक / प्रशासकीय सेतू - Antariksha): Administrative intermediate layer staffed by elevated mortal officers from the Astral Realm. Hosts tactical, jurisdictional, and resource allocation conflicts.
3. TIER 3 — ASTRAL REALM (The Shared Living Realm): Where 60-70% of the cosmic population resides (Devas, Asuras, Humans, Animals/Beasts). Birth Equality rule applies: status depends on Karma, Tapasya, and Kruti (feats). Coexistence of modern Magitech megacities and ancient kingdoms; non-powered citizens use Yantra-tech and beast companions. No religious monopoly (cultural definition of divinity). Main mortal conflicts, trade, and wars occur here.
4. TIER 4 — CORRUPTED REALM: Habitation of Rakshasas and exiled convicts in a toxic energy zone governed by 'Might is Right'. Features Dual Civilizations (Native Corrupted Lords claiming fake godhood + Penitent Outlaws / Exiled Convicts), fluid relationships (conflicts, contraband trade, hybrid fortresses), and extreme forbidden quarantine zones holding Dark Entities under impenetrable cosmic power-system seals.
*(Boundary Realm: KSHIRA OCEAN — separating Birth Side and Death Side; serves as the transit ocean for event-triggered Route B arrivals).*

3.2 HIGH COUNCIL JOB TITLES & CONSTITUTIONAL UPADHIS
[CANON] High Council structure operates via constitutional offices (Upadhis), not hereditary claims:
- Six Divine Constitutional Offices: Yama-pada, Vidhata-pada, Chitragupta-pada, Chief Sage, Mahā-Pālaka (High Guardian), Prime Seeker.
- Three Grand Assemblies & Subordinate ranks across Buffer Zone:
  - The Assembly of Sages (ऋषी मंडळ / Sage Council / Auditor): Observes anomalies, conducts research, delivers audit reports.
  - Palak Order (पालक परिषद / Guardian): Law-keeper, border defense on Great Barrier Wall, maintains order.
  - Seeker Order (संशोधक संघ / Pioneer): Tests system limits (stress-testing), researches alternative paths, exposes loopholes.

3.3 KSHIRA SAGARA & TRANSIT OCEAN

[CANON] - Kshira Sagara is relevant as the main boundary between the
Birth Side and Death Side in Universe B.

[OPEN] - Exact geography. - Who can cross it and under what
conditions. - Which authorities govern each side. - Whether crossing
requires a process, permission, cost, or special ability.

3.3 COSMIC OCEAN TRANSIT

[CANON at concept level; mechanics OPEN] - Some souls arriving through
the event-triggered route may pass through the Cosmic Ocean. - Transit
may be associated with changes to abilities, powers, or authority. - The
exact cause, activation conditions, and source of these effects are not
finalized.

3.4 KARANA OCEAN EXCLUSION

[EXCLUDED from Universe B’s internal geography] - Karana Ocean must not
be placed as an internal boundary or region within Universe B. - The
broader Mahavishnu connection is a separate cosmological reference and
does not make Karana Ocean part of Universe B’s internal map.

4. SOUL ARRIVAL: TWO DISTINCT ROUTES
======================================================================

The following distinction is essential. Official Soul Migration and
event-triggered arrival must not be treated as the same process.

4.1 ROUTE A — OFFICIAL SOUL MIGRATION

Status: [CANON at high level; detailed workflow partly OPEN]

Purpose: - An official process through which a soul may be brought into
Universe B.

Established concepts: - Universe B’s Vidhata handles the decision or
coordination to bring a soul into Universe B. - Karma and Cosmic
Compatibility are relevant to the decision. - Migration Eligibility is a
separate major check. - Chitragupta prepares the Soul ID. - Ananta
reviews the relevant record and may approve it or suggest changes. -
After the process is approved, Universe B’s Vidhata connects the soul to
a new birth through a karmic bond and completes the process.

Working workflow: 1. The relevant soul becomes available for
consideration under the applicable origin-universe circumstances. 2.
Universe B’s Vidhata considers whether migration should proceed. 3.
Migration Eligibility and Cosmic Compatibility are assessed. 4.
Chitragupta prepares the Soul ID. 5. Ananta reviews the record, approves
it, or suggests changes. 6. Following approval, Universe B’s Vidhata
completes the connection to a new body and birth.

Important boundary: - This workflow describes the custom process for
Universe B. - It does not authorize the custom system to operate inside
Universe A. - Any references to external-universe authorities must not
be used to rewrite Universe A’s own Soul Administration.

[OPEN] - Exact sequence and responsibilities at every stage. - Whether
Ananta’s approval is always mandatory or varies by case. - Formal
rejection, appeal, exception, and pending-review procedures. - Exact
rules governing the karmic bond and the new body.

4.2 ROUTE B — COSMIC REINCARNATION / EVENT-TRIGGERED ARRIVAL

Working label only; final name not approved.

Status: [CANON at concept level; mechanics OPEN]

Established concepts: - Not every soul’s arrival must be decided through
the Official Soul Migration System. - Certain events or activities in
Universe B may be associated with a soul’s arrival. - Some such souls
may pass through the Cosmic Ocean. - During transit, the soul may gain
or undergo changes involving abilities, powers, or authority. - The
effects may vary in strength. An arrival may produce a strong effect, a
weak effect, no new power, or a change whose nature is not yet
understood. - Strong influence does not automatically mean greater
combat strength. Authority and combat power are different dimensions. -
Ananta automatically records changes that actually occur. - A soul’s
future may remain open and develop through its decisions. This does not
mean Karma is absent.

Do not assume the following until approved: - That every event-triggered
soul passes through the Cosmic Ocean. - That every soul receives a new
power. - That Ananta causes the arrival or grants powers. - That a
strong influence always produces a stronger fighter. - That open destiny
removes Karma or all consequences.

[OPEN] - What types of events can trigger arrival. - Why the Cosmic
Ocean is involved. - Where the transit effects originate. - Whether
transit awakens existing traits, creates new capabilities, changes
authority, or can do different things in different cases. - How this
route interacts with birth, Kul, Soul ID, and Atma-Bhagya.

5. SOUL ADMINISTRATION OF UNIVERSE B
======================================================================

5.1 YAMA — CHIEF ADMINISTRATOR

[CANON] - Universe B’s Yama is the chief administrator for soul-related
administration. - Yama cannot override Ananta’s approval where Ananta’s
review is required.

[OPEN] - The complete jurisdiction and division of responsibilities. -
Exact appointment, succession, and retirement procedures.

5.2 VIDHATA — MIGRATION AND BIRTH CONNECTION

[CANON] - Universe B’s Vidhata handles soul migration and the connection
of a soul to a new birth. - Karma and Cosmic Compatibility inform
migration decisions. - Vidhata completes the connection between the soul
and the new body through a karmic bond.

[OPEN] - Detailed selection criteria and exception procedures. - How
decisions are recorded and challenged. - Exact relationship between
Migration Eligibility and Cosmic Compatibility.

5.3 CHITRAGUPTA — SOUL ID PREPARATION

[CANON] - Chitragupta is Yama’s assistant. - Chitragupta personally
prepares the Soul ID. - The Soul ID may include Kul, innate powers or
skills, and other required information. - The record is sent to Ananta
for review; Ananta may approve it or suggest changes.

[OPEN] - Complete Soul ID schema and field definitions. - Which
information is mandatory, optional, hidden, or inaccessible to
characters. - How updates are handled after birth.

5.4 ANANTA — THE SYSTEM

[CANON] - Ananta is not an independent person or living official. -
Ananta is the System itself. - Ananta has no soul, consciousness,
emotions, or independent will. - Ananta follows Supreme Trinity’s
Supreme Orders. - Ananta serves as the Voice of the World and manages
the Archives. - Absolute Immunity: Soulless and immutable, no Deva, Asura,
Sage, Rakshasa, or power in Universe B can harm, corrupt, or alter Ananta;
only the Supreme Trinity holds authority over it. - Voice of the World Dual
Audio Rules:
  1. Personal Audio (अंतर्नाद / Personal Ping): When an ability/skill evolves
     or unlocks, it is heard ONLY within that individual's own mind.
  2. Cosmic Audio (वैश्विक घोषणा / Cosmic Mandate): When a cosmic law is
     amended by Supreme Trinity sanction, the announcement is heard ONLY by
     High Council title-holders (Sages, पालक, Seekers).
- Ananta has system-wide authority but cannot
independently change core rules without Supreme Trinity orders. - Ananta
reflects and records reality; it does not determine destiny or
independently cause a soul’s arrival. - Ananta automatically registers
real changes in a soul’s abilities, powers, or authority when those
changes occur. - Ananta does not independently grant powers. - As a
System rather than a living office-holder, Ananta has no job tenure or
retirement.

[OPEN] - Exact Archive access rules. - Whether every archive is
accessible to every character. - Limits, delays, errors, or special
cases in the records. - The exact scope of Ananta’s review authority
beyond the established approval process.

6. MIGRATION ELIGIBILITY VS COSMIC COMPATIBILITY
======================================================================

These are separate concepts and must not be merged.

6.1 MIGRATION ELIGIBILITY

Core question: “Is this soul eligible to be brought into Universe B
through Official Soul Migration?”

[CANON] - It is a major standard in the decision made by Universe B’s
Vidhata. - Its design should be inspired by Hindu scriptural concepts.

[OPEN] - Its final criteria and decision logic have not been approved. -
Previously suggested criteria such as Karmic Bond, Rebirth Eligibility,
Destination Eligibility, and Exception/Dharma Compatibility are
proposals only. - Do not treat those proposed criteria as canon unless
explicitly approved.

6.2 COSMIC COMPATIBILITY

Core question: “How compatible is this soul with the cosmic conditions
of Universe B?”

[CANON: FIVE COMPONENTS] 1. Conduct — actions and their consequences. 2.
Desire and Attachment — intention behind actions and the direction of
the mind. 3. State of the Three Gunas — Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas; no
single guna decides the outcome by itself. 4. Karma-results — which
results remain to be experienced and which have been completed. 5.
Liberation Question — the ordinary rebirth cycle or a distinct path
associated with liberation.

These five components belong to Cosmic Compatibility. They must not
automatically be relabeled as the final Migration Eligibility checklist.

6.3 SCRIPTURAL INSPIRATION

Potential conceptual references include: - Bhagavad Gita 14.14–15: the
gunas and post-death destinations. - Bhagavad Gita 9.21: merit, heavenly
experience, and return. - Bhagavad Gita 8.6: the significance of the
final mental state. - Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 4.4.5–6: desire, will,
action, and becoming. - Chandogya Upanishad 5.10.7: conduct and
subsequent rebirth. - Rigveda 10.14: Yama and the path of the departed.

These are potential inspirations for fictional design, not proof that
the custom administrative checklist exists verbatim in scripture. Verify
exact references before using them in an explanatory or scholarly
passage.

7. SOUL ID, KARMA, ATMA-SHUDDHI, ATMA-BHAGYA, AND DESTINY
======================================================================

7.1 ESTABLISHED PRINCIPLES

-   Past physical body, money, caste, and social or religious status do
    not automatically transfer as direct status in Universe B.
-   New-world birth and status are associated with Atma-shuddhi, Sukshma
    Karma, and Atma-Bhagya.
-   Atma-Bhagya is not equivalent to a completely prewritten future.
-   Atma-Bhagya is currently understood as Karma-influenced fortune,
    especially concerning the Kul or family into which a soul is born.
-   Chitragupta creates the Soul ID; the record can include Kul and
    innate powers or skills.
-   A destiny record may be Written, partial, or Blank, depending on the
    still-developing rules.
-   Isekai souls have been described as having a Blank Ananta page and
    not being bound by a prewritten destiny. The exact relationship
    between this idea and the event-triggered route remains open.

7.2 DISTINCTIONS TO PRESERVE

Atma-shuddhi: - Relates to soul purity.

Sukshma Karma: - Relates to subtle karmic factors associated with the
soul.

Atma-Bhagya: - Relates to Karma-influenced fortune, especially birth or
Kul under the current concept.

Soul ID: - An administrative record created by Chitragupta.

Destiny record: - The System’s available record of destiny, not
automatically identical to destiny itself.

Open destiny: - A future not fully prewritten in the available record;
it does not necessarily erase Karma or consequences.

7.3 DESTINY RECORD STATES

Written: - A destiny record is available.

Blank: - A complete prewritten future record is unavailable.

Potential changes: - Karma shifts or divine intervention may destabilize
an existing record; it may change or become blank. - The exact triggers
and rules are [OPEN].

7.4 UNRESOLVED QUESTIONS

-   Does Atma-Bhagya determine only birth/Kul, or can it also influence
    innate powers?
-   How does Atma-Bhagya interact with Soul ID?
-   Do event-triggered or Isekai souls still have their birth/Kul
    determined by Karma?
-   Is a Blank record always equivalent to open destiny, or are these
    distinct conditions?
-   What criteria determine whether a destiny record is complete,
    partial, or blank?

Do not resolve these questions by assumption.

8. POWER SYSTEM
======================================================================

8.1 DESIGN PRINCIPLES

[CANON] - The Power System balances mythological depth with
anime/fantasy combat. - There are no visible numerical levels as the
primary measure of strength. - Progress is shown through abilities,
mastery, and demonstrated feats. - Rank and Authority are not the
same. - No single stat guarantees victory. - The Supreme Trinity stands
outside the ordinary limits of this system.

8.2 FIVE FIXED PILLARS

1.  AUTHORITY Cosmic rights, lawful jurisdiction, seals, and special
    permissions.

2.  ENERGY Prana, Tejas, and energy used to perform or sustain abilities
    and combat.

3.  KARMA A cosmic probability and consequence-related principle.

4.  TAPASYA Discipline, mastery, and Atma-shuddhi-related development.

5.  BOONS Contracts, conditional benefits, and special advantages. Not
    every boon must have a weakness.

8.3 SECRET SIXTH PRINCIPLE

-   A hidden principle related to existence or the soul.
-   Its mechanics are intentionally undisclosed.
-   Do not expose its rules prematurely or assume its complete function.

8.4 POWER CATEGORIES

The current catalogue contains fourteen categories: 1. Elements 2. Space
3. Time 4. Mind / Maya 5. Soul 6. Life / Death 7. Physical abilities 8.
Weapons 9. Astras 10. Seals / Barriers 11. Transformation 12. Eye powers
13. Domains / Reality Marbles 14. Sixth Principle

This is a catalogue, not a finalized technical rulebook. Categories may
overlap, and not every being can use every category.

8.5 POWER, AUTHORITY, AND PURITY

Keep these dimensions separate: - Combat power: ability to fight,
damage, defend, or overcome an opponent. - Authority: cosmic or
administrative rights and jurisdiction. - Soul purity: a separate
spiritual dimension. - Social rank: position in a hierarchy or
institution.

For example, an Asura may possess greater raw combat energy while a Deva
holds a relevant cosmic seal. Neither fact automatically decides every
encounter.

9. DEVAS, ASURAS, AND RISHIS
======================================================================

9.1 DEVAS

-   Devas are associated with a celestial bureaucracy or federation.
-   Indra’s exact position and role are still being developed.
-   Their institutions, political structure, and internal disagreements
    remain open.

9.2 ASURAS

-   Asuras are not automatically evil and are not natural enemies of
    Devas by default.
-   Their conflicts may be political, personal, ideological, or
    systemic.
-   Possible faction concepts previously suggested:
    -   Sovereign — statecraft or governance-oriented.
    -   Scholar — knowledge-oriented.
    -   Warrior — strength or honour-oriented.
    -   Independent — wanderers or unaffiliated individuals.
-   These are proposals, not final canon. They may represent ideologies
    or factions rather than biological categories.

9.3 RISHIS

-   Rishis are associated with the highest forms of soul purity in the
    current concept.
-   Their precise social authority, combat rank, and jurisdiction are
    not yet defined.
-   Do not assume that spiritual purity automatically grants authority
    over every other being.

9.4 HIERARCHY

-   The hierarchy is soul-based in the current concept.
-   Devas may be more pure on average and Asuras may occupy lower
    average positions, but individual exceptions are possible.
-   Group averages do not determine the nature, morality, power, or rank
    of every individual.
-   Soul purity, social hierarchy, and combat strength must remain
    separate dimensions.

10. MYSTERY ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================

The story’s mystery is planned to unfold through four broad stages:

STAGE 1 — LOCAL HORIZON - Characters encounter local events, unanswered
questions, and apparent irregularities.

STAGE 2 — SYSTEM CONTRADICTIONS - Evidence suggests that known rules or
accepted explanations do not fully align.

STAGE 3 — COSMIC EXPANSION - The story reveals a broader cosmic context
and connects previously separate character storylines.

STAGE 4 — SUPREME FRONTIERS - The story approaches the limits of
existing understanding and raises questions at the highest level of the
setting.

This is a structural framework only. The specific mysteries, clues,
reveal order, antagonists, and protagonists have not all been defined.

Established constraints: - Multiple protagonists and interconnected
storylines. - No direct Rama, Krishna, or Kalki avatars entering
Universe B. - No predetermined Kali demon as the final boss. - The
central threat should be original and may be systemic, but its exact
nature remains open. - Mystery reveals should be gradual; do not explain
the entire cosmology too early.

11. SUCCESSION, TENURE, AND RETIREMENT
======================================================================

[CANON at general level] - Yama, Vidhata, and Chitragupta are
role-holders whose positions operate more like offices than eternal
personal identities. - They have lifespans or tenures, and retirement
exists. - Current or previous office-holders can choose or nominate
successors.

[OPEN] - The exact appointment hierarchy. - Whether nomination requires
approval by another office or by a Supreme authority. - Whether all
three positions follow identical succession rules. - How vacancies,
disputes, and failed succession are handled.

Ananta is not a living office-holder and does not have a tenure or
retirement.

======================================================================
14. CONTINUITY RULES FOR FUTURE DEVELOPMENT
======================================================================

When adding a new idea:

1.  Identify the universe it applies to.
2.  Identify the relevant system: geography, soul administration,
    destiny, power, hierarchy, or story mystery.
3.  Check whether it conflicts with existing canon.
4.  Mark it as [PROPOSAL] until approved.
5.  If approved, update the correct section and record any dependent
    changes.
6.  Leave unresolved mechanics marked [OPEN].
7.  Keep scriptural inspiration distinct from fictional inventions.
8.  Never change Universe A to solve a problem in Universe B.
9.  Do not make one system responsible for every phenomenon. Soul
    administration, destiny records, powers, and cosmic geography have
    distinct purposes.
10. Prefer a clear, limited rule over a broad rule that creates several
    unintended contradictions.
