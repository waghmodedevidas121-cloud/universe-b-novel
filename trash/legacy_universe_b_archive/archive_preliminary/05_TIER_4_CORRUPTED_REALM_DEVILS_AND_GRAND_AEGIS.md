## 1. The Nature of Devils: Era 2 Soul-Sorting Collapse
*(दैत्यांचे स्वरूप: द्वितीय युगातील आत्म-विघटनाचा परिणाम)*

In Universe B, the beings of the Corrupted Realm are **NOT biological mortals, nor generic folklore demons**. They are officially designated as **Devils (अधो-दैत्य / Corrupted Soul-Lifeforms)**:

### 1.1 The Genesis of Corruption
- During the **Era 2 Void Cataclysm**, the High Council's cosmic soul-sorting apparatus (*Karmic Reincarnation Engine*) sustained direct void damage.
- Hundreds of millions of dying souls were trapped in the toxic abyssal basin. Stripped of biological flesh and bombarded by void entropy, their soul-matrices calcified, mutating into predatory spiritual entities driven by existential hunger.

### 1.2 Biology vs. Spiritual Form
- Unlike Humans, Asuras, Devas, or Beasts, **Devils possess NO true biological birth or organic flesh**.
- In Tier 4, they construct artificial external chassis by welding together black volcanic slag, fossilized bone, scrap iron, and phosphorus-soaked wires to project physical mass.
- When destroyed, their physical shells crumble into ash, leaving only a fading karmic soul-thread (*Atma-Sutra*).

## 2. The Grand Aegis & The Soul-Only Summoning Filter
*(महा-प्राकार व काय-त्याग नियम)*

```

    ═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    [ THE GRAND AEGIS (महा-प्राकार) ]
    - 5,000-mile runic basalt wall + high-frequency spiritual resonance shield.

    ═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                           │
       [ TIER 4: CORRUPTED REALM (TAMO-LOKA) ]
```

### 2.1 The Grand Aegis (महा-प्राकार)
- A monumental 5,000-mile defensive barrier separating the southern borders of the living realms from the abyssal wastes.
- Anchored by high-frequency spiritual resonance pylons that generate a lethal vibrational curtain. Any devil attempting to physically climb or tunnel through The Grand Aegis has its soul-threads incinerated into vapor within milliseconds.

### 2.2 The "Kay-Tyag" (Soul-Only) Rule (काय-त्याग नियम)
When an outlaw cultist or rogue scholar performs a forbidden blood ritual to summon a devil across The Grand Aegis:
1. **The Physical Barrier Holds:** The Grand Aegis physically blocks 100% of non-living matter and biological tissue. A devil cannot bring its 10-ton basalt armor, spiked iron clubs, toxic scrap weapons, or abyssal chemical reserves into the Astral Realm.
2. **The Spiritual Filter:** Only the devil’s pure, naked spiritual consciousness (*Sukshma-Atman*) can squeeze through the ritual breach.
3. **Mortal Vulnerability:** Because summoned devils lack their abyssal armor in the Astral Realm, they arrive disoriented, fragile, and desperate for a biological host. An ordinary human squad armed with rifled carbines, silver-tipped cartridges, or cold-iron salt shells can successfully destroy an un-anchored summoned devil.

## 3. Wasteland Survival & The Armored Scrap-Crawlers
*(नाही रेल्वे, केवळ चिलखती क्रॉलर्स)*

Unlike the civilized sub-realms connected by double-gauge railway tracks, **the Corrupted Realm possesses ZERO conventional railway lines**:
- The shifting, corrosive acid marshes, toxic tectonic fissures, and violent soul-storms destroy stationary steel rails within forty-eight hours.
- **The Armored Scrap-Crawlers (चिलखती क्रॉलर्स):** Transportation is executed via colossal, treaded wasteland crawlers salvaged from Era 2 war-machinery. These diesel-and-coal behemoths feature two-inch riveted boiler-plate armor, heavy cow-catchers, chemical air-scrubbers, and rooftop mortar tubes to withstand fiend ambushes.

### 4.1 The Sovereign Abyss & The "Fake Gods" (अधो-देव)
- Governed by ancient, powerful devil entities who proclaim themselves to be the "True Cosmic Gods" and brand the High Council above as counterfeit usurpers.
- **Paundraka (The Counterfeit Sovereign):** Sits upon the Obsidian Throne of Netherholme. Projects dazzling celestial illusions of golden light while his true vessel decomposes beneath barbed iron braces; wields a serrated mechanical disc (*Yantra-Chakra*) burning toxic phosphorus.
- **Shataghati (Warlord of Dread-Hold):** A 10-foot biological terror composed of fossilized dinosaur bone and riveted scrap-iron; wields a 1,200-pound spiked mace (*Vajra-Musala*).
- **Trishira (The Three-Mind Alchemist):** Master of neurotoxins and soul-dissolving acids; brews stabilizers to keep devil vessels from decomposing.

### 4.2 The Penitent Outlaws & Scavengers
- Population: ~200,000 exiled human, asura, and rogue cultivators cast over the wall for high treason, murder, or forbidden research.
- **Rust Haven:** A lawless, multi-tiered shantytown of welded boiler plates and rusted cargo hulls. Governed through armed truce by **Hamsa and Dimbaka**—twin mercenary brothers with an unbreakable bond of mutual loyalty.
- **Contraband King Maricha:** Operates *The Trench Exchange*, smuggling high-grade obsidian ores and ancient pre-Sundering relics past The Grand Aegis to corrupt merchant houses in Samudra-Pattanam.

### 4.3 The Seventh Cosmic Seal & Shrutayudha
- In the pitch-black void of the deepest southern trench sits the ancient warrior **Shrutayudha**. Chained to a meteoric iron pillar for millennia, he wears runic cold-iron blindfolds, guarding the volatile **Seventh Primeval Dark Entity** with his petrified familiar, *Andha-Kāka*.
