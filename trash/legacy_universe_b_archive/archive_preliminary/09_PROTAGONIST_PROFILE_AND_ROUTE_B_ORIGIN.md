## 1. Executive Concept & Anti-Trope Mandate
*(नायकाची मूळ संकल्पना व वास्तववादी चौकट)*

The Protagonist of Universe B is **NOT an overpowered edgelord, a teenage shut-in granted cheats by a laughing goddess, or an emotionless assassin**.

### Core Pillars of Character Construction:
1. **Zero Cheat Skills at Arrival [CANON LOCK]:** Under High Council Soul Administration laws, Route B arrivals receive **zero instant system cheats, zero pre-loaded divine astras, and zero level-up screens**. His survival is earned exclusively through observational grit, applied intellect, physiological conditioning, and high-stakes crisis choices (*Kruti*).
2. **Grounded Civilian Background:** His past life on Earth provided no supernatural martial arts, but gave him a structured analytical mindset—practical systems engineering, thermodynamic principles, and institutional psychology.
3. **Severe Mortal Vulnerability:** He inhabits a fragile Tier 1 mortal human vessel. High ambient aether burns his lungs, heavy Asura hammers can turn his bones to dust, and a Deva solar beam can vaporize him in a microsecond. Every confrontation requires respect for physical limits and tactical caution.
4. **The Blank Destiny Paradox (*Rikta*):** His soul bears the administrative stamp `[STATUS: RIKTA / UNINDEXED]`. This does not make him immune to karma or fate; it simply means his future is unwritten—an anomaly that draws the quiet attention of both cosmic auditors and earthly oligarchs.

- **Past Life:** Neil was an industrial safety inspector and junior thermodynamic systems analyst working on power-grid infrastructures in India. 
- **The Tragedy:** Died during an unexpected structural failure and steam turbine rupture while evacuating factory workers during a midnight blackout. His final mortal act was physical sacrifice under high thermal pressure—a karmic imprint that resonates with his soul's stubborn resilience.
- **Cognitive Assets:**

## 4. Physical Vessel, Senses & The Aether Shock
*(भौतिक शरीर, शारीरिक मर्यादा व आयामी धक्का)*

### 4.1 The First Breath: Sensory Reality
Awakening on the cold shingle beach of Kshira-Spur is not poetic. It is visceral and harsh:
- **The Scent:** Bitter brine, dead sea kelp, wet coal dust, and the sharp sulfur tang of distant steam-cutter boilers.
- **The Cold:** Mud and wet gravel soaking through a coarse burlap prisoner-smock. Shivering violently as mortal nervous systems reboot.
- **The Hearing:** The deafening, rhythmic clanking of pneumatic harbor cranes, steam sirens echoing across deep-water docks, and the harsh, polyglot shouting of dockworkers in Anglo-Dutch and Prakrit maritime slang.

### 4.2 Biological Bottlenecks & Aether Shock
- **Mortal Frailty:** The human vessel he inhabits is completely uncultivated (*Tier 1 Mundane*). It has narrow, blocked Nadis (energy channels).
- **Aetheric Burning:** Attempting to breathe in areas of dense aether concentration causes coughing fits, throat constriction, and severe nausea. He must acclimate through disciplined breath regulation (*Pranayama*).
- **Chronic Symptoms:** Sharp phantom stings along his left collarbone where the *Rikta* soul-mark is inscribed, flaring whenever high-tier spiritual entities (such as a passing Deva skyship or an Asura war-golem) pass overhead.

## 5. Psychological Voice & Internal Philosophy
*(मानसिकता, विचारसरणी व भावनिक सूर)*

### 5.1 Internal Voice & Monologue
- **Language Register:** Sharp, modern, grounded Hinglish/Marathi pragmatic realism in his private thoughts; courteous, cautious, and calculating when speaking aloud to outer-world residents.
- **No Grandiosity:** Does not dream of "conquering the cosmos" or "building a harem". His immediate priorities are food, warm boots, clean water, and securing a safe, legal identity papers (*Pravesha-Patra*).
- **Dark Humor:** Uses dry, sarcastic internal wit to cope with terrifying cosmic realities:
  > *"Brilliant. Back home I worried about quarterly rent and boiler pressure valves. Here, the trains are powered by bottled soul-fire, the aristocrats literally glow in the dark, and an oversized bear in the woods can crush a locomotive with one paw. Progress."*

### 5.2 Moral Code: The Unbending Fulcrum
- **Grey Survivalism:** Will pick pockets from dead smugglers, forge cargo manifests, and bribe railway conductors without moral agonizing if it means surviving the night.
- **The Red Line:** Refuses to harm innocent children, will not sell out a comrade who showed him kindness, and despises corrupt oligarchs who treat human lives as cheap coal for factory furnaces.

## 7. Opening Narrative Arc: "The Shingle Beach of Kshira-Spur"
*(कथानकाची सुरुवात: क्षीर-तीर बंदर ते समुद्र-पट्टणम)*

### Chapter 1–3 Trajectory:
1. **The Awakening:** Neil washes up on the freezing gravel spit outside Kshira-Spur. He has no shoes, soaked clothes, and a forged provisional transit slip stamped `[STATUS: RIKTA]`.
2. **The Encounter with the Port Authorities:** Dodging the corrupt naval press-gangs of High Admiral Silas Suhotra. He barters his modern understanding of mechanical gear ratios to a grumpy drydock mechanic for a hot bowl of rice gruel and a pair of greasy canvas trousers.
3. **The First Job:** Hired as a junior ledger clerk and telegraph line-tester for the *Samudra Salvage Guild* under Diver Finn Matsya. Neil uses his systems engineering eye to detect anomalies in sunken pre-Sundering cargo manifests.
4. **The Looming Shadow:** Intercepts an encoded telegraph signal indicating that a shipment of forbidden abyssal obsidian from the Corrupted Realm is being smuggled through the port—putting him directly in the crosshairs of both Contraband King Maricha’s outlaws and Central Auditor Satyaketu’s investigators.
