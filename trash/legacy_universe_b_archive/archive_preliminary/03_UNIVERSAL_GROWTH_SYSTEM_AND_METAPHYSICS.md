## 1. Core Metaphysical Bedrock: No Video-Game Stats

In Universe B, cultivation and personal power are **not a floating holographic video-game menu**. There are no numerical hit points, level-up popups, or arbitrary stat screens. 

Power evolution is governed by:
1. **Thermodynamic & Biological Reality:** Channeling supernatural energy causes physical friction—elevated body temperature, meridian micro-tears, neural exhaustion, and bone strain.
2. **The Tri-Guna Balance (त्रिगुण):** Every soul balances *Sattva* (clarity/harmony), *Rajas* (passion/kinetic drive), and *Tamas* (density/inertia). Imbalance leads to physical degeneration or madness.
3. **The Karmic Recoil of Astras:** High-tier weapons and divine astras do not consume arbitrary mana; they place an existential lien upon the caster's soul-threads. Misuse causes permanent spiritual burns (*Kāca-Dāha* - glass-burns).

### 2.1 Humans: Nadi-Shuddhi (नाडी-शुद्धी) & Technical Adaptation
- **The Mortal Limitation:** Humans possess the most fragile physical vessels. Ambient aether above baseline concentrations causes **Aether-Burn** (cellular mutation and internal hemorrhage).
- **The Path of Mastery:**
  - *Internal Breath Regulation (Pranayama):* Methodical cleansing of the 72,000 internal *Nadis* (energy conduits).
  - *Yantra & Talismanic Integration:* Because mortal bodies cannot store immense aether internally, humans transcribe runic circuits onto paper talismans, copper plates, or firearm ammunition, using machines as external amplifiers.
  - *Tier Ceiling:* 95%+ of humans remain Tier 1 non-powered commoners; only 1-2% achieve Tier 2 Awoken Martialist status, and fewer than one in a million become a Tier 4 Maha-Siddha (like Maharishi Dhaumya or Gargi).

### 2.2 Asuras: Dhatu-Dharana (धातू-धारणा) & Kinetic Stress Hardening
- **The Chthonic Engine:** Asura physiology thrives on physical resistance and extreme environments. Their skin consists of dense, multi-layered keratin and basalt-like chitin.
- **The Path of Mastery:**
  - *Thermal Smelting (Agni-Sadhana):* Asuras sit near active magma vents, absorbing volcanic sulfur and thermal currents directly into their bone marrow.
  - *Trauma Adaptation:* Every bone fracture that heals under runic pressure calcifies into harder density. Asura warlords deliberately subject themselves to artillery concussions and heavy hammer duels to force cellular hardening.
  - *Occupational Cost:* Centuries of inhaling volcanic soot cause chronic occupational pneumoconiosis (*Shwasa-Kshaya*), forcing veteran smiths (like Mayasura) to rely on brass respiratory harnesses.

### 2.3 Devas: Rta-Samyama (ऋत-संयम) & Celestial Harmonic Resonance
- **The Celestial Engine:** Devas are born with natural internal luminescence and high spiritual conductivity. They do not sweat, bruise easily, or generate crude physical body heat.
- **The Path of Mastery:**
  - *Acoustic & Solar Alignment:* A Deva does not "lift weights"; they meditate under crystal spires, aligning their internal soul frequency with the celestial vibration of the sun and stars (*Surya-Mandala*).
  - *Light-Weaving (Prakasha-Rachanā):* Advanced Devas manifest golden aether-wings and supersonic plasma lances by condensing light into solid geometric lines.
  - *Fatal Vulnerability:* Devas have near-zero tolerance for discordant frequencies, toxic gases, or industrial grime. A burst of coal-smoke and sulfur can destabilize a Deva's internal resonance, causing spiritual nausea (*Patana*).

### 2.4 Beasts: Kaya-Kosa (काया-कोश) & Molting Cycles
- **The Primal Engine:** Wild beasts and apex titans lack written language and formal academies; their growth is strictly Darwinian, ecological, and generational.
- **The Path of Mastery:**
  - *Mineral & Flora Grazing:* Consuming thousand-year-old World-Tree roots, Aether-crystals, and deep-soil fungal networks.
  - *The Great Molt (Nirmoka - कात-टाकणे):* When an apex predator (like King Ursus-Krodha or Empress Kadru) reaches systemic saturation, it undergoes a deep hibernation cycle, violently shedding its outer skin and horn-plates to emerge with denser, bullet-proof calcified bone armor.

## 3. The Kruti Breakthrough Law & The Ananta Chime
*(कृती-सिद्धांत व अनंत-ध्वनि)*

### 3.1 Why Passive Meditation Fails
In Universe B, sitting in an isolated mountain cave for a thousand years will **never** grant higher cosmic tiers. Passive isolation leads to energetic stagnation (*Tamasic Stasis*).

### 3.2 The Kruti Crisis Mandate (कृती)
Evolution occurs **only** when a soul is pushed to the razor-thin brink of total destruction:
1. **The Existential Dilemma:** A mortal soldier defending a trench against an armored titan with their last cartridge; an engineer trapped in an exploding locomotive boiler solving a pressure formula in three seconds; a healer holding together a severed soul-thread while dying of blood loss.
2. **The Shattering of the Vessel:** In that moment of absolute peril, the practitioner's conventional limits shatter, forcing their *Atman* to forcefully expand its energetic capacity.
3. **The Sound of the World (Ananta-Dhvani):**
   - The moment the breakthrough completes, **Ananta's chime** rings out.
   - It is not a loud noise in the sky; it is an intimate, crystal-pure bell sound (*Ghantā-Nāda*) vibrating inside the cultivator's third eye (*Ajna Chakra*), signaling that the universe has acknowledged their systemic ascension.

## 4. Cultivation Costs, Failures & Soul Strain
*(प्रगतीची किंमत, धोके व मर्यादा)*

Advancement is fraught with permanent biological consequences:

1. **Aetheric Calcification (Aether-Toxicity):** Channeling unrefined aether directly into mortal Nadis turns blood vessels brittle and crystallizes lung tissue into stone.
2. **Subtle Body Rupture (Sukshma-Bhanga):** Attempting to cast an Astra beyond one's tier tears the boundary between physical flesh and spiritual soul, causing permanent amnesia, blindness, or immediate soul-rot.
3. **Acoustic Blindness:** Over-tuning celestial harmonic senses leaves Deva seers (such as High Priestess Rohini Selene) physically blind to mundane light.
4. **The Weight of Immortality:** Sages who live for centuries (like Maharishi Dhaumya, age 300+, or Maya Richter, age 400+) suffer severe emotional numbness and traumatic flashbacks from witnessing civilizations rise and burn.
