## 1. The Two-Universe Model (द्वि-विश्व प्रारूप) [CANON]

To protect the sanctity of traditional Hindu scripture while allowing boundless creative freedom for our web novel, reality is divided into two distinct, strictly separated universes:

```
[ UNIVERSE A — TRADITIONAL HINDU MYTHOLOGY ]
- Sacred, immutable timeline (Satya -> Treta -> Dvapara -> Kali Yuga).
- Avatāras descend (Rāma, Kṛṣṇa, Kalki).
- Universe A's own traditional Soul Administration operates normally.
- EXCLUDED: Universe B's custom migration system DOES NOT touch Universe A.
                              │
               (Boundary Rule: Separate Continua)
                              │
[ UNIVERSE B — THE NOVEL'S DIVERGENT UNIVERSE ]
- An independent fictional universe within a broader multiverse.
- Independent cosmic geography: Birth Side, Kshira Sagara boundary, Death Side.
- ZERO-AVATAR PROTOCOL: Direct Rāma, Kṛṣṇa, or Kalki avatars do not enter Universe B.
- No predetermined Kali demon as final boss.
- Custom Soul Administration: Yama (Chief Admin), Vidhata, Chitragupta, and Ananta (The System).
- Balanced Mix Power System (5 Pillars + Secret 6th Principle).
```

---

## 2. Cosmic Geography of Universe B [CANON]

- **Birth Side:** The conceptual expanse where souls descend into incarnation.
- **Kṣīra Sāgara (Cosmic Ocean):** The foundational boundary separating the Birth Side from the Death Side in Universe B.
- **Death Side:** The conceptual expanse where departed souls are gathered.
- **Exclusion Rule:** The Kāraṇa Ocean is **NOT** part of Universe B's internal map or geography [EXCLUDED]. Mahā-Viṣṇu breathing out universes (Brahma-samhitā 5.48) is an external cosmological inspiration, not an internal territory.

---

## 3. Two Distinct Routes of Soul Arrival [CANON]

Arrival into Universe B occurs via two strictly separated pipelines:

### Route A: Official Soul Migration
- Coordinated by **Vidhata** (Universe B).
- Governed by two distinct tests: **Migration Eligibility** and **Cosmic Compatibility** (5 components: Conduct, Desire/Attachment, Gunas, Karma-results, Liberation).
- **Chitragupta** prepares the Soul ID.
- **Ananta (The System)** reviews the record and approves or suggests modifications.
- **Vidhata** links the soul to a new vessel via a karmic bond.

### Route B: Cosmic Reincarnation / Event-Triggered Arrival
- Triggered by monumental events or anomalies within Universe B.
- Some souls pass through the Cosmic Ocean, undergoing variable changes in abilities, powers, or authority.
- **Ananta** automatically registers and records changes that actually occur (Ananta does not grant powers).
- Destiny remains open (*Blank / Rikta*) and develops through free agency and Karma.
