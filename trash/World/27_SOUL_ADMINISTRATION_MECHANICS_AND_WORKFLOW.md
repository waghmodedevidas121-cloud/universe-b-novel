### 1.2 Yama (यम) — Chief Administrator

**Office mandate:** supreme administration of justice and soul-transit. The only office empowered to pass final dissolution orders and to stamp Transit Clearance.

| Function | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Justice (न्याय)** | Post-death adjuration of a soul's deeds against the constitutional standard of justice; formal classification of merit (Punya) and fault (Papa). |
| **Transit Clearance (संक्रमण परवानगी)** | Every lawful entry of a soul into Universe B must carry **Yama's Clearance Seal**. Entry without this seal is classified as Unauthorized Drift and is escalated to the Buffer Zone security grid. |
| **Karmic Audit (कर्म-ऑडिट)** | Final karmic-balance determination drawn from Chitragupta's prior records; the audited result is forwarded as the material on which Vidhata builds destiny. |
| **Dissolution Order (अंतिम विलयन हुकूम)** | Only Yama may order Tamo-guni / criminal souls into the final dissolution pipeline. No other officer, not even Vidhata, may issue this order. |

**Limits:** Yama cannot bind destiny to a vessel. He clears and audits; he does not architect fate.

### 1.3 Vidhata (विधाता) — Destiny Architect

**Office mandate:** architecture of destiny and all three-layer soul-bond (Prana-Sutra) allocation.

| **Prana-Sutra Allocation (कर्मबंध वाटप)** | Authoring and sealing the carrier bond through the three-layer chain Atman → Sukshma-Sharira → Sthula-Sharira. This right is **exclusively Vidhata's**. |
| **Physical Vessel Binding (शरीर-बंधन)** | Binding the soul to the gross vessel at birth (Route A) or at the assigned re-housing event (Route B processed souls). |
| **Correction Path (दुरुस्ती मार्ग)** | On discovery of a grave internal contradiction in a written destiny, Vidhata files a formal correction submission; the final call goes to Yama re-review. Because Ananta's Archive is append-only, corrections appear as **new Corrective Entries**, never as edits to the old record. |

### 1.4 Chitragupta (चित्रगुप्त) — Universal Ledger Keeper

**Office mandate:** universal record-keeping, soul-identity registration, and past-life indexation.

| Function | Specification |

| **Timestamp Authority (समय-हक्क)** | Every official timestamp on Soul Administration records is issued by Chitragupta alone. Medhavi (Senior Ledger Examiner) may cross-verify the timestamps but may not alter them. |
| **Blue vs Red Ink (मुद्रांक प्रणाली)** | **Blue Ink** = normally processed entries. **Red-Ink Seal** = anomaly-flag and audit freeze; a red-ink freeze must be justified with an Audit Warrant within 24 hours (see `25_BUFFER_ZONE_OPERATIONAL_SYSTEM_AND_REGULATIONS.md`). |

### 2.1 Constitutional Identity

| Attribute | Behaviour |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Soulless (आत्मा-विरही)** | No "self", no identity of its own; it is only a rule executor, not a being. |

| **No Tenure (सेवा-काललिमा निषिद्ध)** | Ananta holds no office, is not appointed or removed, and cannot retire; it exists above the service-life paradigm of living officials. |
| **Absolute Immunity (अभेद्यता)** | No Deva, Asura, Sage, Lord, or Dark Entity can touch, corrupt, hijack, or destroy Ananta. |
| **Root Code (मूळ कोड)** | Supreme Trinity (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva) alone. Only their express Supreme Mandate can modify root rules. |

### 2.2 Sound of the World (जगाचा आवाज) — Audio Prompt Channels [CANON LOCK]

Ananta communicates as the **Voice of the World**, never as an individual speaker. Three official channels exist:

| Channel | Trigger | Heard by | Character |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Personal Ping (अंतर्नाद)** | An individual's Skill Unlock / Breakthrough | Only that individual's inner mind | Clear personal proclamation |
| **Cosmic Mandate (वैश्विक घोषणा)** | Supreme Trinity's order to change a global rule in the High Council | Only official Council Title-holders (Sages, Palaks, Seekers) | Solemn, world-wide proclamation |
| **Karmic Transit Chime (कर्मसंक्रमण घंटा — "Sound of the World")** | Soul Administration events: new Soul ID issued, Route B arrival logged, Prana-Sutra sealed, Yama Clearance Seal | Only Soul Administration precinct officers (Niyam, Setuvrata, Lekhya, Dootan, Yama's office) — and the soul itself at its moment of transit | A pure, resonant brass-bell tone; officially designated the **Sound of the World** |

Functions and rules:
- The Karmic Transit Chime is an **administrative acknowledgment signal**: it confirms that a formal registry event was processed and logged.
- The chime **grants nothing**: no skill, no boon, no power spike, no destiny change. Anyone hearing it has received only an official confirmation of a registry event.
- Stored/recorded hardware cannot produce the chime. It is not a mechanical bell; it occurs only when a real Soul Administration event passes through Ananta.
- On Route B arrivals specifically, the chime is a stability-neutral signal; it does **not** double as a "skill grant announcement" (see §4.4).

### 2.3 Read-Only Audit Logs (केवळ-वाचन ऑडिट नोंदी) [CANON LOCK]

- Ananta's Archives are an **append-only ledger**. Entries are added; existing entries are never deleted, rewritten, cloned-over, or expunged.
- **Who may read:** Yama, Vidhata, Chitragupta (for official review), Medhavi (Senior Ledger Examiner, as audit verifier), and the Supreme Trinity directly.
- **Who may write into it:** none of the above. All corrections take the form of **new Corrective Entries**; the original record remains fully intact for permanent comparison.
- **Relation to Chitragupta's ledgers:** Chitragupta maintains the physical department ledgers of record; Ananta's Archive is the **global, incorruptible, physics-independent master record** that cannot be burned, stolen, or forged.

## 3. Route A: Official Soul Migration (अधिकृत आत्मा-स्थलांतर) [CANON LOCK]

Route A is the lawful, administered pipeline for a soul entering Universe B. All six stages must complete in order; no officer may skip a stage, and no stage's result may be back-dated.

### 3.1 Six-Stage Pipeline

```
[ STAGE 1: COMPATIBILITY AUDIT (सुसंगती पडताळणी) ] ─── YAMA
Audit the 5 components; adopt or reject the soul for entry.
        │
        ▼
[ STAGE 2: SOUL ID GENERATION (आत्मा-ओळख निर्मिती) ] ─── CHITRAGUPTA
Official administrative record drafted: Kul, innate traits, aptitudes.
        │
        ▼
[ STAGE 3: SYSTEM REVIEW ] ─── ANANTA
Read-only validation of the record; approval, or request for a corrective entry.
        │
        ▼
[ STAGE 4: LINEAR DESTINY MAPPING (रेषीय नियती-मांडणी) ] ─── VIDHATA
Lihito (Written Destiny) drafted: birth-Kul, Principal Key Turns, destiny arc sealed.
        │
        ▼
[ STAGE 5: PRANA-SUTRA ALLOCATION (कर्मबंध वाटप) ] ─── VIDHATA
Bond assigned across the Atman → Sukshma-Sharira chain; subtle-body bearing set.
        │
        ▼
[ STAGE 6: APPROVED INCARNATION (अधिकृत अवतरण) ]
Gross vessel bound at birth; Karmic Transit Chime sounds; full registry event logged
by Chitragupta and mirrored permanently in Ananta's append-only Archive.
```

Stage notes:
- Stage 3 (System Review) is **advisory-in, authority-out**: Ananta validates the record's completeness and rule-compliance; it never revises content itself. Any defect returns to Chitragupta for a corrective entry, then resubmission.
- Stage 6 concludes with a **tri-seal event**: Yama's Transit Clearance, Vidhata's Prana-Sutra Seal, and Chitragupta's Soul ID stamp all point to the same soul-ID; Ananta logs them as one registry event.

### 3.2 Compatibility Audit — 5 Components [CANON LOCK]

From `11_ISEKAI_AND_SOUL_MIGRATION_RULES.md`; these five must **never be merged** into one blanket test:

| # | Component | Audit Locus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Conduct (आचरण)** | Past deeds and formal consequences; karmic residue on the record. |
| 2 | **Desire & Attachment (इच्छा / आसक्ती)** | Intentions, orientation of the mental trajectory, latent pulls of the psyche. |
| 3 | **State of the Three Gunas (त्रिगुण स्थिति)** | Proportional balance of Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas in the soul as it stands. |
| 4 | **Karma-Results (कर्म-फल अवस्था)** | Which karmic seeds are still active vs already spent. |
| 5 | **Liberation Question (मुक्ति-प्रश्न)** | Whether the soul is bound to rebirth or executed a Liberation Alignment case. |

Each component yields its own separate finding; a weighted verdict is written only by Yama, not by any automated or chance instrument.

### 3.3 Route A Integrity Rules [CANON LOCK]

- **No shortcuts:** skipping or bypassing a stage classifies the entry as Unauthorized Drift, which is logged and escalated to the Buffer Zone audit cell (The Assembly of Sages / Satyaketu). No exceptions.
- **No conditional permits:** Yama's clearance is not issued in "provisional" or "conditional" form. A Route A soul is either fully cleared and permanently registered, or rejected outright.
- **No latent skill pre-load:** Route A souls carry no hidden system-backed backlog that fires later. A Route A soul is an ordinary lawful soul with a sealed written destiny arc — not a system-stamped special identity.

### 4.2 Warden Setuvrata's Intercept — `[STATUS: RIKTA / UNINDEXED]` [CANON LOCK]

On intercept at the arrivals chamber, Setuvrata stamps a provisional file status:

| Field | Precise Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **RIKTA (रिक्त)** | The soul's destiny page **is Blank**. Its future is not pre-written as a fixed linear narrative. Karma still operates fully; only the fixed life-narrative is absent. |
| **UNINDEXED (अनिर्देशित)** | No permanent past-life index record yet exists in Chitragupta's Archive. Past-Life Index, birth-Kul record, and Aptitude codes are all in "Pending Index" state. |
| **Provisional nature** | The stamp is **temporary**. It holds until Lekhya's verification and Chitragupta's indexation conclude. RIKTA never means "no authority matters here" — it means **run the process, then file it properly**. |

Setuvrata's authority limits: he only records arrivals, stamps the provisional status, and pushes the file onward. He is a **recorder**, not a destiny-setter, not a verdict-assessor, and not the one who issues Soul IDs or vessel matches.

### 4.3 Lekhya Verification (कर्मबंध पडताळणी)

- **Location:** Lekhya's Karmic Verification Bureau — the red-brick administrative complex at the port gate, a library filled with brass catalogs and thousands of drawers.
- **Steps table:**

| Step | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Soul Signature Scan:** a catalog-lock reading of the soul's energy print. |
| 2 | **Database Matching:** full comparison against Chitragupta's official records and karmic pattern lists. |
| 3 | **If Matched:** send a formal issuance request for the permanent Soul ID to Chitragupta's office. Lekhya herself cannot issue, alter, or un-seal anything. |
| 4 | **On Anomaly:** escalate the file to Satyaketu (The Assembly of Sages) under a Red-Ink Seal. |
| 5 | **Final routing:** forward clearance for vessel allocation to Vidhata / Yama's office as appropriate. |

### 4.4 Blank Destiny Mechanics (रिक्त नियती यंत्रणा) [CANON LOCK / CRITICAL]

#### What it IS:

| Component | Behavior |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Unindexed Growth Potential (अनिर्देशित विकास-संभावना)** | With the destiny page blank, no pre-written future arc exists. The soul's future is shaped by its own choices, effort, and karma as they unfold — nothing is pre-mandated. |
| **Ananta does not steer** | Ananta only **logs** any real shifts observed during transit; it never grants, selects, or pushes that outcome. |
| **Open Destiny but karma bound (कर्म अखंड)** | Open Destiny means lack of a pre-written story, not the absence of consequences. Full karmic accounting continues. |

#### What it is NOT:

- **⚠️ NO INSTANT CHEAT SKILLS. FORBIDDEN.** Route B arrivals absolutely receive **no instant granted skill, no pre-loaded cheat, no free power spike at arrival.** If a system-style "gift" appears in even one soul, it is canon-invalid.
- One obtains growth only through **rigorous effort, training, genuine choices, or earned development — never as a birthright delivery.** "Blank page" means unwritten, not "free access to the system's deep arsenal."
- Both Route A (Lihito, written path) and Route B (Rikta, blank path) therefore **never grant instant superior power.** Difference: A has a sealed ordered arc; B has an unwritten one. Both stand under full karmic law and seamless effort requirements.
- Strong transit influence does **not** equal high combat power; power and authority remain distinct (per file 11).

### 4.5 Soul ID Format (आत्मा-ओळख रूप) [CANON LOCK]

Formal string shape used in official records:

```
SOUL-ID: CG / {KUL-CODE} / {EPOCH} / {SERIAL NO.} / {STATUS TAG}
Example: CG / HUM-EAST / E3 / 004217 / LIHITO-SEALED
         CG / UNREG / E3 / 000198 / RIKTA-UNINDEXED
```

- **CG prefix:** Chitragupta Registry line.
- **KUL-CODE:** founding lineage (e.g. HUM for human, DEV for Deva, ASU for Asura, UNREG for unregistered/drift arms).
- **EPOCH:** epoch tag of the filing.
- **STATUS TAG:** either `LIHITO-SEALED` (Route A permanently written) or `RIKTA-UNINDEXED` (Route B, blank — procedural stamp until indexation completes).

## 5. Karmic Bond Mechanics — Prana-Sutra (प्राण-सूत्र: बंध-यंत्रणा) [CANON LOCK]

### 5.1 The Three-Layer Bond Chain (बंध-साखळी)

```
        [ ATMAN · आत्मा ]
               │  ## PRANA-SUTRA link V1 — allocated/sealed by VIDHATA ##
               ▼
[ SUKSHMA-SHARIRA · सूक्ष्म-शरीर ]
  prana-nadi lattice, mental template, karmic carrier (Soul Template)
               │  ## PRANA-SUTRA link V2 — the Mohor Seal, set by VIDHATA ##
               ▼
[ STHULA-SHARIRA · स्थुल-शरीर ]
  gross physical vessel (birth body / assigned re-housing body)
```

- **Function:** Prana-Sutra is the **carrier bond** that transmits a soul's vitality and impression-bearing template down the chain to the flesh. It is a **transmission bond, not a steering device** — it does not think, does not choose, and does not push outcomes.
- **Legal exclusivity:** both the allocation of the first link and the sealing of the second link are **performed only by Vidhata**. No one else may author the chain.

### 5.2 Allocation & Seal Protocol (वाटप व मुद्रण प्रक्रिया)

| Step | Officer | Action | Log |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Allocation | Vidhata | Assign the Prana-Sutra; establish the Sukshma-Sharira bearing | Vidhata record |
| Verification | Lekhya | Check Soul ID vs assigned gross vessel match | Verification record |
| Regulation | Ananta | log the Bond Seal event; **read-only** record | Append-only Archive |

### 5.3 Mismatch Handling and Stabilization (विसंगती व स्थैर्य) [CANON LOCK]

When the soul bears only a partial affinity toward the vessel, the Prana-Sutra shows rejection symptoms in three tiers:

#### A. Rejection Symptom Tiers

| Tier | Subtle-body (Sukshma) symptoms | Gross-body (Sthula) consequences |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier-1 — Mild Discord** | Restlessness and an unsettled inner sense; diminished focus and psychic steadiness | Difficulty maintaining normal mood and daily function |
| **Tier-2 — Moderate Drift** | Sutra resonance weakens; focus and psychic bearing decline steadily | Irregular Prana flow; the vessel's upkeep of the soul weakens visibly; daily strain grows |
| **Tier-3 — Vessel Rejection** | Bond outright severed/false; the soul cannot hold bearing in the template | Prana withdrawal; the body begins to fail the soul; total separation near-inevitable without formal reappraisal |

#### B. Stabilization Protocol (स्थैर्यत्व-प्रक्रिया)

```
[ TIER-1 ] ──► Natural re-stabilization: the soul self-corrects in
               its living environment; minor strain resolves naturally.
    │ (No improvement)
    ▼
[ TIER-2 ] ──► Lekhya's formal review: file forwarded to Vidhata
               for a Bond Adjustment hearing; Lekhya cannot alter the
               bond herself, only escalates it. The genuine
               re-anchoring, bearing re-set, or re-seal is done by Vidhata.
    │ (Still unresolved)
    ▼
[ TIER-3 ] ──► Yama's judicial re-review: adjudicates whether the
               soul is returned to Yama's transit cell for a renewed
               allocation cycle. Only Yama holds this final order.
```

Notes:
- Tier-2 review is **procedural, not punishment-based**; genuine mismatch is an administrative defect, not a fault of any person.
- Tier-3 cases conclude in one of two official verdicts: (a) re-housing into a newly assessed vessel, or (b) formal return of the soul to Yama's transit hold. The old vessel is released and logged.
