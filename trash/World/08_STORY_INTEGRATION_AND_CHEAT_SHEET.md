## 2. The 8 Immortals (Aṣṭa-Cirañjīvīs) - Living Story Anchors

These eight historical figures are cursed or blessed to live on Earth until the end of the current Kali Yuga:

1. **Aśvatthāmā:** Cursed by Lord Kṛṣṇa for attempting to murder the unborn Parīkṣit with a Brahmaśirāstra. Suffers from bleeding, unhealing ulcers on his forehead where his divine gem was gouged out; wanders remote forests in agony until the end of the age.
2. **Mahābali:** The Daitya Emperor residing in Sutala. Visited annually by his subjects; will become the Indra of the next Manvantara.
3. **Veda Vyāsa:** The compiler of the Vedas, author of the Mahābhārata; living chronicler of cosmic time.
4. **Hanumān:** Blessed with eternal life as long as the name of Rāma is spoken on Earth. Resides in deep meditation on Mount Gandhamādana.
5. **Vibhīṣaṇa:** Righteous brother of Rāvaṇa, crowned king of Laṅkā; serves as the moral guide of Rakshasa dharma.
6. **Kṛpācārya:** Master of archery and statecraft; survives as the royal martial preceptor.
7. **Paraśurāma:** The 6th Avatar of Viṣṇu. Master of all Astras, teacher of Bhīṣma, Droṇa, and Karṇa; currently awaiting the arrival of the 10th Avatar (**Kalki**) to train Him in divine weaponry on Mount Mahendra.
8. **Mārkaṇḍeya:** Sage blessed with eternal youth at 16 years of age after Lord Śiva rescued him from Yamarāja’s noose (*Kālāntaka*).

## 3. The Novelist's Logic Checklist (Prevent Plot Holes)

Before finalizing a combat or major lore scene, run through this five-point sanity check:

- [ ] **Astra Check:** Does the character know **both** the invocation (*Prayoga*) and the recall formula (*Upasaṁhāra*)? If not, what is their plan to stop the collateral disaster?
- [ ] **Target Legitimacy:** Is the target an armed combatant? If an Astra is used against an unarmed person, has the karmic backlash (*Tapo-kṣaya* or curse) been calculated?
- [ ] **Boon Loophole Integrity:** If a villain has an "invincible" boon, are you defeating them through a genuine semantic/logical loophole, rather than brute force?
- [ ] **Ascetic Cost:** If a sage utters a curse (*Śrāpa*), remember they must sacrifice their own accumulated Tapobala. Sages do not curse casually without substantial consequence.
- [ ] **Location Rules:** Is the fight in Bhūrloka (Karma-Bhūmi) or a higher/lower realm? Remember that *only on Bhūrloka does action generate fresh karmic destiny*.
