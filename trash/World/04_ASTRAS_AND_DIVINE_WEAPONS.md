## 1. Classification of Weapons (Śastra vs Astra)

In the ancient martial sciences (*Dhanurveda*), armaments are divided into physical weapons (*Śastras*) and metaphysically invoked armaments (*Astras*):

```
                        [ DHOṆURVEDA ARSENAL ]
                                  │
         ┌────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                                 ▼
   [ ŚASTRAS (Mundane/Steel) ]                   [ ASTRAS (Metaphysical) ]
1. Amukta      - Handheld (Sword, Mace).         1. Mantra-Mukta  - Released via Mantra.
2. Mukta       - Projectiles (Spear, Dart).      2. Upasaṁhāra    - Recalled via Counter-formula.
3. Muktāmukta  - Tethered (Discus, Lasso).       3. Divyāstra     - Manifested Divine Payloads.
4. Yantra-Mukta- Siege Engines, Ballistas.
```

### The Delivery Medium of an Astra
An Astra is **not** a physical arrow forged by a blacksmith. It is a primordial cosmic concept or deity-frequency downloaded from the astral plane and bound to a physical carrier. A master warrior can bind a Brahmāstra to an arrow, a spear, a single blade of sacred grass (*Kuśa/Darbha*), or even a drop of water.

### Naming & Terminology Rule [CANON]
- **Original Names:** Weapons, Astras, and Mantras retain their authentic Sanskrit/Hindi names (*Brahmāstra, Sudarśana Cakra, Pāśupatāstra, Nāgapāśa, Vajra, Garuḍāstra*) to preserve cultural weight and grandeur.
- **Modern/English Terms for Mechanics:** Operational mechanics and technical terms stay in modern English (*Piercing Beam, Homing Discus, Constriction Lock, Recoil, Backlash, Retraction Code, Barrier Absorption*) to ensure high readability and dynamic combat pacing.
- **Obscure Concepts Translated:** Deep or obscure metaphysical jargon is simplified into intuitive modern tactical concepts.

## 2. The Four Stages of Astra Mastery

To wield an Astra without self-destruction, a warrior must master four distinct disciplines:
1. **Āvāhana (The Calling):** Attuning one's mind and vocal vibration (*Śabda*) to the ruling deity of the Astra to manifest its energy into the weapon tip.
2. **Prayoga (The Release):** Firing the Astra with precise directional intent, velocity, and metaphysical locks.
3. **Upasaṁhāra / Pratiṣmāra (The Retraction):** Calling back the destructive radiation into one's own subtle body or dissolving it into the aether before catastrophic biosphere damage occurs. (*Ashwatthāma failed here; he knew Prayoga but not Upasaṁhāra, sealing his doom*).
4. **Pratighāta (The Counter/Nullification):** Inscribing an opposing elemental or higher-tier Astra to neutralize an incoming attack mid-flight.

### 3.2 Tier 1: High Deva / Elemental Weapons

- **Brahmāstra:**
  - *Mechanism:* Concentrated metaphysical nuclear yield. Obliterates entire mountain ranges, fortified cities, and armies in a blinding shockwave.
  - *Environmental Cost:* Complete water evaporation, sterility of soil, birth defects in surrounding regions for a generation.
- **Vajra (The Adamantine Thunderbolt):**
  - *Origin:* Forged by Tvaṣṭā from the living spinal column of Sage Dadhīci, who sacrificed his life so his bones (hardened by endless Tapasya) could pierce Vṛtrāsura’s skin.
  - *Effect:* Indra’s premier weapon. Strikes with the speed of natural lightning but with infinite kinetic piercing mass.
- **Āgneyāstra (Weapon of Agni):**
  - Unleashes raging sheets of self-sustaining celestial fire that ordinary water cannot quench.
- **Vāruṇāstra (Weapon of Varuṇa):**
  - Unleashes torrential celestial tidal waves and high-pressure water spouts. The primary counter to extinguish the Āgneyāstra.
- **Vāyavyāstra (Weapon of Vāyu):**
  - Manifests atmospheric gale-force cyclones that lift entire infantries into the clouds, scattering them or dispersing dense poisonous gasses.
- **Yamadaṇḍa / Kāladaṇḍa (The Staff of Death):**
  - The black rod of Yamarāja. Striking an enemy snatches their subtle Prāṇa immediately from the body, causing instantaneous biological death regardless of external armor.

### 3.3 Tier 2: Specialized & Status-Effect Astras

- **Nāgapāśa (The Serpent Bonds):**
  - Arrows transform into venomous celestial vipers that coil around the target, paralyzing muscle groups, constricting breathing, and sealing Prāṇa circulation.
  - *Counter:* The **Garuḍāstra**, which summons golden celestial eagles that devour the serpent cords.
- **Mohanāstra / Saṁmohanāstra:**
  - Affects the Manomaya Kośa (mind sheath). Casts an irresistible wave of slumber, hallucination, and loss of consciousness over an entire battlefield.
  - *Counter:* **Prajñāstra** (Weapon of Awakening/Wisdom) or intense internal Prāṇa surge to wake the senses.
- **Tvaṣṭrāstra:**
  - Induces profound optical and psychic distortion among opposing troops, making comrades appear as demonic enemies. Causes opposing armies to butcher one another in fratricidal confusion.
- **Sūryāstra:**
  - Emits blinding solar radiance that strips away darkness, evaporates water mists, and burns through all forms of Asuric illusion (*Māyā-yuddha*).

## 4. Inviolable Cosmic Rules of Astra Combat

1. **The Purity Failure Clause:**
   - Astras rely on the practitioner's mental harmony and focus. If the warrior's heart is seized by terror, moral decay, or breaks an absolute vow, the sacred syllables (*Mantra-vismaraṇa*) evaporate from memory at the critical second (as happened to Karṇa due to Paraśurāma's curse).
2. **The Disproportionate Retribution Rule:**
   - Discharging a Tier 0 or Tier 1 weapon against an unarmed combatant, fleeing enemy, or civilian enclave causes an immediate karmic backfire (*Pāpa-prapti*), rotting the user’s subtle energy channels (*Nāḍīs*).
3. **The Dual-Collision Catastrophe:**
   - If two Tier 0 Astras (such as two Brahmāśirāstras) detonate against one another in mid-air, the collision creates a dimensional tear that will render the entire planet barren for decades. Universal sages (*Maharṣis*) are cosmically obligated to intervene and halt the clash.
