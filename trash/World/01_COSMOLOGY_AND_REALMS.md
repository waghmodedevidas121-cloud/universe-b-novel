## 1. Traditional Scriptural Cosmology (Universe A Reference)

The universe is shaped like a cosmic egg (*Brahmāṇḍa*), floating in the infinite Causal Ocean (*Kāraṇa Ocean* / *Virajā River*). Within this egg sits a vertical axis centered on **Mount Meru**, divided into 14 major planes of existence (*Caturdaśa Bhuvanāni*):
- **7 Upper Realms (Ūrdhva Lokas):** Spiritual, mental, and celestial planes.
- **7 Lower Realms (Adho Lokas / Bila-Svarga):** Subterranean subterranean realms of sensory luxury, high maya, and subterranean beings.
- **Naraka (Hell Realms):** Located beneath the subterranean planes, above the Garbhodaka ocean; strictly temporary corrective purgatories governed by Yamarāja.

### Cosmic Water Foundations
- **Kāraṇodaka (Causal Ocean):** The ocean where Mahā-Viṣṇu reclines; millions of universes emerge from the pores of His skin with every exhalation and dissolve upon inhalation.
- **Garbhodaka (Womb Ocean):** Fills the lower half of each individual Brahmāṇḍa egg. Garbhodakaśāyī Viṣṇu rests here on the serpent bed of Ādi-Śeṣa. From His navel sprouts the cosmic lotus holding the 14 realms.

### 2.1 The Upper Seven Realms (Ūrdhva Lokas)

1. **Satyaloka (Brahmaloka):**
   - **Nature:** Highest material realm. Inhabitants exist in pure consciousness, devoid of physical grossness, aging, sickness, or physical death.
   - **Lifespan:** Co-terminus with Brahmā’s lifespan (100 Brahmā years = 311.04 trillion human years).
   - **Access:** Only achievable through unblemished Jñāna, highest Bhakti, or unbroken lifetime Tapasya.

2. **Tapoloka:**
   - **Nature:** Realm of pure meditation and ascetic fire (*Tapas*). Inhabited by the four Sanat-kumāras, great ṛṣis who can withstand the heat of universal destruction.

3. **Janaloka:**
   - **Nature:** Populated by demigods and enlightened mystics who wander the cosmos. Resilient against the daily dissolution (*Naimittika Pralaya*).

4. **Maharloka:**
   - **Nature:** The bridge realm. Inhabited by Prajāpatis (cosmic progenitors) and sages like Bhṛgu. When Brahmā’s night falls and lower realms burn, inhabitants ascend to Janaloka.

5. **Svarloka (Svarga / Devaloka):**
   - **Nature:** Celestial paradise capital (Amarāvatī) ruled by Indra. Full of wishing trees (*Kalpavṛkṣa*), wish-granting cows (*Kāmadhenu*), celestial nymphs (*Apsarās*), and divine musicians (*Gandharvas*).
   - **Rule of Stay:** **Puṇya-kṣaya** (Karmic expiration). Inhabitants stay only until their accumulated pious deeds burn out, after which they fall back to Bhūrloka. "Kṣīṇe puṇye martyalokaṁ viśanti".
   - **Vulnerability:** Highly susceptible to invasion by Asuras who out-perform Devas in Tapasya.

6. **Bhuvarloka (Antarīkṣa / Intermediate Realm):**
   - **Nature:** The expanse between Earth and the Sun. Abode of Siddhas, Cāraṇas, Gandharvas, and the realm of the ancestors (*Pitṛloka*).
   - **Function:** Conduit where departed souls transit and where ancestral offerings (*Śrāddha*) are metabolized.

7. **Bhūrloka (Mortal Earth / Karma-Bhūmi):**
   - **Nature:** Gross physical matter. The central and most strategic realm in the entire cosmos.
   - **Cardinal Rule of Bhūrloka:** **It is the ONLY realm where new Karma is generated.** In Svarga and Naraka, souls merely consume past karmic accounts (*Bhoga-Bhūmi*). In Bhūrloka, choices have moral weight, determining ascent to Satyaloka or descent to Pātāla/Naraka.

### 2.2 The Seven Lower Realms (Adho Lokas / Bila-Svarga)
*Crucial distinction:* These are NOT hells. They are subterranean paradises (*Bila-Svarga*), described in the Bhāgavata Purāṇa as possessing greater material luxury, architectural brilliance, and sensual delights than Svarga itself, illuminated by the gems on serpents' hoods rather than the sun.

1. **Atala:** Ruled by Bala (son of Mayāsura). Famous for magical sensory potions (*Hāṭakī*) and illusions that trap mortal desires.
2. **Vitala:** Ruled by a form of Lord Śiva known as Hāṭakeśvara. Source of magical golden metal (*Hāṭaka*) used to forge legendary divine armor.
3. **Sutala:** The most blessed lower realm. Ruled by the Daitya King **Mahābali**. Lord Viṣṇu personally stands guard as a dwarven sentry (*Vāmana*) at Bali’s gate. Indra himself envies Sutala’s prosperity and peace.
4. **Talātala:** Realm ruled by **Mayāsura**, the divine architect of the Asuras and master of *Māyā-vidyā* (sorcery, advanced spatial mechanics, flying fortresses/Tripura).
5. **Mahātala:** Abode of gigantic serpentine beings, sons of Kadrū, led by Takṣaka, Kuhaka, and Suṣeṇa. Fierce, possessing venomous breath, yet deeply devoted to Lord Śiva.
6. **Rasātala:** Stronghold of the formidable Daityas, Dānavas, Panis, Nivātakavacas, and Kāleyas. Warriors born with physical durability capable of challenging Indra's thunderbolt.
7. **Pātāla (Nāgaloka):** The deepest subterranean realm, capital Bhogavatī. Ruled by Nāgarāja **Vāsuki**. At the very bottom sits **Ādi-Śeṣa (Ananta)**, the thousand-headed serpent supporting the entire universe upon one of His hoods.

### 2.3 Naraka (The 28 Purgatorial Spheres)
Located in the space between the lower realms and the Garbhodaka Ocean.
- **Ruler:** **Yamarāja** (The Lord of Justice & Death) and his ledger-keeper **Citragupta**.
- **Nature:** Not eternal damnation. It is a cosmic sanitarium/furnace where stubborn negative karma (*Pāpa*) is incinerated through measured suffering before the soul (*Jīva*) can be given another mortal birth.
- **Notable Divisions:**
  - *Tāmisra:* For thieves and defrauders of others' wealth.
  - *Andhatāmisra:* For deceivers who exploit blind trust.
  - *Raurava:* For those who tormented living beings for physical pleasure (tormented by Ruru beasts).
  - *Kumbhīpāka:* For those who cooked or butchered living beings without cause.
  - *Asipatravana (Forest of Sword-Leaves):* For those who abandoned their sacred oaths (*Pratijñā*) and duties.

## 3. Cosmic Time Cycles (Kāla-Cakra)

Time is cyclical, non-linear, and relativistic across different planes.

### 3.1 Relative Time Flow
- **1 Human Year** = 1 Day and Night of the Devas (6 months Uttarāyaṇa = Day, 6 months Dakṣiṇāyana = Night).
- **12,000 Deva Years** = 1 Mahāyuga (Chaturyuga = 4,320,000 human years).
- **71 Mahāyugas** = 1 Manvantara (reign of one Manu).
- **14 Manvantaras (1,000 Mahāyugas)** = 1 Kalpa = 1 Day of Brahmā (4.32 Billion human years).
- **1 Night of Brahmā** = Equal duration (4.32 Billion years) during which the three lower worlds dissolve in sleep.

## 4. The Dissolutions (Pralaya)

1. **Nitya Pralaya (Perpetual Dissolution):**
   - The momentary death and transformation of cells, bodies, and mortal entities occurring continuously every second.
2. **Naimittika Pralaya (Incidental Dissolution):**
   - Occurs at the end of each Kalpa (day of Brahmā). Brahmā enters cosmic slumber. The lower three lokas (Bhūr, Bhuvar, Svar) are consumed by the fire from the mouth of Saṅkarṣaṇa/Śeṣa and submerged in ocean waters. Higher realms endure.
3. **Prākṛtika Pralaya (Elemental Dissolution):**
   - Occurs at the end of Brahmā’s lifespan (100 Brahmā years / Mahākalpa).
   - The entire material cosmos, all 14 Lokas, the five gross elements (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Space), subtle mind, and intellect dissolve backwards into unmanifest Prakṛti.
4. **Ātyantika Pralaya (Absolute Dissolution):**
   - Personal liberation (**Mokṣa**). When a Jīva shatters the veil of Māyā, realizes unity with Para-Brahman, and exits the cycle of birth and death forever.
