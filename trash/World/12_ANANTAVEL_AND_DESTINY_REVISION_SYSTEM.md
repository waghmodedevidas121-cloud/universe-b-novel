## 1. Core Principle of Ananta (अनंत - The System) [CANON]

**Ananta is NOT a living deity, soul, or independent official. Ananta is THE SYSTEM ITSELF.**
- Has no soul, no consciousness, no emotions, and no independent personal agenda.
- Obeys the **Supreme Orders of the Supreme Trinity (Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Śiva)**.
- Operates as the **Voice of the World** and **Manager of the Archives**.
- **Reflects Reality:** Ananta does not arbitrarily dictate destiny or independently cause soul arrivals. Ananta simply records objective reality with absolute precision.

## 3. Destiny Record States & Dynamic Revision [CANON]

Ananta maintains the dynamic record of existence under two primary states:

1. **Written (लिखित - Lihito):**
   - A complete or partial destiny projection is registered in the Archive based on existing *Prārabdha Karma* and current momentum.
2. **Blank (रिक्त - Rikta):**
   - A prewritten future record is unavailable or unformed.
   - **Open Destiny:** Typical of Isekai / event-triggered souls. They possess free agency to develop through their choices, though **Karma still applies to every action**.
3. **Dynamic Revision:**
   - When a soul undergoes a monumental karmic breakthrough (*Tapasya*, immense sacrifice, broken vow) or when a Supreme Entity intervenes, the recorded entry may destabilize.
   - Ananta automatically updates the ledger: a locked line may mutate, or dissolve entirely into a **Blank (Rikta)** state.
