## 1. The Two Distinct Arrival Routes [CANON]

Soul arrival into Universe B operates through two strictly separated mechanisms:

```
                          [ SOUL ARRIVAL PIPELINES ]
                                      │
         ┌────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                                         ▼
[ ROUTE A: OFFICIAL SOUL MIGRATION ]              [ ROUTE B: EVENT-TRIGGERED ARRIVAL ]
- Administered by Vidhata.                        - Triggered by monumental events in Universe B.
- Assessed via Eligibility & Compatibility.       - Some souls transit through the Cosmic Ocean.
- Chitragupta creates Soul ID.                    - Transit may induce shifts in power/authority.
- Ananta reviews & approves record.               - Ananta automatically logs real changes.
- Vidhata seals Karmic Bond to new birth.         - Destiny remains open (Blank / Rikta).
```

---

## 2. Route A: Official Soul Migration Workflow

1. **Origin Availability:** A soul becomes available under external circumstances.
2. **Vidhata's Review:** Universe B's Vidhata considers whether migration should proceed.
3. **Assessment Criteria (Must NOT be merged):**
   - **Migration Eligibility:** Assesses whether the soul is qualified to enter Universe B.
   - **Cosmic Compatibility (5 Components):**
     1. *Conduct:* Past actions and consequences.
     2. *Desire & Attachment:* Intentions and mental trajectory.
     3. *State of the Three Gunas:* Proportional balance of Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas.
     4. *Karma-Results:* Which karmic seeds remain active vs spent.
     5. *Liberation Question:* Whether bound for rebirth or aligned with a path to liberation.
4. **Soul ID Preparation:** Chitragupta personally prepares the administrative record (Kul, innate traits, aptitudes).
5. **System Review:** Ananta reviews the record, approves it, or suggests adjustments.
6. **Karmic Bond Incarnation:** Following approval, Vidhata attaches the soul to its designated mortal vessel through a karmic bond.

---

## 3. Route B: Event-Triggered Arrival & Cosmic Ocean Transit

- Souls arriving through this route are drawn by critical events, metaphysical rifts, or phenomena in Universe B.
- **Cosmic Ocean Transit:** Souls may traverse the Cosmic Ocean (*Kṣīra Sāgara* boundary).
- **Variable Transit Effects:** The transit may awaken latent traits, alter authority, grant new capacities, or leave the soul unchanged. (Strong influence does NOT automatically equal high combat power; authority and power remain distinct).
- **System Role:** Ananta does **not** cause the arrival, choose the souls, or grant powers. Ananta **automatically logs** whatever shifts actually occur.
