## 0. Universal Naming & Clan Sociology Doctrine
*(बहु-सांस्कृतिक नामकरण, जैविक कुळ-परंपरा व सामाजिक वास्तव)*

Universe B is an expansive, ancient cosmos where millennia of trade, migration, and isolation after the Great Partition created **distinct cultural, linguistic, and naming traditions** across each sub-realm:

1. **Human Sub-Realm:** Dual heritage—classical Indo-Aryan dynastic traditions in the agrarian heartland (Kuru-Panchala) combined with Anglo-Dutch maritime and industrial vernacular in coastal port syndicates.
2. **Asura Sub-Realm:** Heavy Germanic, Nordic, and Slavic industrial naming reflecting volcanic forge culture, pneumatic engineering, and chthonic mineral wealth.
3. **Deva Sub-Realm:** Greco-Roman, Latinate, and Elysian aristocratic naming reflecting solar resonance courts, crystal spires, and acoustic light-weaving.
4. **Beast Sub-Realm:** Gaelic, Nordic, and mythic apex predator titles rooted in ancient territorial instincts and Darwinian molting cycles.
5. **The Central Sub-Realm:** Cosmopolitan global naming representing the neutral diplomatic crossroads of the cosmos.
6. **Tier 4 Corrupted Realm:** Outlaw and ancient Danava monikers, scavenger cants, and titles of the "Fake Gods".
7. **Tier 2 Buffer Zone & Tier 1 High Council:** Classical Sanskrit administrative titles (*Vidhana-Patra*) and primordial Vedic offices (*Deva-Bhasha*).
