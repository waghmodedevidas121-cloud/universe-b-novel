## 1. Nature & Mandate of Tier 2 (The Buffer Zone)
*(मध्यम-लोक / प्रशासकीय व सैनिकी सेतू — Antariksha)*

Tier 2 is the **cosmic shock-absorber and administrative bridge** of Universe B. Positioned directly between the mortal, industrial tumult of Tier 3 and the sanctified 100% non-violent High Council of Tier 1:

1. **Physical Reality:** The Buffer Zone is not a flat planet; it is an engineered celestial bridge—**The Grand Setu (महा-सेतू)**—constructed of petrified starlight granite, runic iron beams, and gravitational anchors (*Guru-Keela*).
2. **Administrative Function:** Houses the customs screening depots, quarantine hospitals, military mobilization rails, and higher academic archives of the cosmos.
3. **Linguistic Atmosphere:** Operates in **Classical Sanskrit Administrative Jurisprudence** (*Vidhana-Patra*). All decrees, quarantine seals, and transit permits are issued in strict Sanskrit administrative formulas.

### 2.1 Sector 1: Eastern Transit & Customs Screening
- Monitors incoming souls arriving from the Kshira Ocean (Route A standard migration and Route B drift souls).
- Screens travelers for abyssal soul-taint, illegal dark grimoires, and contraband dimensional artifacts before allowing transit to outer realms.

### 2.2 Sector 2: Western Frontier & Lauha-Prakara (Iron Frontier)
- Heavily fortified battlements and heavy artillery bunkers guarding the cosmic approach to the Grand Aegis.
- Houses the elite **Order of Guardians (पालक सेना)** trained to repel void-rift leaks and rogue abyssal entities.

### 2.3 Sector 3: Northern Administrative & Academic Sector
- **Setu Vidya-Peeth (सेतू विद्यापीठ):** The supreme university of metaphysics, dimensional engineering, and karmic law.
- **The Assembly of Sages (ऋषि-मंडळ):** Deliberative chambers where field auditors, research fellows, and senior proctors draft anomaly reports for the High Council.

### 2.4 Sector 4: Southern Railway Terminus & Transit Hub
- The military rail yard where double-gauge armored steam locomotives (*Setu-Vajra-Ratha*) are fueled and dispatched to seal spatial rifts across the five sub-realms.

## 4. Key Personnel of the Grand Setu

1. **Acharya Shrutasena (आचार्य श्रुतसेन) — Chief Proctor of Setu Vidya-Peeth:**
   - Strict disciplinarian, master of classical Sanskrit debate (*Vada-Shastra*). Carries a black petrified runic cane and crystal memory-slates. Fiercely protects young scholars from political corruption.
2. **Trainmaster Susharma (सुशर्मा) — Chief Traffic Controller of the Setu Lines:**
   - Obsessive railway genius. Cannot tolerate a 5-second delay in scheduled train movements. Personally pilots locomotives through void-storms during military mobilizations.
3. **Akampana (अकंपन) — Chief Sapper & Siege Architect:**
   - Stoic, soft-spoken structural genius. Designed the reinforced concrete and basalt bunkers of the Western Iron Frontier.
4. **Senior Field Auditor Satyaketu (सत्यकेतु):**
   - Chief Field Inspector of the Assembly of Sages. Wields the **24-Hour Red-Ink Seal (Rakta-Mudra)**; has authority to freeze bank vaults and suspend royal rail shipments pending corruption inquiries.
5. **Marshal-General Vajraketu (वज्रकेतु):**
   - Commander of the Setu Vanguard; patrols the boundary corridors with twin bolt-action aether-rifles and bonded armored hounds.
6. **Grand Master Yuktinath (युक्तिनाथ):**
   - Director of the Seeker Guild; tests cosmic anomalies using steam-and-prana calipers and micro-telescopes.
