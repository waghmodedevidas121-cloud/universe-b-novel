## 1. The Supreme Trinity & Ananta (परम-सत्य व अनंत)

Universe B is not an accidental void or a static world. It is an evolving, living cosmic organism (*Bālya-Avasthā* - Childhood Stage) governed by fundamental metaphysical forces:

### 1.1 The Supreme Trinity (त्रिदेव - Almighty & Transcendent)
- **Brahma (परम-ब्रह्म / विधाता-मूळ):** Supreme Creator. Unbound by mortal cultivation tiers, levels, or physical constraints. Operates as the initial source code of cosmic creation.
- **Maha-Vishnu (महा-विष्णु / पालक-मूळ):** Supreme Sustainer. Rests upon the cosmic foundation of *Ananta-Shesha*. Maintains cosmic equilibrium (*Sanātana-Rta*). 
- **Rudra / Shiva (सदा-शिव / महेश):** Supreme Destroyer. Sovereign of cosmic dissolution (*Maha-Samhāra*). Beyond beginning and end (*Anādi-Ananta*).
- **The Zero-Avatar Protocol [CANON]:** Direct personal incarnations (like Rama, Krishna, or Kalki) do not physically walk Universe B. The cosmos is intentionally designed to allow souls to evolve through their own karmic friction and free will.

### 1.2 Ananta (अनंत - The Cosmic System Engine & Silent Watcher)
- **Nature:** Ananta is the sentient living substrate upon which Universe B is anchored. It is neither an intrusive video-game interface nor an emotional deity.
- **The Sound of the World (Ananta-Dhvani - अनंत-ध्वनि):** When a soul shatters mortal bottlenecks through genuine crisis, sheer will, and definitive action (*Kruti*), Ananta resonates with an acoustic chime heard across the soul's inner meridians, acknowledging systemic evolution.
