## ⚡ The Ten Inviolable Canonical Invariants

1. **Zero Floating Blue Menus:** No floating game screens, stat sheets, or artificial level-ups. Power is unlocked through physical martial discipline, *Nadi-Sadhana*, and high-stakes survival *Kruti*.
2. **The 4-Bracket Power Pyramid:** 100% of mortals possess spiritual affinity (Bracket 1: 0–60% Ember/Dormant; Bracket 2: 60–80% D-Rank Active; Bracket 3: 80–90% C-Rank Reincarnates; Bracket 4: 90–100% High-Rank B/A/S Elites).
3. **The Grounded Transit Law:** No magical sky-trains or 19th-century steam pistons. Transport consists of Beast-Carts (Bulls, Boars, Horses) on land, Water-Drakes on rivers, and Bonded Flying Beasts in the air.
4. **Physical Soul-Bond Beasts:** Zero pocket storage or Pokéball tattoos. Beasts are living, breathing companions (like Neel's one-eared Dusk-Scrapper, *Sheru*).
5. **The Grand Aegis Doctrine:** Built as a reverse-engineered miniature *Kshira-Sagara*, the 5,000-mile crystalline barrier radiates ambient nourishment to sustain Tier-4 mutated souls, ensuring zero cosmic injustice.
6. **Dual-Summoning Codex:** Type-1 Otherworlders summoned via sacred Rigvedic hymns; Type-2 Devils summoned across flesh-severed planar barriers via Atharvavedic/Tantric vessel anchoring.
7. **The S-Rank Newbie Reality:** S-Rank is the peak of mortal glory in Tier 3 Astral Shards, but merely an entry-level recruit (*Ardha-Rathi*) upon ascending to the Tier 2 Great Setu.
8. **Divine Boon Requirement (*Varadan*):** True *Divyastras* cannot be learned via training; they require intense *Tapasya* and a Direct Divine Boon conferring sacred invocation mantras.
9. **Ascending Linguistic Gradient:** Rough street slang in Tier 4 → Vedic-Anime Hinglish in Tier 3 → Classical Sanskrit jurisprudence in Tier 2 → Pure Vedic Chants in Tier 1.
10. **The 3-Volume Narrative Veil:** Volumes 1–3 present as a gritty Central City tournament adventure with zero explicit Hindu mythology names, setting up the massive cosmic twist at the end of Volume 3.
11. **Dual-Language Primary Authoring Mandate:** Every chapter, prologue, interlude, and epilogue is authored natively in **both Canonical English Novel Prose and Official Muse/Crunchyroll-Style Hindi Dub Novel Prose**. Both editions are primary canon; proper nouns and skills stay Roman-script English, with casual Hindi prose ending with `।`.
